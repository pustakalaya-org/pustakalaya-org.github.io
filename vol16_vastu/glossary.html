    Glossary¶

    Terms are Sanskrit unless marked (Ta. = Tamil, Ma. = Malayalam, Gu. = Gujarati, Hi. = Hindi, Bn. = Bengali, Pe. = Persian, Ar. = Arabic). Dates and fuller explanations are given in the chapters.

    Term Meaning
    abhaya Gesture of reassurance: the hand raised, palm outward
    adhiṣṭhāna Moulded base of a southern temple
    agrahāra, brahmadeya Village or settlement granted to brāhmaṇas by a king
    āmalaka Ribbed, disc-shaped crowning member of a northern temple tower
    aṇḍa “Egg”: the hemispherical dome of a stūpa
    aṅgula “Finger”: the basic unit of measurement; in images, one-twelfth of a tāla
    antarāla Vestibule between the sanctum and the hall
    ardhamaṇḍapa Entrance porch in front of the hall of a temple
    āsana Seated posture of an image; also a seat
    āyādi Astrological calculations testing the dimensions of a building for auspiciousness
    bāḍa (Odia) Wall zone of an Odishan temple
    bāhāḥ (Newari) Courtyard monastery of the Kathmandu valley
    baṅglā (Bn.) Curved roof derived from the bamboo-and-thatch hut of Bengal, adopted in Mughal and Rajput architecture
    bāolī (Hi.) Stepwell
    basadi (Kannada) Jain temple in Karnataka
    bhadra Central projection of each face of a northern temple
    bhaṅga Bend of the body in a standing image (samabhaṅga, ābhaṅga, tribhaṅga, atibhaṅga)
    bhūmija Northern temple type with rows of miniature spires
    bhūparīkṣā Examination of a building site
    brahmapada, brahmasthāna Central squares of the vāstupuruṣamaṇḍala, assigned to Brahmā
    caitya A sacred object or place; in architecture, the apsidal hall of worship with a stūpa
    caturmukha “Four-faced”: a shrine with four images facing the four directions, entered from four sides
    chahār bāgh (Pe.) “Four gardens”: a walled garden divided into quarters by walkways and water channels
    chattra Umbrella; the parasols crowning a stūpa
    chhajjā (Hi.) Deep sloping stone eave carried on brackets
    chhatrī (Hi.) Small domed pavilion on pillars
    dargāh (Pe.) Shrine that grows around the tomb of a Sufi saint
    daśatāla Proportional canon of ten face-lengths, used for the principal deities
    devadāsī “Servant of the god”: a woman dedicated to a temple as dancer or singer
    devakoṣṭha Niche for an image in a temple wall
    dhyānaśloka Verse describing a deity, used for visualisation
    dikpāla Guardian of one of the directions
    Drāviḍa Southern temple form with a tiered superstructure; in southern texts, a class with an octagonal cupola
    gaṇḍī (Odia) Body of the tower or roof of an Odishan temple
    garbhagṛha “Womb-house”: the sanctum
    garbhanyāsa Consecration deposit placed in the foundations
    gavākṣa, candraśālā Horseshoe-arch motif derived from the caitya window
    ghāṭ (Hi.) Flight of steps leading down to a river or tank
    gopura Gateway tower of a southern temple enclosure
    gṛhapraveśa Rite of first entry into a new house
    hāra Parapet of miniature pavilions round the edge of each roof of a southern superstructure
    harmikā Square railing on top of a stūpa dome
    hasht bihisht (Pe.) “Eight paradises”: plan of a central domed chamber surrounded by eight subsidiary spaces
    hasta, kiṣku Cubit, usually of 24 aṅgulas
    imāmbāṛā (Hi.) Hall for the Shiʿi commemoration of Muḥarram
    īwān (Pe.) Vaulted hall or deep arched portal open on one side
    jagamohana (Odia) Hall of an Odishan temple, with a pyramidal roof
    jagatī Terrace or platform; also a base moulding
    jālī (Hi.) Pierced stone screen
    jaṅghā Wall zone of a northern temple
    jātaka Story of one of the previous lives of the Buddha
    jharokhā (Hi.) Projecting balcony or window, often screened
    jīrṇoddhāra Renovation of a temple
    kalaśa Pot; the pot-shaped finial of a northern temple
    kapota Curved projecting cornice of a southern building
    karṇa Corner projection of a temple wall
    khākharā deuḷa (Odia) Oblong Odishan shrine with a barrel-vaulted roof
    khānqāh (Pe.) Hospice for Sufis
    kīrttimukha “Face of glory”: a grimacing monster mask used as an auspicious and protective motif
    kumuda Torus moulding of a southern base
    kuṇḍa Stepped tank or pool
    kūṭa, śālā, pañjara Square, oblong and smaller miniature pavilions on a southern superstructure
    lāñchana Emblem identifying a Jain tīrthaṅkara
    Latina Northern temple type with a single curved spire
    liṅga Emblem of Śiva, the principal object of Śaiva worship
    madrasa (Ar.) College of religious learning
    maṇḍala Diagram; in architecture, the grid on which a building is laid out
    maṇḍapa Pillared hall
    maṇḍūka (caṇḍita) The grid of 64 squares (8 \(\times\) 8)
    maqbara (Ar.) Tomb
    marman Vulnerable point of the vāstupuruṣa, where pillars and doors should not be placed
    mastaka (Odia) Crowning members of an Odishan temple
    maṭha Monastery of a Hindu ascetic order
    miḥrāb (Ar.) Niche in the qibla wall of a mosque
    minbar (Ar.) Stepped pulpit beside the miḥrāb of a mosque
    mistrī (Hi.) Master builder or craftsman
    mithuna Amorous couple, an auspicious motif
    mudrā Symbolic gesture of the hands
    Nāgara Northern temple form with a curved tower; in southern texts, a class with a square cupola
    nālukeṭṭu (Ma.) Kerala house with four ranges round a court
    navakalevara Periodic renewal of the wooden images of Jagannātha at Puri
    netronmīlana Ritual opening of the eyes of an image
    nirandhāra, sāndhāra Temple without, and with, an inner ambulatory round the sanctum
    pādabandha, pratibandha Two of the principal families of southern temple base
    pañcāyatana “Five-shrined”: a principal shrine with four subsidiary shrines at the corners of its terrace
    paramaśāyika The grid of 81 squares (9 \(\times\) 9)
    parchīn kārī (Pe.) Inlay of coloured and semi-precious stones in marble (pietra dura)
    phāṃsanā Northern roof form of stacked horizontal slabs
    pīḍhā deuḷa (Odia) Odishan building with a stepped pyramidal roof
    pīḍhā (Odia) Horizontal tier of an Odishan pyramidal roof
    pīshtāq (Pe.) Monumental arched portal projecting above the façade
    pol (Gu.) Gated residential street or neighbourhood in Ahmadābād
    pradakṣiṇāpatha Path for circumambulation
    prākāra Enclosure wall of a temple
    prāṇapratiṣṭhā “Establishment of breath”: the consecration of an image
    prāsāda Temple (literally “palace”)
    prastara Entablature of a southern building
    pratimālakṣaṇa The characteristics of images; the iconographic part of the texts
    puṣkariṇī, kuḷam (Ta.) Temple tank
    qibla (Ar.) Direction of Mecca, towards which a mosque is oriented
    rāgamālā “Garland of rāgas”: a series of paintings personifying the musical modes
    ratha Projection of a temple wall; also a chariot, and the monolithic shrines of Māmallapuram
    rauza (Ar.) “Garden”: a tomb set in a garden
    rekhā deuḷa (Odia) Odishan sanctum with a curved tower
    sarāʾī (Pe.) Caravanserai: fortified court with lodgings for travellers
    Śekharī Northern temple type with a central spire clustered with smaller spires
    śikhara In northern usage, the temple tower; in southern texts, the crowning cupola
    śilpaśāstra Texts on the crafts, especially image-making
    śilpin, sthapati, sūtradhāra Craftsman; master architect; “holder of the cord”, architect or surveyor
    śreṇī Guild
    stūpa Solid mound over relics
    stūpi Finial of a southern temple
    śukanāsā “Parrot’s beak”: the large gable projecting from the front of a northern tower over the vestibule
    tala Storey of a southern superstructure
    tāla Face-length, the module of image proportions
    tīrthaṅkara, Jina “Ford-maker”: one of the twenty-four Jain teachers who attained liberation and showed the way across the stream of rebirth
    toraṇa Gateway; arched ornamental frame
    uṣṇīṣa Cranial protuberance of the Buddha, one of the marks of a great man
    utsavamūrti Processional image
    vāhana Mount of a deity
    varada Gesture of granting a boon: the hand lowered, palm outward
    vāstu Site, dwelling; vāstuśāstra, the science of building
    vāstupuruṣa, vāstupuruṣamaṇḍala The being of the site; the grid of squares on which buildings are laid out
    vāv (Gu.) Stepwell
    vedha “Piercing”: an obstruction in front of a door
    vedikā Railing
    Vesara Name used for temples of the Deccan, especially the later Cālukya and Hoysaḷa; in southern texts, a class with a round cupola
    vihāra Buddhist monastery
    vimāna In southern usage, the shrine building as a whole
    vyāla Composite, lion-like mythical beast in temple sculpture
    yakṣa, yakṣī Nature spirits, male and female
    yāḷi (Ta.) Rearing composite beast of southern pillars and friezes, the southern form of the vyāla
    ʿīdgāh (Pe.) Open prayer-ground with a long qibla wall, used for the two great festivals
