    Chronology¶

    All dates are approximate unless an inscription or a contemporary record gives an exact year. Where the dating of a text or monument is disputed, the range accepted in current scholarship is given.

    Date Event, text or monument
    c. 2600–1900 BCE Mature Harappan cities: Mohenjo-daro, Harappā, Dholāvīrā, Lothal
    c. 1500–1200 BCE Composition of the core of the Ṛgveda
    c. 800–200 BCE Śulbasūtras: geometry of the Vedic fire altars
    c. 5th c. BCE Second urbanisation in the Gaṅgā valley; fortified cities
    c. 300 BCE Megasthenes at Pāṭaliputra
    c. 268–232 BCE Reign of Aśoka: pillars, Barābar caves, early stūpas
    2nd c. BCE Bhārhut stūpa; enlargement of the Great Stūpa at Sāñcī; Bhājā caitya hall
    c. 50 BCE–300 CE Composition of the Arthaśāstra (traditionally attributed to Kauṭilya, c. 300 BCE)
    1st c. BCE–1st c. CE Gateways of the Great Stūpa at Sāñcī; Hāthīgumphā inscription of Khāravela
    1st c. CE Great caitya hall at Kārlā
    1st–3rd c. CE Kuṣāṇa period: Buddha images at Mathurā and in Gandhāra
    150 CE Rudradāman’s inscription on the repair of the Sudarśana lake, Junāgaḍh
    2nd c. BCE–4th c. CE Amarāvatī and Nāgārjunakoṇḍa
    401 CE Inscription of Candragupta II at Udayagiri
    5th c. Temple 17, Sāñcī; Nālandā founded; later caves of Ajaṇṭā (c. 460–480 in Spink’s chronology)
    early 6th c. Daśāvatāra temple, Deogaṛh
    6th c. Varāhamihira’s Bṛhatsaṃhitā; Elephanta
    c. 5th–8th c. Viṣṇudharmottara Purāṇa with the Citrasūtra
    578 Dedication of cave 3, Bādāmi
    first half of 7th c. Brick Lakṣmaṇa temple, Sirpur
    7th c. Pallava monuments at Māmallapuram; Paraśurāmeśvara, Bhubaneswar
    c. 700–728 Kailāsanātha temple, Kāñcīpuram; Shore Temple, Māmallapuram
    740s Virūpākṣa temple, Paṭṭadakal
    8th c. Mārtaṇḍ Sun temple, Kashmir
    c. 756–773 Kailāsa temple, Ellora (attributed to Kṛṣṇa I)
    c. 9th–12th c. Mayamata
    c. 950–1050 Temples of Khajuraho
    981–983 Colossal Bāhubali, Śravaṇabeḷagoḷa
    996 Foundation of Tabo monastery, Spiti
    1003–1010 Bṛhadīśvara temple, Thanjavur
    1026–27 Sun temple, Moḍherā; sack of Somnāth by Maḥmūd of Ghazni (1026)
    1031 Vimala Vasahī, Mount Abu
    11th c. Bhoja’s Samarāṅgaṇasūtradhāra; Liṅgarāja temple, Bhubaneswar; Rāṇī kī Vāv, Pāṭaṇ
    12th c. Jagannātha temple, Puri; Hoysaḷa temples of Belūr (1117) and Haḷebīḍ
    c. 12th–13th c. Aparājitapṛcchā
    1190s Qutb mosque and Qutb Mīnār, Delhi
    c. 1200 Sack of Nālandā during the campaigns of Bakhtiyār Khaljī
    1213 Rāmappa temple, Palampet
    1230 Lūṇa Vasahī, Mount Abu
    1231 Sulṭān Ghārī, Delhi: earliest monumental Islamic tomb in India
    mid-13th c. Sun temple, Konārak
    1268 Keśava temple, Somanāthapura
    1311 ʿAlāʾī Darwāza, Delhi
    early 14th c. Ṭhakkura Pherū’s Vatthusāra
    1325 Jāmiʿ Masjid, Khambhāt; tomb of Ghiyāth al-Dīn Tughluq (c. 1325)
    c. 1336–1565 Vijayanagara as imperial capital
    1352 Madrasa of Fīrūz Shāh Tughluq at Ḥauẓ Khāṣ, Delhi
    1367 Great mosque of Gulbarga
    1368–69 Tomb of Khān-i Jahān Tilangānī, Delhi (octagonal)

    1454, 1472 Jāmiʿ Masjid, Māṇḍū (1454); madrasa of Maḥmūd Gāwān, Bīdar (1472)
    1499 Aḍālaj stepwell
    1517–18 Tomb of Sikandar Lodī, Delhi
    16th c. Śilparatna; Manuṣyālayacandrikā (Kerala)
