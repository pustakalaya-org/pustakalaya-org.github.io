    Glossary¶

    Terms are Sanskrit unless marked (Ta. = Tamil, Ka. = Kannada, Pa. = Pali, Pk. = Prakrit, Ur. = Urdu/Persian, Hi. = Hindi, Ma. = Marathi, Mal. = Malayalam, Be. = Bengali, Gu. = Gujarati, Te. = Telugu).

    Term Meaning
    abhaṅga (Ma.) Devotional song form of the Marathi Vārkarī poets
    abhidhā Denotation; the primary, conventional meaning of a word
    abhinaya Acting; of four kinds: bodily (āṅgika), verbal (vācika), costume and make-up (āhārya) and involuntary (sāttvika)
    akam (Ta.) “Interior”: classical Tamil poetry of love
    ākhyāyikā Prose narrative said to be told by the hero, based on real events (e.g. Bāṇa’s Harṣacarita)
    ākhyān (Gu.) Narrative poem retelling an episode of the epics or Purāṇas for public recitation (Premānand)
    alaṅkāra Ornament; a figure of speech
    alaṅkāraśāstra The science of poetics
    anubhāva Consequent; the outward sign of an emotion
    anuṣṭubh The eight-syllable quarter metre; the narrative śloka
    Apabhraṃśa The last stage of Middle Indo-Aryan, used in literature from c. 6th century
    arthaprakṛti The five elements of a dramatic plot (seed, drop, extended episode, brief episode, outcome)
    āryā A moraic metre, inherited from Prakrit gāthā poetry
    aucitya Propriety, fittingness
    avasthā The five stages of the hero’s action in a play
    bayāẓ (Ur.) A poet’s or reader’s personal notebook of verses
    bhakti Loving devotion to a deity
    bhāṇa One-act monologue play spoken by a man-about-town
    bhāva Emotional state
    camatkāra Aesthetic wonder or delight
    campū Narrative kāvya in mixed prose and verse
    citrakāvya “Picture poetry”; verse relying on verbal or visual patterning
    dāstān (Ur.) Long prose romance told in episodes by professional storytellers (dāstān-go)
    dhvani Suggestion; meaning conveyed beyond the expressed sense
    dīvān (Ur.) A poet’s collected lyric poems, arranged by the final letter of the rhyme
    dohā (Hi.) Rhymed couplet of North Indian poetry
    doṣa Fault of composition
    dṛśya kāvya “Poetry to be seen”: drama
    gadya Prose
    gāthā (Pk., Pa.) Verse; a stanza in the āryā metre
    ghazal (Ur.) Lyric of independent couplets sharing rhyme and refrain
    guṇa Quality or excellence of composition
    īhām (Ur.) Wordplay on a word with two meanings; the fashion of early Delhi Rekhta poetry
    inshāʾ (Ur.) The art of ornate letters and documents; model collections of letters
    jātaka (Pa.) Story of a previous life of the Buddha
    kathā Story; invented prose narrative (e.g. Bāṇa’s Kādambarī)
    kavi Poet
    kāvya Literature composed with conscious artistry
    khaṇḍakāvya Short poem (e.g. the Meghadūta)
    lakṣaṇā Indication; secondary meaning arising when the literal sense is blocked
    mahākāvya Court epic in cantos
    malfūẓ (Ur.) Recorded conversations of a Sufi master
    maʿnī-āfirīnī (Ur.) “Meaning-creation”: packing several layers of sense into a verse
    maṅgalkāvya (Be.) “Poem of blessing”: long narrative poem sung in honour of a deity such as Manasā or Caṇḍī
    maṇipravāḷam (Mal.) “Rubies and coral”: mixed Malayalam–Sanskrit literary idiom
    māppiḷappāṭṭu (Mal.) Song literature of the Māppiḷa Muslims of Kerala, in Arabi-Malayalam
    mārga “Way”: Daṇḍin’s two poetic manners, Vaidarbha and Gauḍa
    marsiya (Ur.) Elegy, above all on the martyrdom of Ḥusain at Karbala
    masnavī (Ur.) Long poem in rhyming couplets: romance, history or mystical teaching
    maẓmūn (Ur.) “Theme”: the conceit or proposition a verse develops
    muktaka Single self-contained stanza
    mushāʿira (Ur.) Assembly at which poets recite verses composed in a given metre and rhyme
    nāṭaka The principal type of play, on a legendary theme with a noble hero
    nāṭya Theatre; drama with music and dance
    nāyaka, nāyikā Hero, heroine
    nirguṇa, saguṇa Without and with attributes: devotion to a formless God and to God with form
    nīti Prudent conduct; policy; worldly wisdom
    padam (Te.) Short song for dance, often in the voice of a courtesan addressing the god as lover (Kṣetrayya)
    padya Verse
    phāgu (Gu.) Poem on the spring festival, often on an ascetic tempted by the season
    prabandha Composition; in Telugu, the long ornate narrative poem
    prakaraṇa Play with an invented plot and a hero of middle rank
    Prakrit (prākṛta) Middle Indo-Aryan literary languages
    pratibhā Poetic imagination, creative genius
    puṟam (Ta.) “Exterior”: classical Tamil poetry of war, kingship and public life
    qaṣīda (Ur.) Long monorhyme poem, usually of praise
    rasa Aesthetic experience of a generalised emotion; “flavour”
    rāsa (Gu.) Narrative or didactic poem for singing; the Jain rāsas are the earliest Gujarati literature
    Rekhta (Ur.) “Mixed”: eighteenth-century name of Urdu poetry and its language
    rekhtī (Ur.) Urdu poetry written by men in the idiom of women
    rīti Style; for Vāmana, the soul of poetry
    rūpaka Play (ten types); also metaphor
    sabk-i hindī (Ur.) “Indian style”: later name for the “fresh” Persian poetry of the 16th–18th centuries
    sādhāraṇīkaraṇa Generalisation; the freeing of an emotion from personal reference
    sahṛdaya The sensitive, responsive reader or spectator
    sandeśakāvya Messenger poem
    sandhi Juncture; the five structural divisions of a play
    sarga Canto of a mahākāvya
    ṣaṭpadi (Ka.) Six-line stanza, the principal narrative metre of medieval Kannada
    śataka A “century” of verses
    śleṣa Paronomasia; a word or phrase with two meanings
    śruti “What is heard”: revealed scripture, the Veda, which the theorists did not count as kāvya
    sthāyibhāva Stable or dominant emotion underlying a rasa
    stotra Hymn of praise to a deity, the Buddha or a tīrthaṅkara
    subhāṣita “Well said”: a gnomic or epigrammatic stanza
    sūkta A Vedic hymn
    sūtradhāra Stage manager and director who speaks the prologue
    takhalluṣ (Ur.) Pen-name, usually placed in the last couplet of a ghazal
    tāza-gūʾī (Ur.) “Speaking the fresh”: the poetics of novelty in Persian poetry
    tazkira (Ur.) Biographical dictionary of poets with selections of their verse
    tiṇai (Ta.) Landscape or situation associated with a phase of love or war
    uparūpaka Minor dramatic form
    vacana (Ka.) Rhythmic prose-poem of the Vīraśaiva devotees
    vākh (Kashmiri) Short verse of Lal Ded
    vakrokti Oblique or “crooked” expression
    vibhāva Determinant; the cause or occasion of an emotion
    vidūṣaka Brahmin jester of Sanskrit drama
    vyabhicāribhāva Transitory emotional state (thirty-three in Bharata’s list)
    vyañjanā The power of suggestion
