    About this book¶

    The Kathāsaritsāgara, composed in Sanskrit verse by Somadeva at the court of Kashmir between 1063 and 1081 for Queen Sūryamatī, retells the lost Bṛhatkathā of Guṇāḍhya: the adventures of Naravāhanadatta, son of King Udayana, framing hundreds of tales, among them the twenty-five tales of the Vetāla and versions of the Pañcatantra fables. Translated from the Sanskrit by Charles Henry Tawney (1837–1922), Calcutta, Asiatic Society of Bengal (Bibliotheca Indica), 1880–84, in two volumes.
