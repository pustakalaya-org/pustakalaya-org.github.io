    Chapter 49: Vaakya se paragraph¶

    Vaakya se paragraph — ek baat, saaf tareeke se

    Grammar sahi ho, spelling sahi ho, phir bhi kai baar likha hua padhne me thakaata hai. Wajah aksar ek hi hoti hai: vaakya ya to bahut chhote aur jhatkedaar hote hain, ya ek lambi rail-gaadi jo "and… and… so…" par chalti rehti hai. Is chapter me hum seekhenge ki vaakyon ko kaise jodein, aur unhe ek paragraph me kaise sajaayein jise padhne wala ek baar me samajh jaaye.

    Ek line me

    A paragraph is a group of sentences about one idea: the first sentence states it, the middle sentences support it, and the last sentence closes it. Ek paragraph = ek idea. Pehli line batati hai, beech wali saboot deti hain, aakhri line baat band karti hai.

    49.1 Chhote tukdon se behte vaakya tak¶

    Bahut chhote vaakya padhne me robot jaise lagte hain; bahut lambe vaakya me padhne wala saans lene ki jagah dhoondta reh jaata hai. Achhi likhai in dono ke beech chalti hai.

    Jodo ya todo

    Do chhote vaakya ek hi baat ke hain → jodo (and, but, because, when, which). Ek vaakya 25 shabd se lamba ho gaya, ya usme do "and" aa gaye → todo aur full stop lagao.

    • ✗ I reached the station. The train was late. I waited. I had tea. Chaar tukde — sab sahi, par padhne me jhatka.
    • ✓ When I reached the station, the train was late, so I waited and had a cup of tea. when + so ne chaaron tukdon ko ek behte vaakya me jod diya.
    • ✓ The new manager, who joined last week, has already cut our meeting time in half. "The manager joined last week" ko who se andar fit kar diya.

    49.2 Paragraph ka burger — topic, support, closing¶

    Har achhe paragraph ki ek hi banawat hoti hai, chahe wo college ka essay ho ya office ki report. Ise ek burger ki tarah socho: upar aur neeche bun, beech me bharpoor masala.

    Burger ka formula

    1 topic sentence (kya baat + aapki raay) → 2–4 support sentences (wajah, udaharan, number) → 1 closing sentence (baat ko band karo ya agla kadam batao). Office email ho ya essay — yahi dhaancha.

    Model: ek poora paragraph

    Walking to work is the easiest way to stay fit. Topic sentence — vishay (walking) + raay (easiest way to stay fit).

    It needs no gym fee and no extra time, because you are travelling anyway. For example, a twenty-minute walk each way adds up to more than three hours a week. That is more than the 150 minutes of activity that doctors usually suggest. Teen support — wajah (because), udaharan (for example), saboot (150 minutes).

    So if your office is close, leave the bike at home. Closing — padhne wale ko ek kaam de diya.

    49.3 Topic sentence — pehli line me poori baat¶

    Manager, teacher, examiner — sab jaldi me padhte hain, aur aksar sirf har paragraph ki pehli line. Agar pehli line kuch nahi batati, to baaki paragraph bhi nahi padha jaata.

    Achhe topic sentence ka test

    Topic sentence me do cheezein hon: vishay + us par aapki raay ya point. "Online classes" sirf vishay hai; "Online classes save time but make it harder to ask questions" poora topic sentence hai.

    Kamzor shuruaat Mazboot topic sentence
    In this paragraph I will tell about my city. Pune is a city where old wadas and new IT parks stand side by side.
    As we all know, technology is very important. UPI has made it possible for a street vendor to accept payment from anyone.
    Exercise is a thing which is good. Even ten minutes of exercise a day can lift your mood.
    I want to say something about our project. Our project is two weeks behind because the vendor delivered late.
    Since ancient times, man has used water. Most Indian cities lose a large share of their water to leaking pipes.
    • Our project is two weeks behind because the vendor delivered late. Pehli hi line me kya hua (two weeks behind) aur kyun (vendor late) — manager ko aage padhne ka kaaran mil gaya.

    49.4 Ek paragraph, ek idea¶

    Jab ek paragraph me do-teen alag baatein aa jaati hain, to padhne wala bhool jaata hai ki shuru kis baat se hua tha. Har line ko topic sentence ke saamne khada karke dekho.

    Har line se ek sawaal

    "Kya ye line topic sentence ko support karti hai?" Haan → rakho. Nahi → ya hata do, ya naya paragraph shuru karo. Naya paragraph tab bhi: jab samay, jagah ya bolne wala badle, ya ulti baat shuru ho.

    49.5 Jodne wale shabd — however, therefore, for example¶

    Linking words sadak ke signboard jaise hain: padhne wale ko pehle hi bata dete hain ki agli line kis taraf mudegi — aur baat, ulti baat, natija ya udaharan.

    Linking word ka sahi istemaal

    Pehle kaam pehchano (jodna, ulta, natija, udaharan), phir shabd chuno. Vaakya ki shuruaat me ho to uske baad comma: "However, the cost is high." Har line me mat lagao — ek paragraph me do-teen kaafi hain.

    Kaam Shabd Example
    Aur ek baat moreover, in addition, also The flat is cheap. Moreover, it is near the metro.
    Ulti baat however, on the other hand The flat is cheap. However, it has no lift.
    Natija therefore, as a result The road was closed. As a result, we were late.
    Udaharan for example, for instance Some apps save time. For example, UPI.
    Kram first, then, finally First, fill the form. Then, pay the fee.
    Wajah because, since, due to + noun We stayed in because it rained. · due to the rain
    Saar in short, overall Overall, the trip was worth it.
    • The flat is cheap. However, it is on the fifth floor and has no lift. However ke baad halka sa ruko — sunne wala samajh jaata hai ki ab kami aa rahi hai.

    49.6 Pehle aur baad — ek kamzor paragraph ki marammat¶

    Ab sab kuch ek saath lagaate hain. Neeche ek college student ka asli jaisa paragraph hai — grammar ki galtiyan kam hain, par dhaancha nahi hai. Dekho ki kaise wahi baat saaf ho jaati hai.

    Pehle — kamzor paragraph

    Library is important. Our library closes at 5 pm. Many students are working in day time. And they come in evening and library is closed and they go back and they cannot study. Also canteen food is very bad. So library should open late.

    Baad — sudhra hua paragraph

    Our college library should stay open until 9 pm. At present it closes at 5 pm, which is exactly when many students finish their part-time jobs. For example, in my class of sixty, at least fifteen students work during the day and can study only in the evening. As a result, they either crowd into small rented rooms or do not study at all. Keeping the library open for four more hours would cost little, but it would give these students a fair chance.

    • Topic sentence aa gaya "Library is important" ki jagah saaf maang: open until 9 pm.
    • and… and… and… wali rail tooti Ek lamba vaakya teen saaf vaakyon me badla, which aur either… or ki madad se.
    • Saboot aur number jode "Many students" ki jagah "fifteen out of sixty" — ab baat me wazan hai.
    • Canteen wali line hata di Wo alag idea tha — uska apna paragraph banega.
    • Linking words aur closing sentence For example aur As a result ne lines ko joda; aakhri line ne baat band ki.

    49.7 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye galtiyan grammar ki kitaab me kam dikhti hain, par emails, reports aur exam ke answers me roz milti hain — aksar isliye ki hum Hindi ka dhaancha seedha English me utaar dete hain.

    Paragraph aur vaakya jodne ki asli galtiyan

    • Galat: I reached the office at nine, my manager was not there, I waited for one hour.
    • Sahi: I reached the office at nine, but my manager was not there. I waited for an hour.

    • Kyun: Teen poore vaakya sirf comma se nahi jud sakte. Jodne wala shabd lao (but) ya full stop lagao.

    • Galat: Although it was raining, but we went out.

    • Sahi: Although it was raining, we went out.

    • Kyun: Hindi me "haalaanki… lekin" dono lagte hain; English me although ke saath but nahi aata. Ek hi rakho.

    • Galat: Due to I was ill, I could not submit the assignment.

    • Sahi: Because I was ill, I could not submit the assignment.

    • Kyun: "Due to" ke baad noun aata hai (due to my illness). Poora vaakya (I was ill) aaye to "because".

    • Galat: The hotel was clean. Moreover, it was very expensive.

    • Sahi: The hotel was clean. However, it was very expensive.

    • Kyun: Moreover usi disha me ek aur baat jodta hai. Achhi baat ke baad kami aaye to ulti baat hai — however.

    • Galat: In this paragraph I am going to tell about my city. As we all know, Pune is a nice city.

    • Sahi: Pune is a city where old wadas and new IT parks stand side by side.
    • Kyun: Paragraph ke baare me baat mat karo — seedha baat karo. "As we all know" bhi kuch nahi jodta; use hata do.

    Linking words namak jaise hain

    Har vaakya "Moreover…", "Furthermore…", "Also…" se shuru karoge to likhai bhaari aur banawati lagegi. Zyadatar vaakyon ko linking word ki zaroorat hi nahi — sahi kram apne aap jodta hai.

    49.8 Practice — Paragraphs¶

    Exercise 1

    Cycling ke fayde wale paragraph ke liye sabse achha topic sentence kaunsa hai?

    • In this paragraph I will tell about cycling.
    • Cycling to work saves money, keeps you fit and cuts traffic.
    • Cycling is a thing.
    • As we all know, cycling is very very good.
    Jawaab

    Cycling to work saves money, keeps you fit and cuts traffic.

    Achha topic sentence vishay + raay dono batata hai, aur aage aane wale teen support points ka ishaara bhi deta hai. 'In this paragraph I will tell' kuch nahi batata.

    Exercise 2

    It rained heavily all night. ___, the match was cancelled.

    • As a result
    • However
    • For example
    • Moreover
    Jawaab

    As a result

    Baarish wajah hai aur match cancel hona uska natija — isliye 'As a result' (ya Therefore).

    Exercise 3

    The flat is quite small. ___, it is only ten minutes from my office.

    • However
    • Therefore
    • For example
    • As a result
    Jawaab

    However

    Pehli line kami batati hai, doosri fayda — ulti baat ke liye 'However'.

    Exercise 4

    'Ghar me paani bachana' wale paragraph me kaunsi line fit NAHI hoti?

    • Fix leaking taps as soon as you notice them.
    • Use a bucket instead of a hose to wash the car.
    • Our society also needs a new gym.
    • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.
    Jawaab

    Our society also needs a new gym.

    Gym ek alag idea hai. Ek paragraph = ek idea — baaki teen lines paani bachane ko support karti hain.

    Exercise 5

    The ___ sentence tells the reader what the whole paragraph is about.

    Jawaab

    topic

    Topic sentence paragraph ki pehli line hoti hai — poori baat ka saar, ek vaakya me.

    Exercise 6

    A good paragraph talks about only one main ___.

    Jawaab

    idea / point

    Ek paragraph, ek idea. Naya idea aaye to naya paragraph — isse padhne wala raasta nahi bhatakta.

    Exercise 7

    Many students work during the day. ___ example, fifteen students in my class have part-time jobs.

    Jawaab

    For

    'For example' udaharan laata hai, aur vaakya ki shuruaat me ho to uske baad comma lagta hai.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek shabd faltu hai — us par tap karo.

    Although the train was late, but we reached the meeting on time.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: but.

    Sahi roop: Although the train was late, we reached the meeting on time.

    Hindi me 'haalaanki… lekin' dono lagte hain, English me nahi. 'Although' ke saath 'but' kabhi nahi — ek hi rakho.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Due to I was unwell, I missed the class.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Due.

    Sahi roop: Because I was unwell, I missed the class.

    'Due to' ke baad noun aata hai (due to my illness). Poora vaakya (I was unwell) aaye to 'Because' lagao.

    Exercise 10

    Jodne wale shabd ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    however udaharan
    therefore ek aur baat jodna
    for example aakhri point
    moreover ulti baat
    finally natija
    Jawaab

    however — ulti baat; therefore — natija; for example — udaharan; moreover — ek aur baat jodna; finally — aakhri point

    however = contrast, therefore = result, for example = udaharan, moreover = addition, finally = kram ka ant.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao.

    Shabd: saves / money. / time / new / plan / and / the / However, / both

    Jawaab

    However, the new plan saves both time and money.

    'However' vaakya ki shuruaat me aaye to uske baad comma. Phir seedha subject + verb + object.

    Exercise 12

    Ye line bolo — 'As a result' ke baad halka sa ruko.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: As a result, the project was finished on time.

    Linking word ke baad comma = bolte waqt chhota sa viraam. Sunne wale ko pata chal jaata hai ki ab natija aa raha hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Apna aakhri lamba email ya assignment kholo. Har paragraph ki sirf pehli line padho — kya unse poori baat samajh aati hai? Nahi aati, to wahi pehli lines dobara likho. Ye ek aadat aapki likhai badal degi.
