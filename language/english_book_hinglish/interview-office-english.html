    Chapter 34: Interview aur office¶

    Interview aur office English

    Interview me English se zyada dhaancha maayne rakhta hai. Jo log atak jaate hain, unki English kharaab nahi hoti — unke paas jawaab ka saancha nahi hota, isliye wo soch-soch kar bolte hain aur ghabra jaate hain. Is chapter me aapko taiyaar saanche milenge: apna introduction, aam sawaalon ke jawaab, meeting me bolne ki bhasha, aur email ke phrase. Sab bol kar practise karne layak.

    Interview ka ek hi asli niyam

    Jawaab 30 se 60 second ka ho. Isse chhota = taiyaari nahi lagti. Isse lamba = aap bhatakne lagte hain aur English ulajhne lagti hai. Teen-chaar vaakya. Bas. Phir chup ho jao aur agla sawaal aane do.

    34.1 Apna introduction — teen hisson ka saancha¶

    "Tell me about yourself" sabse pehla aur sabse zyada bigadne wala sawaal hai. Log yahan apni poori zindagi suna dete hain — paida kahan hue, kitne bhai-behen. Interviewer ko wo nahi chahiye. Use sirf teen cheezein chahiye: aaj aap kya karte hain → pehle kya kiya → aage kya chahte hain.

    Taiyaar introduction — anubhav wale ke liye

    • Thanks for having me. I'm Ravi, and I currently work as a support engineer at a telecom company in Pune. Hissa 1 — abhi. "Thanks for having me" ek sharif shuruaat hai.
    • I've been there for about three years, mostly handling customer issues and billing problems. Hissa 2 — pehle. "for about three years" — "about" se aawaz natural lagti hai.
    • Last year I also trained two new team members, which I really enjoyed. Ek thos example — ye line aapko baaki sabse alag karti hai.
    • Now I'm looking for a role where I can work more directly with clients, and that's why this job caught my eye. Hissa 3 — aage, aur yahan kyun. "caught my eye" = nazar me aaya.

    Taiyaar introduction — fresher ke liye

    • Good morning. I'm Priya, and I finished my B.Com from Pune University this year. Fresher ke liye "abhi" ka matlab padhai hai.
    • During my final year I did a three-month internship at a small accounting firm, where I worked on GST filings. Internship, project, part-time kaam — jo bhi ho, ek thos cheez batao.
    • I'm comfortable with Excel and I'm learning Tally at the moment. "I'm comfortable with…" — "I know" se behtar aur imaandaar lagta hai.
    • I'm looking for my first full-time role, and I'd like to start in a team where I can learn quickly. Fresher ke liye "seekhna" sabse imaandaar aur sabse achcha jawaab hai.

    Introduction ki paanch galtiyan

    • Galat: Myself Ravi Kumar.
    • Sahi: I'm Ravi Kumar.

    • Kyun: "Myself" ek pronoun hai, vaakya nahi. Ye sabse jaldi pakdi jaane wali galti hai.

    • Galat: I am belonging to Jaipur.

    • Sahi: I'm from Jaipur.

    • Kyun: "Belong" me -ing nahi lagti. Aur "I'm from…" chhota aur natural hai.

    • Galat: I have did my graduation in 2021.

    • Sahi: I graduated in 2021.

    • Kyun: "Graduation" ek event hai, "graduate" verb hai. Seedha verb use karo.

    • Galat: I am having 4 years experience.

    • Sahi: I have four years of experience.

    • Kyun: "have", na ki "am having". Aur beech me "of" lagta hai.

    • Galat: My father is a businessman and I have two sisters…

    • Sahi: Introduction me parivaar ki baat bilkul nahi.
    • Kyun: Interviewer ko aapke kaam ka introduction chahiye, zindagi ka nahi. Poochhein to batana.

    34.2 Aam interview sawaal — taiyaar jawaab¶

    Kahani ka saancha — har 'example wale' sawaal ke liye

    Haalat → aapka kaam → jo aapne kiya → natija. Chaar vaakya, usi kram me. Jab bhi poochha jaaye "tell me about a time when…", bas is saanche me bhar do. Natija bhoolna nahi — wahi jawaab ko poora karta hai.

    Sawaal Taiyaar jawaab Suno
    Why do you want to leave your current job? I've learnt a lot where I am, but the work has become quite repetitive. I'm looking for something with more responsibility.
    What are your strengths? I'm patient with people, which helps a lot in support work. I also stay calm when things go wrong.
    What is your weakness? I used to take on too much and then struggle with deadlines. I've started saying no more often, and planning my week on Monday.
    Why should we hire you? You need someone who can handle angry customers without losing patience. That's exactly what I've been doing for three years.
    Where do you see yourself in five years? I'd like to be leading a small team by then. For now my focus is on getting really good at the work itself.
    Tell me about a difficult situation at work. Last Diwali two people were on leave and our tickets doubled. I rearranged the queue by urgency and asked a colleague to help with calls. We cleared everything in two days.
    What are your salary expectations? Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking at somewhere between eight and ten lakhs. But I'm open to discussing it.
    Do you have any questions for us? Yes — could you tell me what a typical week looks like in this role? And how is the team structured?

    'Weakness' ka jaal

    "I have no weakness" ya "I am a perfectionist" — dono jawaab har interviewer ne hazaar baar sune hain, aur dono se lagta hai aapne sach nahi bola.
    Sahi saancha: ek asli kami + usse theek karne ke liye jo aap kar rahe hain. Yahi imaandaar aur samajhdaar dono lagta hai.

    Aakhir me sawaal poochhna zaroori hai

    "Do you have any questions?" ka jawaab kabhi "No" nahi hona chahiye — isse lagta hai aapko dilchaspi nahi. Do sawaal pehle se taiyaar rakho: What does a typical week look like in this role?
    What would you want me to achieve in the first three months?

    34.3 Interview me atak gaye to¶

    Ye hoga — har kisi ke saath hota hai. Ghabraane ki zaroorat nahi, bas taiyaar line nikal jaaye.

    Haalat Line Suno
    Sawaal samajh nahi aaya Sorry, could you rephrase that?
    Thoda waqt chahiye That's a good question. Let me think for a second.
    Jawaab nahi aata I haven't worked on that, but I'd be happy to learn it.
    Vaakya ulajh gaya Sorry, let me start that again.
    Sahi samjha ya nahi, confirm karna Just to make sure I understood — you're asking about…?
    Interview khatam Thank you for your time. I enjoyed talking to you.

    'Nahi aata' kehna nuksaan nahi karta

    Jhoot bolne se bahut zyada nuksaan hota hai. "I haven't worked on that, but I'd be happy to learn it" ek pakka, professional jawaab hai — aur zyadatar interviewer ise pasand karte hain.

    34.4 Office aur meeting ki bhasha¶

    Kaam Line Suno
    Meeting me bolna shuru Can I add something here?
    Sorry to interrupt, but I think there's an issue with the timeline.
    If I could just jump in for a second…
    Sehmat / asehmat That makes sense to me.
    I see your point, but I'd do it slightly differently.
    I'm not sure that will work. Can I explain why?
    Samay aur kaam I'll have it ready by Thursday.
    I'm a bit stretched this week — could we push it to Monday?
    Let me check and get back to you in an hour.
    Madad aur saaf karna Could you walk me through it once?
    Just to confirm — you want the report before the call, right?
    I'll need a bit of help with this part.
    Galti aur maafi That was my mistake. I'll fix it today.
    Sorry for the delay — I got held up in another call.
    Let me look into it and come back with an answer.
    Meeting khatam karna So, to sum up — I'll send the draft and you'll review it by Friday.
    Shall we take this offline and discuss it after?

    Poora mini-dialogue — team meeting

    • Manager: So, where are we on the client report? "Where are we on…" = us kaam ka kya haal hai?
    • Aap: I've finished the first half. The numbers for March are still pending from finance. Pehle jo ho gaya, phir jo ruka hai — aur kiske paas ruka hai.
    • Manager: Can you have it done by Thursday? Guruwaar tak ho jaayega?
    • Aap: If I get the numbers by tomorrow, yes. Otherwise Friday would be safer. Shart ke saath haan kehna — "Friday would be safer" bahut professional hai.
    • Manager: Fair enough. I'll chase finance today. "Fair enough" = theek hai, baat sahi hai. "Chase" = peeche padna.
    • Aap: Thanks. Just to confirm — you want the full report, not just the summary? "Just to confirm" aapko dhyan rakhne wala dikhata hai. Ye line roz kaam aati hai.

    34.5 Email ke taiyaar phrase¶

    Kaam Phrase
    Shuruaat (jaan-pehchaan) Hi Anita,
    Hope you're doing well.
    Shuruaat (official) Dear Mr Sharma,
    I hope this email finds you well.
    Kaaran batana I'm writing to ask about… / I'm following up on my last email.
    Kuch bhej rahe hain Please find the report attached. / I've attached the file below.
    Kuch maangna Could you please share the figures for March? / Would you be able to review this by Friday?
    Yaad dilana (narmi se) Just a gentle reminder about the pending approval.
    Deri ki maafi Apologies for the delay in replying.
    Kaam ki pushti Noted, I'll take care of it. / Thanks, that's clear now.
    Samay maangna Would it be possible to extend the deadline to Monday?
    Ant (jaan-pehchaan) Thanks,
    Ravi
    Ant (official) Best regards,
    Ravi Kumar

    Email ki wo lines jo hata deni chahiye

    • Galat: Please do the needful and revert back at the earliest.
    • Sahi: Could you review this and let me know by Friday?

    • Kyun: Teen dikkat: "do the needful" se pata nahi chalta karna kya hai; "revert" ka matlab wapas lautna hota hai, jawaab dena nahi; "at the earliest" ki jagah asli tareekh do.

    • Galat: Respected Sir, I beg to state that…

    • Sahi: Dear Mr Sharma, I'm writing to…

    • Kyun: Ye bahut purani, school ki chitthi wali bhasha hai. Aaj ke office me ajeeb lagti hai.

    • Galat: Kindly intimate me the same.

    • Sahi: Please let me know.

    • Kyun: "Intimate" aur "the same" dafter ki purani bhasha hai. Chhote shabd zyada professional lagte hain.

    • Galat: Thanking you in advance for the same.

    • Sahi: Thanks in advance. / Thanks for your help.
    • Kyun: "For the same" kaatna hi hai. Email me chhoti lines sabse achchi lagti hain.

    34.6 Interview se pehle ki aakhri taiyaari¶

    Ek din pehle — paanch kaam

    1. Apna 45-second introduction zor se 10 baar bolo. Aaine ke saamne.
    2. Teen sawaalon ke jawaab taiyaar karo: kyun chhod rahe ho, kamzori, aur ek mushkil haalat.
    3. Do sawaal taiyaar karo jo aap poochhoge.
    4. Phone par apna introduction record karo aur sun lo — jo ulajha lage, use chhota karo.
    5. Interview ke din rozmarra se bhi dheere bolo. Ghabraahat me tezi aati hai, aur tezi me English ulajhti hai.

    34.7 Practice — Interview aur office¶

    Exercise 1

    'Tell me about yourself' ka sahi dhaancha kya hai?

    • Abhi kya karte hain → pehle kya kiya → aage kya chahte hain
    • Paida kahan hue → padhai → parivaar
    • Parivaar → hobby → naukri
    • Sirf apni degree aur marks

    Hint: Teen hisse, teen waqt.

    Jawaab

    Abhi kya karte hain → pehle kya kiya → aage kya chahte hain

    Teen hisse, lagbhag 15 second har ek. Parivaar aur gaon tab batana jab poochha jaaye.

    Exercise 2

    Interview ka jawaab kitna lamba hona chahiye?

    • 30 se 60 second
    • 5 se 10 second
    • 3 se 4 minute
    • Jitna lamba ho sake
    Jawaab

    30 se 60 second

    Chhota jawaab taiyaari ki kami dikhata hai; lamba jawaab me aap bhatakne lagte hain aur English ulajhti hai.

    Exercise 3

    'What is your weakness?' ka sahi saancha?

    • Ek asli kami + usse theek karne ki koshish
    • I have no weakness
    • I am a perfectionist
    • Ek kami jo asal me taarif ho
    Jawaab

    Ek asli kami + usse theek karne ki koshish

    Imaandaari + samajhdaari. 'No weakness' aur 'perfectionist' har interviewer hazaar baar sun chuka hai.

    Exercise 4

    Koi technical cheez poochhi gayi jo aapko nahi aati. Kya karein?

    • I haven't worked on that, but I'd be happy to learn it
    • Jhoot bol do ki aati hai
    • Chup ho jao
    • Baat badal do
    Jawaab

    I haven't worked on that, but I'd be happy to learn it

    Jhoot aage jaa kar pakda jaata hai. Imaandaar jawaab zyadatar interviewer ko pasand aata hai.

    Exercise 5

    'Do you have any questions for us?' ka sabse kharaab jawaab?

    • No, everything is clear
    • Ek sawaal role ke baare me
    • Ek sawaal team ke baare me
    • Pehle teen mahine ke lakshya ke baare me
    Jawaab

    No, everything is clear

    'No' se lagta hai aapko dilchaspi nahi. Do sawaal pehle se taiyaar rakho.

    Exercise 6

    'Tell me about a difficult situation' ka saancha?

    • Haalat → aapka kaam → jo kiya → natija
    • Sirf natija
    • Kis par ilzaam tha wo batana
    • Haalat → kis ki galti thi

    Hint: Chaar hisse.

    Jawaab

    Haalat → aapka kaam → jo kiya → natija

    Chaar vaakya usi kram me. Natija bataana bhoolo nahi — wahi jawaab poora karta hai.

    Exercise 7

    Theek karo: I have ___ years of experience. (chaar saal)

    Hint: Sankhya shabdon me, aur 'am having' nahi.

    Jawaab

    four / 4

    'I have four years of experience' — 'am having' galat hai, aur beech me 'of' lagta hai.

    Exercise 8

    Meeting me pushti karna: ___ to confirm — you want the full report?

    Hint: Do shabd ka shuruaati phrase, 'J' se.

    Jawaab

    Just / just

    'Just to confirm…' office me roz kaam aane wali line hai — aapko dhyan rakhne wala dikhati hai.

    Exercise 9

    Email: Please find the report ___.

    Hint: File saath bheji hai.

    Jawaab

    attached

    'Please find the report attached' ya 'I've attached the report'. Dono theek.

    Exercise 10

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    Myself Ravi Kumar from Jaipur.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Myself.

    Sahi roop: I'm Ravi Kumar, from Jaipur.

    'Myself' pronoun hai, vaakya shuru karne wala shabd nahi. Interview me ye sabse jaldi pakdi jaati hai.

    Exercise 11

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    I am belonging to a small town near Nashik.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: belonging.

    Sahi roop: I'm from a small town near Nashik.

    'Belong' me -ing nahi lagti. Aur 'I'm from…' chhota aur natural hai.

    Exercise 12

    Email me jo shabd hata dena chahiye, us par tap karo.

    Please review this and revert at the earliest.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: revert.

    Sahi roop: Please review this and let me know by Friday.

    'Revert' ka matlab wapas purani haalat me jaana hai, jawaab dena nahi. Aur 'at the earliest' ki jagah asli tareekh do.

    Exercise 13

    Office ki haalat ko uski line se jodo.

    A B
    Meeting me bolna shuru I'm a bit stretched this week.
    Asehmat, par sharifi se That was my mistake. I'll fix it today.
    Zyada kaam hai Can I add something here?
    Galti maanna I see your point, but I'd do it differently.
    Jawaab

    Meeting me bolna shuru — Can I add something here?; Asehmat, par sharifi se — I see your point, but I'd do it differently.; Zyada kaam hai — I'm a bit stretched this week.; Galti maanna — That was my mistake. I'll fix it today.

    Har haalat ki ek taiyaar line. Meeting me sochne ka waqt nahi hota — isi liye ratna kaam aata hai.

    Exercise 14

    Interview ki shuruaati line banao.

    Shabd: for / Thanks / having / me

    Jawaab

    Thanks for having me

    Chhoti, sharif shuruaat. Iske baad seedha hissa 1 — abhi aap kya karte hain.

    Exercise 15

    Introduction, hissa 1 — abhi aap kya karte hain.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I currently work as a support engineer at a telecom company

    'currently' se saaf hota hai ki ye aaj ki baat hai. Dheere bolo — ye pehla impression hai.

    Exercise 16

    Introduction, hissa 2 — anubhav.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I've been there for about three years, mostly handling customer issues

    'for about three years' — 'about' se aawaz natural lagti hai, ratt hui nahi.

    Exercise 17

    Introduction, hissa 3 — aage kya chahte hain.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Now I'm looking for a role where I can work more directly with clients

    Yahi hissa batata hai ki aap yahan kyun baithe hain. Ise chhodna sabse badi galti hai.

    Exercise 18

    Apni taqat batao — bina bade-bade shabdon ke.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I'm patient with people and I stay calm when things go wrong

    Thos aur sach lagta hai. 'I am a hard worker' se bahut behtar.

    Exercise 19

    Naukri chhodne ki wajah — bina purani company ko bura kehne.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I've learnt a lot where I am but I'm looking for more responsibility

    Purani jagah ki buraai bilkul nahi karni. Aage ki baat karo, peeche ki nahi.

    Exercise 20

    Sawaal samajh nahi aaya. Politely poochho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Sorry, could you rephrase that

    'Rephrase' = dobara dusre shabdon me kahiye. Interview me ye bilkul theek hai.

    Exercise 21

    Technical sawaal jo nahi aata. Imaandaar jawaab do.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I haven't worked on that but I'd be happy to learn it

    Saaf, professional aur sach. Jhoot se bahut behtar.

    Exercise 22

    Aakhir me aap jo sawaal poochhenge.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: What does a typical week look like in this role

    Ye sawaal aapko gambhir dikhata hai, aur aapko asli jaankari bhi deta hai.

    Exercise 23

    Interview khatam. Vidaai ki line.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Thank you for your time. I enjoyed talking to you

    Chhota, garmjoshi se. Aakhri impression bhi yaad rehta hai.

    Exercise 24

    Meeting me shart ke saath haan kehna.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: If I get the numbers by tomorrow yes. Otherwise Friday would be safer

    Jhoothi haan se behtar hai shart ke saath haan. 'Would be safer' bahut professional lagta hai.

    Exercise 25

    Office me zyada kaam hai. Samay maango.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I'm a bit stretched this week. Could we push it to Monday

    'A bit stretched' = thoda dabaav me hoon. 'Push it to' = aage badhana.

    Exercise 26

    Galti maano — bina safai dene.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: That was my mistake. I'll fix it today

    Do chhote vaakye: galti maani, aur ilaaj bataya. Lambi safai se bahut behtar.

    Aage kya karein

    Apna 45-second introduction aaj likho — paanch vaakya se zyada nahi — aur phone par record kar ke sun lo. Jo vaakya bolte waqt ulajha, use chhota karo. Phir 10 baar bolo.

    Interview se pehle sabse zyada faayda do chapters se hoga: Fluency aur filler words (atakne ka ilaaj) aur Indian English ki aadatein (jo galtiyan turant pakdi jaati hain).
