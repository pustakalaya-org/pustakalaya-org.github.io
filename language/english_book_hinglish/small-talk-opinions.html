    Chapter 32: Small talk aur raay dena¶

    Small talk aur raay dena — baat shuru karna, chalana, khatam karna

    Office ki lift, shaadi ka buffet, conference ka chai break — yahi do-teen minute ki baatein rishte banati hain. Hum me se bahut log yahan chup ho jaate hain, kyonki dimaag me ek hi sawaal ghoomta hai: "kya bolun?" Sach ye hai ki small talk me koi gehri baat nahi chahiye — bas ek dhaancha chahiye: shuru kaise karein, aage kaise badhaayein, apni raay kaise rakhein, aur izzat se kaise niklein. Har line par 🔊 hai. Suno, saath me bolo, aur kal kisi se try karo.

    32.1 Small talk ka naksha — chaar kadam¶

    Jab dimaag me raasta saaf ho, to ghabraahat aadhi ho jaati hai. Har achhi chhoti baat-cheet in chaar kadmon se guzarti hai — aur beech ke do kadam baar-baar ghoomte hain.

    Chaar kadam

    Open → Follow up → Share → Close. Beech ke do kadam (sawaal aur apni baat) baar-baar ghoomte hain — yahi baat-cheet ko zinda rakhta hai. Small talk ka maqsad jaankari lena nahi, judna hai.

    Pehli mulaqat me ye topics mat chhedo

    Safe: mausam, traffic, jis event par aap ho, khaana, kaam, travel, movies, cricket.
    Avoid: salary, "shaadi kab kar rahe ho?", weight, dharm, politics. Hamare yahan ye sawaal pyaar se poochhe jaate hain, par English bolne wale maahaul me ye bahut personal lagte hain.

    32.2 Baat shuru karna — mausam, kaam, aur jahan aap dono hain¶

    Sabse aasaan opener wo hai jo aap dono ke saamne hai — wahi jagah, wahi mausam, wahi khaana. Ek chhota statement, phir ek sawaal. Bas itna.

    Kahan English line Suno
    Shaadi / party So, how do you know the bride and groom?
    Conference Is this your first time at this conference?
    Mausam It's so hot today, isn't it?
    Baarish Looks like the rain's finally here.
    Kaam What kind of work do you do?
    Office How's your week going so far?
    Khaana Have you tried the paneer? It's really good.
    Naya chehra Hi, I don't think we've met. I'm Kavya.

    Opener ka formula

    Statement + sawaal: "Great music tonight. Do you know the band?" Statement se maahaul banta hai, sawaal se saamne wale ko bolne ka mauka milta hai. Akela statement ("Hot day.") ya akela sawaal ("Your name?") adhoora lagta hai.

    Mini-dialogue — conference ka chai break

    Haalat: conference me chai break hai. Aap akele khade ho, bagal me ek aur insaan bhi akela hai. Pehle Aap bolo, phir Meera ki line bhi.

    • Aap: Hi! Is this your first time at this conference? Shuruaat — jagah ke baare me sawaal.
    • Meera: No, second time, actually. Last year's was in Bengaluru. "actually" = asal me. Usne extra detail di — achha signal hai.
    • Aap: Oh, nice. I'm Rohan, by the way. I work in logistics. "by the way" se naam beech me aaram se aa jaata hai.
    • Meera: Nice to meet you, Rohan. I'm Meera. I'm a data analyst. Naam ke saath kaam — ab follow-up ka raasta khul gaya.
    • Aap: So what brings you here this year? "Is saal yahan kaise?" — bahut natural follow-up.
    • Meera: Mostly the AI sessions. My team wants to try some new tools. Ab aap poochh sakte ho: "Oh, which tools?" — baat chal padi.

    32.3 Baat aage badhana — follow-up sawaal¶

    Baat-cheet aksar yahin mar jaati hai: sawaal aaya, ek shabd ka jawaab gaya, phir chuppi. Isse bachne ke do hathiyaar hain — ping-pong wala jawaab, aur achhe follow-up sawaal.

    Ping-pong jawaab

    Jawaab + ek extra detail + sawaal wapas. "Pune. I moved here for work last year. What about you?" Ek shabd ka jawaab ball ko zameen par gira deta hai — phir dono khade rehte hain.

    • Oh, really? How did you get into that? Us kaam me kaise aaye? — kisi ke kaam ke baare me sabse achha sawaal.
    • What's that like? Wo kaisa hai? — naya shehar, naya kaam, kuch bhi.
    • That sounds interesting. What do you enjoy most about it? Isme sabse achha kya lagta hai? — log apni pasand ke baare me khushi se bolte hain.
    • How long have you been doing that? Kab se kar rahe ho?
    • And what about you? Aur aap? — ball wapas bhejne ki sabse chhoti line.

    Band sawaal ko khula banao

    Band: "Do you like Pune?" → jawaab sirf haan ya na.
    Khula: "What do you like about Pune?" → jawaab me kahani aati hai. What, how, why se shuru hone wale sawaal baat ko lamba chalaate hain.

    32.4 Apni raay dena — I think, In my view, To be honest¶

    Raay dene se baat me jaan aati hai. Par Hindi se seedha anuvaad karne par raay aksar zyada sakht sunai deti hai. English me raay ko thoda naram karke rakha jaata hai — aur kitna naram, ye aap phrase se tay karte ho.

    Line Kab Suno
    I think the new metro has really helped. Roz ki, normal raay
    In my view, the second half of the film was better. Soch-samajh wali, thodi formal
    To be honest, I didn't enjoy the food much. Sach jo shayad pasand na aaye
    Personally, I prefer working from the office. Apni pasand — doosron par thopna nahi
    I might be wrong, but I think it's going to rain. Bahut naram, pakka nahi pata
    If you ask me, cricket is more fun in the stadium. Casual, doston ke beech

    Raay + wajah

    Raay akeli kamzor lagti hai — use because ke saath do: "I think remote work is great because I save two hours a day." Aur "According to me" ki jagah "I think" ya "In my view" bolo.

    32.5 Sehmat aur asehmat — bina jhagde ke¶

    Asehmat hona bura nahi hai; tareeka bura ho sakta hai. Seedha "No, you are wrong" bolne se baat wahin band ho jaati hai. English bolne wale pehle saamne wale ki baat ko maante hain, phir apni rakhte hain.

    Asehmat hone ka formula

    Maan lo + but + apni baat. "That's a fair point, but I think traffic is the bigger problem." Pehla hissa izzat deta hai, doosra aapki raay. Dono zaroori hain.

    Mini-dialogue — work from home par behas

    Haalat: lunch par colleague Aman kehta hai ki ghar se kaam behtar hai. Aap aadhe sehmat ho, aadhe nahi.

    • Aman: I think working from home is so much better. Raay — "I think" ke saath.
    • Aap: I know what you mean. No traffic, right? Pehle maano — "main samajh raha hoon".
    • Aman: Exactly! I save almost two hours a day. Raay + wajah.
    • Aap: That's true. But to be honest, I find it harder to focus at home. Maan lo + but + apni baat. Bina jhagde ke asehmati.
    • Aman: Fair enough. I guess it depends on the person. "Fair enough" = theek hai, baat me dum hai.
    • Aap: Yeah, I suppose it does. Dono khush — behas khatam, dosti baaki.

    32.6 Baat khatam karna — izzat se¶

    Achhi baat-cheet ka ant bhi achha hona chahiye. Bina kuch kahe mud jaana, ya achaanak "OK, bye" bol dena rude lagta hai. Teen chhote kadam — aur aap ek achhi yaad chhod kar jaate ho.

    • Well, it was really nice talking to you. "Well…" — sabse chhota aur sabse saaf signal.
    • I should let you get back to your friends. "Main aapko roke nahi rakhta" — wajah unki taraf se, bahut sharif tareeka.
    • I'm going to grab some food before it's all gone. Halki-fulki wajah — party me kaam aati hai.
    • I need to catch up with a colleague, but let's talk later. "catch up with" = kisi se milkar haal-chaal lena.
    • Let's stay in touch. Are you on LinkedIn? Agar rishta aage badhana hai.
    • Enjoy the rest of the evening! Aakhri garam line.

    Teen kadam ka ant

    Signal ("Well…") + wajah ("I should go and say hi to my boss") + garam line ("It was lovely meeting you"). Wajah chhoti aur sachchi rakho. Bina bole gaayab hona ya seedha "OK, bye" — dono rude lagte hain.

    32.7 Ek poori baat-cheet — shuru se ant tak¶

    Ab sab kuch ek saath — opener, follow-up, ping-pong, raay, sehmati aur ant. Dono roles bolo; doosre ki line bolne se aapka kaan bhi taiyaar hota hai.

    Mini-dialogue — shaadi ka buffet

    Haalat: reception me buffet ki lambi line. Aage Priya khadi hai, aap use nahi jaante.

    • Aap: This queue is moving so slowly! Everything looks delicious, though. Opener — jo saamne hai uske baare me statement.
    • Priya: I know! Someone told me the biryani is the best thing here. Usne baat pakdi aur detail di.
    • Aap: Good to know. So, are you from the bride's side or the groom's? Shaadi ka sabse aasaan follow-up.
    • Priya: The bride's. We went to college together. And you? Ping-pong: jawaab + detail + sawaal wapas.
    • Aap: I work with the groom. I'm Vikram, by the way. Naam "by the way" ke saath.
    • Priya: I'm Priya. Nice to meet you. What do you two work on? Khula sawaal — "kis cheez par kaam karte ho?"
    • Aap: We build apps for hospitals. It's busy, but I really enjoy it. Jawaab + feeling. Ab Priya ke paas bolne ko kuch hai.
    • Priya: That sounds interesting. I think technology in healthcare is the future. Raay — "I think" ke saath.
    • Aap: I couldn't agree more. Well, I'll let you get your food. It was lovely talking to you. Sehmati, phir signal + wajah + garam line.
    • Priya: You too, Vikram. Enjoy the biryani! Achha ant — dono muskura ke alag.

    32.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye galtiyan grammar ki kam, anuvaad ki zyada hain — Hindi ka saancha English me utaar dene se aati hain. Sunne wala samajh to jaata hai, par baat ajeeb lagti hai.

    Small talk ki aam galtiyan

    • Galat: What is your good name?
    • Sahi: What's your name? / Sorry, I didn't catch your name.

    • Kyun: "Good name" = "shubh naam" ka seedha anuvaad. Hamare yahan izzat hai, English me ajeeb lagta hai.

    • Galat: Myself Rahul, from Pune.

    • Sahi: I'm Rahul. I'm from Pune.

    • Kyun: "Myself" se introduction shuru nahi hota. Seedha "I'm…" — chhota aur natural.

    • Galat: I am agree with you.

    • Sahi: I agree with you.

    • Kyun: "Agree" khud verb hai — "am" ki zaroorat nahi. Ulta bhi: "I don't agree", "I am not agree" nahi.

    • Galat: According to me, the film was boring.

    • Sahi: I thought the film was boring. / In my view, it was boring.

    • Kyun: "According to" doosron ke liye hai — according to the news, according to my doctor. Apni raay = "I think".

    • Galat: So, how much do you earn? When are you getting married?

    • Sahi: So, what do you enjoy most about your job?
    • Kyun: Salary aur shaadi pehli mulaqat me bahut personal maane jaate hain. Kaam ke baare me poochho, kamaai ke baare me nahi.

    32.9 Practice — Small talk¶

    Exercise 1

    Conference ke chai break me kisi anjaan se baat shuru karni hai. Sabse natural opener kaunsa hai?

    • Is this your first time at this conference?
    • What is your good name?
    • How much salary do you get?
    • Myself Rohan, tell me about you.
    Jawaab

    Is this your first time at this conference?

    Jo jagah aap dono share kar rahe ho, uske baare me sawaal — sabse safe aur sabse natural. Baaki teeno ya to anuvaad hain ya bahut personal.

    Exercise 2

    Apni raay ko mazboot banane ke liye uske saath kya jodna chahiye?

    • a reason with 'because'
    • the word 'definitely' twice
    • 'according to me' at the start
    • a louder voice
    Jawaab

    a reason with 'because'

    Raay + wajah = mazboot raay. 'I think X because Y' — sunne wala asehmat ho sakta hai, par aapko halke me nahi lega.

    Exercise 3

    Colleague ki baat se aap asehmat ho. Sabse polite jawaab kaunsa hai?

    • You are wrong.
    • That's a fair point, but I see it differently.
    • No, no, that is not correct.
    • I am not agree.
    Jawaab

    That's a fair point, but I see it differently.

    Formula: maan lo + but + apni baat. Pehla hissa izzat deta hai. 'I am not agree' to grammar ke hisaab se bhi galat hai.

    Exercise 4

    I ___ with you. The traffic is terrible these days.

    Hint: 'am' lagane ki zaroorat nahi.

    Jawaab

    agree / totally agree / completely agree

    'Agree' khud verb hai — 'I agree', 'I am agree' nahi.

    Exercise 5

    Well, it was really nice ___ to you.

    Hint: Baat khatam karne ki line — verb + ing.

    Jawaab

    talking / speaking

    'It was nice talking to you' — baat-cheet ke ant ki sabse garam line.

    Exercise 6

    Is introduction me ek shabd ajeeb hai — us par tap karo.

    Myself Anjali and I work in a bank.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Myself.

    Sahi roop: I'm Anjali and I work in a bank.

    Introduction 'Myself…' se nahi, 'I'm…' se shuru hota hai.

    Exercise 7

    Shabdon ko sahi kram me lagao — ek khula follow-up sawaal banao.

    Shabd: do / What / most / job / you / your / about / enjoy

    Jawaab

    What do you enjoy most about your job

    Wh-word + do + you + verb — ye khula sawaal hai, iska jawaab haan/na me nahi aata.

    Exercise 8

    Line ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    How did you get into that? politely asehmat hona
    To be honest, I didn't like it. baat khatam karna
    I see your point, but… follow-up sawaal
    I'll let you get back to it. sach wali raay
    Jawaab

    How did you get into that? — follow-up sawaal; To be honest, I didn't like it. — sach wali raay; I see your point, but… — politely asehmat hona; I'll let you get back to it. — baat khatam karna

    Har line ka ek kaam hai — sawaal, raay, asehmati, ant. Yahi kadam small talk ko chalaate hain.

    Exercise 9

    Party se nikalne ki line bolo — garam aur saaf.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: It was really nice talking to you

    'talking to you' ko jodkar bolo — 'tawking-tu-yu'. Aakhri 'you' par aawaaz neeche.

    Exercise 10

    Ab politely asehmat ho kar dikhao.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: That's a fair point, but I see it differently

    'but' ke baad zara ruko — isse lagta hai aapne unki baat sach me suni.

    Exercise 11

    Ek opener bolo — sawaal ke ant me aawaaz upar.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Is this your first time here

    Haan/na wale sawaal me aakhri shabd par aawaaz upar jaati hai — 'here?'

    Aage kya karein

    Is hafte ek naye insaan se chaar kadam wali baat karo — chahe wo society ka guard ho ya office ka naya intern. Pehli baar ajeeb lagega, teesri baar aadat ban jaayegi.
