    Chapter 14: Interjection — bhaav wale shabd¶

    Interjection wo shabd hai jo achaanak koi bhaav bahar nikaal deta hai — khushi, dard, hairani, afsos. Ye vaakya ka hissa nahi hota; baaki vaakya se alag khada rehta hai, aur hata do to vaakya ka matlab bilkul nahi badalta — sirf uski aawaz chali jaati hai. Aath parts of speech me ye sabse chhota hissa hai, par bolchaal me sabse zyada kaam aata hai.

    Ek line me

    An interjection is a word or short phrase that expresses sudden feeling and stands apart from the rest of the sentence. Hindi me "arre, waah, haaye, achha, ohho" — bilkul wahi kaam.

    • Wow! That was a brilliant catch. Waah! Kya shaandaar catch thi.
    • Ouch! That hurt. Aah! Dard hua. (Hindi me hum "ssss" ya "aai" bolte hain.)
    • Oh, I forgot my wallet at home. Halka bhaav hai, isliye comma — exclamation mark nahi.

    14.1 Interjection vaakya me kahan baithta hai¶

    Interjection ka baaki vaakya se koi grammar ka rishta nahi hota. Wo na subject hai, na verb, na object. Isliye use hata do, vaakya tootta nahi.

    Interjection ki paanch pehchaan (Features)

    1. Strong / sudden emotion — achaanak, tez bhaav batata hai: khushi, dard, dar, hairani.
    2. Stands apart — baaki vaakya se alag khada rehta hai, aksar shuru me.
    3. Usually "!" — aksar iske baad exclamation mark lagta hai (halka bhaav ho to comma).
    4. No grammatical link — subject, verb ya object se koi grammar ka rishta nahi; hata do, vaakya theek rehta hai.
    5. Same word, different feelings — ek hi shabd alag mauke par alag bhaav deta hai.

    • Oh! What a lovely surprise. "Oh" = hairani / khushi (Surprise).
    • Oh! My knee hurts. Wahi "Oh" = dard (Pain).
    • Oh! I am so sorry to hear that. Wahi "Oh" = afsos (Sorrow). Bhaav shabd se nahi, mauke aur aawaz se pata chalta hai.

    14.2 Prakaar — bhaav ke hisaab se¶

    School ki kitaabon me interjection ke das (10) prakaar padhaaye jaate hain. Har prakaar ka English naam bhi saath me hai, taaki textbook se seedha milaan ho sake.

    Type (bhaav) Typical shabd Example Hindi me
    1. Joy (khushi) Hurrah! Hurray! Yippee! Hurray! We won the match. Waah! Jeet gaye!
    2. Sorrow (dukh / afsos) Alas! Oh! Ah! Alas! He could not make it. Haaye! Afsos!
    3. Surprise (hairani) Oh! Wow! What! Ah! What! You passed already? Arre! Sach me?
    4. Pain (dard) Ouch! Ah! Oh! Ouch! The tea is hot. Aai! Ssss!
    5. Approval / Appreciation (taareef) Bravo! Well done! Excellent! Bravo! What a performance. Shabaash! Kya baat hai!
    6. Anger / Irritation (gussa, jhunjhalahat) Pooh! Bah! Bah! Another traffic jam. Uff! Hadd hai!
    7. Greeting (abhivaadan) Hello! Hi! Hey! Hello! How are you? Namaste! Kaise ho?
    8. Attention (dhyan khinchna) Hey! Look! Listen! Look! The train is coming. Arre! Dekho!
    9. Calling (bulaana) O! Ho! Hey! Hey, Ravi! Come here. Aye Ravi! Idhar aao.
    10. Fear (dar) Help! Oh! Ah! Help! There is a snake! Bachao!
    • Yippee! School is closed tomorrow. Joy — bachchon wali uchhalti khushi.
    • Well done! You topped the class. Approval — do shabd, par kaam ek interjection ka.
    • O God, please help us! Calling — "O" aaj prarthana aur kavita me hi bacha hai; hamesha capital, aur turant naam ke saath.

    'Damn!' — gusse ka shabd, par rude

    Filmon me "Damn!" bahut sunai deta hai, par ye rude / asabhya maana jaata hai. Class, office, bade logon ke saamne kabhi mat bolo. Jhunjhalahat batani ho to "Oh no!", "Ugh!" ya "Come on!" kaafi hai.

    Bolchaal ke aur bhi bhaav¶

    Bhaav Shabd Example Hindi me
    Sehmati Yes! Sure! Indeed! Right! Indeed, that was clever. Bilkul! Sahi!
    Nafrat / ghin Ugh! Yuck! Eww! Yuck! This milk has gone bad. Chhee! Thoo!
    Rukna / sochna Well… Um… Hmm… Er… Hmm, let me check. Hmm… Achha…
    Galti Oops! Whoops! My bad! Oops! I sent the wrong file. Oho! Galti ho gayi.
    Rahat Phew! Thank goodness! Phew! That was close. Uff! Bach gaye.

    Common interjections aur unka use¶

    Interjection Kab bolte hain Example
    Oh! hairani, dard, afsos — teeno Oh! I didn't know that.
    Ah! samajh aana, sukoon, halka dard Ah, now I understand.
    Wow! hairani + taareef Wow! What a view.
    Ouch! achaanak apna dard Ouch! I cut my finger.
    Hurray! jeet ki khushi Hurray! We are on holiday.
    Bravo! kisi ke kaam ki taareef Bravo! Great speech.
    Hello! / Hi! milna, phone uthaana Hi! Good to see you.
    Hey! dhyan khinchna, bulaana (barabar walon ko) Hey! Wait for me.
    Help! dar, musibat Help! I can't swim.
    Oops! chhoti galti Oops! I dropped it.
    Phew! rahat Phew! We made it.
    Alas! gehra dukh (kitaabi) Alas! The king was dead.

    14.3 Punctuation — teen pakke rule¶

    Kahan comma, kahan exclamation mark

    1. Tez bhaav → exclamation mark, aur agla shabd capital se: Wow! That is amazing.
    2. Halka bhaav → comma, aur agla shabd small se: Oh, it's you.
    3. Beech me aaye to dono taraf comma: The result, alas, was disappointing.

    Ek hi shabd, do punctuation — matlab badal jaata hai

    Oh! You came. — hairani, lagta hai umeed nahi thi.
    Oh, you came. — halki pehchaan, lagta hai pata tha. Punctuation yahan bhaav ka volume control hai.

    Exclamation mark ek se zyada nahi

    Chat me "Wow!!!" chalta hai. Email, exam ya kisi bhi likhe kaam me ek hi exclamation mark lagao. Teen lagane se baat zyada nahi, kam gambhir lagti hai.

    14.4 Interjection ya kuch aur — ek shabd ke do kaam¶

    Kai shabd interjection ke roop me bhi aate hain aur normal part of speech ke roop me bhi. Faisla ye dekh kar hota hai ki wo vaakya ke andar hai ya bahar.

    Shabd Interjection ke roop me Normal roop me
    well Well, I am not sure. She sings well. (adverb)
    what What! You are leaving? What is your name? (pronoun)
    right Right, let's begin. Your answer is right. (adjective)
    indeed Indeed! I never expected that. It was indeed difficult. (adverb)
    my My! How you have grown. This is my bag. (possessive)

    Pehchaanne ka test

    Shabd hata ke baaki vaakya padho. Agar vaakya poora aur theek hai → wo interjection tha. Agar vaakya adhoora ho gaya → wo vaakya ka hissa tha. "Well, I am not sure" → "I am not sure" poora hai. ✓ interjection.
    "She sings well" → "She sings" ka matlab badal gaya. ✗ interjection nahi.

    14.5 Hindi se English — asli bolchaal ke jode¶

    Hum log Hindi interjections ko seedha English me le jaate hain — "Arre, where were you?" Ye samajh aa jaata hai par English nahi lagta. Neeche kaam ke jode hain:

    Hum bolte hain English me Kab
    Arre! Hey! / Oh! / Come on! dhyan khinchna ya halki hairani
    Achha? Really? / Is that so? nayi baat sunkar
    Achha, theek hai Right. / Okay then. baat maan lena
    Waah! Wow! / Brilliant! taareef
    Haaye! Oh no! / Alas! (purana) dukh
    Uff / Chalo bach gaye Phew! rahat
    Chhee! Yuck! / Ugh! ghin
    Hmm… dekhte hain Well… let me see. sochte waqt
    Oho, galti ho gayi Oops! My bad. chhoti galti
    Arre nahi! Oh no! / No way! buri khabar sunkar

    'Alas', 'Hurrah', 'Pooh' ka sach — purane shabd

    School ki kitaabon me Alas!, Hurrah! aur Pooh! bahut aate hain, par aaj ki bolchaal me ye lagbhag nahi bole jaate — old-fashioned aur naatakiya lagte hain. Exam aur kahani me likhna theek hai; baat-cheet me neeche wale aaj ke shabd bolo.

    Purana (kitaabi) Aaj bolo Bhaav
    Alas! Oh no! / That's so sad. dukh
    Hurrah! Yay! / Yes! / Woo-hoo! khushi
    Pooh! / Bah! Ugh! / Come on! / Seriously? jhunjhalahat
    O! / Ho! Hey! / Excuse me! bulaana

    14.6 Office aur formal jagah — kahan rukna hai¶

    Jagah Chalta hai Nahi chalta
    Dost ke saath chat Wow! Oops! Yay! Ugh! —
    Office email Well, Right, Indeed (comma ke saath) Yay! Yuck! Oops!
    Interview Well, … (sochne ka waqt lene ke liye) Hmm… bahut baar
    Exam ki writing kahani / dialogue me essay ya report me

    Interview ki ek kaam ki baat

    Sawaal sunkar turant bolne ki jagah "Well, that's an interesting question…" bolna bilkul theek hai — isse do second sochne ka waqt mil jaata hai aur aap taiyaar lagte hain. Par ek hi interview me teen baar ye na bolein.

    14.7 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Interjection ke saath hone wali galtiyan

    • Galat: Myself Ravi. Wow, nice to meet you!
    • Sahi: Hello! I am Ravi. Nice to meet you.

    • Kyun: Do baatein: "Myself Ravi" galat hai — subject ke liye "I" chahiye. Aur milte waqt "Wow" ajeeb lagta hai; wo taareef ke liye hai. Greeting me "Hello" ya "Hi" bolo.

    • Galat: Oh! i forgot to call her.

    • Sahi: Oh! I forgot to call her.

    • Kyun: Exclamation mark ke baad naya vaakya shuru hota hai, isliye agla shabd capital se. Comma lagate to small letter chalta: Oh, I forgot…

    • Galat: Dear Sir, Wow!!! Thank you for the offer.

    • Sahi: Dear Sir, Thank you very much for the offer.

    • Kyun: Formal email me interjection aur teen exclamation mark dono bhaari padte hain. Khushi batani ho to shabdon se batao: I am delighted to accept.

    • Galat: Alas the news was bad.

    • Sahi: Alas, the news was bad.

    • Kyun: Interjection ke baad punctuation zaroori hai — comma ya exclamation mark. Bina kuch lagaye wo vaakya me ghul jaata hai aur padhne me atakta hai.

    • Galat: Ouch, she don't know the answer.

    • Sahi: Oh no, she doesn't know the answer.

    • Kyun: "Ouch" sirf apne dard ke liye hai, kisi aur ki musibat ke liye nahi — uske liye "Oh no". Aur "she" ke saath "doesn't" lagta hai, "don't" nahi.

    • Galat: Hey! Sir, can you discuss about my marks?

    • Sahi: Excuse me, Sir, could we discuss my marks?

    • Kyun: "Hey!" barabar walon ke liye hai; kisi bade ya teacher ke liye "Excuse me" bolo. Aur "discuss" ke baad "about" nahi lagta.

    • Galat: Yuck! This is my cousin brother's cooking.

    • Sahi: Hmm, this is my cousin's cooking — not my favourite.
    • Kyun: "Yuck" kaafi kathor hai, kisi ke saamne uske khane par bolna rukhaai hai. Aur "cousin" apne aap me poora shabd hai — brother jodne ki zaroorat nahi.

    14.8 Chhota naksha¶

    Chaar cheezein yaad rakho

    1. Interjection vaakya ke bahar hota hai — hata do, vaakya tootta nahi.
    2. Tez bhaav → ! aur agla shabd capital. Halka bhaav → , aur agla shabd small.
    3. Formal likhne me na use karo — wahan shabdon se bhaav batao.
    4. Ek exclamation mark kaafi hai.

    14.9 Practice — Interjections¶

    Exercise 1

    ___! We finally won the trophy.

    • Hurray
    • Alas
    • Ouch
    • Yuck

    Hint: Khushi ka bhaav chahiye.

    Jawaab

    Hurray

    Jeet par khushi hai, aur khushi ke liye Hurray / Yay / Wow aate hain. Alas dukh ke liye, ouch dard ke liye, aur yuck ghin ke liye hai.

    Exercise 2

    Kaunsa vaakya punctuation ke hisaab se sahi hai?

    • Oh! I forgot my keys.
    • Oh! i forgot my keys.
    • Oh i forgot my keys.
    • Oh. i forgot my keys!

    Hint: Exclamation mark ke baad kya hota hai?

    Jawaab

    Oh! I forgot my keys.

    Exclamation mark ke baad naya vaakya shuru hota hai, isliye agla shabd capital se. Comma lagate to small letter chalta — 'Oh, I forgot'.

    Exercise 3

    'Well, I am not sure about that.' — yahan 'Well' kya hai?

    • interjection
    • adverb
    • adjective
    • conjunction

    Hint: Shabd hata ke vaakya padho.

    Jawaab

    interjection

    'Well' hataane ke baad bhi 'I am not sure about that' poora vaakya hai, isliye wo bahar khada tha — interjection. 'She sings well' me hataao to matlab badal jaata, wahan wo adverb hai.

    Exercise 4

    Office email me kaunsa roop sabse theek hai?

    • I am delighted to accept the offer.
    • Wow!!! Thanks for the offer.
    • Yay! I accept.
    • Oops, I accept.

    Hint: Formal jagah me bhaav shabdon se batate hain.

    Jawaab

    I am delighted to accept the offer.

    Formal likhne me interjection aur multiple exclamation marks bhaari lagte hain. Khushi shabdon se batana zyada professional lagta hai.

    Exercise 5

    ___! That water is boiling hot. (dard ka shabd)

    Hint: Apne dard par kya bolte hain?

    Jawaab

    Ouch

    Achaanak dard ke liye Ouch / Ow aata hai. Dhyan rakho — ye sirf apne dard ke liye hai; kisi aur ki musibat par 'Oh no' bolte hain.

    Exercise 6

    ___, that was a close call. (rahat ka shabd)

    Hint: Bach jaane par ek saans chhodte hain.

    Jawaab

    Phew

    Rahat ke liye 'Phew' aata hai — Hindi ka 'uff' ya 'chalo bach gaye'. Ye saans chhodne ki aawaz se bana hai.

    Exercise 7

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — galat shabd par tap karo.

    Hey Sir can you discuss about my result?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: about.

    Sahi roop: Excuse me, Sir, could we discuss my result?

    'Discuss' ke baad 'about' nahi lagta. Aur 'Hey' barabar walon ke liye hai — teacher ya kisi bade ke liye 'Excuse me' bolo.

    Exercise 8

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Oh no she don't know about the meeting.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: don't.

    Sahi roop: Oh no, she doesn't know about the meeting.

    'she' third person singular hai, isliye 'doesn't' lagta hai. Aur interjection ke baad comma bhi chahiye tha.

    Exercise 9

    Ek galti hai — dhoondo.

    Myself Ravi and wow I am happy to be here.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Myself.

    Sahi roop: Hello! I am Ravi, and I am happy to be here.

    Reflexive pronoun 'myself' subject nahi ban sakta — 'I am' chahiye. Aur milte waqt 'wow' ajeeb lagta hai; greeting me 'Hello' bolo.

    Exercise 10

    'Bravo! That was a brilliant speech.' — 'Bravo' kis type ka interjection hai?

    • Approval / Appreciation
    • Joy
    • Surprise
    • Calling

    Hint: Kisi ke kaam ki taareef ho rahi hai.

    Jawaab

    Approval / Appreciation

    Bravo!, Well done!, Excellent! — ye kisi ke kaam ki taareef (Approval / Appreciation) ke shabd hain. Apni khushi ke liye Hurray! ya Yippee! (Joy) aata hai.

    Exercise 11

    ___! There is a snake under the bed! (dar ka shabd)

    Hint: Musibat me madad maangte hain.

    Jawaab

    Help

    Fear (dar) ke liye 'Help!' sabse saaf interjection hai. 'Oh!' aur 'Ah!' bhi dar me aate hain, par 'Help!' turant musibat bata deta hai.

    Exercise 12

    Dost ke saath baat-cheet me 'Hurrah!' ki jagah aaj kya bolna zyada natural hai?

    • Yay!
    • Alas!
    • Pooh!
    • Damn!

    Hint: Hurrah purana, kitaabi shabd hai.

    Jawaab

    Yay!

    'Hurrah!', 'Alas!', 'Pooh!' aaj old-fashioned lagte hain. Khushi ke liye aaj 'Yay!' ya 'Yes!' bolte hain. 'Damn!' rude hai — use mat karo.

    Exercise 13

    Interjection ki pehchaan me kaunsi baat GALAT hai?

    • Ye vaakya ka subject banta hai
    • Ye achaanak bhaav batata hai
    • Iske baad aksar '!' lagta hai
    • Ek hi shabd alag bhaav de sakta hai

    Hint: Paanch features yaad karo.

    Jawaab

    Ye vaakya ka subject banta hai

    Interjection ka vaakya se koi grammatical rishta nahi hota — na subject, na object. Baaki teeno baatein uske features hain.

    Exercise 14

    Interjection ko uske bhaav se jodo.

    A B
    Hurray! dard
    Alas! rahat
    Ouch! ghin
    Phew! khushi
    Yuck! dukh
    Jawaab

    Hurray! — khushi; Alas! — dukh; Ouch! — dard; Phew! — rahat; Yuck! — ghin

    Interjection ka chunaav bhaav se hota hai, grammar se nahi. Galat bhaav ka shabd chunne se baat ajeeb lagti hai — jaise taareef me 'Alas' bol dena.

    Exercise 15

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao — interjection pehle aayega.

    Shabd: painting / is / beautiful / this / Wow

    Jawaab

    Wow this painting is beautiful

    Interjection lagbhag hamesha vaakya ke shuru me aata hai, kyunki bhaav pehle nikalta hai aur baat baad me. Likhte waqt uske baad comma ya exclamation mark lagta hai.

    Exercise 16

    Ye line bol ke dekho — 'Oh no' ko thoda kheench kar bolna hai, jaise sach me afsos ho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Oh no I have missed the train

    Interjection ka asar shabd se zyada aawaz se aata hai. Flat bol do to natak lagta hai; bhaav ke saath bolne par natural lagta hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Interjection ratne ki cheez nahi, sunne ki cheez hai. Koi English show dekhte waqt dhyan do ki log hairani aur rahat me kya bolte hain — aur agle din ek naya shabd try karo. Isi ke saath aath parts of speech poore hue; aage vaakya banane ka hissa shuru hota hai.
