    Chapter 56: Asli duniya ki English¶

    Asli duniya ki English — announcement se interview tak

    Station par announcement ek baar hota hai. News reader rukta nahi. Lecture me professor 40 minute bolta hai, aur ad me sabse zaroori baat sabse tez bolte hain. Asli duniya ki listening me aap sab kuch nahi pakad sakte — aur zaroorat bhi nahi. Zaroorat hai strategy ki: pehle se andaaza, key words, signposts, numbers, aur achhe notes. Is chapter me chhe asli formats hain (sab kalpanik, par bilkul asli jaise) — har ek ke apne niyam ke saath.

    56.1 Sunne ki chaar strategies¶

    Advanced listener zyada tez nahi sunta — wo smart sunta hai. Wo jaanta hai kis cheez ka intezaar karna hai. Ye chakra har format par lagta hai:

    Pehle andaaza, phir sunna

    Sunne se pehle 10 second socho: ye kaun bol raha hai, kis liye, aur kaunse shabd aayenge? Announcement = train, platform, minutes. Forecast = degrees, rain, wind. Jo dimaag pehle se intezaar kar raha ho, wo tez English bhi pakad leta hai.

    Signposts — baat ki disha batane wale shabd¶

    Signpost Kya batata hai
    However / But ulti baat aa rahi hai — dhyan do!
    On the other hand doosra pehlu, tulna
    For example udaharan — main baat pehle hi aa chuki
    In other words wahi baat, aasaan shabdon me
    So / As a result nateeja
    Finally / To sum up aakhri point / saar

    Numbers aur names¶

    • Gate thirteen, not gate thirty. thir-TEEN (zor peeche, lamba "n") vs THIR-ty (zor aage). Galat gate = chhooti flight.
    • That's Varghese, V-A-R-G-H-E-S-E. Naam aksar spell karke bolte hain. Letters ki aawaz pakki karo: G (jee) vs J (jay), E (ee) vs I (aai).
    • The fourteen thirty train — that's 2:30 p.m. Station aur airport par 24-ghante ki ghadi: fourteen thirty = dopahar 2:30. (Dekho: Number, date, time, paise.)

    56.2 Notes kaise lein¶

    Lecture, interview ya news — notes me poore vaakya kabhi nahi. Sirf key words, numbers aur nishaan. Ek page ko teen hisson me baanto:

    Nishaan Matlab
    ↑ / ↓ badhna / ghatna (prices ↑, temp ↓)
    → nateeja, ki taraf, badalna
    = / ≠ matlab hai / alag hai
    b/c · w/ · w/o because · with · without
    ~ · approx. lagbhag

    Notes = shabd + number + nishaan

    Poora vaakya likhoge to agla vaakya chhoot jaayega. Noun aur number likho, verb ki jagah ↑ ↓ → lagao. "Fuel costs will be cut by nearly 40%" → fuel cost ↓ ~40%.

    56.3 Railway aur airport announcements¶

    Announcements ka dhaancha hamesha ek jaisa hai: dhyan kheencho → kaun si train/flight → kya badla → aapko kya karna hai. Bhasha bahut formal hoti hai (passengers are requested to…), aur echo ki wajah se shabd dhundhle ho jaate hain. Isliye predict karo: number, platform/gate, time.

    Phrase Matlab
    scheduled to depart chhootne ka tay samay
    running late by itni der se chal rahi hai
    foot overbridge paidal pul (FOB)
    final boarding call flight me chadhne ki aakhri pukaar
    proceed to (formal) ki taraf jaayein

    🎧 Do announcements suno

    • Railway station — Pune Junction Do badlaav hain — dono pakdo.
    • Airport — final boarding call "immediately" do baar — matlab samay khatam ho raha hai.
    Transcript

    Railway: Attention, please. Train number 12794, the Deccan Pearl Express from Pune to Hyderabad, scheduled to depart at 14:30, is running late by approximately 45 minutes. This train will now depart from platform number 6, instead of platform number 2. Passengers are requested to use the foot overbridge to reach platform 6. Please do not cross the railway tracks. We regret the inconvenience caused.

    Airport: Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. This is the final boarding call for SkyIndus flight SI 712 to Kochi. Passengers on this flight are requested to proceed immediately to gate number 23, where boarding is about to close. Passenger Shalini Varghese, travelling to Kochi, please report to gate 23 immediately. Thank you.

    • This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two. Ab train platform 6 se chhootegi, 2 se nahi. "instead of" ke baad purani jaankari — naya number pehle aata hai.
    • We regret the inconvenience caused. Asuvidha ke liye khed hai. Ye line aate hi samjho — announcement khatam, ab koi nayi jaankari nahi.

    Announcement ki aam galti

    • Galat: "…platform six, instead of platform two" → main platform 2 par jaata hoon (aakhri number yaad raha).
    • Sahi: Naya platform = 6. "instead of" ke baad wala number purana hai.
    • Kyun: Kaan aakhri shabd yaad rakhta hai. Isliye "instead of / not / no longer" jaise shabdon par ruk kar socho: kaunsa naya, kaunsa purana?

    56.4 Mausam ki khabar (weather forecast)¶

    Forecast me teen cheezein hamesha hoti hain: kya hoga (rain, sun, wind), kab (tonight, by tomorrow morning, the weekend) aur kitna (degrees, km per hour). Aakhir me aksar ek salaah hoti hai. Suno: taapmaan kitna girega, aur machhuaaron ko kya kaha gaya?

    Phrase Matlab
    humid umas bhara (HYOO-mid)
    relief is on the way raahat aane wali hai
    showers halki, thodi der ki baarish
    are advised not to salaah: na jaayein (chetaavni)

    🎧 Forecast suno

    • Weather — Mumbai aur Konkan coast Notes ke liye: aaj → kal → weekend. Har ek ke aage ek-do shabd.
    Transcript

    Here's the weather for Mumbai and the Konkan coast. After two very hot and humid days, relief is finally on the way. Light showers are expected in the city tonight, and by tomorrow morning the rain will become heavier, especially in the northern suburbs. Temperatures will drop from 34 degrees to around 29. Winds from the south-west may reach 50 km per hour along the coast, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea until Thursday. However, the weekend looks brighter, with sunshine returning on Saturday. If you're commuting tomorrow, leave a little early and carry an umbrella.

    • Winds may reach fifty kilometres per hour, so fishermen are advised not to go out to sea. Hawa 50 km/h tak pahunch sakti hai, isliye machhuaare samundar me na jaayein. Kaaran (tez hawa) → "so" → salaah.
    • However, the weekend looks brighter. Lekin weekend behtar dikh raha hai. "However" = mausam ki kahani ulti disha me mud gayi — baarish ke baad dhoop.

    56.5 News bulletin¶

    News me sabse zaroori baat pehle vaakya me hoti hai (kaun, kya, kab) — baaki vaakya detail dete hain. Har nayi khabar "In other news…" ya "And finally…" se shuru hoti hai. Ye bulletin kalpanik hai — shahar aur ghatnaayein banaayi hui hain. Suno: pehli khabar kis baare me hai, aur kis baat par log khush nahi hain?

    Phrase Matlab
    announced ghoshna ki
    officials say adhikaariyon ka kehna hai (srot)
    commuter groups roz safar karne walon ke sangathan
    rice husk dhaan ki bhoosi
    represent pratinidhitva karna

    🎧 Bulletin suno (2 khabrein)

    • Story 1 — electric buses "However" ke baad doosra paksh — aalochna.
    • Story 2 — science prize "proud, but not surprised" — teacher ko bachchon par pehle se bharosa tha.
    Transcript

    Good evening, and here are tonight's main stories. The city of Ambarpur will get 200 new electric buses by the end of this year, the transport department announced today. Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly 40% and reduce air pollution on the busiest routes. However, some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well. The first 50 buses are expected on the roads in August.

    In other news, four students from a government school in Ranchi have won first prize at a national science fair. Their project, a low-cost water filter made from clay and rice husk, costs less than ₹300 to build. The students will now represent India at an international competition next spring. Speaking to reporters, their teacher said she was proud, but not surprised.

    • The buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent. Buses se fuel ka kharcha lagbhag 40% kam hoga. "nearly" = thoda kam (38–39%), "over" hota to zyada. Notes: fuel ↓ ~40%.
    • Some commuter groups have asked why ticket prices are not coming down as well. Kuch yaatri sangathan pooch rahe hain ki ticket bhi sasta kyon nahi ho raha. News "shikaayat" ko aksar aise sawaal ke roop me batata hai.

    News ka pehla vaakya = poori khabar

    Gist ke liye har khabar ka pehla vaakya dhyan se suno — kaun, kya, kab wahin hai. Baaki vaakya numbers aur raay jodte hain. "In other news" = naya topic, naya notes ka block.

    56.6 Lecture ka hissa — PIN code ki kahani¶

    Lecture lamba hota hai, isliye lecturer (yahan kalpanik Dr Meenakshi Rao) khud naksha deti hai: Today I want to talk about… So, how does it work? For example… Finally… In other words…. Ye signposts aapke notes ke headings ban jaate hain (upar wala template dekho). Suno: PIN code kyon aaya, aur pehla digit kya batata hai?

    Phrase Matlab
    stands for poora naam hai (abbreviation)
    digit ank (0–9)
    narrows it down daayra chhota karta hai
    sorting district dak chhaantne wala zila
    I'd argue that meri raay hai ki (tark ke saath)

    🎧 Lecture suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — kyon? "Why? Well, …" — lecturer khud sawaal poochh kar jawaab deta hai. Jawaab = key point.
    • Part 2 — kaise? Aakhri sawaal agle hisse ka trailer hai — notes me "GPS? → next" likh do.
    Transcript

    Good morning, everyone. Today I want to talk about something you write almost every week, but probably never think about: the PIN code. PIN stands for Postal Index Number, and it was introduced in India in 1972. Before that, letters were often delayed or lost. Why? Well, many towns had similar names, and letters were addressed in many different languages. So the postal department needed a system that worked in numbers, not words.

    So, how does it work? A PIN code has six digits, and each part has a job. The first digit tells you the region. For example, 1 covers Delhi and much of the north, while 6 covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The second digit narrows it down to a sub-region, and the third shows the sorting district. Finally, the last three digits point to the individual post office. In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address. Now, you might ask: do we still need PIN codes in the age of GPS? I'd argue that we do, and here's why.

    • One covers Delhi and much of the north, while six covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala. 1 Delhi aur uttar ke bade hisse ke liye, jabki 6 Tamil Nadu aur Kerala ke liye. "while" yahan samay nahi, tulna hai (jabki).
    • In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address. Yaani number bade se chhote ki taraf jaata hai, address ki tarah. "In other words" = saar — ye line summary me jaani chahiye.

    Lecture notes ki galti

    • Galat: Notes: "For example, one covers Delhi and much of the north…" (poora vaakya likhte rahe, agla point chhoot gaya)
    • Sahi: Notes: "1st = region (eg 1 = N, 6 = TN/KL)"
    • Kyun: "For example" ke baad wali baat udaharan hai, main point nahi. Udaharan chhota likho, main point (first digit = region) pakka likho.

    56.7 Interview — sawaal aur jawaab¶

    Radio interview me host sawaal poochhta hai aur mehmaan kahani ke saath jawaab deta hai. Asli jawaab aksar shuru me nahi, aakhir me hota hai — "That's when I realised…", "For me, that was the moment…". Ye interview kalpanik hai: Kamla ji ek gaanv me chalta-phirta pustakaalaya (mobile library) chalaati hain. Suno: shuruaat kaise hui, aur unhe kab laga ki kaam ho raha hai?

    Phrase Matlab
    by accident bina soche, sanyog se
    access pahunch, milne ka mauka
    convincing manaana, yakeen dilaana
    a waste of time samay ki barbaadi

    🎧 Interview suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — shuruaat Sawaal "?" ke saath khatam hote hain — wahin se jawaab shuru.
    • Part 2 — mushkilein aur safalta "But the bigger challenge…" — pehla jawaab (paisa) asli jawaab nahi tha.
    Transcript

    Host: Kamla ji, thank you for joining us. Your mobile library now visits forty villages. How did it all start?

    Kamla: Thank you for having me. Honestly, it started by accident. In 2019, I gave my old books to a school near my village, and within a week, the children had read all of them. They kept asking for more. That's when I realised the problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    Host: And the library is actually a bus, isn't it?

    Kamla: Yes, an old school bus. We removed the seats, added shelves, and painted it bright yellow, so children can see it coming from far away.

    Host: What has been the biggest challenge?

    Kamla: Money, of course. Fuel is expensive, and books get damaged. But the bigger challenge was convincing some parents that story books are not a waste of time.

    Host: And has that changed?

    Kamla: Slowly, yes. Last year, three mothers asked if they could borrow books for themselves. For me, that was the moment I knew it was working.

    • The problem wasn't interest. It was access. Dikkat ruchi ki nahi thi, kitaabon tak pahunch ki thi. Inference: bachche padhna chahte the, bas kitaabein nahi thin.
    • Three mothers asked if they could borrow books for themselves. Teen maaon ne apne liye kitaabein maangi. Jo parents pehle kitaabon ko "time waste" maante the, wahi ab padhna chahte hain — yahi safalta hai.

    56.8 Advertisement — bechne wali bhasha¶

    Ad sunna ek alag hunar hai: yahan aapko sirf samajhna nahi, parakhna bhi hai. Ad me sawaal (Tired of…?), sabse badhiya wale shabd (quietest), hukm (Order today), jaldi ka dabaav (before Sunday) aur "sab le rahe hain" (thousands of families) hota hai. Aur sabse zaroori baat — terms and conditions — sabse aakhir me, sabse tez. Ye product kalpanik hai.

    Tareeka Ad me Asar
    Rhetorical question Tired of sleepless nights? dard yaad dilaana
    Superlative the quietest fan we have ever made sabse achha lagna
    "up to" save up to 50% zyada se zyada — pakka nahi
    Urgency Order before Sunday jaldi faisla
    Social proof Thousands of families… sab le rahe hain

    🎧 Ad suno

    • Radio ad — CoolNest Breeze fan Gin ke dekho: kitne hukm (imperatives) aur kitne sawaal?
    Transcript

    Tired of sleepless summer nights? Meet the new CoolNest Breeze, the quietest ceiling fan we have ever made. Its smart sensor adjusts the speed automatically, so you sleep better and save up to 50% on electricity. Imagine coming home to a cool, calm room every single night. And that's not all! Order before Sunday and get free installation, plus a five-year warranty. Thousands of families have already made the switch. Why wait? Visit your nearest store or order online today. CoolNest Breeze. Feel the difference. Terms and conditions apply. Savings compared with a standard 75-watt fan.

    Ad ki chaal pakdo

    • Galat: "Save up to 50%" = bill aadha ho jaayega.
    • Sahi: "up to 50%" = zyada se zyada 50% — shayad 10% hi bache.
    • Kyun: Aur fine print kehta hai: tulna ek purane 75-watt fan se hai. Jis ghar me pehle se naya fan ho, wahan bachat aur kam hogi.

    Ad = vaada + dabaav + fine print

    Har ad me teen cheezein suno: kya vaada hai (up to?), kya dabaav hai (before Sunday), aur aakhri tez line me kya chhupa hai. Sabse dheemi bolne wali line bechti hai; sabse tez wali sach batati hai.

    56.9 Practice — Asli duniya ki English¶

    Exercise 1

    Announcement sunne se pehle sabse achhi strategy kya hai?

    • Predict — number, platform, time ka intezaar
    • Har shabd likhne ki koshish
    • Sirf aakhri line sunna
    • Aankhein band karke kuch na sochna

    Hint: Chakra ka pehla kadam.

    Jawaab

    Predict — number, platform, time ka intezaar

    Predict → Gist → Detail → Check. Jo dimaag intezaar kar raha ho, wo numbers jaldi pakadta hai.

    Exercise 2

    Signpost ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    However wahi baat, aasaan shabdon me
    For example aakhri point
    In other words ulti baat
    Finally udaharan
    Jawaab

    However — ulti baat; For example — udaharan; In other words — wahi baat, aasaan shabdon me; Finally — aakhri point

    Signposts baat ka naksha hain — inhe sunte hi notes me naya block shuru karo.

    Exercise 3

    What has changed for the Deccan Pearl Express? (gist)

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Attention, please. Train number one two seven nine four, the Deccan Pearl Express from Pune to Hyderabad, scheduled to depart at fourteen thirty, is running late by approximately forty-five minutes. This train will now depart from platform number six, instead of platform number two.

    • It is late and the platform has changed.
    • It has been cancelled.
    • It will leave early.
    • It now goes to Mumbai.
    Jawaab

    It is late and the platform has changed.

    Do badlaav: 'running late by approximately forty-five minutes' aur 'platform number six, instead of platform number two'.

    Exercise 4

    • platform 2
    • platform 4
    • platform 12
    Jawaab

    platform 6

    'instead of' ke baad purana platform (2). Naya = 6.

Passenger Shalini Varghese, travelling to Kochi, please report to gate twenty-three immediately.

    • Go to gate 33 after dinner.

    Go to gate 23 immediately.

    'please report to gate twenty-three immediately' — final boarding call = aakhri mauka.

    • 34 degrees
    • around 24 degrees
    • 39 degrees
    Jawaab

    around 29 degrees

    'from thirty-four … to around twenty-nine' — from = abhi, to = naya.

    Exercise 7

    • because it is a holiday
    • because of fog
    Jawaab

    because of strong winds

    Kaaran 'so' se pehle: hawa 50 km/h tak. 'are advised not to' = chetaavni.

    Exercise 8

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Good evening, and here are tonight's main stories. The city of Ambarpur will get two hundred new electric buses by the end of this year, the transport department announced today. Officials say the buses will cut fuel costs by nearly forty percent.

    • new electric buses for a city
    • higher bus ticket prices
    • a new metro line
    • a fuel shortage
    Jawaab

    new electric buses for a city

    News ka pehla vaakya = poori khabar: Ambarpur ko 200 electric buses.

    Exercise 9

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    • Ticket prices are not going down.
    • The buses are too noisy.
    • There are too few routes.

    Ticket prices are not going down.

    'However' ke baad aalochna: kharcha sarkaar ka ghata, par ticket sasta nahi hua.

    • about 3,000 rupees
    • exactly 30 rupees

    Jawaab

    less than 300 rupees

    'less than three hundred rupees' — 'less than' bhi jawaab ka hissa hai.

    Exercise 11

    Why were letters often delayed before 1972?

    • Towns had similar names and addresses were in many languages.
    • There were not enough postmen.
    • Trains were very slow.
    • Stamps were too expensive.
    Jawaab

    Towns had similar names and addresses were in many languages.

    Lecturer ne khud 'Why? Well, …' poochha aur do kaaran diye.

    • the region
    • the post office
    • the sorting district
    • the state capital
    Jawaab

    the region

    'The first digit tells you the region.' 'For example' ke baad sirf udaharan hai.

    Exercise 13

    What does the lecturer do after saying 'In other words'?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Finally, the last three digits point to the individual post office. In other words, the number moves from big to small, just like an address.

    • She repeats the idea in a simpler way.
    • She gives the opposite view.
    • She starts a new topic.
    • She asks the students a question.
    Jawaab

    She repeats the idea in a simpler way.

    'In other words' = wahi baat, aasaan shabdon me — summary ke liye sabse achhi line.

    Exercise 14

    What does Kamla mean by 'the problem wasn't interest, it was access'?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    I gave my old books to a school near my village, and within a week, the children had read all of them. They kept asking for more. That's when I realised the problem wasn't interest. It was access.

    • The children wanted to read but had no books.
    • The children were not interested in books.
    • The school did not allow books.
    • The books were too difficult.
    Jawaab

    The children wanted to read but had no books.

    Ek hafte me saari kitaabein padh li aur aur maangi — ruchi thi, kitaabein nahi thin.

    Exercise 15

    Why did they paint the bus bright yellow?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Yes, an old school bus. We removed the seats, added shelves, and painted it bright yellow, so children can see it coming from far away.

    • so children can see it from far away
    • because yellow paint is cheap
    • because it was a school bus
    • to protect the books from the sun
    Jawaab

    so children can see it from far away

    'so children can see it coming from far away' — 'so' ke baad maqsad.

    Exercise 16

    What does 'save up to fifty percent' really promise?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Its smart sensor adjusts the speed automatically, so you sleep better and save up to fifty percent on electricity.

    • You may save less than 50%.
    • You will always save exactly 50%.
    • You will save more than 50%.
    • The fan costs 50% less.
    Jawaab

    You may save less than 50%.

    'up to' = zyada se zyada. Asli bachat kam bhi ho sakti hai — ad ki sabse purani chaal.
