    Chapter 47: Viraam chinh¶

    Viraam chinh — chhote nishaan, bada farq

    Bolte waqt hum saans lete hain, ruk jaate hain, aawaz uthaate hain — isse sunne wale ko matlab saaf ho jaata hai. Likhi hui English me ye kaam viraam chinh karte hain. Ye sirf "sundar likhne" ki baat nahi hai: ek comma idhar-udhar hone se matlab badal jaata hai, aur ek apostrophe galat jagah lagne se padhne wale ko turant pata chal jaata hai. Achhi khabar — gine-chune rule hain, aur wo sab is chapter me hain.

    Ek line me

    Punctuation marks tell the reader where to pause, where to stop, and how to read. Viraam chinh padhne wale ke liye traffic ke nishaan hain — kahan dheema karna hai, kahan rukna hai, aur kahan sawaal hai.

    47.1 Rukne ki lambai — ek sadak ki tarah dekho¶

    Sabse pehle ye samajh lo ki kaunsa nishaan kitni der rukwaata hai. Yahi ek tasveer aadhi confusion khatam kar deti hai.

    47.2 Full stop — sabse zaroori aur sabse andekha¶

    Hindi me hum "।" lagate hain; English me ek chhota bindu. Lamba WhatsApp message bina full stop ke ek hi saans jaisa lagta hai aur padhne wala thak jaata hai.

    • I called him twice. He did not pick up. I will try again tomorrow. Teen chhote vaakya — padhne me saaf. Ek lamba vaakya banane se baat uljh jaati hai.

    Full stop short form me bhi aata hai — Dr. Sharma, Mr. Khan, a.m., p.m., etc. — par aaj ki English me UK, USA, NGO, IIT jaise naam bina bindu likhe jaate hain.

    47.3 Comma — saat asli kaam¶

    Kaam Example
    List banane me I bought rice, dal, oil and sugar.
    Shuruaati phrase ke baad After the meeting, we went for tea.
    Do poore vaakye ko and/but/so se jodte waqt I called him, but he did not answer.
    Beech me aayi gair-zaroori jaankari My uncle, who lives in Surat, is a tailor.
    Kisi ko pukaarte waqt Ravi, please close the window.
    Haan / nahi ke baad Yes, I understand.
    Taarikh aur pate me She was born on 14 March, 1998, in Kanpur.

    Comma matlab badal deta hai

    My brother, who lives in Pune, is a doctor. Mera ek hi bhai hai — wo Pune me rehta hai (extra jaankari). My brother who lives in Pune is a doctor. Mere kai bhai hain — jo Pune me rehta hai, wo doctor hai (zaroori jaankari).

    Comma splice — do vaakya comma se nahi jude

    • Galat: It was raining, we stayed at home.
    • Sahi: It was raining, so we stayed at home.
    • Kyun: Do poore vaakye ko jodne ke liye comma kaafi nahi. Ya full stop lagao, ya comma ke saath and / but / so / because jaisa jodne wala shabd lao, ya semicolon use karo.

    47.4 Semicolon aur colon — kam use, par kaam ke¶

    Nishaan Kaam Example
    ; Do poore vaakye jo matlab se jude hain The road was blocked; we had to take a longer route.
    : Aage list aa rahi hai Bring three things: a pen, a notebook and your ID card.
    : Aage wajah ya khulaasa aa raha hai There was one problem: nobody had the keys.

    47.5 Apostrophe — do kaam, teen galtiyan¶

    Apostrophe ( ' ) sirf do kaam karta hai: kiska batata hai, ya chhoota hua akshar dikhata hai. Teesra kaam — plural banana — wo kabhi nahi karta.

    its vs it's — sabse mehanga confusion¶

    Roop Matlab Example
    it's it is / it has It's raining. · It's been a long day.
    its uska (possessive) The dog hurt its leg.

    Pakka test

    Jahan "it's" likha hai, wahan "it is" bolkar padho. Agar vaakya banta hai to apostrophe sahi hai; nahi banta to "its" chahiye. Yahi test your's/yours, their's/theirs ke saath bhi chalta hai — possessive pronouns me apostrophe kabhi nahi lagta: yours, hers, ours, theirs, its.

    47.6 Capital letters — aath pakke mauke¶

    Mauka Example
    Vaakya ka pehla akshar The bus was late.
    "I" hamesha bada Ravi and I went together.
    Logon ke naam Anita Sharma
    Jagah, desh, nadi, shehar Mumbai, India, the Ganga
    Din, mahina, tyohaar Monday, August, Diwali
    Bhasha aur desh wale shabd Hindi, English, Indian
    Sanstha aur kitaab-film ke naam Delhi University, The Guide
    Padvi jab naam ke saath ho Dr. Verma, Prime Minister Nehru

    Jahan capital NAHI lagta

    Mausam (summer, winter), vishay (science, history — jab subject ka official naam na ho), dishaayein (north, south), aur aam naam (school, bank, doctor — jab kisi khaas ka naam na ho).
    I go to school in the summer. ✓ I go to School in the Summer. ✗

    47.7 Quotation marks — kisi ke shabd jaise ke taise¶

    Jab kisi ki baat jaise ki waise likhni ho, to quotation marks lagte hain. Do baatein yaad rakho: baat ka pehla akshar bada hota hai, aur andar ka viraam chinh quotes ke andar rehta hai.

    • She said, "I will call you tomorrow." "said" ke baad comma, baat ka pehla akshar bada, full stop quotes ke andar.
    • "Where are you going?" he asked. Sawaal pehle aaya to question mark quotes ke andar, aur "he" chhota.

    47.8 Question aur exclamation mark¶

    Nishaan Kab Example
    ? Seedha sawaal Have you eaten?
    . Reported ya shisht sawaal jo asal me request hai He asked where I lived. · Could you pass the salt.
    ! Tez bhaav — khushi, hairaani, chetawni What a match! · Watch out!

    Ek hi kaafi hai

    Thank you so much!!! → Thank you so much!
    Teen exclamation lagane se baat teen guna zyada nahi hoti — bas kachchi lagti hai. Professional email me exclamation ek-do se zyada nahi.

    47.9 Hyphen aur dash¶

    Nishaan Kaam Example
    - hyphen Do shabd jodkar ek adjective banana a well-known singer · a five-year plan
    - hyphen Ginti ke shabd twenty-one, sixty-five
    — dash Achaanak jodi gayi baat He agreed — after an hour of arguing.

    47.10 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Punctuation ki top galtiyan

    • Galat: Fresh Mango's available here.
    • Sahi: Fresh mangoes available here.

    • Kyun: Plural banane me apostrophe kabhi nahi lagta. Ye galti dukaan ke board par sabse zyada dikhti hai.

    • Galat: The company has lost it's way.

    • Sahi: The company has lost its way.

    • Kyun: "it is lost its way" nahi banta — isliye apostrophe nahi. Possessive "its" chahiye.

    • Galat: Please send the file , I need it today.

    • Sahi: Please send the file. I need it today.

    • Kyun: Do alag vaakya hain. Comma se nahi judenge — full stop lagao (ya "as" / "because" jodo).

    • Galat: Respected Sir ,

    • Sahi: Dear Sir,

    • Kyun: Viraam chinh se pehle space nahi, baad me space. Aur aaj ke English email me "Dear Sir" chalta hai — "Respected Sir" purani sarkaari bhasha hai.

    • Galat: she lives in mumbai and speaks marathi.

    • Sahi: She lives in Mumbai and speaks Marathi.

    • Kyun: Vaakya ka pehla akshar, shehar ka naam aur bhasha ka naam — teeno capital.

    • Galat: My brothers bike is new. (ek bhai ki baat)

    • Sahi: My brother's bike is new.

    • Kyun: "Kiska" batane ke liye apostrophe zaroori hai. Bina apostrophe matlab "mere kai bhai" ho jaata hai.

    • Galat: He said that "he was busy".

    • Sahi: He said that he was busy.
    • Kyun: "that" wale reported vaakya me quotes nahi lagte. Quotes sirf jab shabd jaise ke taise likhe jaayein.

    Teen aadat jo likhai turant sudhaar deti hain

    1. Ek vaakya me ek baat. Lamba ho raha hai to full stop lagao.
    2. Apostrophe lagane se pehle poochho — "kiska?" ya "kaunsa akshar chhoota?" Dono nahi, to apostrophe nahi.
    3. Nishaan se pehle space nahi, baad me ek space.

    47.11 Practice — Viraam chinh¶

    Exercise 1

    Kaunsa vaakya sahi hai?

    • The cat licked it's paw.
    • The cat licked its paw.
    • The cat licked its' paw.
    • The cat licked it is paw.

    Hint: it's = it is. Test karke dekho.

    Jawaab

    The cat licked its paw.

    Yahan matlab 'uska panja' hai — possessive. 'It is paw' bemaani hai, isliye apostrophe nahi lagta.

    Exercise 2

    Sahi roop chuno: ladkon ka common room

    • boy's common room
    • boys's common room
    • boys' common room
    • boys common room

    Hint: 'boys' pehle se s par khatam hai.

    Jawaab

    boys' common room

    Plural noun jo s par khatam hota hai, usme sirf apostrophe lagta hai — boys'. Ek ladke ka hota to boy's.

    Exercise 3

    Kaunsa vaakya sahi punctuation ke saath likha hai?

    • It was late, we went home.
    • It was late we went home.
    • It was late, so we went home.
    • It was late; so we went home.

    Hint: Dono hisse poore vaakya hain ya nahi?

    Jawaab

    It was late, so we went home.

    Do poore vaakye ko comma akela nahi jod sakta (comma splice). Comma ke saath 'so' jaisa jodne wala shabd chahiye, ya full stop.

    Exercise 4

    Is me se kis shabd me capital letter NAHI chahiye?

    • monday
    • diwali
    • summer
    • marathi

    Hint: Mausam aur dishaayein socho.

    Jawaab

    summer

    Din (Monday), tyohaar (Diwali) aur bhasha (Marathi) capital se likhte hain, par mausam (summer, winter) chhote akshar se.

    Exercise 5

    Do poore vaakye ko aapas me jodne wale nishaan ka naam ___ hai.

    Hint: Comma se bada ruknaa, full stop se chhota.

    Jawaab

    semicolon

    Semicolon ( ; ) do poore vaakye jodta hai jab unka matlab juda hua ho — 'The road was blocked; we took another route.'

    Exercise 6

    Aage aane wali list ko kholne wale nishaan ka naam ___ hai.

    Hint: Do bindu, ek ke upar ek.

    Jawaab

    colon

    Colon ( : ) list ya khulaasa kholta hai — 'Bring three things: a pen, a notebook and your ID.'

    Exercise 7

    Bhasha, shehar aur din ke naam hamesha ___ letter se shuru hote hain.

    Hint: Bada ya chhota akshar?

    Jawaab

    capital

    English, Mumbai, Monday — khaas naam hamesha capital letter se. Par mausam aur dishaayein chhote akshar se.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek shabd galat likha hai — us par tap karo.

    We sell fresh mango's and bananas.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: mango's.

    Sahi roop: We sell fresh mangoes and bananas.

    Plural banane me apostrophe kabhi nahi lagta. 'Mangoes' sahi roop hai.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    My father works in a bank and he speaks english fluently.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: english.

    Sahi roop: My father works in a bank and he speaks English fluently.

    Bhasha ka naam hamesha capital letter se — English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil.

    Exercise 10

    Ek shabd galat hai — pakdo.

    The company changed it's name last year.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: it's.

    Sahi roop: The company changed its name last year.

    Yahan matlab 'uska naam' hai. 'It is name' bemaani hai, isliye apostrophe hatana padega — 'its'.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao (comma apne aap sahi jagah aa jaayega).

    Shabd: meeting / the / After / for / we / out / tea / went

    Jawaab

    After the meeting we went out for tea

    Likhte waqt isme shuruaati phrase ke baad comma lagega — 'After the meeting, we went out for tea.' Shuruaat me aaye phrase ke baad comma lagta hai.

    Exercise 12

    Nishaan ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    , aage list ya khulaasa
    ; kiska, ya chhoota akshar
    : tez bhaav
    ' chhota ruknaa, list aur phrase
    ! do jude poore vaakya
    Jawaab

    , — chhota ruknaa, list aur phrase; ; — do jude poore vaakya; : — aage list ya khulaasa; ' — kiska, ya chhoota akshar; ! — tez bhaav

    Comma sabse halka break, semicolon do vaakya jodta hai, colon aage ki baat kholta hai, apostrophe possessive ya contraction, exclamation bhaav.

    Exercise 13

    Ab ye line bol ke dekho — comma wali jagah par halka ruknaa hai.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: After the meeting we had tea and then we went home

    Likhne me yahan do comma aayenge — 'After the meeting, we had tea, and then we went home.' Bolte waqt wahi do jagah halka rukte hain. Punctuation asal me aapki saans ka naksha hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Sabse badhiya practice: apna likha hua zor se padho. Jahan saans lene ka man kare, wahan comma chahiye. Jahan baat poori ho jaaye, wahan full stop. Aapke kaan aapke vyaakaran se zyada jaante hain — unki sunno.
