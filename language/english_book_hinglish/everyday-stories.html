    Chapter 55: Roz ki kahaniyan¶

    Roz ki kahaniyan — office, ghar aur safar ki English

    Asli zindagi me English chhoti kahaniyon jaisi saaf nahi hoti. Meeting me log jaldi-jaldi summary dete hain, customer care wala script padhta hai, dost voice note me aadhe shabd kha jaata hai. Is chapter me paanch lambe tukde hain (100–200 shabd). Yahan sirf "kya hua" nahi — kyon hua aur bolne wala kaisa mehsoos kar raha hai, ye bhi pakadna hai. Aur naye shabd? Unka matlab context se nikaalna seekhenge.

    55.1 Line ke beech sunna — inference kya hai¶

    Log aksar seedha nahi kehte "I am angry" ya "I am worried". Wo apne shabdon, aawaz ke utaar-chadhaav aur haalat se bata dete hain. In teen clues ko jodkar matlab nikaalna = inference. Intermediate level ke listening sawaal ("How does she feel?", "Why does he say…?") isi par hote hain.

    Feeling = words + tone + situation

    "How does she feel?" ka jawaab ek shabd se nahi, teen clues se aata hai: kya kaha, kaise kaha, aur kis haalat me kaha. Pehle haalat samjho, phir shabd, phir aawaz.

    • Rohit, I know that's frustrating, but it's the safer choice. Rohit, mujhe pata hai ye chidhaane wali baat hai, par ye zyada surakshit faisla hai. Bolne wali samajh rahi hai ki Rohit naraaz hoga — wo usko manaa rahi hai.
    • Honestly, I don't blame her. Sach kahun to main use galat nahi maanta. Matlab: "uska aisa karna theek hai" — galti meri thi.
    • And please don't be late this time, okay? Aur is baar late mat hona. "this time" chhupa sach bata deta hai — pichhli baar wo late thi.

    55.2 Team meeting ka summary¶

    Kavita ek software team ki lead hai. Meeting ke aakhir me wo saari baatein jaldi se dohraati hai. Aise summary me signpost words naksha banaate hain: First, Second, Third, And finally. Suno: launch date kyon badli, aur stand-up ab kitne baje hoga?

    Phrase Matlab (is context me)
    sum up saar me batana, nichod dena
    launch date product shuru hone ki taarikh
    stand-up roz ki 10–15 minute ki chhoti meeting
    take over kisi ka kaam apne haath me lena
    have a think soch lo (British, dostana)

    🎧 Poora summary suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — good news aur nayi date Do numbers pakdo: purani date aur nayi date.
    • Part 2 — stand-up aur Sana ka kaam "instead of" sunte hi samjho — kuch badal raha hai.
    Transcript

    Kavita: Okay, everyone, let me quickly sum up today's meeting. First, the good news: the new billing app is almost ready, and the client loved the demo on Tuesday. Second, we've moved the launch date from the tenth of March to the seventeenth, because the testing team needs one more week. Rohit, I know that's frustrating after all those late nights, but honestly, it's the safer choice.

    Third, from next Monday, our daily stand-up will start at nine forty-five instead of ten, so please don't be late. And finally, Sana is moving to the design team next month, so someone will need to take over her weekly reports. Have a think and let me know by Friday. Any questions? No? Great. Thanks, everyone, and well done on the demo.

    • We've moved the launch date from the tenth of March to the seventeenth. Launch 10 March se 17 March kar diya. "from … to …" = purana → naya. Kaaran turant "because" ke baad aata hai.
    • After all those late nights. Itni raaton tak der se kaam karne ke baad. Inference: Rohit ne bahut mehnat ki thi — isliye wo naaraz ho sakta hai.

    Signposts = summary ka naksha

    First… Second… Third… And finally… sunte hi notes me 1, 2, 3, 4 likh lo. Har number ke aage ek-do shabd — poora vaakya nahi. "And finally" = ab aakhri baat; dhyan wapas laao.

    55.3 Customer care call — internet band hai¶

    Mrs Lata Iyer ghar se kaam karti hain aur unka internet kal raat se band hai. Wo BlueRiver Broadband ke customer care ko phone karti hain. Yahan ek hi aawaz dono log padhegi — naam ("Hi, Deepak", "Thank you, Mrs Iyer") aur sawaal-jawaab se pehchaano kaun bol raha hai. Suno: unke liye ye itna urgent kyon hai, aur do baje tak theek na hua to kya milega?

    Phrase Matlab (is context me)
    down (machine, net) band, kaam nahi kar raha
    fault takneeki kharaabi
    arrange intezaam karna
    complaint number shikaayat ka number
    patience sabr (PAY-shuns)

    🎧 Poora call suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — problem kya hai ID ke letters aur digits alag-alag bole jaate hain — B R seven two zero five.
    • Part 2 — kab tak theek hoga "If it isn't…" ke baad shart (condition) aur vaada — ise dhyan se suno.
    Transcript

    Deepak: Good morning, thank you for calling BlueRiver Broadband. My name is Deepak. How may I help you today?

    Mrs Iyer: Hi, Deepak. My internet has been down since last night, and I work from home, so this is really urgent.

    Deepak: I'm very sorry to hear that, ma'am. May I have your customer ID, please?

    Mrs Iyer: Yes, it's BR7205.

    Deepak: Thank you, Mrs Iyer. I can see there's a fault in your area, and our engineers are already working on it.

    Mrs Iyer: How long will it take?

    Deepak: It should be fixed by 2 p.m. today. If it isn't, I'll arrange a technician visit, and you'll get a 50% discount on this month's bill.

    Mrs Iyer: Okay, but please make sure it's done. I have an important client call at three.

    Deepak: Absolutely, ma'am. Your complaint number is SR4481. Is there anything else I can help you with?

    Mrs Iyer: No, that's all. Thank you.

    Deepak: Thank you for your patience. Have a good day.

    • I work from home, so this is really urgent. Main ghar se kaam karti hoon, isliye ye bahut zaroori hai. "so" ke pehle kaaran, baad me nateeja.
    • Okay, but please make sure it's done. Theek hai, par pakka kar dena. "Okay, but…" = maan to liya, par bharosa poora nahi. Inference: wo pareshaan aur thodi be-sabr hain.
    • It should be fixed by two p.m. today. Aaj do baje tak theek ho jaana chahiye. "should" = ummeed hai, guarantee nahi. "by 2" = do baje se pehle kabhi bhi.

    Customer care ki English — galat samajhna aasaan hai

    • Galat: "It should be fixed by two" = do baje pakka theek ho jaayega.
    • Sahi: "It should be fixed by two" = ummeed hai, do baje se pehle.
    • Kyun: "should" vaada nahi, andaaza hai. Isiliye Deepak turant plan B deta hai: "If it isn't, …".

    55.4 Safar ki gadbad — teen past tenses¶

    Ek bhai apni behan ke saath Jaipur shaadi me jaane ki kahani sunaata hai. Kahani me teen tarah ka past hai — past simple (ek ke baad ek ghatna), past continuous (jab kuch chal raha tha) aur past perfect (usse bhi pehle). Tense samjho to samay ka kram samajh aata hai. (Poora niyam: Saare 12 tenses.)

    Phrase Matlab (is context me)
    security queue checking ki line (queue = "kyoo")
    realised achaanak samajh aaya
    furious bahut zyada gusse me
    got through (checking) paar kar li
    I don't blame her main use galat nahi maanta

    🎧 Poori kahani suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — airport par jhatka Behan kuch nahi boli — ye chuppi bhi ek clue hai.
    • Part 2 — ek aur musibat "But the adventure wasn't over" = abhi aur gadbad baaki hai.
    Transcript

    Last December, my sister and I were travelling to Jaipur for our cousin's wedding. We had booked an early flight from Bengaluru, so we left home at four in the morning. While we were standing in the security queue, I suddenly realised that I had left my ID card at home. I couldn't believe it. My sister was furious, and for a minute she didn't say a word.

    Luckily, a staff member told me that I could show a digital copy of my ID on my phone. I downloaded it in five minutes, and we got through just in time. But the adventure wasn't over. When we landed in Jaipur, we discovered that our suitcase had gone to Delhi! We spent the first evening of the wedding in our travel clothes. Now, whenever we travel, my sister checks my bag twice. Honestly, I don't blame her.

    • While we were standing in the security queue, I suddenly realised that I had left my ID card at home. Hum line me khade the, tabhi achaanak yaad aaya ki ID ghar par chhod aaya hoon. Ek vaakya, teen tense — background, ghatna, aur usse pehle ki baat.
    • Luckily, a staff member told me that I could show a digital copy. Kismat se staff ne bataaya ki digital copy chal jaayegi. "Luckily" = kahani ki disha achhi taraf mudi.
    • Now, whenever we travel, my sister checks my bag twice. Ab jab bhi hum safar karte hain, behan mera bag do baar check karti hai. Present simple = nayi aadat, kahani ka sabak.

    Kahani ke mod-shabd

    suddenly (achaanak jhatka) · luckily (achhi khabar) · but (ulti baat) · when we landed (naya scene). Ye shabd aate hi kahani ki disha badalti hai. Mod-shabd sunte hi agla vaakya aur dhyan se suno.

    55.5 Podcast — work from home par baat¶

    "Chai and Careers" naam ke podcast me do host — Nikhil aur Aisha — ghar se kaam karne ke fayde aur nuksaan par baat karte hain. Podcast me log raay dete hain, isliye mostly, but, to be honest, for me jaise shabd sabse zaroori hain. Suno: Nikhil ko kya achha lagta hai, kya bura, aur uska hal kya hai?

    Phrase Matlab (is context me)
    remotely office se door, ghar ya kahin aur se
    commute roz ghar se office aana-jaana
    overhear bina iraade ke kaan me padna
    hidden cost chhupa nuksaan
    best of both worlds dono taraf ka fayda

    🎧 Podcast suno (2 hisse)

    • Part 1 — Nikhil ka anubhav "Mostly, yes" — haan, par poori tarah nahi. Ab "but" ka intezaar karo.
    • Part 2 — listener ki baat aur hal "For me, it's…" = ab apni raay aa rahi hai.
    Transcript

    Nikhil: Welcome back to Chai and Careers. I'm Nikhil.

    Aisha: And I'm Aisha. Today we're talking about working from home. Nikhil, you've been working remotely for three years now. Do you still enjoy it?

    Nikhil: Mostly, yes. I save almost two hours a day because I don't have to commute. I cook a proper lunch, and I see much more of my kids. But to be honest, I sometimes feel a bit lonely. There's no chat by the coffee machine, you know?

    Aisha: That's interesting, because a lot of our listeners say the same thing. One listener, Pooja from Indore, wrote in to say that she works better at home, but she misses learning from senior colleagues.

    Nikhil: Yes, I think that's the hidden cost. You don't overhear how experienced people solve problems.

    Aisha: So what's the answer?

    Nikhil: For me, it's a hybrid model. Two days in the office for teamwork, and three days at home for focused work.

    Aisha: That sounds like the best of both worlds.

    • I see much more of my kids. Main bachchon ke saath bahut zyada samay bitaata hoon. "see more of someone" = zyada milna, saath rehna.
    • But to be honest, I sometimes feel a bit lonely. Par sach kahun to kabhi-kabhi akelapan lagta hai. "to be honest" ke baad aksar wo baat aati hai jo kehna thoda mushkil ho.
    • You don't overhear how experienced people solve problems. Aap ye nahi sun paate ki tajurbekaar log mushkil kaise suljhaate hain. Yahan "you" = koi bhi, aam log — sirf Aisha nahi.

    55.6 Dost ka WhatsApp voice note¶

    Tanvi apni dost Riya ko voice note bhejti hai. Ye informal English hai — tez, aadhe vaakya, "kind of", "six-ish", "anyway". Asli zindagi me sabse zyada yahi sunoge. Suno: Karan kyon nahi aa raha, plan kya bana, aur Riya ko kya laana hai?

    🎧 Voice note suno

    • Voice note — Tanvi → Riya Teen kaam ki baatein pakdo: kaun nahi aa raha, kahan milna, kya laana.
    Transcript

    Hey Riya! Sorry, sorry, I know I didn't reply yesterday. It was a crazy day at work. Listen, about Saturday. The plan's changed a bit. Karan can't make it, because his flight got cancelled, so it's just the four of us now. Instead of the movie, how about that new café in Bandra, the one with all the board games? I'm kind of bored of movies anyway. Oh, and can you bring your speaker? Mine's broken. Let's meet at six-ish outside the station. And please don't be late this time, okay? I'm joking. Well, mostly! Anyway, text me when you get this. Bye!

    Voice note (informal) Formal roop Matlab
    Karan can't make it. Karan is unable to attend. Karan nahi aa paayega
    I'm kind of bored of movies. I'm rather tired of films. thoda bore ho gayi hoon
    at six-ish at around six o'clock lagbhag chhe baje
    Mine's broken. Mine is not working. mera kharaab hai
    Anyway, text me. Please reply when you can. khair, message kar dena
    • Karan can't make it, because his flight got cancelled. Karan nahi aa sakta, uski flight cancel ho gayi. "can't" me "t" lagbhag gaayab — "kaan(t) make it". Matlab "na" hai, ye context (because…cancelled) se pakka hota hai.
    • I'm joking. Well, mostly! Mazaak kar rahi hoon. Khair, zyaadatar! Inference: wo sach me chahti hai ki Riya time par aaye.
    • Let's meet at six-ish outside the station. Station ke bahar lagbhag chhe baje milte hain. "-ish" = lagbhag (six-ish, tallish, greenish).

    can vs can't — context se pakdo

    Fast speech me can halka "kn" ho jaata hai aur can't lamba "kaant" — par "t" aksar sunaai nahi deta. Agle shabd dekho: because… cancelled = can't. Aawaz + context = sahi matlab.

    55.7 Context se matlab — aur sunne ke jaal¶

    Lambi English me har baar koi naya shabd aayega. Rukna mat. Teen sawaal poochho: 1) ye achhi baat hai ya buri? 2) aas-paas ke shabd kya bata rahe hain? 3) kahani ka agla hissa kya kehta hai? Aksar itna kaafi hai. (Phrasal verbs ki poori list: Phrasal verbs chapter.)

    Wo phrases jo shabd-dar-shabd galat samajh aate hain

    • Galat: "My internet is down" = internet neeche hai / dheema hai.
    • Sahi: "down" = bilkul band, kaam nahi kar raha.

    • Kyun: Machine, website, server, network ke liye "down" = band. Ulta: "It's up again" = phir chal gaya.

    • Galat: "I don't blame her" = main use dosh deta hoon.

    • Sahi: "I don't blame her" = main use galat nahi maanta, wo sahi hai.

    • Kyun: "don't" chhota sunaai deta hai aur chhoot jaata hai. Ek "not" poora matlab ulat deta hai — hamesha negatives par kaan lagao.

    • Galat: "Karan can't make it" = Karan kuch bana nahi sakta.

    • Sahi: "can't make it" = (party, meeting me) nahi aa paayega.

    • Kyun: "make it" = pahunch paana, aa paana. "Can you make it on Friday?" = kya tum Friday aa sakte ho?

    • Galat: "Take over her reports" = report le jaana.

    • Sahi: "take over" = uska kaam ab aap sambhaaloge.
    • Kyun: Sana design team me ja rahi hai — context hi bata raha hai ki uska kaam kisi aur ko karna padega.

    Naya shabd? Rukna mat

    Agla vaakya suno — wo aksar pichhle shabd ka matlab khol deta hai. Pehle achha ya bura tay karo, phir context se matlab. furious → "didn't say a word" → bahut gussa.

    55.8 Practice — Roz ki kahaniyan¶

    Exercise 1

    Why has the launch date been moved?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Second, we've moved the launch date from the tenth of March to the seventeenth, because the testing team needs one more week.

    • The testing team needs more time.
    • The client didn't like the demo.
    • Rohit is on leave.
    • The app is already finished.
    Jawaab

    The testing team needs more time.

    'because the testing team needs one more week' — kaaran 'because' ke baad.

    Exercise 2

    How does Kavita think Rohit feels about the new date?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Rohit, I know that's frustrating after all those late nights, but honestly, it's the safer choice.

    • frustrated
    • relieved
    • excited
    • bored
    Jawaab

    frustrated

    'I know that's frustrating after all those late nights' — Rohit ne der raat tak kaam kiya, ab date aage badh gayi. Kavita samajh rahi hai ki wo chidha hua hoga.

    Exercise 3

    Third, from next Monday, our daily stand-up will start at nine forty-five instead of ten, so please don't be late.

    • 9:45
    • 10:00
    • 10:15
    • 9:15
    Jawaab

    9:45

    'nine forty-five instead of ten' — 'instead of' ke baad purana time aata hai, pehle naya.

    Exercise 4

    She works from home.

    'I work from home, so this is really urgent' — so ke pehle kaaran.

    • a free new router
    • a full refund for the year
    • a call from the manager
    Jawaab

    a technician visit and a 50% discount

    'If it isn't' ke baad do vaade: technician visit + 50% discount.

    Exercise 6

    • completely relaxed
    • angry with Deepak personally
    • very happy
    Jawaab

    still worried and a little impatient

    'Okay, but…' + 'please make sure' + client call at three = maan gayi, par fikr abhi bhi hai.

    Exercise 7

    What had the speaker forgotten?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    • his ID card
    • his phone
    • the flight tickets
    • his suitcase
    Jawaab

    his ID card

    'I had left my ID card at home' — past perfect: ghar se nikalne se pehle hi chhoot gaya tha.

    Exercise 8

    How did his sister react?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    I couldn't believe it. My sister was furious, and for a minute she didn't say a word.

    • She was very angry and silent.
    • She laughed at him.
    • She cried loudly.
    • She went home alone.
    Jawaab

    She was very angry and silent.

    furious = bahut gussa; 'didn't say a word' = bilkul chup.

    Exercise 9

    What does the speaker mean by 'I don't blame her'?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Now, whenever we travel, my sister checks my bag twice. Honestly, I don't blame her.

    • She is right to check his bag.
    • He is angry with his sister.
    • She made the mistake.
    • He doesn't want her help.
    Jawaab

    She is right to check his bag.

    'I don't blame her' = main use galat nahi maanta — galti meri thi, uska check karna theek hai.

    Exercise 10

    How much time does Nikhil save every day?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    • almost two hours
    • about thirty minutes
    • three hours
    • one day a week
    Jawaab

    almost two hours

    'almost two hours a day' — commute nahi karna padta.

    Exercise 11

    • five days at home
    • five days in the office
    • working only at weekends
    Jawaab

    two days in the office, three at home

    'For me, it's a hybrid model' — 2 din office (teamwork), 3 din ghar (focused work).

    Exercise 12

    Why can't Karan come on Saturday?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    • He is ill.

    His flight got cancelled.

    'can't make it, because his flight got cancelled' — make it = aa paana.

    Exercise 13

    What does Tanvi ask Riya to bring?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Oh, and can you bring your speaker? Mine's broken. Let's meet at six-ish outside the station.

    • her speaker
    • board games
    • movie tickets
    • snacks
    Jawaab

    her speaker

    'can you bring your speaker? Mine's broken.' Board games to cafe me hi hain.

    Exercise 14

    What can we guess about Riya?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Let's meet at six-ish outside the station. And please don't be late this time, okay? I'm joking. Well, mostly!

    • She was late last time.
    • She lives far from the station.
    • She doesn't like cafes.
    • She is Tanvi's sister.
    Jawaab

    She was late last time.

    'this time' + 'Well, mostly!' — pichhli baar wo late thi, aur Tanvi aadhi seriously keh rahi hai.

    Exercise 15

    Phrase ko uske context wale matlab se jodo.

    A B
    sum up nahi aa paana
    take over dono taraf ka fayda
    can't make it saar me batana
    the best of both worlds kisi ka kaam sambhaalna
    Jawaab

    sum up — saar me batana; take over — kisi ka kaam sambhaalna; can't make it — nahi aa paana; the best of both worlds — dono taraf ka fayda

    Ye phrases shabd-dar-shabd nahi, poore matlab se samjho — context hi chaabi hai.

    Exercise 16

    Kaunsi line informal (dost ke voice note jaisi) hai?

    Hint: -ish aur kind of dhoondo.

    Jawaab

    I'm kind of bored of movies, so six-ish?

    'kind of' aur 'six-ish' informal baat-cheet ke nishaan hain. Baaki teen office ya customer care ki English hain.
