    Chapter 48: Spelling ke niyam¶

    Spelling ke niyam — ratna kam, samajhna zyada

    Email me ek galat spelling — recieve, seperate, definately — aur padhne wala turant soch leta hai ki likhne wale ne dhyan nahi diya. Achhi khabar ye hai ki English spelling jitni ulti-pulti lagti hai, utni hai nahi. Kuch gine-chune niyam zyadatar shabd sambhaal lete hain; bache hue apvaad hum ek list me yaad kar lenge.

    Ek line me

    Most English spelling follows a few rules about adding endings — double the consonant, drop the e, change y to i. Learn the rules first, then memorise the exceptions. Pehle niyam samjho, phir sirf apvaad yaad karo — poori dictionary nahi.

    48.1 Consonant kab double hota hai¶

    stopped ya stoped? beginning ya begining? Ye sawaal roz office ke email me aata hai. Iska jawaab teen chhote sawaalon me chhupa hai — neeche ka naksha ek baar follow kar lo.

    1-1-1 ka niyam

    Ek syllable, ek vowel, ek aakhri consonant + vowel wala suffix = consonant double: stop → stopped, big → bigger. Lambe shabd me sirf tab, jab zor aakhri hisse par ho: begin → beginning, par visit → visited.

    • We stopped the meeting at five. stop = ek syllable, ek vowel (o), ek consonant (p) → stopped.
    • This is only the beginning. be-GIN — zor aakhri hisse par, isliye n double: beginning.
    • She visited her parents on Sunday. VI-sit — zor pehle hisse par, isliye t single: visited.

    British twist — travel, cancel, label

    British English me aakhri l hamesha double hota hai, chahe zor kahin bhi ho: travelled, cancelled, labelled, modelling. American me single: traveled, canceled. Bharat me British chalti hai, isliye school aur office me travelled likho.

    48.2 Silent e kab girta hai¶

    make ka aakhri e bola nahi jaata — aur yahi e suffix lagate waqt sabse zyada galtiyan karwata hai. Niyam bas itna hai: suffix ka pehla akshar dekho.

    Silent e ka faisla

    Suffix vowel se shuru ho (-ing, -able, -ed) → e girao: make → making, use → usable. Suffix consonant se shuru ho (-ful, -ment, -ly) → e rakho: care → careful, move → movement.

    Shabd Vowel suffix (e gira) Consonant suffix (e raha)
    make making —
    hope hoping hopeful
    care caring careful
    move moving movement
    use usable useful
    manage managing management
    achieve achieving achievement

    Apvaad jo yaad karne padenge

    c / g wale shabd e rakhte hain taaki aawaz naram rahe: noticeable, manageable, courageous.
    Bina wajah e girta hai: truly, argument, ninth, awful.
    ee wale shabd e nahi girate: seeing, agreeing, fleeing.

    48.3 y kab i ban jaata hai¶

    happyness ya happiness? studys ya studies? Bas y se theek pehle wala akshar dekho — wahi jawaab hai.

    y ka niyam

    Consonant + y → i: happy → happiness, study → studied. Vowel + y → y rehta: play → played. Aur -ing se pehle y kabhi nahi badalta: studying, carrying.

    • She studies late and still looks happier than all of us. stud-y → studies (d consonant hai); happ-y → happier.
    • The children enjoyed the play. enjo-y → o vowel hai, isliye y bacha raha: enjoyed.

    Chaar puraane apvaad

    day → daily, pay → paid, say → said, lay → laid. Inme vowel + y hai, phir bhi i ban gaya — bas yaad kar lo.

    48.4 i before e — aur uske apvaad¶

    receive ya recieve, believe ya beleive — ie aur ei ka confusion Indian writers ki sabse aam spelling galti hai. Purani tukbandi aaj bhi kaam aati hai, bas uska poora roop yaad rakho.

    ie ya ei — teen line ki trick

    "ee" ki aawaz → ie: believe, piece, achieve.
    c ke turant baad → ei: receive, ceiling, receipt.
    "ay" ki aawaz → ei: eight, weigh, neighbour.

    Kab Spelling Examples
    "ee" ki aawaz ie believe, achieve, piece, field, chief, niece, brief
    c ke baad, "ee" ki aawaz ei receive, receipt, ceiling, deceive, perceive
    "ay" ki aawaz ei eight, weight, neighbour, vein, reign
    Apvaad — rat lo ei seize, weird, either, neither, protein, foreign, leisure, their
    c ke baad bhi ie (aawaz "ee" nahi) ie science, ancient, efficient, society
    • I received the receipt from my neighbour. receive, receipt — c ke baad ei. neighbour — "ay" ki aawaz, isliye ei.

    48.5 -able ya -ible¶

    acceptable ya acceptible? Dono ki aawaz ek jaisi hai, isliye kaan madad nahi karte. Par ek seedha test hai jo zyadatar shabdon par kaam karta hai.

    -able / -ible ka test

    Suffix hatane par poora shabd bache → -able (acceptable, comfortable). Adhoora tukda bache → -ible (possible, visible, terrible). Zyadatar shabdon par ye test sahi baithta hai.

    Kuch shabd test todte hain

    responsible, flexible, accessible, sensible — inke andar poore shabd chhupe hain, phir bhi -ible lagta hai. Aur noticeable, changeable me e isliye bacha hai taaki c/g ki aawaz naram rahe. Ye chhe shabd alag se yaad kar lo.

    48.6 British ya American — colour ya color¶

    Do spelling systems hain, aur dono sahi hain. Par Bharat me school, board exam, sarkari kaam aur zyadatar akhbaar British spelling follow karte hain. Dikkat tab hoti hai jab software chupchaap American spelling laga deta hai.

    Pattern British (Bharat) American
    -our / -or colour, favour, behaviour color, favor, behavior
    -re / -er centre, metre, theatre center, meter, theater
    -ise / -ize organise, realise, apologise organize, realize, apologize
    -ll / -l travelled, cancelled, jewellery traveled, canceled, jewelry
    -ogue / -og catalogue, dialogue catalog, dialog
    -ce / -se licence (noun), defence license, defense
    -yse / -yze analyse, paralyse analyze, paralyze
    Alag shabd programme, cheque, tyre, grey program, check, tire, gray

    Asli galti mix karna hai

    "color" likhna galti nahi — galti hai ek hi email me colour aur color dono likhna. Word ya Google Docs me language English (India) ya English (UK) set kar lo. Computer ke liye program dono systems me chalta hai, aur -ize British me bhi galat nahi — bas ek chuno aur poore document me wahi rakho.

    48.7 30 shabd jo hum sabse zyada galat likhte hain¶

    Ye list Indian offices, colleges aur job applications me sach me dikhne wali galtiyon se bani hai. Har shabd ke saath ek chhoti si trick hai — trick yaad rahegi to spelling bhi.

    Double letters ka khel¶

    Sahi Aam galti Yaad kaise rakhein
    accommodation accomodation Do c, do m — kamra itna bada ki dono double fit ho jaayein.
    occurrence occurence Do c, do r, aur ant me -ence.
    recommend reccomend re + commend: ek c, do m.
    necessary neccessary Shirt me ek Collar, do Sleeves — ek c, do s.
    committee commitee Sab double: mm, tt, ee.
    embarrass embarass Itni sharam ki do r aur do s.
    beginning begining be-GIN + n + ing — 1-1-1 ka niyam.
    tomorrow tommorow to + morrow: ek m, do r.

    Vowel ki gadbad¶

    Sahi Aam galti Yaad kaise rakhein
    separate seperate "There is a rat in separate."
    definitely definately Andar "finite" chhupa hai: de-FINITE-ly.
    privilege priviledge Na d, na a: pri-vi-lege.
    grammar grammer Ant me -ar; poore shabd me do a.
    calendar calender Ant me -ar, bilkul grammar jaisa.
    independent independant Ant me -ent: in-de-pen-dent.
    existence existance exist + ence — teeno vowel e.
    maintenance maintainance maintain se bana, par "ai" gir gaya: main-te-nance.

    ie/ei aur chhupe hue akshar¶

    Sahi Aam galti Yaad kaise rakhein
    receive recieve c ke turant baad e-i.
    achieve acheive "ee" ki aawaz, aur c nahi — to i-e.
    believe beleive "Never believe a lie."
    foreign foriegn Apvaad hai — ei. Ise rat lo.
    government goverment govern + ment — n mat chhodo.
    environment enviroment environ + ment — yahan bhi n.
    Wednesday Wensday Likhte waqt mann me bolo: Wed-nes-day.

    Indian English ke khaas¶

    Sahi Aam galti Yaad kaise rakhein
    until untill till me do l, until me sirf ek.
    across accross a + cross — ek hi c.
    truly truely true ka e gir jaata hai (apvaad).
    pronunciation pronounciation pronounce me "ou", par pronunciation me sirf "u".
    argument arguement argue ka e gir jaata hai.
    colleague collegue col + league — cricket league wali spelling.
    guarantee gaurantee g-u-a: pehle u, phir a.

    Ek hafte me paanch shabd

    Poori list ek din me mat rato. Har hafte paanch shabd chuno, unhe haath se paanch-paanch baar bolte hue likho, aur us hafte ke har email me kam se kam ek baar use karo. Chhe hafte me list khatam.

    48.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye vaakya asli office emails aur applications jaise hain. Har ek me spelling ki galti hai — aur har galti ke peeche ek niyam hai jo hum upar padh chuke hain.

    Office aur college ki asli spelling galtiyan

    • Galat: I am writting to inform you about the delay.
    • Sahi: I am writing to inform you about the delay.

    • Kyun: write ka silent e girta hai → writing (ek t). written me do t hain — wo alag shabd hai.

    • Galat: We will definately recieve the payment by Monday.

    • Sahi: We will definitely receive the payment by Monday.

    • Kyun: de-FINITE-ly. Aur c ke baad hamesha e-i: receive.

    • Galat: Kindly accomodate my request for a seperate room.

    • Sahi: Please accommodate my request for a separate room.

    • Kyun: accommodate me do c, do m. separate me "a rat" chhupa hai.

    • Galat: It was a priviledge to work with such a commited team.

    • Sahi: It was a privilege to work with such a committed team.

    • Kyun: privilege me d nahi hota. com-MIT — zor aakhri hisse par, isliye t double: committed.

    • Galat: I do not want to loose this oppurtunity.

    • Sahi: I do not want to lose this opportunity.
    • Kyun: lose = khona, loose = dheela. op-por-tu-ni-ty — "port" ki tarah do p ke saath shuru.

    Spell-check sab nahi pakadta

    loose/lose, their/there, its/it's, then/than, affect/effect — ye sab asli shabd hain, isliye laal line nahi aati. Email bhejne se pehle ek baar zor se padho; kaan wo pakad lete hain jo aankh chhod deti hai.

    48.9 Practice — Spelling¶

    Exercise 1

    Is vaakya me ek spelling galat hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    We will definately send the report today.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: definately.

    Sahi roop: We will definitely send the report today.

    Andar 'finite' chhupa hai: de-FINITE-ly. Is shabd me 'a' kahin nahi hai.

    Exercise 2

    Galat spelling wala shabd dhoondo.

    I have made a seperate folder for each client.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: seperate.

    Sahi roop: I have made a separate folder for each client.

    'There is a rat in separate' — sep-A-rate. Beech me a hai, e nahi.

    Exercise 3

    Ek shabd ki spelling galat hai — dhoondo.

    The hotel could not give accomodation to all the guests.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: accomodation.

    Sahi roop: The hotel could not give accommodation to all the guests.

    accommodation me do c aur do m. Ek m chhod dena sabse aam galti hai.

    Exercise 4

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    Did you recieve my email about the meeting?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: recieve.

    Sahi roop: Did you receive my email about the meeting?

    c ke turant baad e-i aata hai: receive, receipt, ceiling.

    Exercise 5

    -ing lagaane par kis shabd ka aakhri consonant double hoga?

    • begin
    • visit
    • open
    • happen

    Hint: Har shabd bolke dekho — zor kahan padta hai?

    Jawaab

    begin

    be-GIN me zor aakhri hisse par hai, isliye beginning. VI-sit, O-pen, HAP-pen me zor pehle hisse par hai — isliye visiting, opening, happening, single consonant ke saath.

    Exercise 6

    Kaunsi spelling sahi hai?

    • comfortible
    • visible
    • acceptible
    • dependible
    Jawaab

    visible

    'vis' akela koi shabd nahi, isliye -ible. comfort, accept, depend poore shabd hain — unke saath -able lagta hai: comfortable, acceptable, dependable.

    Exercise 7

    Indian school, board exam aur sarkari kaam me kaunsi spelling system chalti hai?

    • British
    • American
    • Dono ek hi document me mix karke
    • Koi niyam nahi
    Jawaab

    British

    Bharat me British spelling chalti hai — colour, centre, organise, travelled. American galat nahi hai, par ek document me ek hi system rakho.

    Exercise 8

    American English me 'centre' ko ___ likhte hain.

    Jawaab

    center

    British -re, American -er: centre/center, metre/meter, theatre/theater.

    Exercise 9

    Silent e tab girta hai jab suffix ek ___ se shuru ho — jaise make + ing = making.

    Hint: Consonant ya vowel?

    Jawaab

    vowel

    Vowel se shuru suffix (-ing, -able, -ed) aaye to e girta hai: making, usable. Consonant se shuru suffix (-ful, -ment) aaye to e rehta hai: careful, movement.

    Exercise 10

    Shabd + suffix ko uske sahi roop se jodo.

    A B
    happy + ness stopped
    make + ing careful
    stop + ed played
    care + ful happiness
    play + ed making
    Jawaab

    happy + ness — happiness; make + ing — making; stop + ed — stopped; care + ful — careful; play + ed — played

    happy: consonant + y, to y → i. make: vowel suffix, to e gira. stop: 1-1-1, to double. care: consonant suffix, to e raha. play: vowel + y, to y raha.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao.

    Shabd: this / colleagues / to / definitely / recommend / my / would / I / hotel

    Jawaab

    I would definitely recommend this hotel to my colleagues

    Subject (I) + would + adverb (definitely) + verb (recommend) + object. Spelling bhi dekho: definitely, recommend (ek c, do m), colleagues.

    Exercise 12

    Ab ye line bolo — har mushkil shabd ko saaf, hisson me.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: It is necessary to separate the old receipts

    Hisse yaad rakho: NE-ces-sa-ry, SEP-a-rate, re-CEIPT (p silent hai). Hisson me bolne se spelling bhi pakki hoti hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Apne phone ke notes me ek "meri spelling" list banao. Jab bhi koi shabd galat likho aur spell-check pakde, use list me daal do. Mahine ke ant me wahi list aapki sabse kaam ki dictionary hogi.
