    Chapter 29: Fluency aur filler words¶

    Fluency — rukna seekho, "basically" chhodo

    Fluency ka matlab tez bolna nahi hai. Fluency ka matlab hai bina ghabraye bolte rehna — ruk kar bhi. Duniya ke sabse achche bolne wale log dheere bolte hain aur bahut rukte hain. Farq ye hai ki wo rukte waqt "um, uh, basically, matlab" se khaali jagah nahi bharte — wo chup rehte hain, ya ek taiyaar phrase bol dete hain jo unhe sochne ka waqt de deta hai. Yahi hunar is chapter me hai.

    Fluency ka asli matlab

    Fluency = bolna jaari rehna, galti ke baad bhi, atakne ke baad bhi. Ek fluent insaan wo hai jo beech vaakya me phas kar bhi baat poori kar deta hai. Tezi, perfect grammar aur bada vocabulary — teeno baad ki cheezein hain.

    29.1 Filler ki aadat — seedhi baat¶

    Hum sab fillers use karte hain. Thode fillers normal hain — native log bhi karte hain. Dikkat tab hoti hai jab wo har teen shabd ke baad aate hain, kyunki phir sunne wala aapki baat nahi, aapki aadat sunta hai. Apni list imaandari se dekho:

    Filler Asli dikkat Kya karein
    basically Lagbhag hamesha bemaani hota hai. "Basically I went to the market" me "basically" ka koi kaam nahi. Bilkul hata do. Vaakya usse behtar ho jaayega.
    actually Iska sahi matlab hai "asal me / sachchai ye hai". Hum ise har vaakya me jod dete hain, jisse wo apna matlab kho deta hai. Sirf tab bolo jab aap kisi galatfehmi ko theek kar rahe ho.
    like "It was like, you know, like very big." — ek vaakya me teen baar. Umar se chhota aur taiyaari se kamzor lagta hai. Ruk jao. Chuppi "like" se behtar hai.
    matlab Sabse aam Hinglish filler. English baat-cheet ke beech Hindi filler turant pakda jaata hai. Badlo: "I mean…" ya "What I'm trying to say is…"
    na / haan / theek hai na Vaakya ke aakhir me jud jaata hai: "It is good, na?" Badlo: "…, isn't it?" / "…, right?"
    only (galat jagah) "I am from Delhi only." — yahan "only" ka English me koi matlab nahi banta. Hata do: "I'm from Delhi."
    you know Jab aap maan lete hain ki saamne wala jaanta hai, par wo nahi jaanta. Jo kehna hai poora kaho. Ya "Let me explain…"
    umm / uhh Ye sabse natural hai — par lambi "ummmm" ghabraahat dikhati hai. Us jagah chup raho. 2 second ki chuppi confident lagti hai.

    Pehle galat, phir sahi

    • Galat: Basically, umm, I am, like, working in sales only, matlab since two years.
    • Sahi: I work in sales. I've been there for two years.

    • Kyun: Chhote vaakye todo. Teen chhote vaakye ek lambe atakte vaakya se bahut behtar hain.

    • Galat: Actually the thing is that actually I actually forgot.

    • Sahi: To be honest, I forgot.

    • Kyun: Ek vaakya me ek hi "signpost" shabd. Baar-baar aane se wo apna asar kho deta hai.

    • Galat: It is very good, na?

    • Sahi: It's really good, isn't it?
    • Kyun: "na" ki jagah question tag. Yahi kaam karta hai, par English me.

    29.2 Chuppi ka hunar — ruko, bharo mat¶

    Jab dimaag agla shabd dhoondh raha ho, hamara muh us khaali jagah ko bharne lagta hai. Asli ilaaj ye hai: us jagah ko khaali rehne do. Do second ki chuppi aapko sochne wala dikhati hai; do second ki "ummmm" ghabraya hua.

    29.3 Sochne ka waqt khareedne wale phrase¶

    Ye is chapter ka sabse qeemti hissa hai. In phrases ko rat lo — itna ki bina soche nikal jaayein. Ye aapko 2-4 second de dete hain, aur sunne wale ko lagta hai aap soch-samajh kar bol rahe hain.

    Kaam Phrase Suno
    Sawaal ke baad waqt chahiye That's a good question.
    Let me think about that for a second.
    Hmm, I haven't thought about it that way.
    Well, it depends.
    Baat shuru karne se pehle The way I see it…
    To be honest with you…
    In my experience…
    Baat ko dobara samjhana What I mean is…
    Let me put it another way.
    In other words…
    Beech me atak gaye Sorry, what's the word… give me a second.
    How do I say this…
    I'm blanking on the word — it's the thing you use for…
    Samajh nahi aaya Sorry, could you repeat that?
    I didn't catch the last part.

    Teen phrase se shuru karo

    Poori list ratne ki koshish mat karo. Aaj sirf teen chuno — mera sujhaav: "That's a good question." · "What I mean is…" · "Sorry, could you repeat that?" Inhe 20 baar bolo, aaj. Kal ye khud nikal aayenge.

    29.4 Speed control — dheere bolna taqat hai¶

    Ghabraahat me hum tez bolne lagte hain, aur tez bolne me aawazein mil jaati hain, aur phir samajh me nahi aata, aur phir aur ghabraahat hoti hai. Ye chakkar todna hai.

    Haalat Speed Kyun
    Interview, presentation Dheere — rozmarra se bhi dheere Dheere bolna confident lagta hai, aur aapko sochne ka waqt deta hai.
    Phone par Dheere + saaf Phone par aadhi aawazein kat jaati hain. Number aur naam ek-ek akshar me bolo.
    Apna naam / email batate waqt Bahut dheere, tod kar "R-A-M-E-S-H. Ramesh." Dohraana shishtachaar hai, kamzori nahi.
    Dost ke saath Jo aaraam se nikle Yahan practice karo — galti ki keemat kam hai.
    • Let me slow down — my name is Ramesh. R for Rakesh, A, M, E, S, H. Phone par naam batane ka sabse saaf tareeka. Akshar ko shabd se jodo.
    • Sorry, I'm speaking too fast. Let me start again. Khud ko theek karna sabse professional cheez hai. Isme sharam ki koi baat nahi.

    29.5 Beech vaakya me atak gaye — ab kya?¶

    Sabse badi ghabraahat yahi hoti hai: aadhe vaakya me shabd nahi mila, aur dimaag khaali ho gaya. Iske teen pakke ilaaj hain. Teeno sikhe hue hunar hain, qismat nahi.

    Ilaaj 1 — Ghuma kar kaho (paraphrase)

    Shabd nahi mil raha? Us cheez ka kaam bata do. Ye sabse taqatwar hunar hai — isi se log 500 shabd me poori duniya ki baat kar lete hain.

    Shabd yaad nahi Aise keh do Suno
    stapler The thing you use to join papers together.
    landlord The person who owns the house I live in.
    deadline The last date to finish the work.
    puncture There's a hole in my tyre — the air went out.

    Ilaaj 2 — Shuru se dobara shuru karo

    Vaakya ulajh gaya? Use bachaane ki koshish mat karo. Chhoti si maafi ke saath nayi, chhoti line se dobara shuru karo. "Sorry, let me start that again." Native log ye roz karte hain. Ye galti nahi, tareeka hai.

    Ilaaj 3 — Madad maang lo

    Kisi cheez ka English naam poochhna kamzori nahi hai — ye seekhne wale ka sahi vyavhaar hai. "What do you call the thing that…?"
    "Is that the right word?"

    29.6 Galti ke baad — ruko mat, sudhaar kar aage badho¶

    English me khud ko sudhaarne ka ek chhota, natural tareeka hai: galat shabd bolo, phir "sorry" ya "I mean" bol kar sahi shabd daal do, aur chalte raho. Rukna aur maafi maangna zaroori nahi.

    • I went there yesterday — sorry, last week. Galti pakdi, ek shabd me theek kar diya, aage badh gaye.
    • She don't— she doesn't work here any more. Grammar galti ko beech me hi theek kar liya. Yahi sabse natural tareeka hai.
    • We have three— I mean four meetings today. "I mean" sudhaar ka sabse aam aur sahi auzaar hai.

    Jo bilkul nahi karna

    Galti hone par "Sorry, sorry, my English is very poor, sorry" wala chakkar mat shuru karo. Ek chhoti maafi kaafi hai, aur usse bhi behtar ye hai ki aap bina rukey sahi shabd daal kar aage badh jao. Baar-baar maafi maangne se sunne wala aapki baat par dhyan hi nahi de paata.

    29.7 Vaakya ko lamba karne ka saancha¶

    Fluent lagne ka ek saral trick: ek-shabd ke jawaab mat do. Har jawaab me ek jaankari + ek wajah jodo. Isse baat chalti rehti hai aur aapko sochne ka waqt bhi mil jaata hai.

    Sawaal Kamzor jawaab Fluent jawaab Suno
    Do you like cricket? Yes. Yes, I do. I watch most matches, especially the one-day games.
    Where are you from? Delhi. I'm from Delhi, but I've been living in Pune for three years now.
    How was your weekend? Good. It was good, thanks. I mostly stayed home and caught up on sleep.
    Can you finish it today? No. Not today, I'm afraid. I could have it ready by tomorrow afternoon.

    Saancha yaad rakho

    Jawaab + ek detail + ek wajah ya aage ki baat. Teen hissa, bas. Isse zyada ki zaroorat nahi, aur isse kam me baat ruk jaati hai.

    29.8 Roz ki fluency drill — 5 minute¶

    Ek minute ka monologue

    Phone ka timer 60 second par lagao. Ek aam vishay chuno — aaj ka din, apna kamra, apna kaam — aur 60 second lagataar bolo. Niyam sirf ek: ruk kar chuppi theek hai, par Hindi me switch nahi karna. Shabd na mile to ghuma kar kaho (Ilaaj 1). Roz ek minute. Do hafte me aap khud farq sunenge.

    • Today I woke up at seven, had tea, and then I checked my phone for a while. Monologue aise shuru karo — chhote vaakye, "and then" se jode hue.
    • It's hard to explain, but let me try. Atak jao to ye line bol do — aur phir koshish karo. Ye bilkul native wali line hai.

    29.9 Practice — Fluency¶

    Exercise 1

    Fluency ka sahi matlab kya hai?

    • Bina ghabraye bolte rehna, ruk kar bhi
    • Bahut tez bolna
    • Bina ek bhi galti bolna
    • Bada vocabulary hona

    Hint: Tezi ka isse koi lena-dena nahi.

    Jawaab

    Bina ghabraye bolte rehna, ruk kar bhi

    Achche bolne wale dheere bolte hain aur bahut rukte hain. Farq sirf ye hai ki wo khaali jagah filler se nahi bharte.

    Exercise 2

    'basically' ke saath sabse bada masla kya hai?

    • Zyadatar vaakyon me iska koi matlab hi nahi hota
    • Ye ek galat shabd hai
    • Iska uchchaaran mushkil hai
    • Ye sirf likhne me chalta hai
    Jawaab

    Zyadatar vaakyon me iska koi matlab hi nahi hota

    'Basically I went to the market' me usse hata do — vaakya behtar ho jaata hai. Shabd galat nahi hai, uski aadat galat hai.

    Exercise 3

    Agla shabd soch rahe hain. Sabse behtar kya karein?

    • Chup raho, ya ek taiyaar phrase bolo
    • Lambi 'ummmmm' kheencho
    • Tez bolne lago
    • Hindi me keh do
    Jawaab

    Chup raho, ya ek taiyaar phrase bolo

    Do second ki chuppi sochta hua dikhati hai; do second ki 'ummmm' ghabraya hua. Dono me waqt barabar lagta hai.

    Exercise 4

    Kisi cheez ka English naam yaad nahi aa raha. Sabse achcha tareeka?

    • Us cheez ka kaam bata do — 'the thing you use to…'
    • Baat chhod do
    • Hindi shabd daal do
    • Chup ho jao jab tak yaad na aaye

    Hint: Ilaaj 1.

    Jawaab

    Us cheez ka kaam bata do — 'the thing you use to…'

    Ghuma kar kehna sabse taqatwar hunar hai. Isi se log kam shabdon me har baat keh lete hain.

    Exercise 5

    Interview me speed kaisi rakhein?

    • Rozmarra se bhi dheere
    • Jitna tez ho sake
    • Bahut tez, taaki waqt bach jaaye
    • Shuru me tez, baad me dheere
    Jawaab

    Rozmarra se bhi dheere

    Dheere bolna confident lagta hai aur aapko agla vaakya sochne ka waqt deta hai. Tezi me aawazein mil jaati hain.

    Exercise 6

    Beech vaakya me grammar galti ho gayi. Sahi tareeka?

    • 'I mean' ya 'sorry' bol kar sahi shabd daal do aur aage badho
    • Poora vaakya dobara se bolo, bina kuch kahe
    • Baar-baar maafi maango
    • Galti chhod do, kuch mat karo
    Jawaab

    'I mean' ya 'sorry' bol kar sahi shabd daal do aur aage badho

    Native log bilkul yahi karte hain: 'She don't— she doesn't work here.' Ruk kar lambi maafi maangna baat ko todta hai.

    Exercise 7

    'matlab' ki jagah English me bolo: What I ___ is…

    Hint: Baat dobara samjhane wala shabd.

    Jawaab

    mean

    'What I mean is…' — 'matlab' ka seedha English roop, aur sochne ka waqt bhi de deta hai.

    Exercise 8

    'It is good, na?' ko theek karo: It's good, ___ it?

    Hint: Question tag.

    Jawaab

    isn't / isnt / is not

    Hindi ka 'na' English me question tag banta hai: isn't it? / right? / don't you think?

    Exercise 9

    Is vaakya me ek shabd faltu hai — us par tap karo.

    I am from Mumbai only.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: only.

    Sahi roop: I'm from Mumbai.

    Hindi ke 'hi / sirf' ki aadat se 'only' jud jaata hai. English me yahan uska koi matlab nahi banta.

    Exercise 10

    Filler shabd dhoondo jo kaat dena chahiye.

    Basically I finished the report yesterday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Basically.

    Sahi roop: I finished the report yesterday.

    Hata do aur vaakya saaf ho jaata hai. Yahi test hai: hatane se matlab na badle to wo filler hai.

    Exercise 11

    Filler ko uske sahi English badle se jodo.

    A B
    matlab do second ki chuppi
    na? kuch nahi — hata do
    ummmm What I mean is…
    basically isn't it?
    Jawaab

    matlab — What I mean is…; na? — isn't it?; ummmm — do second ki chuppi; basically — kuch nahi — hata do

    Har Hinglish filler ka ek saaf English roop hai. Ek baar rat lo, phir khud nikalne lagta hai.

    Exercise 12

    Sochne ka waqt khareedne wala phrase banao.

    Shabd: for / that / about / a / Let / me / think / second

    Jawaab

    Let me think about that for a second

    Ye 3-4 second de deta hai aur sunne wale ko lagta hai aap gaur kar rahe hain.

    Exercise 13

    Sawaal ke baad waqt khareedo. Aaraam se bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: That's a good question. Let me think about that for a second

    Do phrase ek saath — 5 second mil gaye. Interview me ye sona hai.

    Exercise 14

    Baat dobara samjhao — 'matlab' nahi bolna.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: What I mean is we should wait one more week

    'What I mean is' ke baad poora vaakya. Filler ki jagah ek kaam ka phrase.

    Exercise 15

    Shabd nahi mil raha — ghuma kar kaho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: It's the thing you use to join papers together

    stapler yaad nahi aaya to kaam bata do. Baat ruk hi nahi — yahi fluency hai.

    Exercise 16

    Ulajh gaye. Nayi shuruaat karo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Sorry, let me start that again

    Chhoti maafi, nayi chhoti line. Native log roz ye karte hain.

    Exercise 17

    Ek-shabd ke jawaab ki jagah fluent jawaab do — 'Where are you from?'

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I'm from Delhi but I've been living in Pune for three years now

    Jawaab + detail + aage ki baat. Teen hissa — isi se baat chalti rehti hai.

    Exercise 18

    Samajh nahi aaya. Politely poochho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Sorry, could you repeat that

    Aakhir me aawaz halki oopar. Ye line roz kaam aayegi — isliye bina soche nikalni chahiye.

    Exercise 19

    Atak gaye par himmat nahi haari. Ye line bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: It's hard to explain but let me try

    Bilkul native wali line. Ye bolte hi aapko sochne ka waqt bhi mil jaata hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj ka kaam: teen thinking phrases chuno aur 20 baar bolo. Aur ek minute ka monologue karo — aaj hi, abhi.

    Agla chapter: Roz ki baat-cheet — dukaan, auto, doctor, phone. Wahan aapko taiyaar lines milengi jo kal se kaam aa jaayengi.
