    Chapter 59: IELTS, TOEFL aur aapka level¶

    IELTS, TOEFL aur aapka level — A1 se C2 tak

    "Meri English kaisi hai?" — iska jawaab "achhi" ya "theek-thaak" nahi, ek level hai. Duniya bhar me English ko chhe seedhiyon me naapa jaata hai: A1 se C2. Is chapter me har level par aap kya kar sakte ho (apne aap check karne ki list ke saath), bade exams — IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Cambridge — ka seedha parichay, kitne ghante lagte hain, aur har level ke liye is kitaab ke kaunse chapters padhne hain.

    59.1 CEFR — chhe seedhiyan¶

    CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference) ek international paimaana hai. Iske teen bade hisse hain: A = shuruaat (basic user), B = apne dam par (independent user), C = mahir (proficient user). Har hisse me do level. Zyaadatar naukri aur university B2 ya C1 maangti hain.

    Level ek line me

    A = survive karna · B = apna kaam khud chalana · C = jaisa chaho waisa kehna. Har agla level pichhle se zyada samay leta hai. B1 se B2 ka safar aksar A1 se B1 jitna lamba lagta hai — ghabraao mat, ye normal hai.

    • My English is at B2 level, but my speaking is closer to B1. Apna level batane ka sahi tareeka — "at B2 level".
    • I'd like to reach C1 by the end of next year. Lakshya — reach + level + by + samay.
    • Which level do I need for this job? Naukri ke liye kitna level chahiye?

    59.2 A1 aur A2 — shuruaat¶

    Har level ko "can-do" vaakyon se samjhaya jaata hai — yaani aap kya kar sakte ho. Neeche ki list padho aur imaandaari se tick karo. Zyaadatar "haan" = aap us level par ho.

    A1 — Beginner¶

    • ☐ Main apna naam, umr, sheher aur kaam bata sakta/sakti hoon.
    • ☐ Main aasaan sawaal poochh aur samajh sakta hoon: What's your name? Where do you live?
    • ☐ Main number, keemat aur samay samajh leta hoon jab koi dheere bole.

    • ☐ Main ek chhota form bhar sakta hoon (naam, pata, phone).

    • Hi, I'm Pooja. I'm from Indore. I'm a nurse. A1 — chhote, pakke vaakya.

    • How much is this? Can you say that again, please? A1 — keemat poochhna, dobara bolne ko kehna.

    A2 — Elementary¶

    • ☐ Main apne parivaar, kaam aur roz ki routine ke baare me kuch line bol sakta hoon.
    • ☐ Main dukaan, bank ya station par aam kaam kar leta hoon.
    • ☐ Main chhota message ya note likh sakta hoon: I'm late. See you at 6.

    • ☐ Main beete kal ke baare me aasaan baat kar sakta hoon: Last Sunday I went to…

    • I work in a hospital. I usually start at eight and finish at four. A2 — routine, "usually", do vaakya jude hue.

    • Last weekend I visited my aunt in Bhopal. We went to the lake. A2 — simple past ke saath chhoti kahani.

    59.3 B1 aur B2 — apne dam par¶

    B1 — Intermediate¶

    • ☐ Safar me aane wali zyaadatar situations main khud sambhaal leta hoon.
    • ☐ Main apne anubhav, sapne aur plans ke baare me jud-jud ke bol sakta hoon.
    • ☐ Main apni raay ki chhoti wajah de sakta hoon: I prefer trains because…
    • ☐ Main jaani-pehchaani cheezon par seedha email ya chhota paragraph likh sakta hoon.

    • ☐ Saaf, aam raftaar ki English me main main baat samajh leta hoon.

    • I prefer working in a team because I learn a lot from other people. B1 — raay + "because" wali wajah.

    • When I was in college, I wanted to be a pilot, but I changed my mind. B1 — anubhav aur plans, jud-jud ke.

    B2 — Upper-intermediate¶

    • ☐ Main native speakers se bina zyada ruke, aaram se baat kar leta hoon — dono taraf tension nahi.
    • ☐ Main kisi mudde par apni raay fayde-nuksaan ke saath samjha sakta hoon.
    • ☐ Main news, lambe articles aur apne field ke technical text ka main point pakad leta hoon.
    • ☐ Main saaf, vistaar wala text likh sakta hoon — jaise essay ya report.

    • ☐ Main apni galti aksar khud pakad ke sudhaar leta hoon.

    • Working from home saves time, but it can make teamwork harder, especially for new joiners. B2 — fayda + nuksaan + "especially" se baarikee.

    • The article argues that cities should ban cars, although it doesn't really explain how. B2 — lambe text ka main point + apni aalochna.

    B1 aur B2 ka asli farq

    B1 = kaam chal jaata hai. B2 = aaram se chalta hai — tez baat samajh lete ho, aur raay ko kaaran aur example ke saath rakhte ho. Zyaadatar Indian graduates B1 aur B2 ke beech hote hain. Is kitaab ka bada hissa isi chhalaang ke liye hai.

    59.4 C1 aur C2 — mahir¶

    C1 — Advanced¶

    • ☐ Main shabd dhoonde bina, behte hue bol leta hoon.
    • ☐ Main lambe, mushkil text samajh leta hoon — aur jo seedha nahi kaha gaya (implied meaning) wo bhi.
    • ☐ Main meeting, presentation aur padhai me English ko lachila tareeke se use karta hoon.

    • ☐ Main mushkil vishay par achhe dhaanche wala, jude hue vaakyon ka text likh leta hoon.

    • I take your point, but I'm not convinced the savings would outweigh the risks. C1 — vinamra asehmati, "outweigh" jaisa sateek shabd.

    • What she didn't say was more telling than what she did. C1 — ankahi baat samajhna (implied meaning).

    C2 — Proficient¶

    • ☐ Main sunaayi aur padhi lagbhag har cheez aasaani se samajh leta hoon.
    • ☐ Main alag-alag srot ki jaankari ka saar bana kar, jud-jud ke samjha sakta hoon.

    • ☐ Main baarik farq bhi sahi shabdon me keh leta hoon — mazaak, vyang, ishaara bhi.

    • It's not that the plan is wrong, exactly; it's more that it's been oversold. C2 — baarik farq: galat nahi, bas zyada badha-chadha ke bataaya gaya.

    • Taken together, the three reports suggest a slowdown rather than a crisis. C2 — kai srot ka saar, sateek nateeja.

    Har skill ka level alag ho sakta hai

    Bahut log reading me B2 aur speaking me B1 hote hain. Ye bilkul normal hai. Isliye exams har skill ka alag score dete hain — aur aapko apni sabse kamzor skill par sabse zyada kaam karna chahiye.

    59.5 Bade exams — ek nazar me¶

    Neeche har exam ka moti jaankari wala parichay hai. Format, fees, score aur niyam samay-samay par badalte hain — exam book karne se pehle hamesha official website par current details check karo, aur jahan apply kar rahe ho (university, visa, company) wahan ki zaroorat bhi.

    Exam Format (moti baat) Score
    IELTS Academic Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking · lagbhag 2 ghante 45 minute · speaking examiner ke saamne Band 0–9 (aadhe band bhi)
    IELTS General Training Wahi chaar hisse; Reading aur Writing roz ki zindagi aur kaam ke text par Band 0–9
    TOEFL iBT Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing · computer par · speaking mic me record 2026 se naya 1–6 scale (purana 0–120 kuch samay saath)
    PTE Academic Speaking & Writing, Reading, Listening · computer par, lagbhag 2 ghante · computer score karta hai 10–90
    Cambridge B2 First / C1 Advanced Reading & Use of English, Writing, Listening, Speaking (aksar jodi me) Cambridge English Scale + grade

    IELTS — Academic ya General Training?¶

    Academic = university me padhai ya professional registration (jaise doctors, nurses). General Training = kaam, migration, ya school-level padhai. Listening aur Speaking dono me same hote hain; Reading aur Writing alag. Writing Task 1 Academic me graph ya chart samjhana hai, General me ek letter likhna.

    • I'm taking IELTS Academic next month. I need a band seven overall. "take" an exam; "band seven overall" — kul milaakar 7.
    • The speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner. IELTS speaking — examiner se aamne-saamne baat (lagbhag 11–14 minute).
    • Most universities accept scores that are less than two years old. IELTS, TOEFL, PTE ke score aam taur par 2 saal tak maane jaate hain. Cambridge certificate expire nahi hota — par koi institution recent result maang sakti hai.

    Exam pass-fail nahi hota

    IELTS, TOEFL aur PTE me pass ya fail nahi hota — sirf score. Sawaal ye hai ki aapki manzil kitna score maangti hai. Isliye "IELTS clear karna hai" ki jagah: "I need a band 7 in IELTS."

    59.6 Kaunsa exam chunein?¶

    Exam chunne ka pehla sawaal English nahi, manzil hai: kahan jaana hai aur kyun? Wahi institution batayegi ki kaunse exam maanya hain.

    Exam chunne ka niyam

    Manzil → uski list → aapka comfort. Computer par typing aur mic me bolna aaram se ho to TOEFL/PTE; insaan se baat karna behtar lage to IELTS ka speaking. Do exam ek saath taiyaar mat karo — ek chuno, uske sample test do.

    • Which English tests does your university accept? University se poochhne ki pehli line.
    • What's the minimum score for each skill? Kai jagah har skill ka alag minimum hota hai.
    • I'm aiming for at least a seven in writing. aim for = nishaana rakhna.
    • I've booked the computer-delivered test for the fifteenth of March. computer-delivered = computer par hone wala test.

    59.7 Kitna samay lagega? — study plan¶

    Ek level upar jaane me aam taur par 150 se 250 ghante lagte hain, aur upar ke levels par zyada. Roz ek ghanta = mahine ke lagbhag 30 ghante. Yaani B1 se B2: lagbhag 6 se 8 mahine, agar roz padhai ho.

    Din Kya karein Samay
    Roz Is kitaab ka ek hissa, sab kuch zor se bol ke 15–20 min
    Roz English sunna — podcast, news, YouTube (subtitles English me) 20 min
    Roz 5 naye tukde (chunks) phone ke note me 5 min
    Hafte me 3 din Likhna — email, paragraph ya essay 20–40 min
    Hafte me 2 din Kisi se bolna, ya khud ko record karke sunna 15 min
    Mahine me ek baar Ek mock test ya chapter ka quiz dobara 1 ghanta
    • I study for an hour every day, and I take a mock test once a month. Apni routine English me bolna — wo bhi practice hai.
    • My weakest skill is speaking, so I record myself twice a week. Sabse kamzor skill par sabse zyada samay.
    • I've improved a lot, but I still make mistakes with articles. Apni pragati imaandaari se batana.

    Ghante gino, din nahi

    "Teen mahine me IELTS" ka koi matlab nahi jab tak ye na pata ho ki roz kitne ghante. Ek chhoti diary rakho: tareekh + minute + kya kiya. Hafte ke 5 ghante x 30 hafte = 150 ghante = lagbhag ek level.

    Kitaab ko seedhi ki tarah use karo

    Apne level ke chapters ke quiz do. 80% se upar = agla level. Neeche = wahi chapter ek baar aur, is baar sab kuch zor se bol ke. Poori kitaab ka mixed test Aakhri imtihaan me hai.

    59.9 Exam ki English — wo galtiyan jo hum bolte hain¶

    Exam ke baare me baat karte waqt bhi kuch aam Indian English aadatein aati hain. Interview ya visa officer ke saamne ye saaf roop bolo.

    ✗

    I have given IELTS last month.

    ✓

    I took IELTS last month. / I sat the IELTS test last month. (UK)

    dete

    take

    sit

    ✗

    I got 7 bands in IELTS.

    ✓

    I got a band 7 / an overall band score of 7.

    I got 6.5 in writing.

    ✗

    I want to clear IELTS.

    ✓

    I need a band 7 in IELTS for my university.

    ✗

    Speaking me rata hua jawaab: "Well, that's a very interesting question. Since my childhood…"

    ✓

    Seedha, apna jawaab: "I'd say my favourite place is the old market near my house, mainly because…"

    dhaancha

    • I'd say my favourite place is the old market near my house, mainly because of the food. "I'd say…" + "mainly because…" — speaking test ki natural shuruaat.
    • Sorry, could you repeat the question, please? Sawaal samajh na aaye to poochhna bilkul theek hai.
    • That's not something I've thought about much, but I suppose… Mushkil sawaal par sochne ka samay lena — chup rehne se behtar.

    59.10 Practice — Exams aur level¶

    Exercise 1

    CEFR me sabse ooncha level kaunsa hai?

    • C2
    • C1
    • B2
    • A1
    Jawaab

    C2

    A1 → A2 → B1 → B2 → C1 → C2. C2 = proficient.

    Exercise 2

    'Main safar ki zyaadatar situations khud sambhaal leta hoon, aur apne anubhav jod ke bata sakta hoon.' — ye kaunsa level hai?

    • B1
    • A1
    • C1
    • C2
    Jawaab

    B1

    B1 = independent user ki shuruaat — travel, anubhav, chhoti wajah.

    Exercise 3

    Level ko uske bade hisse se jodo.

    A B
    A1, A2 Independent user
    B1, B2 Proficient user
    C1, C2 Basic user
    Jawaab

    A1, A2 — Basic user; B1, B2 — Independent user; C1, C2 — Proficient user

    A = shuruaat, B = apne dam par, C = mahir.

    Exercise 4

    IELTS me Academic aur General Training me kya alag hota hai?

    • Reading aur Writing
    • Listening aur Speaking
    • Sirf Speaking
    • Kuch nahi
    Jawaab

    Reading aur Writing

    Listening aur Speaking dono me same; Reading aur Writing alag.

    Exercise 5

    IELTS ka score kis scale par aata hai?

    • Band 0–9
    • 10–90
    • 0–100 percent
    • A se F grade
    Jawaab

    Band 0–9

    IELTS: band 0–9, aadhe band bhi (6.5). PTE: 10–90.

    Exercise 6

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    I have given IELTS last month.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: given.

    Sahi roop: I took IELTS last month.

    Exam 'take' kiya jaata hai (UK: sit). Last month = simple past.

    Exercise 7

    Score ki galti dhoondo.

    I got 7 bands in IELTS.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: bands.

    Sahi roop: I got a band 7 in IELTS.

    Band ek score hai — 'a band 7' ya 'an overall band score of 7'.

    Exercise 8

    I'm ___ IELTS Academic next month. (exam dena)

    Hint: give nahi.

    Jawaab

    taking / sitting

    take an exam (UK me sit an exam bhi).

    Exercise 9

    Kaunsa exam computer score karta hai aur 10–90 scale par result deta hai?

    • PTE Academic
    • IELTS
    • Cambridge B2 First
    • Koi nahi
    Jawaab

    PTE Academic

    PTE Academic computer-based aur automated scoring wala hai, score 10–90.

    Exercise 10

    Exam chunne ka sabse pehla kadam kya hai?

    • Manzil (university / visa) ki official list dekhna
    • Sabse sasta exam
    • Dost ne jo diya wahi
    • Sabse aasaan lagne wala
    Jawaab

    Manzil (university / visa) ki official list dekhna

    Pehle manzil ki list, phir exam — wahi batati hai kaunsa score maanya hai.

    Exercise 11

    IELTS speaking test kiske saath hota hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    The IELTS test has four parts: listening, reading, writing and speaking. The speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner, and it lasts between eleven and fourteen minutes.

    • With an examiner
    • With a computer
    • With another candidate
    • On the phone
    Jawaab

    With an examiner

    'a face-to-face conversation with an examiner'.

    Exercise 12

    Speaking test kitni der chalta hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    The IELTS test has four parts: listening, reading, writing and speaking. The speaking test is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner, and it lasts between eleven and fourteen minutes.

    • 11 to 14 minutes
    • 40 minutes
    • 2 hours
    • 5 minutes
    Jawaab

    11 to 14 minutes

    'it lasts between eleven and fourteen minutes'.

    Exercise 13

    Arjun ka level sabse zyada kaunsa lagta hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Hi, I'm Arjun. I can talk about my work and my family, and I can handle most situations when I travel. But in meetings, when people speak fast, I often get lost.

    • B1
    • A1
    • C1
    • C2
    Jawaab

    B1

    Travel aur roz ki baat sambhaal leta hai (B1), par tez baat me kho jaata hai — abhi B2 nahi.

    Exercise 14

    Arjun ko sabse zyada dikkat kahan hoti hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Hi, I'm Arjun. I can talk about my work and my family, and I can handle most situations when I travel. But in meetings, when people speak fast, I often get lost.

    • In meetings when people speak fast
    • When he travels
    • Talking about his family
    • Writing emails
    Jawaab

    In meetings when people speak fast

    'in meetings, when people speak fast, I often get lost' — iske liye 'Native speed English' chapter.

    Exercise 15

    Suno aur likho.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    Jawaab

    I need an overall band score of seven to study in Canada.

    an overall band score of seven — score bolne ka sahi tareeka.

    Exercise 16

    B1 se B2 tak pahunchne me roz ek ghanta padhai se lagbhag kitna samay?

    • 6 se 8 mahine
    • 2 hafte
    • 1 mahina
    • 5 saal
    Jawaab

    6 se 8 mahine

    Ek level ≈ 150–250 ghante; roz 1 ghanta ≈ 30 ghante mahina.

    Exercise 17

    Sahi kram me lagao.

    Shabd: every / weakest / is / I / skill / speaking / so / practise / My / day

    Jawaab

    My weakest skill is speaking so I practise every day

    Sabse kamzor skill + uska hal — apni taiyaari ke baare me natural line.

    Exercise 18

    Ab bol ke dekho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I need a band seven in IELTS

    'clear IELTS' nahi — score batao.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj apni self-check list ke tick gino aur apna level likh lo. Phir us level ke do chapters chuno aur is hafte poore karo. Mahine ke ant me Aakhri imtihaan do — aur dekho aap kitna upar chadhe.
