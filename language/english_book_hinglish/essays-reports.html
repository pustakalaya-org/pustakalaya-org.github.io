    Chapter 53: Essay aur report¶

    Essay aur report — intro, body, conclusion, IELTS style bhi

    School me humne essay "ratte" the — "Since ancient times, man has…" se shuru, teen page lambe. Asli duniya ka essay ulta hai: chhota, saaf, aur ek raay ke saath. Is chapter me poora dhaancha hai — thesis, PEEL paragraph, teen tarah ke essay, essay ke linking words, formal aawaz, IELTS Writing Task 2 ka seedha parichay, ek annotated model essay, aur office wali report ka format.

    53.1 Essay ka naksha — intro, body, conclusion¶

    Har essay teen hisson me chalta hai. Intro bade topic se shuru hokar aapki raay (thesis) tak pahunchta hai. Body ke do-teen paragraph us raay ko saboot dete hain. Conclusion raay ko dobara kehkar thoda bada sochta hai.

    Chaar paragraph ka essay

    Intro (2–3 line) → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion (2–3 line). 250–300 words ke essay ke liye yahi kaafi hai. Har body paragraph = ek main idea. Paragraph ki buniyaad (topic sentence, ek idea) ke liye Vaakya se paragraph dekho.

    53.2 Thesis statement — poore essay ki ek line¶

    Thesis intro ki aakhri line hoti hai. Ye batati hai ki aap kya sochte ho aur aage kya saabit karoge. Kamzor thesis sirf topic dohraata hai; majboot thesis ek saaf raay leta hai — aur aksar wajah bhi bata deta hai.

    Kamzor Majboot
    This essay will discuss online classes. Online classes are a useful addition to school, but they cannot replace a real teacher.
    Pollution is a big problem. The most effective way to cut urban pollution is to make public transport faster, not just cheaper.
    There are many advantages and disadvantages. Although working from home saves time, its effect on teamwork outweighs that benefit.
    • While technology has made learning easier, it cannot replace a good teacher. "While…" se doosri taraf ko maano, phir apni raay — ek line me santulan.
    • I firmly believe that cities should invest more in cycling lanes than in flyovers. Opinion essay me "I believe" bilkul theek hai.

    ✗

    Since ancient times, education has been a burning question in today's modern world.

    ✓

    In many Indian cities, students now spend more time on coaching than in school.

    asli, aaj ki

    53.3 PEEL — body paragraph ka formula¶

    Har body paragraph chaar kadmon me chalta hai. Ye formula yaad ho jaaye to "kya likhun?" wala sawaal khatam.

    Kadam Kaam Shuruaat ke shabd
    Point Paragraph ka ek main idea Firstly, … · One major advantage is …
    Explain Kyun? Kaise? This is because … · This means that …
    Evidence Example, data, anubhav For instance, … · A recent survey found …
    Link Thesis se jodo Therefore, … · This shows that …

    Ek PEEL paragraph — tukde pehchano

    • [P] One major benefit of online classes is flexibility. Point — ek idea, saaf.
    • [E] Students can watch lessons at any time, which helps those who work part-time or live far from a college. Explain — ye fayda kaise hai.
    • [E] For instance, a student in a small town can now attend lectures from a university in Delhi without moving there. Evidence — ek thos example.
    • [L] This shows that online learning can widen access to higher education. Link — wapas thesis se jodo.

    PEEL ek line me

    Kya (Point) → kyun (Explain) → saboot (Evidence) → to kya (Link). Evidence ke bina paragraph sirf raay hai. Exam me data nahi hota? Ek realistic example kaafi hai — "For instance, in my city…".

    53.4 Teen tarah ke essay — sawaal pehchano¶

    Essay likhne se pehle sawaal ka type pehchano. Galat type ka jawaab = achhi English ke baad bhi kam marks. Sawaal ki aakhri line me hi type chhupa hota hai.

    Type Sawaal kaisa dikhta hai Dhaancha
    Opinion To what extent do you agree or disagree? Raay intro me → 2 kaaran → raay dobara
    Discussion Discuss both views and give your opinion. View A → View B → apni raay (intro aur conclusion me)
    Problem–solution What are the causes? What can be done? Kaaran / samasya → hal
    Advantages–disadvantages Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? Fayde → nuksaan → kaun bhaari
    • Some people think that all university education should be free. To what extent do you agree? Opinion — saaf haan/naa/aadha, aur kyun.
    • Some believe children should start school at four, while others think seven is better. Discuss both views. Discussion — dono paksh, phir aapki raay.
    • Many young people in cities feel lonely. Why is this, and what can be done about it? Problem–solution — kaaran + hal, dono.

    Sawaal ke har hisse ka jawaab do

    "Discuss both views and give your opinion" me raay dena zaroori hai. "Why is this, and what can be done?" me dono sawaal. Ek hissa chhoota = Task Response me seedha nuksaan.

    53.5 Linking words — kaam ke hisaab se¶

    Vaakya se paragraph me humne however, therefore, for example dekhe. Essay me thode aur chahiye — khaaskar maanna-phir-kaatna (concession) aur doosron ki raay batana. Har shabd ka grammar alag hai, wahi sabse zaroori hai.

    Kaam Shabd Grammar
    Maano, phir kaato although, while, whereas + poora clause
    Maano, phir kaato despite, in spite of + noun / -ing
    Doosron ki raay It could be argued that…, Critics claim that… + clause
    Natija consequently, as a result, thus vaakya ki shuruaat + comma
    Zor dena in particular, indeed, above all vaakya me kahin bhi
    Ant In conclusion, On balance, Overall conclusion ki pehli line
    • Although metro fares are high, the service saves commuters a great deal of time. although + clause (subject + verb).
    • Despite the high fares, many people prefer the metro. despite + noun. ("despite of" nahi!)
    • It could be argued that exams measure memory rather than understanding. Doosri raay ko izzat se rakhna — discussion essay ke liye.
    • Fuel prices rose sharply. Consequently, more people switched to electric scooters. consequently = iske nateeje me (therefore se thoda formal).
    • On balance, the benefits of the scheme outweigh its costs. On balance = sab taul ke — conclusion ki achhi shuruaat.

    ✗

    Although the metro is expensive, but it is fast.

    ✓

    Although the metro is expensive, it is fast. / The metro is expensive, but it is fast.

    although

    but

    ✗

    Despite of the rain, the match continued.

    ✓

    Despite the rain… / In spite of the rain…

    despite

    in spite of

    53.6 Formal register — essay ki aawaz¶

    Essay me aap dost se baat nahi kar rahe. Contractions nahi (don't → do not), slang nahi, aur bolchaal ke chhote shabdon ki jagah thode formal shabd. Par yaad rakho: formal ka matlab bhaari nahi, saaf hai.

    Bolchaal Essay me
    a lot of / lots of many, a large number of, a great deal of
    kids children, young people
    get better improve
    go up / go down rise / fall, increase / decrease
    things factors, issues, aspects
    really bad serious, harmful
    I think I believe, In my view (opinion essay me)
    • A large number of young people now rely on social media for news. "Lots of kids get news from Insta" ka formal roop.
    • Air quality tends to improve when private vehicles are restricted. "tends to" — har baar nahi, aksar. Essay me hedge karna achha lagta hai.
    • This issue is becoming increasingly serious in smaller cities as well. "really bad" ki jagah "increasingly serious".
    • Several factors contribute to this problem, the most important being cost. "many things" ki jagah "several factors".

    Hedge karo — sab pe 'always' mat lagao

    tend to, can, may, often, in many cases — ye shabd aapki baat ko imaandaar aur majboot banate hain. "Social media always destroys students" se behtar: "Social media can distract many students."

    ✗

    In my opinion, I think that it doesn't work.

    ✓

    In my view, this approach does not work.

    53.7 IELTS Writing Task 2 — ek nazar me¶

    IELTS Writing me do task hote hain. Task 2 ek essay hai — Academic aur General Training dono me lagbhag ek jaisa. Task 1 se iska wazan zyada hai, isliye ispe zyada samay do. (Exam ke format ki poori jaankari IELTS, TOEFL aur aapka level me.)

    Band kaise milta hai — chaar criteria, seedhi bhasha me¶

    Criterion Seedhi bhasha me
    Task Response Sawaal ke har hisse ka jawaab diya? Raay saaf hai? Ideas ko samjhaya aur example diya?
    Coherence & Cohesion Paragraph ka kram theek hai? Ek paragraph = ek idea? Linking words sahi aur zaroorat jitne?
    Lexical Resource Shabdon me vividhta aur sahi jodi (collocation)? Spelling theek?
    Grammatical Range & Accuracy Chhote-bade dono tarah ke vaakya? Galtiyan kam?

    IELTS Task 2 ke pakke niyam

    40 minute, kam se kam 250 words, chaaron criteria ka barabar wazan. 250 se kam = penalty. Rati hui lines aur sawaal ke shabd copy karna = nuksaan. Exam ke niyam badal sakte hain — hamesha official IELTS website par current details dekho.

    53.8 Model essay — tukde-tukde samjho¶

    Neeche ek opinion essay hai (lagbhag 270 words). Har paragraph ke neeche likha hai ki wo kya kaam kar raha hai. Pehle 🔊 se suno, phir padho.

    Sawaal

    • Some people believe that public transport in cities should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Type: opinion ("To what extent…"). Aapko saaf raay deni hai.

    Introduction

    • In many Indian cities, roads are choked with private vehicles, while buses and metro trains often run below capacity. Some argue that the best solution is to make public transport free for all. While this idea has clear benefits, I believe that free travel should be offered only to those who need it most, rather than to everyone. Line 1: background (asli, aaj ki baat). Line 2: sawaal apne shabdon me (paraphrase). Line 3: thesis — "While… I believe…" — santulit par saaf raay.

    Body 1 — doosri taraf ko maano

    • Admittedly, free public transport could significantly reduce traffic and pollution. If travel cost nothing, many commuters would leave their cars and motorbikes at home, which would mean cleaner air and shorter journeys. It would also help families on low incomes, who often spend a significant part of their earnings on daily travel. For example, Luxembourg made all public transport free in 2020, partly to tackle congestion. This suggests that, in principle, removing fares is a serious option for crowded cities. P: Admittedly… reduce traffic (maana). E: If travel cost nothing… (second conditional). E: Luxembourg ka example. L: This suggests that…

    Body 2 — apni raay ka kaaran

    • However, offering free travel to everyone would be extremely expensive. Ticket sales pay for a large share of maintenance and new routes, so governments would have to raise taxes or cut services. In a country with limited public funds, this money might be better spent on schools or hospitals. As a result, buses might become older and more crowded, which would discourage the very people the policy aims to attract. It would therefore be more sensible to target support at students, senior citizens and low-income workers. P: However… expensive. E: ticket ka paisa kahan jaata hai. E: nateeja — purani, bhari buses. L: It would therefore… — wapas thesis par.

    Conclusion

    • In conclusion, although free public transport for all is an attractive idea, its cost would be difficult to sustain. On balance, I believe that free or discounted passes for those in need, combined with more frequent and reliable services, would achieve the same goals far more effectively. Raay dobara, naye shabdon me. Koi naya kaaran nahi — sirf saar aur aage ka raasta.

    Model se kya churaayein

    Dhaancha churaao, lines nahi: background → paraphrase → thesis, maano → kaato, aur "On balance" wala ant. Rati hui poori lines examiner pakad leta hai — unke marks nahi milte. Sawaal se copy kiye shabd bhi word count me nahi gine jaate.

    53.9 Report — headings, findings, recommendations¶

    Essay raay deta hai; report jaankari aur sujhaav deti hai. Office, college project aur kuch exams (jaise Cambridge B2 First) me report likhni padti hai. Report ki pehchaan: headings, chhote paragraph, aur aakhir me saaf recommendations.

    Heading Kya jaata hai Taiyaar shuruaat
    Title Report kis baare me Report on the staff canteen
    Introduction Maqsad aur tareeka The purpose of this report is to…
    Findings Kya pata chala (data) It was found that… · The majority of…
    Conclusion Ek line ka saar Overall, …
    Recommendations Kya karna chahiye It is recommended that… · The company should…

    Mini report — staff canteen

    • Introduction. The purpose of this report is to assess staff satisfaction with the office canteen. It is based on an online survey of 120 employees conducted in March. Maqsad + data kahan se aaya.
    • Findings. The majority of respondents, 68 per cent, said the food was good value. However, nearly half complained about long queues between one and two pm. Only a few mentioned hygiene. Numbers ke shabd: the majority of, nearly half, only a few. Raay nahi — sirf tathya.
    • Recommendations. It is recommended that lunch be served in two shifts and that a pre-order option be added to the office app. "It is recommended that + subject + base verb (be served)" — formal. Aasaan roop: "The company should serve lunch in two shifts."

    Report ki taiyaar lines¶

    • This report is based on interviews with twenty shop owners. Data kahan se aaya — Introduction me.
    • A significant number of respondents said they would pay more for faster delivery. respondents = jinhone survey ka jawaab diya.
    • Only a small minority were unhappy with the timings. the majority ↔ a small minority — numbers ko shabdon me.
    • The management should consider extending the opening hours. Recommendation ka aasaan roop — should + consider + -ing.

    Report ki pehchaan

    Headings + tathya + sujhaav. Findings me raay nahi; Recommendations me "should" ya "It is recommended that…". Essay = "I believe…"; report = "It was found that…".

    ✗

    I think the canteen is very bad and people are very angry.

    ✓

    Nearly half of the respondents complained about long queues at lunchtime.

    • Title me

    Exercise 2

    PEEL ke har akshar ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    Point example ya data
    Explain thesis se jodna
    Evidence paragraph ka main idea
    Link kyun aur kaise
    Jawaab

    Point — paragraph ka main idea; Explain — kyun aur kaise; Evidence — example ya data; Link — thesis se jodna

    Kya → kyun → saboot → to kya.

    Exercise 3

    'Discuss both views and give your opinion.' — ye kaunsa type hai?

    • Discussion
    • Opinion
    • Problem–solution
    • Report
    Jawaab

    Discussion

    Dono paksh + apni raay = discussion essay.

    Exercise 4

    Ek shabd hatana hai — kaunsa?

    Although the metro is expensive, but it is very fast.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: but.

    Sahi roop: Although the metro is expensive, it is very fast.

    although aur but ek saath nahi — ek hi chuno.

    Exercise 5

    ___ the heavy rain, the match continued. (although nahi)

    Hint: Noun ke pehle aane wala concession shabd — 'of' ke bina.

    Jawaab

    Despite / despite / In spite of / in spite of

    despite / in spite of + noun. 'Despite of' galat hai.

    Exercise 6

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Despite of the cost, many families buy smartphones.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: of.

    Sahi roop: Despite the cost, many families buy smartphones.

    despite ke baad 'of' nahi aata.

    Essay ke liye 'lots of kids' ka sabse achha roop?

    • a large number of children
    • plenty kids
    • a lot of kiddos
    • many many kids

    a large number of children

    Formal register: kids → children, lots of → a large number of.

    • 150
    • 200
    • 350
    Jawaab

    250

    Exercise 9

    IELTS ka 'Coherence and Cohesion' kya check karta hai?

    • Paragraph ka kram aur linking
    • Spelling aur shabd
    • Sawaal ka jawaab diya ya nahi
    • Accent
    Jawaab

    Paragraph ka kram aur linking

    Coherence = kram aur saafi; cohesion = linking words aur jodna.

    Exercise 10

    Hint: Headings, findings, recommendations wala document.

    Jawaab

    'The purpose of this report is to…' — report ki pakki shuruaat.

    Exercise 16

    Exercise 17

    Essay ki is line me kya faltu hai?

    Exercise 18

    Opinion essay me 'I firmly believe that…' saaf raay deta hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Is hafte ek essay 40 minute me likho — upar ke chaar sawaalon me se koi bhi. Phir model se milao: thesis saaf hai? Har body me PEEL hai? Essay ke shabdon ke liye Kaam aur duniya, aur apna level samajhne ke liye IELTS, TOEFL aur aapka level.
