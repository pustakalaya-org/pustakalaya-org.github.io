    Chapter 46: Advanced shabd¶

    Advanced shabd — jo padhe-likhe log asal me bolte hain

    Advanced shabd ratne ka koi faayda nahi hota — "Hegemony means dominance" yaad karke aap use kabhi theek se nahi bol paayenge. Shabd dimaag me jagah se baithta hai: wo kis tarah ke vaakya me aata hai, kis tarah ke log bolte hain, aur uske saath kaunse shabd chipke rehte hain. Isliye is chapter me har shabd ke saath uski duniya di gayi hai — definition ke bajaye.

    Ek line me

    Naya shabd tab tak aapka nahi hai jab tak aap uska ek asli vaakya bina soche na bol sakein. Matlab yaad rakhna aadha kaam hai; jagah yaad rakhna poora kaam.

    46.1 Atharah shabd, ek-ek karke¶

    nefarious — buraai wala, sochi-samjhi badmaashi¶

    Ye shabd khaali "bura" nahi hai — isme chhupi hui, plan ki hui buraai hai. News, crime story aur film ki baat me aata hai. Mazedaar baat: aaj kal log ise mazaak me bhi bolte hain — "nothing nefarious, I promise". Saath chalne wale shabd: nefarious scheme, nefarious purpose, nefarious activities.

    • The account was being used for something nefarious — fake invoices, mostly. Us account ka istemaal galat kaam ke liye ho raha tha — zyada tar naqli invoices.

    myriad — ginti se bahar, anek¶

    Matlab "bahut saare", par isme ginna mumkin nahi wala bhaav hai. Sabse badi galti yahi hai ki log "a myriad of" ke baad singular laga dete hain. Dono roop chalte hain: myriad reasons (saaf aur chhota) ya a myriad of reasons. Par uske baad noun hamesha plural.

    • There are myriad reasons why the launch slipped, but three matter most. Launch late hone ki anek wajahein hain, par teen sabse zaroori hain.

    bucolic — gaon jaisa, shaant aur hara-bhara¶

    Bucolic khet, charaagaah, gaay-bakri aur dheemi zindagi wali tasveer hai — jaise kisi painting ka gaon. Isme hamesha pyaar aur sukoon ka bhaav hota hai, garibi ya pichhdepan ka nahi. Kab use karein: ye literary shabd hai — travel writing, kitaab, review. Bolne me peaceful countryside ya rural zyada natural. Saathi shabd: bucolic setting, bucolic charm, bucolic landscape. Uchchaaran: byoo-KOL-ik.

    • The homestay sits in a bucolic valley of tea gardens and slow rivers. Homestay chaay ke baagon aur dheemi nadiyon wali ek shaant, hari-bhari ghaati me hai.

    guile — chalaaki, chaalbaazi¶

    Guile noun hai — doosron ko bewakoof banane wali hoshiyari. Dhyan do: iska adjective roop guileful nahi chalta; log cunning bolte hain. Par guileless (bhola, chaalbaazi se khaali) bahut khoobsurat shabd hai aur taareef me aata hai.

    • He won that negotiation with pure guile, not numbers. Wo baat-cheet usne chalaaki se jeeti, aankdon se nahi.

    onerous — bhaari, thaka dene wala (kaam ya shart)¶

    Kaam mushkil nahi — bojh jaisa. Sabse zyada ye contract, rules, paperwork, duty ke saath aata hai. Office email me ye shabd polite tareeke se shikayat karta hai: aap "ye bakwaas hai" nahi keh rahe, bas "ye bojh hai" keh rahe hain.

    • The reporting requirements are onerous for a team of three. Teen logon ki team ke liye reporting ke niyam bahut bhaari hain.

    enigmatic — paheli jaisa, samajh me na aane wala¶

    Insaan, muskaan, jawaab, ya kisi ka rawaiya — jab saaf na ho ki andar kya chal raha hai. Isme dilchaspi hai, buraai nahi. Agar koi sirf chup hai to wo reserved hai; enigmatic wo hai jiske baare me aap sochte reh jaate hain.

    • Her reply was enigmatic — a single line that could mean yes or no. Uska jawaab paheli jaisa tha — ek line, jiska matlab haan bhi ho sakta hai aur na bhi.

    lurid — sansani-khez, bhadkila¶

    Do matlab, dono me "zaroorat se zyada" ka bhaav: (1) kahaani ya khabar jo jaan-boojh kar chaunkane wali, gandi ya khooni detail se bhari ho; (2) rang jo chubhne wale, bhadkile hon. Ye shabd hamesha thodi naapasandgi ke saath aata hai. Kab use karein: news, review, aalochana — sabse aam jodi lurid details. Baat-cheet me kam, par samajh sab lete hain. Uchchaaran: LOOR-id.

    • The tabloid printed every lurid detail of the case. Us akhbaar ne case ki har sansani-khez, chatpati detail chhaap di.

    hegemony — kisi ek ka dabdaba¶

    Politics, history aur business analysis ka shabd. Matlab: ek desh, company ya group ka itna dabdaba ki baaki sab usi ke hisaab se chalte hain. Rozmarra ki baat me ye shabd mat bolo — bahut bhaari hai. Uchchaaran dhyan se: hi-JEM-uh-nee.

    • Two companies share almost complete hegemony over the search market. Search ke bazaar par lagbhag poora dabdaba do hi companies ka hai.

    felicity — gehri khushi; ya baat kehne ka sateek, sundar tareeka¶

    Felicity noun hai, aur iske do matlab hain: (1) gehri, sukoon bhari khushi — purani, likhi hui bhasha me; (2) shabdon ka ekdam sahi aur sundar chunaav — felicity of expression. Ulta shabd infelicitous (bedhanga, galat jagah ka) editors aur critics khoob bolte hain. Kab use karein: bahut literary — shaadi ke card, bhaashan, sahitya ki aalochana. Rozmarra me happiness ya joy. Uchchaaran: fuh-LIS-ih-tee.

    • Her essays are admired for their felicity of expression. Uske nibandh apne sateek aur khoobsurat shabd-chunaav ke liye pasand kiye jaate hain.
    • We wish the couple a lifetime of felicity. Hum jode ko umr bhar ki khushiyon ki shubhkaamna dete hain.

    censure — sarkari ya aupchaarik nindaa¶

    Ye aam naraazgi nahi hai — isme official bhaav hai. Committee, board, parliament, ya institution kisi ko censure karta hai. Dhyan rakho: ye censor (kaat dena, hissa hata dena) se bilkul alag shabd hai, bas aawaz milti hai.

    • The board voted to censure him, though he kept his seat. Board ne uski nindaa ka prastaav paas kiya, halaanki uski kursi bani rahi.

    jettison — bojh utaar dena, chhod dena¶

    Asli tasveer: jahaaz ya hawai jahaaz se saamaan phenk dena taaki wo halka ho jaaye. Isi se iska aaj ka matlab aaya — kisi plan, feature ya aadat ko majboori me chhod dena. Isme bachne ke liye chhodna ka bhaav hai, isliye business aur project ki baat me khoob chalta hai.

    • We had to jettison half the features to ship on time. Time par release karne ke liye humein aadhe features chhodne pade.

    disparate — bilkul alag kism ke, mel na khaane wale¶

    Sirf "different" nahi — itne alag ki inhe ek saath rakhna mushkil hai. Isliye ye aksar disparate sources, disparate systems, disparate groups ke roop me aata hai. Uchchaaran: DIS-puh-rut.

    • The report pulls together data from three disparate systems. Ye report teen bilkul alag-alag systems ka data ek jagah laati hai.

    insidious — dheere-dheere andar se nuksaan karne wala¶

    Sabse zaroori baat: isme dheemi raftaar hai. Jo cheez pehle dikhai nahi deti aur jab dikhti hai tab tak kaafi nuksaan ho chuka hai — wahi insidious hai. Bimari, aadat, afwaah, aur soch — in sabke saath aata hai. Dhamaka insidious nahi hota.

    • The most insidious part is how normal it starts to feel. Sabse khatarnak baat ye hai ki dheere-dheere ye sab normal lagne lagta hai.

    keen — bahut utsuk; ya tez (nazar, dimaag)¶

    Is list ka sabse kaam ka shabd, aur sabse aasaan. Do matlab: (1) keen to / keen on = kisi cheez ke liye utsuk — interview me sona. (2) a keen eye, a keen sense = tez nazar, paini samajh. Ek aur kaam ki baat: British English me "I'm not keen on it" mana karne ka bahut polite tareeka hai.

    • I'm keen to take on more responsibility this year. Is saal main aur zimmedari lene ke liye utsuk hoon.
    • She has a keen eye for detail — nothing gets past her. Uski nazar bahut paini hai — koi chhoti cheez bachti nahi.

    auspicious — shubh, achha shagun wala¶

    India me ye shabd roz kaam aata hai — shaadi, griha pravesh, naya kaam. Angrezi bolne wale bahar ise kam bolte hain, par samajhte sab hain. Dhyan do: cheez auspicious occasion / auspicious day hoti hai; insaan nahi. Ulta shabd inauspicious hai.

    • They picked an auspicious date for the opening. Unhone udghaatan ke liye ek shubh din chuna.

    ubiquitous — har jagah maujood¶

    Technology aur chalan ki baat ka pasandeeda shabd: smartphones are ubiquitous. Isme halka sa "ab to aam baat ho gayi hai" wala bhaav hai. Uchchaaran: yoo-BIK-wi-tus.

    • QR codes were rare five years ago; now they're ubiquitous. Paanch saal pehle QR code kam dikhte the; ab har jagah hain.

    ephemeral — pal bhar ka, jaldi khatam ho jaane wala¶

    Isme khoobsurti aur thodi udaasi dono hain. Status, story, fashion, mausam, khushi — jo aaya aur gaya. Ye shabd thoda literary hai, isliye likhai me zyada jamta hai. Uchchaaran: ih-FEM-er-ul.

    • Social media fame is ephemeral — gone in a week. Social media ki shohrat pal bhar ki hoti hai — hafte bhar me gayab.

    pragmatic — vyavhaarik, jo chale wahi theek¶

    Is list ka doosra sabse kaam ka shabd. Iska matlab hai: sidhant ki jagah nateeja dekhna. Interview me apne baare me batane ke liye behtareen — aur taareef ka shabd hai.

    • Let's be pragmatic — ship the simple version now, improve it later. Vyavhaarik banein — abhi aasaan version nikaalein, baad me behtar karein.

    46.2 Kahan milenge ye shabd — ek naksha¶

    Shabd ko jagah se jodo. Neeche do cheezein ek saath dikhai gayi hain: shabd kitna formal hai (daayein jitna, utna formal), aur aap use kitni baar sunenge (upar jitna, utna aam). Neeche-daayein kone wale shabd bolne me mat aazmao — wo padhne ke liye hain.

    46.3 Ek nazar me — shabd, saathi shabd, uchchaaran¶

    "Saathi shabd" (collocation) sabse kaam ka column hai: ye batata hai ki shabd ke aage-peeche kya aata hai. Native log inhe jodiyon me yaad rakhte hain, akele shabd ki tarah nahi.

    46.4 Shabdon ke jaal — jo aawaz me milte hain¶

    Ye jodiyan dhyan se

    censure (nindaa karna) vs censor (hissa kaat dena) — bilkul alag kaam.
    ephemeral (pal bhar ka) vs ethereal (halka, swargik) — dono poetic, par matlab alag.
    disparate (alag kism ke) vs desperate (bechain) — spelling ek akshar ki door hai, matlab kosson door.
    pragmatic (vyavhaarik) vs dogmatic (apni baat par ada) — ek taareef, doosra ilzaam.

    46.5 Galtiyan jo in shabdon ke saath hoti hain¶

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: There are a myriad of reason for this.
    • Sahi: There are myriad reasons for this.

    • Kyun: Myriad ke baad noun hamesha plural. Aur "myriad reasons" zyada saaf hai — "a myriad of" thoda bhaari lagta hai.

    • Galat: The bomb had an insidious effect on the building.

    • Sahi: The bomb had a devastating effect on the building.

    • Kyun: Insidious me dheemi raftaar zaroori hai. Ek jhatke me hone wale nuksaan ke liye devastating.

    • Galat: He is a very auspicious person.

    • Sahi: It was an auspicious day to start.

    • Kyun: Auspicious din, mauka aur shuruaat ke liye hai — insaan ke liye nahi.

    • Galat: The committee decided to censor the minister for his remarks.

    • Sahi: The committee decided to censure the minister for his remarks.

    • Kyun: Censure = nindaa karna. Censor = hissa kaat kar hata dena. Ek akshar, poora alag matlab.

    • Galat: I am keen for learning new tools.

    • Sahi: I am keen to learn new tools.

    • Kyun: Do hi roop hain: keen to + verb, ya keen on + -ing. "Keen for" nahi chalta.

    • Galat: Our team has hegemony in this office.

    • Sahi: Our team has a lot of influence in this office.
    • Kyun: Hegemony desh, bazaar aur sanskriti ke paimane ka shabd hai. Office ke liye bahut bada pad jaata hai.

    46.6 Ek paragraph — paanch shabd, apni jagah par¶

    Padh kar dekho, phir zor se bolo

    Cheap smartphones are now ubiquitous, and that has created myriad small businesses that did not exist ten years ago. Most are ephemeral — up one month, gone the next. The ones that last are run by pragmatic people who jettison whatever stops working. Dhyan do: paanch bhaari shabd hain par vaakya kahin bhi akda hua nahi lagta, kyunki har shabd apni jagah par hai.

    46.7 Practice — Advanced vocabulary¶

    Exercise 1

    Ek aadat jo saalon tak chhupi rehti hai aur dheere-dheere nuksaan karti hai. Kaunsa shabd?

    • insidious
    • nefarious
    • ephemeral
    • onerous

    Hint: Raftaar dheemi honi chahiye.

    Jawaab

    insidious

    Insidious me dheemi, chhupi hui raftaar zaroori hai. Nefarious me plan ki hui badmaashi hai, aur ephemeral pal bhar ka hai.

    Exercise 2

    Interview me khud ke baare me batane ke liye sabse behtar shabd?

    • pragmatic
    • enigmatic
    • nefarious
    • onerous
    Jawaab

    pragmatic

    Pragmatic taareef ka shabd hai — nateeja dekhne wala insaan. Enigmatic ka matlab hoga ki aapko koi samajh nahi paata, jo interview me achha nahi.

    Exercise 3

    Kaunsa vaakya sahi hai?

    • There are myriad reasons for the delay.
    • There are a myriad of reason for the delay.
    • There is myriad reason for the delay.
    • There are myriads reason for the delay.
    Jawaab

    There are myriad reasons for the delay.

    Myriad ke baad noun plural hota hai. 'Myriad reasons' sabse saaf roop hai.

    Exercise 4

    Project time par nikaalne ke liye aadhe features chhodne pade. Kaunsa verb?

    • jettison
    • censure
    • enhance
    • demonstrate
    Jawaab

    jettison

    Jettison = bachne ke liye bojh utaar dena. Jahaaz se saamaan phenkne wali tasveer isi se aayi hai.

    Exercise 5

    I'm ___ to take on more responsibility this year.

    Hint: Utsuk — interview wala shabd.

    Jawaab

    keen / eager

    Keen to + verb. Dhyan rakho: 'keen for learning' galat hai, 'keen to learn' ya 'keen on learning' sahi.

    Exercise 6

    The report pulls together data from three ___ systems that were never meant to talk to each other.

    Hint: Itne alag ki mel nahi khaate.

    Jawaab

    disparate

    Disparate = bilkul alag kism ke. Sirf 'different' se zyada strong hai — isme 'jodna mushkil hai' ka bhaav hai.

    Exercise 7

    Ek shabd galat chuna gaya hai — us par tap karo.

    The board voted to censor the minister for his remarks.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: censor.

    Sahi roop: The board voted to censure the minister for his remarks.

    Censure = aupchaarik nindaa. Censor = hissa kaat kar hata dena. Aawaz milti hai, matlab nahi.

    Exercise 8

    Ye shabd insaan par nahi lagta — use dhoondo.

    My uncle is a very auspicious man in our town.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: auspicious.

    Sahi roop: My uncle is a well-respected man in our town.

    Auspicious din, mauka aur shuruaat ke liye hai. Insaan ke liye 'respected' ya 'well-regarded'.

    Exercise 9

    Shabd ko uske asli bhaav se jodo.

    A B
    hegemony bojh jaisa bhaari kaam
    guile pal bhar me khatam
    onerous ek ka poora dabdaba
    ephemeral chalaaki se kaam nikaalna
    Jawaab

    hegemony — ek ka poora dabdaba; guile — chalaaki se kaam nikaalna; onerous — bojh jaisa bhaari kaam; ephemeral — pal bhar me khatam

    Chaaron bhaari shabd hain — pehle pehchaano aur padho, bolna baad me.

    Exercise 10

    Shabd ko uske saathi shabd se jodo.

    A B
    keen has become ubiquitous
    nefarious an enigmatic smile
    ubiquitous a keen eye for detail
    enigmatic a nefarious scheme
    Jawaab

    keen — a keen eye for detail; nefarious — a nefarious scheme; ubiquitous — has become ubiquitous; enigmatic — an enigmatic smile

    Native log shabd akele yaad nahi rakhte — jodi me rakhte hain. Jodi yaad ho to shabd apne aap sahi jagah girta hai.

    Exercise 11

    Vaakya banao — technology ke baare me.

    Shabd: were / they / five / ago / QR / are / rare / but / years / now / codes / ubiquitous

    Jawaab

    QR codes were rare five years ago but now they are ubiquitous

    Ubiquitous ke saath aksar 'now' aur pehle-baad ka muqabla aata hai — yahi iska natural ghar hai.

    Exercise 12

    Travel blog me ek shaant, hare-bhare gaon ka varnan karna hai. Kaunsa shabd?

    • bucolic
    • lurid
    • onerous
    • insidious

    Hint: Khet, charaagaah, sukoon.

    Jawaab

    bucolic

    Bucolic = gaon jaisa shaant aur sundar. Ye literary shabd hai, isliye blog aur kitaab me jamta hai. Lurid ulta hai — bhadkila aur sansani-khez.

    Exercise 13

    Ek shabd galat chuna gaya hai — us par tap karo.

    The tabloid printed every bucolic detail of the murder.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: bucolic.

    Sahi roop: The tabloid printed every lurid detail of the murder.

    Chaunkane wali, khooni detail = lurid details. Bucolic gaon ki shaanti ke liye hai.

    Exercise 14

    Critics praised the poet for the ___ of her expression — every word felt exactly right.

    Hint: Sateek, sundar shabd-chunaav — ek noun.

    Jawaab

    felicity

    'Felicity of expression' = shabdon ka ekdam sahi aur sundar chunaav. Bahut literary shabd — rozmarra me nahi.

    Exercise 15

    Naye shabdon ko unke bhaav se jodo.

    A B
    bucolic gehri khushi ya sateek shabd
    lurid majboori me chhod dena
    felicity gaon jaisa shaant
    jettison sansani-khez, bhadkila
    Jawaab

    bucolic — gaon jaisa shaant; lurid — sansani-khez, bhadkila; felicity — gehri khushi ya sateek shabd; jettison — majboori me chhod dena

    Bucolic aur felicity literary hain, lurid news me aam. Teeno pehle padh kar pehchaano, phir likhai me aazmao.

    Exercise 16

    Naya shabd kab tak aapka nahi hua maana jaata hai?

    • Jab tak uska ek asli vaakya bina soche na bol sako
    • Jab tak uski spelling yaad na ho
    • Jab tak dictionary ki definition yaad na ho
    • Jab tak uska Hindi matlab yaad na ho
    Jawaab

    Jab tak uska ek asli vaakya bina soche na bol sako

    Definition yaad rakhna aadha kaam hai. Shabd tab aata hai jab uski jagah aur jodi yaad ho.

    Exercise 17

    Ye line bolo. 'Pragmatic' me zor beech me — prag-MA-tik.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Let us be pragmatic and ship the simple version now

    Office meeting ki asli line hai. 'Let's be pragmatic' bolne se aap samajhdaar lagte hain, zidda nahi.

    Aage kya karein

    Atharah shabd ek saath mat uthao. Naksha dekho, narangi gole wale paanch chuno — keen, pragmatic, ubiquitous, auspicious, enigmatic — aur is hafte unhe bolo. Neele gole wale shabd padhte waqt pehchaanne ke liye kaafi hain; wo apne waqt par zabaan par aa jaayenge.
