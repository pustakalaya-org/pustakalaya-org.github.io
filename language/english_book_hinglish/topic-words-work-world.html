    Chapter 39: Kaam aur duniya¶

    Kaam aur duniya — office, technology, environment, khabrein

    Meeting me "stakeholder" aur "deliverable" sunte ho, news me "inflation" aur "emissions", aur IELTS essay me inhi shabdon ki zaroorat padti hai. Ye chapter chaar bade vishayon ke shabd deta hai — par akele nahi. Har shabd ke saath uska parivaar (economy, economic, economical) aur uski jodi (meet a deadline, reduce emissions). Yahi do cheezein B1 ko B2–C1 banati hain.

    39.1 Ek shabd, poora parivaar, pakki jodi¶

    B2–C1 level par aapse sirf shabd ka matlab nahi poochha jaata — aapko pata hona chahiye ki wo noun hai ya adjective, aur uske saath kaunsa verb lagta hai. Ek shabd seekho to uske chaar-paanch roop saath me seekh lo.

    Teen sawaal har naye shabd par

    (1) Iska parivaar kya hai? (2) Iske saath kaunsa verb ya adjective lagta hai? (3) Ek apna vaakya kya banega? Prefix-suffix ke liye Ek shabd se das shabd, jodiyon ke liye Kaunsa shabd kiske saath.

    39.2 Office aur meetings¶

    Meeting se pehle agenda, beech me discussion, baad me minutes aur action items, aur phir sabka follow-up — jab tak deadline na aa jaaye. Ye ek chakra hai.

    Shabd Matlab Pakki jodi
    agenda meeting ke mudde set / circulate the agenda
    minutes meeting ka likhit record take / share the minutes
    deadline aakhri taarikh meet / miss / extend a deadline
    follow up (v) / follow-up (n) baad me pata karna follow up on the order
    stakeholder jiska is kaam me hit judaa key stakeholders
    deliverable jo cheez deni hai agree on the deliverables
    priority pehle kya a top priority · prioritise
    escalate upar tak le jaana escalate an issue
    delegate kaam saunpna delegate a task to someone
    bandwidth (bolchaal) waqt aur himmat I don't have the bandwidth.
    • I'll circulate the agenda by Monday evening. Somvaar shaam tak agenda sabko bhej dunga.
    • We're on track to meet the deadline. Hum deadline tak kaam poora kar lenge. (on track = sahi raaste par)
    • Could we extend the deadline to Friday? Deadline Friday tak badha sakte hain?
    • I'll follow up with the vendor and update everyone. Vendor se pata karke sabko bataunga.
    • We need sign-off from all the key stakeholders. Sabhi khaas logon ki manzoori chahiye. (sign-off = final haan)
    • If the client doesn't reply by Wednesday, let's escalate it. Budhvaar tak jawaab na aaye to upar baat karte hain.

    follow up ya follow-up?

    Verb = do shabd: I'll follow up tomorrow. Noun / adjective = hyphen: a follow-up email, a quick follow-up. Yahi niyam log in / login, set up / setup, back up / backup par. Kaam karna hai to do shabd; cheez ka naam hai to ek shabd.

    ✗

    I will do the follow up and revert back.

    ✓

    I'll follow up and get back to you.

    follow up

    Indian English ki aadatein

    ✗

    We must finish before deadline.

    ✓

    We must meet the deadline. / We must finish before the deadline.

    deadline

    meet the deadline

    Meeting me bolne ki lines (interrupt karna, sehmat hona, kaam lena) Interview aur office me hain — yahan sirf shabd.

    39.3 Technology¶

    Tech ke zyaadatar shabd hum English me hi bolte hain, isliye galti shabd me nahi, verb aur preposition me hoti hai: log in to the app, download **from, upload to**.

    Shabd Matlab Jodi
    download / upload utaarna / chadhaana upload a file to the drive
    log in / log out andar / bahar log in to your account
    sign up naya account banana sign up for a free trial
    update naya version install the latest update
    crash achanak band hona The app keeps crashing.
    freeze atak jaana (hang) My laptop froze.
    back up copy bachaakar rakhna back up your photos
    bandwidth net ki chaudaai low bandwidth
    outage service band a network outage
    encrypted code me band, surakshit end-to-end encrypted
    • I can't log in to the portal. It says my password has expired. Portal me login nahi ho raha — password expire ho gaya.
    • The app has been crashing since the last update. Pichhle update ke baad se app baar-baar band ho raha hai. ("since" ke saath has been + -ing; bina since: "It keeps crashing.")
    • Please back up your files before you install the update. Update se pehle files ki copy bana lo.
    • The video call keeps breaking up. I think my bandwidth is low. Call toot-toot ke aa raha hai — net dheema hai.
    • There was a nationwide outage, so payments failed for two hours. Poore desh me service band thi, do ghante payment fail hue.

    ✗

    My laptop got hanged.

    ✓

    My laptop froze. / My laptop hung.

    hang

    hung

    froze

    stopped responding

    ✗

    I will login and check.

    ✓

    I'll log in and check.

    log in

    login

    your login details

    the login page

    ✗

    Please send me the soft copy.

    ✓

    Please send me the file / a PDF.

    the file, a digital copy, a printed copy / a printout

    39.4 Environment — pollution se renewable tak¶

    Environment ke shabd essays, news aur interviews — teeno me aate hain. Yahan parivaar wala niyam sabse zyada kaam aata hai: pollute (verb), pollution (noun), polluted (adj), pollutant (pradooshan wala padarth).

    Shabd Matlab Jodi
    pollution pradooshan air / water / noise pollution
    emissions dhuaan, gas ka nikalna cut / reduce emissions
    carbon footprint aapki wajah se nikla carbon reduce your carbon footprint
    renewable khatam na hone wala renewable energy
    fossil fuels koyla, petrol, gas depend on fossil fuels
    sustainable lambe samay tak chal sake sustainable development
    deforestation jangal katna tackle deforestation
    biodiversity jeev-jantu ki vividhta loss of biodiversity
    climate change mausam ka lamba badlaav tackle / address climate change
    • Every winter, air pollution in Delhi reaches dangerous levels. Har sardi Delhi me pradooshan khatarnaak star tak pahunchta hai.
    • The AQI is over 400 today, so schools are closed. AQI = Air Quality Index. "over" = se zyada.
    • India wants to get half its electricity from renewable sources. Aadhi bijli renewable (solar, wind) se.
    • Electric buses could cut the city's emissions significantly. Electric bus se shahar ka dhuaan kaafi kam ho sakta hai.
    • Growth has to be sustainable, not just fast. Vikas lamba chalne wala ho, sirf tez nahi.

    sustain ka parivaar

    sustain (v) → sustainable (adj) → sustainability (n) → sustainably (adv) → unsustainable (ulta). "The current pace of growth is unsustainable" — essay ke liye ek majboot line.

    ✗

    The pollution is very much in Delhi.

    ✓

    Pollution is very high in Delhi. / Delhi's air is badly polluted.

    high / low

    39.5 Khabrein aur samaaj — economy, election, policy¶

    Akhbaar padhna ho, news sunna ho ya essay likhna ho — ye shabd baar-baar aayenge. Inke saath badhne-ghatne wale verb bhi seekho: rise, fall, grow, drop, remain stable.

    Shabd Matlab Jodi
    economy arthvyavastha the economy grew by 7%
    inflation mehngaai ki dar inflation rose / fell to 5%
    unemployment berozgaari high youth unemployment
    budget sarkaari aay-vyay present the budget
    election chunaav hold / win an election
    turnout kitne logon ne vote diya a record turnout
    policy niti announce / introduce a policy
    survey sarvekshan conduct a survey · the survey found…
    subsidy sarkaari chhoot a fuel subsidy
    • Inflation fell to four per cent last month. Pichhle mahine mehngaai dar 4% par aa gayi. (fell to = us star tak; fell by = itni kam hui)
    • The government has announced a new policy on electric vehicles. Sarkaar ne EV par nayi niti ghoshit ki.
    • Voter turnout was the highest in thirty years. Tees saal me sabse zyada matdaan hua.
    • A recent survey found that 60 per cent of young people want to work abroad. Survey found that — essay aur report ki pakki shuruaat.
    • The new tax is controversial; small traders strongly oppose it. Naya tax vivaadit hai — chhote vyapari khilaaf hain.

    ✗

    Unemployment is an economical problem.

    ✓

    Unemployment is an economic problem.

    economic

    economical

    ✗

    The survey says 60% people use UPI daily.

    ✓

    The survey found that 60% of people use UPI daily.

    of

    of

    of

    found / showed

    39.6 Headline English — chhote shabd, bada matlab¶

    Akhbaar ki headline apni hi grammar me likhi jaati hai: articles gaayab, present simple = beeta hua kal, to + verb = aane wala kal, aur chhote-chhote shabd. Inhe samajh liya to news bahut aasaan ho jaati hai.

    Headline Poora matlab Trick
    RBI holds rates steady The RBI has kept interest rates unchanged. present = ho chuka
    Govt to launch EV policy The government is going to launch a policy. to = hone wala
    Floods hit Assam Floods have affected Assam. hit = asar daala
    Minister quits A minister has resigned. quit = istifa
    Prices soar Prices have risen sharply. soar = bahut badhna
    Talks fail Negotiations have failed. talks = baatcheet
    • RBI holds rates steady as inflation eases. Mehngaai kam hui, isliye RBI ne byaaj dar nahi badli. (ease = narm padna)
    • Govt to boost spending on rural roads. Sarkaar gaon ki sadkon par kharch badhaayegi. (boost = badhaana)
    • Heatwave grips north India; schools shut. Uttar Bharat me loo ka kehar, school band. (grip = jakad lena)

    Headline ke chhote shabd

    bid = koshish · curb = rokna/seemit karna · slam = kadi aalochna · probe = jaanch · row = vivaad · back = samarthan karna · axe = khatam karna. News sunne ki practice ke liye Asli duniya ki English dekho.

    39.7 B2–C1 shabd — ek shabd, teen jodiyan¶

    Ye das shabd meeting, news aur essay — teeno me kaam aate hain. Har ek ke saath uski jodi aur parivaar seekho; tabhi wo aapki apni English ka hissa banenge. (Bade verbs ke liye Verb upgrade aur Advanced shabd bhi dekho.)

    Shabd (level) Matlab Jodi · parivaar
    significant (B2) kaafi bada, ahem a significant increase · significantly
    implement (B2) laagu karna implement a plan · implementation
    allocate (B2) baantna (paisa, samay) allocate funds · allocation
    mitigate (C1) asar kam karna mitigate the risk · mitigation
    unprecedented (C1) pehle kabhi nahi hua an unprecedented rise
    substantial (C1) bada, thos a substantial amount · substantially
    transparent (B2) khula, saaf a transparent process · transparency
    accountable (C1) jawaabdeh hold someone accountable · accountability
    viable (C1) chal sakne layak a viable option · viability
    scalable (C1) bada kiya ja sake a scalable solution · scale up
    • We need a backup supplier to mitigate the risk of delays. Der ka khatra kam karne ke liye ek aur supplier chahiye.
    • The city has allocated ₹200 crore to clean the river. Nadi saaf karne ke liye ₹200 crore rakhe gaye.
    • Leaders must be held accountable for their promises. Netaon ko apne vaadon ke liye jawaabdeh banana chahiye.
    • Solar power is now a viable option for most homes. Ab zyaadatar gharon ke liye solar ek chalne layak vikalp hai.
    • The pandemic caused an unprecedented shift to online work. Mahamari ne online kaam ki taraf aisa badlaav laaya jo pehle kabhi nahi hua.

    Ek paragraph — chaaron vishay ek saath

    • At this week's meeting, the key stakeholders agreed to implement the new travel policy. Most meetings will now be online, which should cut emissions and save a substantial amount of money. A staff survey found that 70 per cent of employees support the change. Office (stakeholders, implement), environment (cut emissions), khabar (survey found) — aur ek C1 shabd (substantial).

    39.8 Practice — Kaam aur duniya¶

    Exercise 1

    We're on track to ___ the deadline.

    • meet
    • do
    • reach
    • make

    Hint: Deadline ki sabse pakki jodi.

    Jawaab

    meet

    meet / miss / extend a deadline.

    Exercise 2

    I'll ___ up with the client tomorrow and let you know.

    Hint: Baad me pata karna — do shabd ka verb.

    Jawaab

    follow

    follow up (verb) — follow-up (noun): a follow-up email.

    Exercise 3

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    My laptop got hanged during the meeting.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: hanged.

    Sahi roop: My laptop froze during the meeting.

    Computer ke liye hung / froze. 'Hanged' sirf faansi ke liye.

    Exercise 4

    Sahi kaunsa hai?

    • I'll log in and check.
    • I'll login and check.
    • I'll do login and check.
    • I'll log-in and check.
    Jawaab

    I'll log in and check.

    Verb = log in (do shabd). login = noun: login details.

    Exercise 5

    Shabd ko matlab se jodo.

    A B
    emissions kitne logon ne vote diya
    renewable sarkaari chhoot
    turnout dhuaan aur gas ka nikalna
    subsidy khatam na hone wala
    Jawaab

    emissions — dhuaan aur gas ka nikalna; renewable — khatam na hone wala; turnout — kitne logon ne vote diya; subsidy — sarkaari chhoot

    emissions, renewable — environment; turnout, subsidy — khabron ke shabd.

    Exercise 6

    Ek shabd galat hai.

    Unemployment is a serious economical problem.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: economical.

    Sahi roop: Unemployment is a serious economic problem.

    economic = arthvyavastha ka; economical = kifayati.

    Exercise 7

    A recent survey found that 60 per cent ___ young people want to work abroad.

    Hint: Percentage ke baad chhota shabd.

    Jawaab

    of

    60 per cent of people, half of the class.

    Exercise 8

    Headline: 'Govt to launch EV policy'. Iska matlab?

    • The government is going to launch it.
    • The government has launched it.
    • The government launched it last year.
    • The government refused to launch it.
    Jawaab

    The government is going to launch it.

    Headline me 'to + verb' = aane wala kal.

    Exercise 9

    Electric buses could ___ the city's emissions.

    • cut
    • do
    • make
    • low
    Jawaab

    cut

    cut / reduce emissions — pakki jodi.

    Exercise 10

    Inflation ke saath kya hua?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Here is the business news. Inflation fell to four point two per cent in September, the lowest level this year. Economists say lower food prices were the main reason. The central bank is expected to keep interest rates unchanged next week.

    • It fell
    • It rose
    • It stayed the same
    • It doubled
    Jawaab

    It fell

    'Inflation fell to four point two per cent' — gir kar 4.2% par aayi.

    Exercise 11

    Economists ke hisaab se main reason kya tha?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Here is the business news. Inflation fell to four point two per cent in September, the lowest level this year. Economists say lower food prices were the main reason. The central bank is expected to keep interest rates unchanged next week.

    • Lower food prices
    • Higher interest rates
    • Cheaper petrol
    • A new tax
    Jawaab

    Lower food prices

    'lower food prices were the main reason' — khaane ki keemat kam hui.

    Exercise 12

    Minutes kaun bhejega?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Okay, let's wrap up. Priya will send the minutes by this evening. Arjun, can you follow up with the client about the budget? And just a reminder, the deadline for the first draft is Thursday.

    • Priya
    • Arjun
    • The client
    • Nobody
    Jawaab

    Priya

    'Priya will send the minutes by this evening.'

    Exercise 13

    First draft ki deadline kab hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    Okay, let's wrap up. Priya will send the minutes by this evening. Arjun, can you follow up with the client about the budget? And just a reminder, the deadline for the first draft is Thursday.

    • Thursday
    • This evening
    • Tuesday
    • Next week
    Jawaab

    Thursday

    'the deadline for the first draft is Thursday.'

    Exercise 14

    Suno aur likho.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    Jawaab

    The server crashed last night, so please back up your files.

    crash = achanak band hona; back up (verb) do shabd me.

    Exercise 15

    'mitigate the risk' ka matlab?

    • khatre ka asar kam karna
    • khatra badhaana
    • khatre ko nazarandaaz karna
    • khatre ko maapna
    Jawaab

    khatre ka asar kam karna

    mitigate (C1) = asar kam karna; noun: mitigation.

    Exercise 16

    Sahi kram me lagao.

    Shabd: very / Pollution / Delhi / winter / in / high / is / this

    Jawaab

    Pollution is very high in Delhi this winter

    Pollution 'high' hota hai, 'very much' nahi.

    Exercise 17

    Meeting me ye line bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could we extend the deadline to Friday

    extend a deadline — vinamra 'Could we…?' ke saath.

    Aage kya padhein

    In shabdon ko likhai me lagao: Essay aur report me poora dhaancha hai, aur CV aur LinkedIn me office ke action verbs. Roz ke shabdon ke liye Roz ke vishay.
