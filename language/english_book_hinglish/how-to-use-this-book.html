    Chapter 1: Is kitaab ko kaise padhein¶

    Agar aap ye page khol kar bhi thoda ghabra rahe hain — ki "mujhe kuch nahi aata", "main bolne me atak jaata hoon" — to aap bilkul sahi jagah par hain. Ye kitaab aapko English ke baare me padhaane ke liye nahi hai. Ye aapko English bolwaane ke liye hai. Aur bolne ka ek hi tareeka hai: bolna. Aaj se, akele kamre me, zor se.

    Poori kitaab ka ek hi niyam

    Jo bhi English line is kitaab me dikhe — use aankhon se mat padho, muh se bolo. Chup-chaap padhi hui English kabhi zubaan par nahi aati. Silent reading se samajh aati hai; bolne se fluency aati hai. Dono alag cheezein hain.

    1.1 Roz 15 minute — bas itna¶

    Hafte me ek din do ghante padhne se kuch nahi hota. Roz 15 minute se sab hota hai. Dimaag bhasha ko dohraav se seekhta hai, lambai se nahi. 15 minute ko aise todo:

    15 minute kahan se laayein

    Nahane se pehle. Bus ya metro me. Raat ko sone se pehle bistar par. Ek fix jagah aur fix waqt chun lo — phir yaad rakhne ki zaroorat hi nahi padegi, aadat khud kheench legi.

    1.2 Har cheez zor se bolo — yahi poora khel hai¶

    Log saalon tak grammar padhte hain aur phir bhi bolte waqt atakte hain. Kyun? Kyunki grammar dimaag me chali gayi, par zubaan ki muscle me nahi gayi. English ki aawazein banane ke liye aapke hoth, zubaan aur jabde ko nayi position seekhni padti hai — aur muscle sirf dohraav se seekhti hai, samajhne se nahi.

    • I am learning English, and I am getting better every day. Pehli line jo aaj bolni hai. 🔊 dabao, suno, phir teen baar khud bolo.
    • I make mistakes, but I keep speaking. Galti karta hoon, par bolna band nahi karta. Isi vaakya ko apna niyam bana lo.
    • Could you say that again, please? Samajh na aaye to ye poochhna hai — sharmaana nahi hai. Ye line native log bhi roz bolte hain.

    Akele kamre me bolna — 'pagalpan' nahi, tareeka hai

    Bahut log soch kar ruk jaate hain: "akele kaise bolun, bewkoof lagega." Sach ye hai ki har fluent bolne wale insaan ne pehle akele hazaar baar bola hota hai. Darwaza band karo, aaina saamne rakho, aur bolo. Kisi ko sunai nahi de raha — isliye galti ki koi keemat nahi hai. Yahi sabse surakshit practice hai jo mil sakti hai.

    1.3 🔊 button aur 🎤 mic — kaise kaam karte hain¶

    Cheez Kya karti hai Aapko kya karna hai
    🔊 Say button Us line ko aapke device ki aawaz me bolta hai. Pehle suno. Phir rok kar wahi line khud bolo. Phir dobara suno aur milao.
    Dheemi aawaz Kuch mushkil lines dheere boli jaati hain, taaki har aawaz saaf pakdi jaaye. Pehli baar dheere bolo. Tez baad me aayega — tez bolna lakshya nahi hai, saaf bolna hai.
    Quiz ke 6 prakaar MCQ, khaali jagah, galti dhoondo, vaakya jodo, jodi milao, aur bol kar dikhao. Sab karo. Chhodo mat. Galat jawaab ke baad jo explanation aata hai, wo sabse qeemti hissa hai.
    🎤 Bolo (mic) "Bol kar dikhao" wale sawaal me mic aapki aawaz sun kar check karta hai. Pehli baar browser permission maangega — "Allow" dabao. Shaant kamre me, phone se 20 cm door bolo.

    Mic na chale to?

    Koi dikkat nahi — har browser me speech recognition nahi hota. Us soorat me page khud likh dega ki sun kar dohra lo. Mic sirf ek sahayak hai, shart nahi. Aapka khud ka kaan sabse achcha judge hai: 🔊 suno, record karne wale app me khud bolo, dono bajao, farq suno. Yahi professional log karte hain.

    1.4 Kahan se shuru karein — apna raasta chuno¶

    Teen tarah ke padhne wale hote hain. Apni line dhoondo:

    A. 'Mujhe kuch nahi aata, zero se shuru karna hai'

    Seedha kram se chalo. Agla chapter — English ki aawazein — pehle padho, kyunki wahi neev hai. Phir Grammar ki buniyaad, phir Bolna seekho. Rozana ek hissa. Teen mahine me aap pehchaan nahi paayenge khud ko.

    B. 'Grammar aati hai, par bolte waqt atak jaata hoon'

    Ye sabse aam haalat hai — aur iska ilaaj grammar nahi hai. Seedha "Bolna seekho" hisse par jao: Uchchaaran → Rhythm → Fluency → Roz ki baat-cheet. Grammar wale chapters sirf tab kholo jab koi khaas cheez atke.

    C. 'Interview hai / office join kar raha hoon'

    Pehle Interview aur office chapter, phir Fluency, phir Indian English ki aadatein (jo galtiyan interview me turant pakdi jaati hain). Baaki kitaab baad me, aaraam se.

    Kahin bhi atko to

    Sidebar me saari chapters ki list hai — jahan se marzi kholo. Kitaab aapka progress khud yaad rakhti hai: jo quiz aap kar lete hain, unka nishaan contents page par lag jaata hai. Koi login nahi, kuch bharna nahi.

    1.5 Jab man kare chhod dene ka¶

    Ek baat seedhi keh deta hoon, kyunki koi kitaab ye nahi kehti: do-teen hafte baad aapko lagega ki kuch sudhaar nahi ho raha. Ye bilkul normal hai. Bhasha seekhna seedhi chadhaai nahi, seedhiyan hain — kai din ek jaisa lagta hai, phir achaanak ek din aap ek poora vaakya bina soche bol dete hain.

    • It is normal to feel stuck. I will speak anyway. Atakna normal hai. Phir bhi bolunga. Jis din man na kare, ye line bol do aur 5 minute kar lo.
    • Sorry, my English is improving. Please bear with me. Asli duniya me jab atko to yahi bolo — "thoda sambhaal lijiye". Log hamesha madad karte hain.

    Teen aadatein jo sab se zyada nuksaan karti hain

    • Galat: "Pehle grammar perfect karunga, phir bolunga."
    • Sahi: Aaj se galat-salat bolo, grammar saath-saath theek hoti jaayegi.

    • Kyun: Jo log bolne se pehle perfect hone ka intezaar karte hain, wo kabhi shuru hi nahi karte.

    • Galat: Hindi me soch kar, dimaag me translate karke bolna.

    • Sahi: Chhote ready-made vaakye ratna, aur unhe seedha bolna.

    • Kyun: Translate karne me 3-4 second lagte hain — isi gap me confidence toot jaata hai. Isliye is kitaab me taiyaar lines di gayi hain.

    • Galat: Sirf aankhon se padhte rehna, kabhi aawaz nahi nikaalna.

    • Sahi: Har line kam se kam ek baar zor se bolna.
    • Kyun: Yahi is kitaab aur baaki sab kitaabon ka farq hai. Chup-chaap padhoge to natija bhi chup rahega.

    1.6 Chhota sa pehla kadam — abhi¶

    Neeche ka quiz choti si shuruaat hai. Isme kuch sawaal bol kar dene hain. Kamre ka darwaza band karo, aur shuru karo. Paanch minute, bas.

    1.7 Practice — Shuruaat¶

    Exercise 1

    Is kitaab ka sabse bada niyam kya hai?

    • Har English line zor se bolna
    • Roz ek poora chapter khatam karna
    • Pehle saari grammar yaad karna
    • Sirf dhyan se padhna

    Hint: Chaar hisson me sabse bada hissa kaunsa tha?

    Jawaab

    Har English line zor se bolna

    Padhna samajh deta hai, bolna fluency deta hai. Zubaan ki muscle sirf dohraav se seekhti hai.

    Exercise 2

    Roz kitna waqt dena sabse behtar hai?

    • 15 minute, roz
    • 2 ghante, hafte me ek din
    • 45 minute, hafte me do din
    • Jab man kare, jitna man kare
    Jawaab

    15 minute, roz

    Dimaag bhasha dohraav se seekhta hai, lambai se nahi. Roz thoda > kabhi-kabhi bahut.

    Exercise 3

    15 minute me sabse zyada waqt kis par jaata hai?

    • Zor se bolna (6 minute)
    • Padhna (4 minute)
    • Quiz (3 minute)
    • Dohraav (2 minute)
    Jawaab

    Zor se bolna (6 minute)

    Diagram me narangi hissa sabse bada tha — bolna. Padhna sirf 4 minute.

    Exercise 4

    Quiz me galat jawaab aaye to?

    • Explanation dhyan se padho — wahi seekhna hai
    • Quiz chhod do
    • Chapter dobara poora ratna padega
    • Aage ke quiz skip kar do

    Hint: Galti ka sabse bada faayda kya hai?

    Jawaab

    Explanation dhyan se padho — wahi seekhna hai

    Sahi jawaab sirf confirm karta hai. Galat jawaab ke baad wala 'kyun' asli seekh hai.

    Exercise 5

    Agar aapki grammar theek hai par bolte waqt atakte hain, to kahan se shuru karein?

    • Seedha Bolna seekho hisse se
    • Shabd ke 8 prakaar se
    • Idioms se
    • Final test se
    Jawaab

    Seedha Bolna seekho hisse se

    Atakna grammar ki kami nahi, practice ki kami hai. Uchchaaran, rhythm aur fluency se shuru karo.

    Exercise 6

    It is ___ to feel stuck.

    Hint: Atakna 'aam baat' hai — English me?

    Jawaab

    normal

    'It is normal to…' ek bahut kaam ka saancha hai: It is normal to feel nervous. It is normal to make mistakes.

    Exercise 7

    I make mistakes, ___ I keep speaking.

    Hint: Do ulti baatein jodne wala shabd.

    Jawaab

    but

    'but' do ulti baaton ko jodta hai. Is poore vaakye ko apna niyam bana lo.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — us shabd par tap karo.

    I am learning English since two months.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: since.

    Sahi roop: I have been learning English for two months.

    Kitne samay se = 'for'. 'since' tab aata hai jab shuruaat ka point ho — since June.

    Exercise 9

    Shuruaat ki pehli line. Zor se bolo — dheere, saaf.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I am learning English

    Chhoti line se shuru karo. 'learning' me 'ning' ko puri tarah bolo, 'learnin' nahi.

    Exercise 10

    Ab apna niyam bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I make mistakes but I keep speaking

    Bolte waqt 'mistakes' aur 'speaking' par halka zor aata hai — yahi baat ka matlab hai.

    Exercise 11

    Samajh na aaye to ye poochhna hai. Politely bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could you say that again please

    'Could you' se shuruaat sabse polite hai. Aawaz aakhir me halki oopar jaati hai — ye sawaal hai.

    Exercise 12

    Aaj ka vaada. Bol kar khatam karo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I will practise for fifteen minutes every day

    'fifteen' me zor doosre hisse par — fif-TEEN. 'fifty' me pehle par — FIF-ty. Yahi farq hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Agar aap zero se shuru kar rahe hain, agla chapter chhodna mat — English ki aawazein. Wahi neev hai jiske bina baaki sab kuch thoda-thoda galat sunai deta rahega. Aur haan: darwaza band, aawaz zor se. 🔊
