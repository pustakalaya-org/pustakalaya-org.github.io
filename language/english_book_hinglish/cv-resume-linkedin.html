    Chapter 52: CV aur LinkedIn¶

    CV aur LinkedIn — action verbs, bullet points, profile summary

    Recruiter ek CV par aksar das-pandrah second lagata hai. Us waqt wo aapki "hard-working and sincere" personality nahi dhoondta — wo dhoondta hai ki aapne kya kiya aur uska kya nateeja nikla. Ye chapter sikhata hai ki CV ka dhaancha kaisa ho, har bullet ek formula se kaise likhein, freshers kya karein, Indian CVs ki purani aadatein kaun si chhodni hain, aur LinkedIn par apna headline aur About kaise likhein.

    52.1 CV ya resume — kya farq hai?¶

    India me dono shabd lagbhag ek hi matlab me chalte hain, aur koi aapko "galat shabd" ke liye reject nahi karega. Par farq jaan lena achha hai, khaaskar agar aap bahar apply kar rahe ho.

    Baat Resume CV
    Lambaai 1–2 page UK/India: 2 page · Academic: lamba
    Kahan zyada US, Canada, India ki private jobs UK, Europe, academic jobs
    Kya hota hai Is job ke liye chuna hua kaam UK me resume jaisa; academic me poora record
    Har job ke liye badlo? Haan Haan (academic CV kam)

    Ek line me

    Fresher ya 10 saal se kam experience: 1 page. Usse zyada: 2 page, bas. Har job ke liye CV ko us job ke shabdon ke hisaab se thoda badlo. Ek CV sab jagah bhejna = kahin bhi fit na hona.

    • Please find my CV attached. Email me CV bhejne ki seedhi line. Poora email Email likhna aur cover note Application aur message me hai.
    • I've tailored my resume to the job description. tailor = naap se silna — yahan: job ke hisaab se badalna.

    52.2 Dhaancha — upar se neeche¶

    Recruiter ki aankh upar-baayein se shuru hoti hai aur neeche jaati hai. Isliye sabse zaroori cheez sabse upar. Experience wale log pehle experience, freshers pehle education aur projects.

    Hissa Kya likhein Kya mat likhein
    Header Naam, city, phone, email, LinkedIn Poora pata, "Curriculum Vitae" heading
    Profile 3–4 line ka parichay "Objective: To work in a reputed organisation…"
    Experience Role, company, saal, 3–5 bullets Har duty ki lambi list
    Skills Tools, languages, certifications "Hard-working, team player"
    Education Degree, college, saal, CGPA Class 5 se poori kahani

    52.3 Profile summary — teen line ka parichay¶

    Profile summary CV ka "trailer" hai. Ise bina "I" ke, chhote vaakyon me likhte hain. Formula: kaun ho + kitna experience + kis cheez me khaas + ek bada nateeja + ab kya chahte ho.

    Summary formula

    Role + years + specialty + one result + goal. "Data analyst with 3 years' experience in fintech. Built dashboards that cut reporting time by 50%. Looking to move into product analytics." "I am" aur "my" chhod do — CV me ye apne aap samjha jaata hai.

    ✗

    Objective: To work in a challenging environment where I can utilise my skills for the growth of the organisation.

    ✓

    Profile: Mechanical engineer with 2 years' experience in quality control at an auto-parts plant. Reduced defect rates by 18% through new inspection checklists. Seeking a process-improvement role.

    kaun ho

    kya kar chuke ho

    • Customer support lead with five years' experience in e-commerce. five years' experience — apostrophe s ke baad (years ka experience).
    • Final-year B.Com student with strong Excel and Tally skills. Fresher ka profile — degree + skills.
    • Seeking an entry-level role in digital marketing. "Seeking" / "Looking to move into" — goal wali aakhri line.

    52.4 Action verbs — kaam ko zinda karne wale shabd¶

    "Responsible for" aur "worked on" se recruiter ko pata nahi chalta ki aapne khud kya kiya. Har bullet ek majboot past-tense verb se shuru karo. Abhi wali job ke liye present tense bhi chalta hai — par ek job ke andar ek hi tense rakho.

    Kaam ka prakaar Verbs Example
    Team chalana led, managed, coordinated, mentored Led a team of six.
    Kuch banana built, designed, developed, launched Launched a mobile app.
    Behtar karna improved, increased, streamlined Increased sales by 20%.
    Kam karna reduced, cut, lowered, saved Cut costs by ₹4 lakh.
    Samajhna analysed, identified, researched Identified a billing error.
    Baat karna presented, negotiated, trained Trained 40 new staff.
    Hal nikaalna resolved, fixed, solved Resolved 50+ tickets a day.
    • Led a team of six to deliver the project two weeks early. Chhe logon ki team ke saath project do hafte pehle diya.
    • Built an inventory tracker in Excel used by four branches. Excel me stock tracker banaya — chaar branch use karti hain.
    • Reduced customer complaints by 30% in six months. Chhe mahine me shikayatein 30% kam ki.
    • Negotiated a new contract with the courier partner, saving ₹2 lakh a year. Courier ke saath naya contract — saal ke ₹2 lakh bache.
    • Trained 25 interns on the new billing software. 25 interns ko naya software sikhaya.

    Verb ki jagah 'I' nahi

    Bullet seedha verb se: Led…, Built…, Reduced… — "I led…" nahi, "I was responsible for…" bilkul nahi. Upgrade wale verbs ke liye Verb upgrade chapter bhi dekho.

    ✗

    Responsible for handling social media.

    ✓

    Grew Instagram followers from 2,000 to 15,000 in a year.

    badla

    52.5 Bullet ka formula — verb + task + result + number¶

    Har achha bullet chaar tukdon se banta hai. Number na ho to bhi nateeja zaroor likho — kitne log, kitna samay, kitna paisa, kitne percent.

    • Automated monthly GST reports using Python scripts, cutting preparation time from three days to four hours. "cutting / saving / increasing…" — -ing se nateeja jodo.
    • Redesigned the onboarding emails, which increased trial sign-ups by 22%. "which…" se bhi nateeja jod sakte ho.
    • Streamlined the loan approval process from ten steps to six, halving waiting time. streamline = faaltu kadam hata kar seedha karna; halve = aadha karna.
    • Resolved an average of 45 customer queries a day with a 95% satisfaction score. Number na badle to bhi "kitna" batao — roz kitne, kitna santosh.
    • Identified a pricing error that was costing the company ₹50,000 a month. Chhoti khoj bhi bada nateeja ho sakti hai — paise me batao.

    Number kahan se laayein?

    Poochho: kitne log? kitna samay? kitna paisa? kitne percent? Pakka number na ho to imaandaar andaaza: about 30%, 50+ clients. Andaaza theek hai, jhooth nahi — interview me poochha jaayega.

    ✗

    Worked on sales.

    ✓

    Closed 40 new B2B accounts in Gujarat, exceeding the annual target by 15%.

    ✗

    Did coding of website and handled the testing part.

    ✓

    Developed a college events website in React and tested it with 300 students before launch.

    Developed

    tested

    ✗

    Increased sales. Am managing a team of 4. Prepared reports.

    ✓

    Increased sales… · Managed a team of four… · Prepared reports…

    52.6 Skills, education aur freshers ka CV¶

    Skills — dikhao, sirf bolo mat¶

    Skills me wo cheezein likho jo test ki ja sakein: software, tools, programming languages, bhaashaayein, certificates. "Hard-working, honest, team player" ko bullets me saboot ke saath dikhao, skills list me nahi.

    • Skills: Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP), SQL, Power BI, Tally ERP Bracket me batao ki tool me kya-kya aata hai.
    • Languages: English (fluent), Hindi (native), Marathi (conversational) Level saath me — fluent / native / conversational / basic.

    Education — degree, college, saal¶

    • B.Tech in Computer Science, Shivaji Institute of Technology, Pune, 2025 · CGPA 8.4/10 Degree in subject, college, saal, score. Score "out of 10" likho — bahar CGPA scale alag hota hai.
    • Class XII (CBSE), 2021 · 91% Experience ho gaya to Class X/XII hata sakte ho.

    Fresher ho to — experience ki jagah kya?¶

    Fresher ke paas job nahi, par kaam hai: internships, college projects, hackathons, freelance, college fest organise karna, NSS/NCC, tuition padhana. Inhe bhi usi formula se likho — verb + task + result + number. Order: Profile → Education → Projects / Internships → Skills → Achievements.

    • Organised a three-day tech fest with 1,200 participants and a ₹3 lakh budget. College fest bhi asli kaam hai — budget, log, deadline.
    • Completed a two-month internship at a CA firm; reconciled accounts for 15 clients. reconcile = hisaab milaana.
    • Taught maths to 20 Class 8 students as a volunteer; average scores rose by 12 marks. Volunteer kaam + nateeja.

    Pehle → baad me: ek fresher ka CV

    Pehle

    CURRICULUM VITAE · [photo] · Name: Rohit Kumar · Father's Name: Shri R. P. Kumar · DOB: 12/03/2003 · Marital Status: Unmarried · Objective: To work in a reputed company and grow with it. · Strengths: Hard-working, sincere, punctual. · Hobbies: Listening music, surfing net. · Declaration: I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge.

    Baad me

    • ROHIT KUMAR · Lucknow · 98xxx xxxxx · rohit.k@mail.com Heading me sirf naam — "Curriculum Vitae" nahi. Photo, pita ka naam, DOB, marital status hata diye.
    • Profile: BBA graduate with a summer internship in retail sales. Comfortable with Excel and customer calls. Seeking a sales-trainee role. Objective ki jagah teen line ka profile.
    • Sales Intern, a fashion retail store, Lucknow (May–July 2024): Helped the store hit its monthly target by upselling accessories; added ₹60,000 in sales. "Hard-working" likhne ki jagah saboot — number ke saath.

    Declaration aur hobbies hata diye. Bacha hua space projects aur skills ko diya.

    52.7 Indian CV ki aam galtiyan¶

    Kai aadatein purane sarkaari forms se aayi hain. Private companies aur bahar ki naukriyon me ye CV ko purana dikhati hain — aur kuch to bhedbhaav ka khatra bhi badhaati hain. Haan, agar job ad khud photo ya DOB maange (kuch sarkaari, Gulf ya airline jobs), to wahi do.

    Ye sab hata do

    • Galat: Photo, Father's Name, Date of Birth, Marital Status, Religion
    • Sahi: Name · City · Phone · Email · LinkedIn

    • Kyun: Inka kaam se koi lena-dena nahi, aur ye unconscious bias badhaate hain. Jagah bachao, kaam dikhao. (Job ad maange tab hi do.)

    • Galat: I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge.

    • Sahi: (Kuch nahi — line hata do.)

    • Kyun: Ye affidavit ki bhasha hai. Private CV me iski zaroorat nahi; sarkaari form khud maange to alag baat.

    • Galat: Heading: CURRICULUM VITAE / RESUME

    • Sahi: Heading: ANANYA IYER

    • Kyun: Sab jaante hain ki ye CV hai. Sabse badi line aapka naam ho.

    • Galat: Hobbies: Listening music, surfing net, reading books.

    • Sahi: Interests: Long-distance running (2 half-marathons), Carnatic music.

    • Kyun: Grammar: listening to music. Aur "surfing net" kuch nahi batata. Hobby tab likho jab wo kuch kahe — warna section hata do.

    • Galat: I have done my B.Com from Delhi University.

    • Sahi: B.Com, University of Delhi, 2024
    • Kyun: CV me poore vaakya nahi, tukde. Bolte waqt: I did / completed my B.Com at Delhi University.

    Jhooth aur copy-paste — kabhi nahi

    Number badha-chadha ke mat likho. Interview me har bullet par sawaal aa sakta hai: "Walk me through how you cut the time by 50%." Jo bullet aap 2 minute tak samjha nahi sakte, wo CV par mat rakho. Taiyaari ke liye Interview aur office.

    • Walk me through your CV. Interviewer: apna CV shuru se samjhao.
    • Sure. I'll start with my current role and then go back. Jawaab bhi CV ke kram me — naya pehle.

    52.8 LinkedIn — headline aur About¶

    LinkedIn par aapka headline naam ke theek neeche dikhta hai — search me bhi yahi aata hai. Default "Student at XYZ College" ki jagah batao ki aap kya karte ho aur kis cheez me achhe ho.

    Headline formula

    Role | Specialty | Proof ya value — "Data Analyst | SQL & Power BI | Turning sales data into weekly decisions". Lagbhag 220 characters milte hain — chhota aur saaf rakho.

    Kamzor headline Majboot headline
    Looking for opportunities Final-year B.Tech (CSE) | Java & Spring Boot | 2 internships
    Working at ABC Pvt Ltd HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025
    Hard-working professional Chartered Accountant | GST & audit | Helping SMEs stay compliant

    About section — first person, baat-cheet jaisa¶

    CV ke ulte, LinkedIn ka About section "I" ke saath likhte hain — jaise aap kisi se mil ke apna parichay de rahe ho. Teen chhote paragraph: aap kya karte ho, ek-do nateeje, aur aap kis tarah ke kaam ya logon se judna chahte ho.

    • I'm a data analyst who helps sales teams make faster decisions. Pehli line — aap kya karte ho aur kiske liye.
    • At my current company, I built a weekly dashboard that replaced six manual reports. Ek nateeja — wahi bullet formula, par "I" ke saath.
    • I'm currently exploring roles in product analytics. Feel free to reach out. Aakhri line — kya chahte ho. reach out = sampark karna.

    ✗

    Myself Priya, a passionate and dynamic professional.

    ✓

    I'm Priya, a UX designer who makes banking apps easier for first-time users.

    Indian English ki aadatein

    karte kya ho

    52.9 Practice — CV aur LinkedIn¶

    Exercise 1

    Ek fresher ka CV kitne page ka hona chahiye?

    • 1 page
    • 2 pages
    • 3 pages
    • Jitna lamba utna achha
    Jawaab

    1 page

    Fresher ya kam experience: 1 page. 10+ saal: 2 page tak.

    Exercise 2

    Kaunsa bullet sabse majboot hai?

    • Reduced invoice errors by 40% by adding Excel checks.
    • Responsible for invoices.
    • Worked on invoice-related tasks.
    • I was handling the invoice part.
    Jawaab

    Reduced invoice errors by 40% by adding Excel checks.

    Verb + task + result + number — baaki sirf duty batate hain.

    Exercise 3

    ___ a team of six to deliver the project two weeks early. (lead ka past)

    Hint: Team chalaana — past tense.

    Jawaab

    Led / led

    lead → led. CV bullets seedha past-tense verb se shuru hote hain.

    Exercise 4

    Hobby wali line me galti dhoondo.

    Hobbies: listening music and travelling.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: listening.

    Sahi roop: Hobbies: listening to music and travelling.

    listen ke baad 'to' chahiye: listening to music.

    Exercise 5

    Private company ke CV me in me se kya rakhna chahiye?

    • LinkedIn profile link
    • Father's name
    • Marital status
    • I hereby declare…
    Jawaab

    LinkedIn profile link

    Header me sirf naam, city, phone, email, LinkedIn. Baaki tab hi jab job ad maange.

    Exercise 6

    Kaam ko sahi verb se jodo.

    A B
    Team chalaayi Launched
    Kharcha kam kiya Trained
    App banaya aur shuru kiya Led
    Logon ko sikhaya Cut
    Jawaab

    Team chalaayi — Led; Kharcha kam kiya — Cut; App banaya aur shuru kiya — Launched; Logon ko sikhaya — Trained

    Har kaam ka ek majboot action verb hota hai.

    Exercise 7

    Is bullet ka kamzor hissa dhoondo.

    Responsible for managing the company Instagram page.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Responsible.

    Sahi roop: Grew the company Instagram page from 2,000 to 15,000 followers.

    'Responsible for' sirf duty hai — action verb aur nateeja chahiye.

    Exercise 8

    Profile summary me kya nahi hona chahiye?

    • To work in a challenging environment for the growth of the organisation
    • Kitne saal ka experience
    • Ek bada nateeja
    • Aap aage kya chahte ho
    Jawaab

    To work in a challenging environment for the growth of the organisation

    Ye purana 'objective' hai — har CV me same, kuch nahi batata.

    Exercise 9

    Customer support lead with five ___ experience in e-commerce.

    Hint: years ka experience — apostrophe kahan?

    Jawaab

    years' / years’

    five years' experience — plural 'years' ke baad apostrophe.

    Exercise 10

    LinkedIn headline ka sabse achha roop?

    • HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025
    • Looking for opportunities
    • Hard-working and sincere professional
    • Working at ABC Pvt Ltd
    Jawaab

    HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025

    Role | Specialty | Proof — headline formula.

    Exercise 11

    LinkedIn ka About section kis tarah likhte hain?

    • First person — 'I'm…, I built…'
    • Bina verb ke tukdon me
    • Third person — 'He is…'
    • Sirf skills ki list
    Jawaab

    First person — 'I'm…, I built…'

    CV bina 'I' ke; LinkedIn About 'I' ke saath, baat-cheet jaisa.

    Exercise 12

    Is baat se kaunsa CV bullet sabse sahi banta hai?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    In my last job I handled customer complaints. I set up a new ticket system, and our average response time came down from two days to six hours.

    • Set up a ticket system that cut response time from two days to six hours.
    • Responsible for customer complaints.
    • Handled complaints and tickets.
    • Worked in customer support for two days.
    Jawaab

    Set up a ticket system that cut response time from two days to six hours.

    Verb (Set up) + task (ticket system) + result + number (2 din se 6 ghante).

    Exercise 13

    Response time kitna ho gaya?

    Ise zor se padho, ya kisi se padhwao:

    In my last job I handled customer complaints. I set up a new ticket system, and our average response time came down from two days to six hours.

    • Six hours
    • Two days
    • Two hours
    • Six days
    Jawaab

    Six hours

    'came down from two days to six hours' — 2 din se ghat kar 6 ghante.

    Exercise 14

    Ye bullet suno aur likho.

    Dictation. Kisi se ye vaakya zor se padhwao, phir likh lo.

    Jawaab

    Led a team of five to launch the app in three months.

    Led (lead ka past) + team + launch + number.

    Exercise 15

    Bullet ko sahi kram me lagao.

    Shabd: complaints / percent / in / 30 / customer / months / by / Reduced / six

    Jawaab

    Reduced customer complaints by 30 percent in six months

    Verb + task + result (by 30 percent) + samay.

    Exercise 16

    Ek job ke bullets me tense ki galti dhoondo.

    Increased sales by 20%. Am managing a team of four.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: Am.

    Sahi roop: Increased sales by 20%. Managed a team of four.

    CV bullet me 'am' jaisa helping verb nahi, aur ek job me ek hi tense.

    Exercise 17

    Interview me apna ek bullet bol ke dekho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I reduced customer complaints by thirty per cent

    Bolte waqt 'I' lagao — CV me nahi likhte, par baat-cheet me zaroori.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj apne CV ke teen bullets formula se dobara likho. Phir CV ke saath bhejne wala cover note Application aur message se, aur interview ki taiyaari Interview aur office se.
