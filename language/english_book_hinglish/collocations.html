    Chapter 37: Kaunsa shabd kiske saath¶

    Kaunsa shabd kiske saath — collocations

    "I did a mistake" — grammar me koi galti nahi, phir bhi kaan ko chubhta hai. Kyon? Kyonki English me kuch shabd jodi me chalte hain, jaise Hindi me "chai banana" aur "khaana pakaana" — koi "chai pakaana" nahi kehta. In pakki jodiyon ko collocations kehte hain. Inka koi logic nahi, sirf aadat hai — aur yahi aadat aapki English ko "sahi" se "natural" banati hai.

    37.1 Collocation kya hai — shabdon ki pakki jodiyan¶

    Collocation galat ho to log samajh to jaate hain, par turant pata chal jaata hai ki aap anuvaad kar rahe ho. Dictionary ka matlab sahi hone se kaam nahi chalta — saathi shabd bhi sahi hona chahiye.

    Shabd nahi, jodi seekho

    Collocation = do shabd jo aadat se saath aate hain: strong tea, heavy rain, make a mistake. Inka logic nahi hota — isliye naya shabd hamesha uske saathi ke saath likho aur bolo. "mistake" mat likho; "make a mistake" likho.

    37.2 make ya do?¶

    Hindi me dono "karna" hain, isliye ye sabse badi gadbad hai. Ek mota niyam kaafi kaam aata hai — aur jo niyam me na baithe, wo bas jodi me yaad karne hain.

    • I made a few mistakes, but I did my best. Galti "banti" hai (make); koshish ek kaam hai (do).
    • Could you do me a favour and make a quick call to the bank? Ek hi vaakya me dono — favour = do, phone call = make.

    make vs do

    make = kuch naya banana ya paida karna (a decision, a mistake, money, a noise, a call). do = kaam, duty ya kriya (homework, the dishes, exercise, a favour, your best). "Do a mistake" sabse aam Indian galti hai — galti hamesha make hoti hai.

    37.3 have, take, give, get — chaar chhote verb, saikdon jodiyan¶

    Ye chaar verb itne chhote hain ki inka apna matlab lagbhag kho jaata hai — matlab jodi se aata hai. Isliye inhe akela nahi, hamesha saathi ke saath seekho.

    Verb Pakki jodiyan Suno
    have a meal · a bath · a good time · a look · a party · a chat
    take a photo · a break · a seat · a bus · a chance · medicine
    give a speech · advice · an example · a hand · a call · a smile
    get ready · a job · home · lost · better · married

    Exam aur dawai — 'take'

    Student exam take karta hai (British: sit) — teacher exam give ya set karta hai. Dawai bhi take hoti hai, "eat" nahi: take your medicine. "I gave my exam" India me sab samajhte hain, par bahar iska matlab ulta lagta hai — jaise aapne exam liya ho.

    37.4 Adjective jodiyan — heavy rain, strong tea, high fever¶

    Hindi me hum "tez baarish", "tez chai", "tez bukhaar" — sabke liye ek "tez" bol dete hain. English me har noun ka apna adjective hai. Yahi jodiyan sabse zyada pakdi jaati hain.

    • There was heavy traffic because of the heavy rain. Traffic aur baarish dono "heavy" — "zyada" ke liye.
    • She has a high fever, so she's in deep sleep now. Bukhaar "high", neend "deep".
    • I need a cup of strong tea and some fresh air. Kadak chai = strong tea. Taazi hawa = fresh air.

    'Tez' ke chhah roop

    heavy rain / traffic · strong tea / coffee / wind · high fever / price / temperature · deep sleep. Hindi ka ek "tez" English me noun dekh kar badalta hai. "big rain", "big fever", "expensive price" — teeno se bacho.

    37.5 Verb + preposition — depend on, listen to¶

    Kuch verb apne saath ek khaas preposition laate hain — aur galat preposition se matlab badal bhi sakta hai. Inhe ek shabd ki tarah yaad karo: depend-on, listen-to.

    Jodi Example Suno
    depend on It depends on the weather.
    listen to Listen to me carefully.
    wait for I'm waiting for the bus.
    agree with (insaan) I agree with you.
    agree to (prastaav) They agreed to our plan.
    apologise for I apologise for the delay.
    look forward to + -ing I look forward to meeting you.
    believe in She believes in hard work.

    Jinke saath kuch nahi lagta

    Kuch verbs ke baad koi preposition nahi: discuss the plan, enter the room, reach Delhi, marry someone, answer the question. "discuss about", "reach at", "answer to the question" — teeno galat. Aur "look forward to" ke baad -ing: "looking forward to meeting you", "to meet you" nahi.

    37.6 Bolne wale verbs — say, tell, speak, talk¶

    Charon ka matlab "bolna/kehna" hai, par jodi alag-alag. Yahin "say a lie" aur "tell me sorry" jaisi galtiyan janam leti hain. Sabse pehle say aur tell ka dhaancha samjho.

    • Always tell the truth, even when it's hard. Sach "tell" hota hai — aur jhooth bhi: tell a lie.
    • He said sorry and told us what happened. say sorry · tell + us + baat.
    • Do you speak Tamil? Let's talk about it later. Bhasha "speak" hoti hai; kisi vishay par "talk about".

    say vs tell

    tell + insaan (tell me, tell her) — aur hamesha tell the truth / a lie / a story / a joke. say + baat (say hello, say sorry, say thank you) — insaan chahiye to say … to me. Bhasha speak hoti hai, vishay par talk about.

    37.7 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye jodiyan India me itni aam hain ki sahi lagne lagti hain. Par client call, interview ya IELTS me yahi chhoti jodiyan aapki English ko "translated" bana deti hain.

    Collocation ki aam galtiyan

    • Galat: I did a big mistake.
    • Sahi: I made a big mistake.

    • Kyun: Galti hamesha make hoti hai. "Do" kaam ke liye hai.

    • Galat: My son has big fever.

    • Sahi: My son has a high fever.

    • Kyun: Bukhaar ka number hota hai, isliye wo high hota hai. Aur "a" mat bhoolo: a high fever.

    • Galat: Don't say a lie.

    • Sahi: Don't tell a lie.

    • Kyun: Jhooth aur sach dono tell hote hain: tell a lie, tell the truth.

    • Galat: I gave my exam yesterday.

    • Sahi: I took my exam yesterday. / I sat my exam yesterday.

    • Kyun: Student exam take karta hai. "Give an exam" bahar teacher ka kaam lagta hai.

    • Galat: Please close the light.

    • Sahi: Please switch off the light. / Please turn off the light.
    • Kyun: "Light band karo" ka anuvaad. Light, fan, TV switch on / switch off hote hain; darwaaza close hota hai.

    Ye bhi dhyaan do — aur ek achhi khabar

    eat medicine → take medicine · do a party → have / throw a party · strong rain → heavy rain.
    Achhi khabar: take a decision bilkul sahi hai (British aur Indian English), aur make a decision bhi — dono me se koi bhi bolo.

    37.8 Collocation kaise seekhein — shabd ka jaal banao¶

    Collocation ka koi formula nahi, isliye tareeka hi sab kuch hai. Har naye zaroori noun ke chaaron taraf uske saathi likho — kaunse verb, kaunse adjective, kaunsa preposition. Ek jaal, ek page.

    Roz ki teen aadatein

    1. Padhte waqt shabd nahi, jodi underline karo — "heavy traffic", "made a decision".
    2. Notebook me har noun ka chhota jaal banao, upar jaisa.
    3. Shak ho to jodi ko quotes me search karo — "big fever" vs "high fever" — jo zyada mile, wahi natural.

    37.9 Practice — Collocations¶

    Exercise 1

    I need to ___ a phone call to the bank.

    • make
    • do
    • give
    • say
    Jawaab

    make

    Phone call 'make' hoti hai — kuch naya paida karna. 'Give me a call' bhi chalta hai, par call karne wala 'makes a call'.

    Exercise 2

    We had very ___ rain last night.

    • heavy
    • strong
    • big
    • high
    Jawaab

    heavy

    Baarish 'heavy' hoti hai. 'Strong' hawa ke liye (strong wind), 'big rain' bilkul nahi.

    Exercise 3

    My son has a ___ fever. I'm taking him to the doctor.

    • high
    • big
    • heavy
    • strong
    Jawaab

    high

    Bukhaar ka number hota hai — isliye 'high fever', jaise high temperature, high price.

    Exercise 4

    Can you do me a ___? Please pick up my parcel.

    Hint: Ehsaan — 'do' ki pakki jodi.

    Jawaab

    favour / favor

    'Do someone a favour' = kisi par ehsaan karna. Favour 'do' hota hai, 'make' nahi.

    Exercise 5

    It depends ___ the weather.

    Hint: depend ke saath kaunsa preposition?

    Jawaab

    on

    Hamesha 'depend on' — 'depend upon' bhi sahi hai par purana lagta hai. 'depend of' galat.

    Exercise 6

    Is vaakya me galat verb par tap karo.

    I did a big mistake in the exam.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: did.

    Sahi roop: I made a big mistake in the exam.

    Galti hamesha 'make' hoti hai — make a mistake.

    Exercise 7

    Ek shabd galat jodi bana raha hai — dhoondo.

    Never say a lie to your parents.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: say.

    Sahi roop: Never tell a lie to your parents.

    Jhooth aur sach 'tell' hote hain — tell a lie, tell the truth.

    Exercise 8

    Verb ko uski pakki jodi se milao.

    A B
    make a photo
    do a speech
    take a mistake
    give your homework
    Jawaab

    make — a mistake; do — your homework; take — a photo; give — a speech

    make = paida karna, do = kaam, take a photo, give a speech — inhe jodi me hi yaad karo.

    Exercise 9

    Adjective ko sahi noun se milao.

    A B
    heavy temperature
    strong sleep
    high traffic
    deep coffee
    Jawaab

    heavy — traffic; strong — coffee; high — temperature; deep — sleep

    Hindi ka 'tez/zyada' English me noun dekh kar badalta hai — heavy traffic, strong coffee, high temperature, deep sleep.

    Exercise 10

    Sahi kram me lagao — email ki aakhri line.

    Shabd: forward / you / I / to / meeting / looking / am

    Jawaab

    I am looking forward to meeting you

    'look forward to' ke baad -ing: meeting. 'to meet you' yahan galat hai.

    Exercise 11

    Ye vaakya bolo — do pakki jodiyan ek saath.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I made a mistake, but I told the truth

    make a mistake + tell the truth. 'made a' ko jodkar bolo — 'mei-da'.

    Aage kya karein

    Is hafte ek "jodi diary" rakho. Roz jo bhi English padho ya suno, usme se teen jodiyan likho. Mahine ke ant tak nabbe jodiyan — aur unme se aadhi aapki zubaan par hongi.
