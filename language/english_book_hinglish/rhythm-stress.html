    Chapter 27: Rhythm aur stress¶

    Rhythm aur stress — kaunsa shabd zor se

    Aapki saari aawazein theek ho sakti hain, grammar bhi theek ho sakti hai, aur phir bhi English robotic lag sakti hai. Wajah ek hi hoti hai: hum har shabd ko barabar zor se bolte hain. English ek asamaan bhasha hai — kuch shabd chamakte hain, baaki shabd peeche chale jaate hain, lagbhag gayab. Jo log is ek cheez par kaam kar lete hain, wo turant "fluent" lagne lagte hain — chahe unka vocabulary wahi purana ho.

    Ek line me

    Hindi lagbhag samaan taal wali bhasha hai — har akshar ko lagbhag barabar waqt milta hai. English zor ki taal wali bhasha hai — zor wale hisse lambe aur tez hote hain, baaki hisse dabe aur chhote. Isliye seedha Hindi ki taal me English bolna "flat" lagta hai.

    27.1 Word stress — ek shabd me ek hi hissa chamakta hai¶

    English ke har bade shabd me ek hissa (syllable) zor wala hota hai: wo lamba, zor se, aur thoda oonchi awaaz me bolte hain. Baaki hisse dheele aur chhote. Galat hisse par zor dene se shabd kabhi-kabhi samajh me hi nahi aata.

    • He is a photographer, and this is his photograph. Dono shabd ek line me — zor ki jagah ka farq saaf sunai dega.

    Zor badalne se matlab badal jaata hai¶

    Angrezi me kai shabd noun aur verb dono hote hain, aur farq sirf zor ka hota hai: noun me zor pehle hisse par, verb me doosre hisse par.

    Shabd Noun (naam) Verb (kaam) Suno
    record RE-cord — rikaard, lekha re-CORD — rikaard karna
    present PRE-sent — tohfa pre-SENT — pesh karna
    increase IN-crease — badhotri in-CREASE — badhana
    object OB-ject — vastu ob-JECT — virodh karna
    contract CON-tract — samjhauta con-TRACT — sikudna
    conduct CON-duct — vyavhaar con-DUCT — karwana

    Yaad rakhne ka chhota naksha

    Naam = aage zor. Kaam = peeche zor. Noun pehle, verb baad me — jaise shabd me bhi wahi kram ho.

    Kuch pakke patterns¶

    Ant me Zor kahan Examples Suno
    -tion / -sion usse theek pehle wale hisse par inforMAtion, deciSIon, educAtion
    -ity usse theek pehle aBIlity, opporTUnity, uNIversity
    -ic usse theek pehle ecoNOmic, fanTAStic, speCIfic
    -ate (verb) teesre hisse par peeche se CElebrate, EDUcate, comMUnicate
    -ee aakhir wale hisse par employEE, trainEE, agrEE
    Do hisson wale naam lagbhag hamesha pehle par TAble, WINdow, DOCtor, TEAcher

    Jo zor hum sabse zyada galat lagate hain

    • Galat: de-VE-lop-MENT me do zor
    • Sahi: de-VEL-op-ment — sirf ek zor

    • Kyun: Ek shabd me ek hi zor hota hai. Do jagah zor dene se shabd do shabd jaisa lagta hai.

    • Galat: hos-pi-TAL

    • Sahi: HOS-pi-tal

    • Kyun: Aakhri hissa "tul" ban jaata hai, dabi aawaz me. Hum use poora "TAL" bol dete hain.

    • Galat: in-ter-es-TING

    • Sahi: IN-tres-ting — teen hisse, chaar nahi

    • Kyun: Beech wali "e" lagbhag gayab ho jaati hai. Yahi schwa ka kamaal hai.

    • Galat: com-FOR-TA-BLE (chaar barabar hisse)

    • Sahi: KUMF-tuh-bul
    • Kyun: Angrez log beech ka "or" nigal jaate hain. Teen hisse, pehle par zor.

    27.2 Sentence stress — vaakya me kaunse shabd chamakte hain¶

    Yahi asli raaz hai. Ek poore vaakya me sirf kaam ki baat wale shabd zor se bolte hain. Baaki shabd — jo sirf vaakya jodne ke liye hain — dabi aawaz me, tezi se, lagbhag nigal kar bolte hain.

    Content words — zor se Function words — dabe hue
    Kaun Noun, main verb, adjective, adverb, question words a/an/the, is/am/are/was, can/will/have (helper), of/to/in/at, and/but, I/he/she/it, that/this (jab zor na ho)
    Kyun Inme asli jaankari hai. Inhe kaat do, baat samajh nahi aayegi. Ye sirf dhaancha hain. Inhe kaat do, baat phir bhi samajh aa jaayegi.
    Test Telegram bhejna ho to kaunse shabd rakhoge? Wahi content words hain.
    • I want to buy a ticket for the morning train. Paanch beat. Pehle sirf paanch bade shabd thaap ki tarah bolo, phir poora vaakya.
    • She was waiting for me at the bus stop. Beat: WAIT-ing · BUS · STOP. "was, for, at, the" sab dabe hue.
    • Can you send me the file before the meeting? Beat: SEND · FILE · be-FORE · MEET-ing. "Can you" lagbhag "kuhnyuh" jaisa sunai deta hai.

    27.3 Dabe shabd — asal me kaise sunai dete hain¶

    Jab function words par zor nahi hota, to unki vowel schwa ban jaati hai aur wo bilkul chhote ho jaate hain. Ye "aalsi" English nahi hai — yahi sahi English hai.

    Likha hai Bola jaata hai Vaakya me Suno
    to tuh I want tuh go.
    of uhv a cup uhv tea
    and un / n bread n butter
    for fuh waiting fuh you
    can kuhn I kuhn do it.
    at uht look uht this
    are uh What uh you doing?
    have (helper) uhv I uhv finished.

    Ek zaroori apwaad

    Jab function word par khaas zor dena ho, to wo poora bolte hain: "I said I can do it, not I can't." Matlab dabana niyam hai, par zor dene ki chhoot hamesha hai.

    27.4 Ek hi vaakya, paanch matlab — sirf zor badal kar¶

    Ye drill aapke kaan ko hamesha ke liye khol degi. Ek hi vaakya hai — "I didn't say she stole my bag." Zor jahan padega, matlab wahin badal jaayega.

    Zor is shabd par Matlab Suno
    I didn't say she stole my bag. Maine nahi kaha — kisi aur ne kaha hoga.
    I didn't say she stole my bag. Maine ye baat kahi hi nahi — aap galat samjhe.
    I didn't say she stole my bag. Chori kisi aur ne ki — usne nahi.
    I didn't say she stole my bag. Usne li thi, par chori nahi ki — udhaar liya hoga.
    I didn't say she stole my bag. Bag kisi aur ka tha, mera nahi.

    Isse kaise practise karein

    Paanch baar bolo, har baar alag shabd par zor. Zor dene ka matlab hai: wo shabd thoda lamba, thoda zor se, aur thodi oonchi aawaz me. Jab aap paanchon farq khud sun paayein — samajh lo kaan taiyaar ho gaya.

    27.5 Jude hue shabd — English "chipakti" hai¶

    Native log shabdon ke beech rukte nahi. Ek shabd ka ant agle shabd ke shuru se chipak jaata hai. Isi liye sunte waqt lagta hai "bahut tez bol rahe hain" — asal me tez nahi, juda hua hai.

    Likha hua Sunai deta hai Suno
    What is your name? Wot-sure-naim
    Do you want to go? Juh-wanna-go
    I am going to call him. Aim-gonna-call-im
    Did you eat? Dijoo-eet
    Can I help you? Kun-ai-help-yoo
    Let me know. Lem-me-no

    Dhyan rahe

    Ye sunne ke liye seekhna hai, bolne ke liye zaroori nahi. Aap poora "What is your name?" bolein — bilkul theek hai, saaf hai, sahi hai. Par jab koi "Wot-sure-naim" bole, to aap atkein nahi — yahi faayda hai.

    27.6 Aawaz ka chadhna-utarna (intonation)¶

    Kis tarah ka vaakya Aawaz Example Suno
    Saadharan baat Aakhir me neeche ↘ I live in Pune.
    Haan/nahi wala sawaal Aakhir me oopar ↗ Do you live in Pune?
    WH- sawaal Aakhir me neeche ↘ Where do you live?
    List ginna Beech me oopar ↗, aakhri par neeche ↘ I need pens, books and a bag.
    Politely poochhna Halka oopar ↗ — zyada seedha sakht lagta hai Could you help me, please?

    Ek aadat jo sakht lagti hai

    • Galat: "Give me the file." — sapaat, neeche girti aawaz
    • Sahi: "Could you give me the file?" — halki oopar jaati aawaz
    • Kyun: Shabd hi nahi, aawaz ka rukh bhi tehzeeb banata hai. Girti aawaz me poochha gaya sawaal order jaisa lagta hai.

    27.7 Roz ki taal wali drill¶

    Ye drill 2 minute ki hai. Har vaakya me pehle sirf bade shabd taali bajate hue bolo, phir poora vaakya bhar do.

    • I NEED to FINish this WORK by TOmorrow. Chaar thaap. "to, this, by" bilkul dabe hue.
    • Could you TELL me where the STAtion IS? Teen thaap. Aakhir me aawaz halki oopar — ye sawaal hai.
    • The MEEting has been POSTponed to NEXT WEEK. Chaar thaap. "has been" ko nigal jao — "uhz-bin".
    • I'm SORry, I didn't CATCH that LAST part. Teen thaap. Ye line roz kaam aati hai — "samajh nahi aaya" ka sabse natural roop.

    27.8 Practice — Rhythm aur stress¶

    Exercise 1

    Hindi aur English ki taal me buniyadi farq kya hai?

    • Hindi lagbhag samaan taal wali hai, English zor ki taal wali
    • English me har akshar barabar hota hai
    • Dono bilkul ek jaisi hain
    • Hindi me zor hota hai, English me nahi

    Hint: Kis bhasha me har akshar ko barabar waqt milta hai?

    Jawaab

    Hindi lagbhag samaan taal wali hai, English zor ki taal wali

    Isi liye Hindi ki taal me boli gayi English sapaat lagti hai. English me kuch shabd chamakte hain, baaki dab jaate hain.

    Exercise 2

    'photographer' me zor kis hisse par hai?

    • TOG (doosra hissa)
    • PHO (pehla hissa)
    • ra (teesra hissa)
    • pher (chautha hissa)
    Jawaab

    TOG (doosra hissa)

    pho-TOG-ra-pher. Dhyan do: 'photograph' me zor PHO par, par roop badalne se zor jagah badal deta hai.

    Exercise 3

    Noun aur verb ka stress niyam kya hai? (record, present, increase)

    • Noun me pehle hisse par, verb me doosre hisse par
    • Noun me doosre hisse par, verb me pehle par
    • Dono me pehle hisse par
    • Koi niyam nahi hai

    Hint: Naam pehle, kaam baad me.

    Jawaab

    Noun me pehle hisse par, verb me doosre hisse par

    RE-cord (cheez) / re-CORD (karna). PRE-sent (tohfa) / pre-SENT (pesh karna).

    Exercise 4

    Vaakya me kaunse shabd zor se bolte hain?

    • Content words — noun, main verb, adjective, adverb
    • Articles aur prepositions
    • Sirf pehla aur aakhri shabd
    • Saare shabd barabar
    Jawaab

    Content words — noun, main verb, adjective, adverb

    Jinme asli jaankari hai wo chamakte hain. a/the/of/to/is jaise shabd sirf dhaancha hain — wo dab jaate hain.

    Exercise 5

    'information' me zor kahan hai?

    • MA — infor-MA-tion
    • IN — IN-formation
    • for — in-FOR-mation
    • tion — informa-TION

    Hint: -tion se theek pehle wala hissa.

    Jawaab

    MA — infor-MA-tion

    -tion aur -sion wale shabdon me zor hamesha usse theek pehle wale hisse par: deci-SI-on, educ-A-tion.

    Exercise 6

    'I didn't say SHE stole my bag' ka matlab kya hai?

    • Chori kisi aur ne ki, usne nahi
    • Maine kuch nahi kaha
    • Bag mera nahi tha
    • Usne chori nahi, udhaar liya
    Jawaab

    Chori kisi aur ne ki, usne nahi

    Zor 'she' par hai, isliye vaakya usko hata kar kisi aur par ishaara karta hai. Zor badlo, matlab badal jaata hai.

    Exercise 7

    Ek shabd me kitne zor wale hisse hote hain? ___

    Hint: Sankhya likho.

    Jawaab

    ek / 1 / one

    Ek shabd = ek chamakta hissa. Do jagah zor dena (de-VE-lop-MENT) sabse aam galti hai.

    Exercise 8

    Vaakya me 'to' par zor nahi hota — wo bol kar ___ jaisa sunai deta hai.

    Hint: Schwa wali dheeli aawaz.

    Jawaab

    tuh / tu / ta

    I want tuh go. Bina zor wale function words ki vowel schwa ban jaati hai.

    Exercise 9

    Is vaakya ke kis shabd par zor NAHI hona chahiye? Us par tap karo.

    I want to buy a ticket.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: a.

    Sahi roop: I WANT to BUY a TICKet — 'a' bilkul dabi hui 'uh' hai.

    Article 'a' function word hai — sabse dabne wala shabd. 'ay' nahi, 'uh'.

    Exercise 10

    Vaakya ke prakaar ko uski aawaz ke rukh se jodo.

    A B
    I live in Pune. WH- sawaal, neeche
    Do you live in Pune? polite, halka oopar
    Where do you live? aakhir me neeche
    Could you help me? aakhir me oopar
    Jawaab

    I live in Pune. — aakhir me neeche; Do you live in Pune? — aakhir me oopar; Where do you live? — WH- sawaal, neeche; Could you help me? — polite, halka oopar

    Haan/nahi wale sawaal oopar jaate hain; baat aur WH- sawaal neeche. Girti aawaz me sawaal order jaisa lagta hai.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me vaakya banao, phir thaap ke saath bolo.

    Shabd: to / need / I / by / finish / work / tomorrow / this

    Jawaab

    I need to finish this work by tomorrow

    Thaap: NEED · FIN-ish · WORK · TO-morrow. Beech ke 'to, this, by' dabe hue.

    Exercise 12

    Paanch thaap wala vaakya. Pehle sirf bade shabd taali ke saath, phir poora.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I want to buy a ticket for the morning train

    WANT · BUY · TICK-et · MOR-ning · TRAIN. 'to, a, for, the' lagbhag gayab.

    Exercise 13

    Noun-verb stress. Dono roop ek saath bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: This is a record. Please record it.

    RE-cord (noun) phir re-CORD (verb). Farq saaf sunai dena chahiye.

    Exercise 14

    -tion wala zor. Sahi hisse par chamkao.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I need more information about the decision

    infor-MA-tion, deci-SI-on. Dono me zor -tion/-sion se theek pehle.

    Exercise 15

    Polite sawaal — aakhir me aawaz halki oopar le jao.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could you tell me where the station is

    TELL · STA-tion · IS par thaap. Aawaz girne se ye order jaisa lagega, sawaal jaisa nahi.

    Exercise 16

    Ab zor badal kar bolo — 'she' par zor dena hai.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I didn't say she stole my bag

    'she' ko lamba, zor se, thoda oonchi aawaz me. Baaki shabd dabe rakho — tab farq sunai dega.

    Exercise 17

    Roz kaam aane wali line. Teen thaap ke saath.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I'm sorry I didn't catch that last part

    SOR-ry · CATCH · LAST. 'I didn't' ko tezi se nigal jao — aidunt.

    Aage kya karein

    Ek hafte tak ek hi kaam karo: koi bhi English sunte waqt sirf ye dhyan do ki kaunse shabd zor se aa rahe hain. Matlab samajhne ki koshish bhi mat karo — sirf taal suno. Saat din me aapka kaan badal jaayega.
