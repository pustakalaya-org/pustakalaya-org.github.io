    Chapter 36: Ek shabd se das shabd¶

    Ek shabd se das shabd — prefix aur suffix ka jaadu

    Vocabulary badhaane ka sabse sasta tareeka naye shabd ratna nahi hai — balki jo shabd aap pehle se jaante ho, unke rishtedaar pehchaanna hai. happy jaante ho? To aap unhappy, happiness, happily, unhappiness bhi jaante ho — bas pata nahi tha. Shuru me jude tukde (prefix) matlab badalte hain, aur ant me jude tukde (suffix) shabd ka kaam badalte hain. Is chapter ke baad har naya lamba shabd aapko tukdon me dikhega.

    36.1 Ek jad, poora parivaar — word family¶

    Ek shabd ke parivaar ko saath seekhne se do faayde hain: chaar shabd ek ki mehnat me, aur vaakya me sahi roop chunna aasaan. "He spoke happy" ya "Her happy was clear" jaisi galtiyan yahin se rukti hain.

    • She looks happy. She smiled happily. Her happiness was clear. Ek hi jad, teen kaam: kaisi dikhti hai (adjective), kaise muskuraayi (adverb), kya dikha (noun).

    Do tarah ke tukde

    Prefix (shuru me) shabd ka matlab badalta hai: happy → unhappy. Suffix (ant me) shabd ka kaam badalta hai: happy (adj) → happiness (noun) → happily (adverb).

    36.2 Prefix — shuru me jodo, matlab badlo¶

    Saat prefix itne aam hain ki roz ki English ke saikdon shabd inhi se bante hain. Inka matlab yaad kar liya, to anjaan shabd ka andaaza bhi lag jaata hai — misread = galat padhna.

    Prefix Matlab Examples
    un- nahi / ulta unhappy · unfair · unlock
    dis- nahi / ulta dislike · disagree · disconnect
    im- / in- nahi impossible · incorrect · invisible
    re- dobara / wapas redo · rewrite · return
    mis- galat tareeke se misunderstand · misspell · mislead
    pre- pehle preview · prepaid · preheat
    over- zaroorat se zyada overeat · overload · overconfident
    • I think you misunderstood me. Let me rephrase that. Aapne mujhe galat samjha. Main dobara, alag shabdon me kehta hoon.
    • Don't overcook the rice, and please preheat the oven. Chawal zyada mat pakaana, aur oven pehle se garam kar lo.

    Prefix spelling nahi chheda karta

    Prefix bas aage jud jaata hai — jad ki spelling wahi rehti hai. Isliye kabhi-kabhi do akshar saath aa jaate hain: un + necessary = unnecessary, mis + spell = misspell, dis + satisfied = dissatisfied.

    36.3 Kaunsa "not" — un, dis, in, im, il, ir¶

    Ulta matlab banane ke chhah prefix hain, aur yahi sabse zyada gadbad karte hain — "unpossible", "unregular". Achhi khabar: aadhe ka pakka niyam hai, aur wo niyam sirf jad ka pehla akshar dekhta hai.

    Pehle akshar ka niyam

    p, m, b → im- (impossible) · l → il- (illegal) · r → ir- (irregular). Baaki un- / dis- / in- ka niyam nahi — inhe shabd ke saath jodi me yaad karo. Shak ho to dictionary — "unpossible" jaisa shabd hota hi nahi.

    36.4 Noun banane wale suffix — -tion, -ment, -ness¶

    Formal English, emails aur reports noun se bhari hoti hain — "We decided" ki jagah "We made a decision". Teen suffix se zyaadatar noun ban jaate hain: kaam ka naam (-tion, -ment) aur gun ka naam (-ness).

    Shabd → noun Suffix Example
    inform → information -tion Thanks for the information.
    educate → education -tion Education changes lives.
    decide → decision -sion It was a hard decision.
    develop → development -ment The city needs development.
    agree → agreement -ment We signed an agreement.
    pay → payment -ment The payment is due today.
    kind → kindness -ness Thank you for your kindness.
    happy → happiness -ness Money can't buy happiness.

    Kaam ka naam, gun ka naam

    Verb + -tion / -ment = noun (inform → information, agree → agreement). Adjective + -ness = noun (kind → kindness). Ant ka y, -ness se pehle i ban jaata hai: happy → happiness. Aur ant ka chup "e" aksar gir jaata hai: educate → education, decide → decision.

    36.5 Adjective aur adverb — -ful, -less, -able, -ly¶

    Ye chaar suffix description ki jaan hain. Ek hi noun se do ulte adjective ban jaate hain (-ful aur -less), aur unpar -ly lagao to wo verb ko describe karne lagte hain.

    Suffix Matlab Examples
    -ful se bhara careful · useful · hopeful
    -less ke bina careless · useless · hopeless
    -able kiya ja sakta hai readable · washable · comfortable
    -ly adjective → adverb quickly · carefully · happily

    Ek l, do l

    -ful me hamesha ek hi l: careful, useful, beautiful — "carefull" nahi. Par uspar -ly lagao to do l aa jaate hain: careful + ly = carefully. Dhyaan do: friendly, lovely, lonely -ly par khatam hote hain par adjective hain — "a friendly person".

    36.6 -ize aur kuch purani jadein¶

    -ize noun ya adjective ko kaam (verb) bana deta hai — office English me ye har jagah hai. Aur bahut se lambe shabd purani Latin jadon se bane hain; ek jad pehchaan li, to poora guchha samajh aata hai.

    Shabd + -ize = verb Example
    modern modernize We need to modernize the office.
    real realize I didn't realize it was so late.
    summary summarize Can you summarize the report?
    priority prioritize Let's prioritize the urgent tasks.
    apology apologize I apologize for the delay.

    British spelling -ise (realise, apologise) bhi bilkul sahi hai, aur India me dono chalte hain. Bas ek document me ek hi rakho.

    Jad Matlab Shabd
    port le jaana transport · export · portable
    dict bolna predict · dictionary · contradict
    spect dekhna, dhyaan se inspect · respect · spectator
    vis / vid dekh paana visible · vision · video
    scrib / script likhna describe · script · subscribe

    Lamba shabd? Tod ke padho

    Prefix + jad + suffix: in-spect-ion = andar dekhne ka kaam (jaanch); pre-dict-able = pehle se bola ja sake. Anjaan shabd ko tukdon me todo — aadha matlab khud nikal aayega.

    36.7 Chhah parivaar — ek table me¶

    Ye wo parivaar hain jinke roop sabse zyada galat lagte hain. Har line ko left se right zor se padho — ek baar me chaar shabd pakke ho jaayenge.

    Verb Noun Adjective Adverb
    decide decision decisive decisively
    succeed success successful successfully
    create creation creative creatively
    care care careful carefully
    differ difference different differently
    attract attraction attractive attractively
    • She succeeded because she worked carefully. Her success was no accident. Teen parivaar, teen kaam — verb ke saath adverb, akela noun.

    Notebook ki aadat

    Naya shabd likho to uske saath uska poora parivaar bhi likho — verb, noun, adjective, adverb. Jo khaana khaali rahe (jaise "differ" ka koi aam adverb), wo bhi ek jaankari hai.

    36.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye galtiyan isliye hoti hain ki hum jad sahi chunte hain par roop galat — noun ki jagah verb, adverb ki jagah adjective, ya galat "not" wala prefix.

    Word formation ki aam galtiyan

    • Galat: This is unpossible.
    • Sahi: This is impossible.

    • Kyun: "possible" p se shuru hota hai → im-. Isi tarah unregular → irregular, unlegal → illegal.

    • Galat: Her behave in the meeting was rude.

    • Sahi: Her behaviour in the meeting was rude.

    • Kyun: "Her ___ was" ke beech noun chahiye. behave = verb, behaviour (US: behavior) = noun.

    • Galat: He answered very confident.

    • Sahi: He answered very confidently.

    • Kyun: Verb (answered) ko describe karne ke liye adverb: -ly. "He is confident" me adjective theek hai.

    • Galat: I am very boring in this class.

    • Sahi: I am very bored in this class.

    • Kyun: -ed = aapki feeling (bored, tired, interested). -ing = jo feeling deta hai (a boring class). "I am boring" = main bore karta hoon!

    • Galat: Can you repeat it again?

    • Sahi: Can you repeat it? / Can you say it again?
    • Kyun: re- ka matlab hi "dobara" hai — "repeat again" = dobara dobara. Isi tarah "revert back" me "back" faltu hai.

    36.9 Practice — Word formation¶

    Exercise 1

    'possible' ka ulta kya hai?

    • impossible
    • unpossible
    • dispossible
    • nonpossible
    Jawaab

    impossible

    Jad 'p' se shuru hoti hai, isliye im-. Yaad rakho: p, m, b → im-.

    Exercise 2

    'regular' ka ulta kya hai?

    • irregular
    • unregular
    • disregular
    • misregular
    Jawaab

    irregular

    Jad 'r' se shuru → ir-. Isi tarah irrelevant, irresponsible.

    Exercise 3

    In me se adverb kaunsa hai?

    • happily
    • happiness
    • unhappy
    • happy
    Jawaab

    happily

    -ly adjective ko adverb bana deta hai: happy → happily. happiness noun hai, unhappy adjective.

    Exercise 4

    Please drive ___. The road is wet. (care)

    Hint: Verb 'drive' ko describe karna hai — adverb chahiye.

    Jawaab

    carefully

    care → careful (adjective) → carefully (adverb). Do l, kyonki careful + ly.

    Exercise 5

    We need to make a ___ by Friday. (decide)

    Hint: 'a' ke baad noun.

    Jawaab

    decision

    decide → decision. Ant ka 'de' '-sion' ban jaata hai. 'make a decision' pakki jodi hai.

    Exercise 6

    Thank you for your ___. (kind)

    Hint: 'your' ke baad noun — gun ka naam.

    Jawaab

    kindness

    Adjective + -ness = noun: kind → kindness, dark → darkness.

    Exercise 7

    Is vaakya me ek shabd ka roop galat hai — us par tap karo.

    Her behave in the meeting was very rude.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: behave.

    Sahi roop: Her behaviour in the meeting was very rude.

    'Her ___ was' me noun chahiye — behave verb hai, behaviour noun.

    Exercise 8

    Galat prefix wala shabd dhoondo.

    Finishing this by tonight is unpossible.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: unpossible.

    Sahi roop: Finishing this by tonight is impossible.

    'possible' p se shuru → im-possible. 'unpossible' shabd hi nahi hai.

    Exercise 9

    Prefix ko uske matlab se jodo.

    A B
    re- pehle
    mis- zaroorat se zyada
    pre- dobara
    over- galat tareeke se
    Jawaab

    re- — dobara; mis- — galat tareeke se; pre- — pehle; over- — zaroorat se zyada

    rewrite = dobara likhna, misread = galat padhna, preview = pehle dekhna, overeat = zyada khaana.

    Exercise 10

    Jad ko uske matlab se jodo.

    A B
    port dekhna
    dict likhna
    spect le jaana
    scrib bolna
    Jawaab

    port — le jaana; dict — bolna; spect — dekhna; scrib — likhna

    transport (le jaana), predict (pehle bolna), inspect (andar dekhna), describe (likh ke batana).

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me lagao.

    Shabd: all / confidently / She / questions / the / answered

    Jawaab

    She answered all the questions confidently

    Adverb (confidently) verb ke kaam ko describe karta hai, aur aksar vaakya ke ant me aata hai.

    Exercise 12

    Ye vaakya bolo — har shabd ka poora suffix saaf sunao.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: It was a careful and creative decision

    'careful' ka -ful halka bolo (kair-fl), aur 'decision' me zor beech par: di-SI-zhn.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj ki akhbaar ya kisi bhi article se paanch lambe shabd chuno aur unhe prefix + jad + suffix me todo. Phir har ek ka parivaar likho. Das minute, aur vocabulary chupchaap badh jaayegi.
