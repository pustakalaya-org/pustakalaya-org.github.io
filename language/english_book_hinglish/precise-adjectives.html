    Chapter 45: Sahi adjective chuno¶

    Sahi adjective chuno — "very" ko chhutti do

    Very English ka sabse mehnati aur sabse bekaar shabd hai. Wo kuch nahi batata — bas aawaz oonchi kar deta hai. Very hot sunke pata nahi chalta ki garmi 43 degree hai ya 33. Par sweltering sunte hi pasina mehsoos hota hai. Is chapter ka maqsad ek hi hai: jahan aap very + adjective bolte ho, wahan ek sahi shabd rakhna seekho — aur jaan lo ki wo shabd kahan fit hai aur kahan nahi.

    Ek line me

    very + adjective ka matlab hai ki sahi shabd abhi nahi mila. Dhoondh lo, aur "very" khud hat jaayega. Ek theek shabd, do dheele shabdon se hamesha bhaari padta hai.

    45.1 Paanch asli swaps¶

    very upset → distraught¶

    Distraught ka matlab hai itna dukhi ya ghabraya hua ki insaan sambhal nahi raha — haath kaanp rahe hain, baat poori nahi ho rahi. Ye bahut bhaari shabd hai. Train chhoot gayi to aap annoyed ho, upset ho — distraught nahi. Bachcha kho jaaye, tab distraught.

    • She was distraught when she heard about the accident. Haadse ki khabar sunkar wo bilkul toot gayi thi.

    Casual chat me ajeeb

    Dost ko WhatsApp par "I'm distraught, the cafe was closed" likhna mazaak lagega. Wahan gutted (casual, nirash), annoyed ya seedha bummed chalega. Bada shabd chhoti baat par lagane se wo baat majaak ban jaati hai.

    very hot → sweltering¶

    Sweltering us garmi ke liye hai jo chipchipi, dum ghotne wali ho — Mumbai ka May, ya band kamre ki bheed. Dhyan rakho: ye sirf mausam aur jagah ke liye hai. Chai ke liye kabhi nahi — chai scalding ya piping hot hoti hai.

    • It was sweltering on the platform — no fan, no breeze. Platform par dum ghutne wali garmi thi — na pankha, na hawa.

    Garmi ke teen alag shabd

    sweltering = chipchipi garmi (mausam) · scorching = jhulsa dene wali dhoop · scalding = jalaane wala garam paani ya chai.

    very rude → boorish¶

    Boorish ka matlab hai ganwaar, badtameez, doosron ka khayal hi nahi — tez bolna, beech me kaatna, line todna. Ye shabd thoda kitaabi hai, aur bolte waqt kam sunai deta hai. Likhai me, review me, ya kisi ki shikayat likhte waqt ye bahut strong hai. Aam baat-cheet me obnoxious ya incredibly rude zyada natural lagta hai.

    • His boorish behaviour at the dinner embarrassed everyone. Dinner par uski ganwaar harkaton ne sabko sharminda kar diya.

    very big → mammoth¶

    Mammoth (us purane barfani haathi se bana shabd) ka matlab hai bahut bada, sambhalne me mushkil. Ye zyada tar kaam, project, task, effort ke saath aata hai — size se zyada paimane ki baat karta hai.

    • Shifting the whole office in one weekend was a mammoth task. Ek weekend me poora office shift karna ek bahut bada kaam tha.

    'Bada' ke chaar roop

    mammoth = bada kaam ya paimana · colossal = dekhne me vishaal (building, statue) · vast = door tak faila (maidan, samudra, farq) · hefty = bhaari (bill, fine, kitaab).

    very hungry → ravenous¶

    Ravenous matlab bhookh se bechain, jo kuch mile kha jaane wali haalat. Achhi baat ye hai ki ye shabd bhaari hone ke baawjood casual me bhi chalta hai — mazaak aur exaggeration ke saath. Starving isi ka zyada aam roop hai.

    • We skipped lunch, so by eight we were ravenous. Humne lunch chhoda tha, to aath baje tak bhookh se haal kharab tha.

    45.2 Teevrata ki seedhi — kahan khade ho¶

    Adjectives ek seedhi par chadhte hain. Galti tab hoti hai jab hum sabse upar wala shabd uthakar sabse neeche wali baat par chipka dete hain. Neeche ki tasveer dhyan se dekho — isi se overcorrection rukti hai.

    45.3 Badi swap table¶

    Teesra column phir se sabse zaroori hai. Shabd yaad karna aasaan hai; uski jagah yaad rakhna asli hunar hai.

    very + … Ek shabd Kab theek, kab nahi
    very upset distraught Sirf gehre sadme me. Chhoti nirasha par mazaak lagta hai.
    very hot (mausam) sweltering Garmi aur jagah ke liye. Khaane ya chai ke liye bilkul nahi.
    very rude boorish Likhai aur shikayat me strong. Bolne me obnoxious natural.
    very big (kaam) mammoth Task, project, effort. Kamre ya kitaab ke liye large.
    very hungry ravenous Casual me bhi chalta hai, mazaak ke saath bhi.
    very tired exhausted Har jagah safe — office, doctor, dost.
    very cold freezing Bahut aam aur bilkul natural. Rozmarra ki pehli pasand.
    very happy thrilled Khushkhabri par. Email me bhi achha: "I'm thrilled to…"
    very scared terrified Har jagah chalta hai. Petrified isse bhi tez.
    very surprised astonished Thoda formal. Casual me stunned.
    very dirty filthy Rozmarra. Kapde, kamra, haath — sab chalta hai.
    very tasty delicious Har jagah. Mouth-watering dekhne se pehle ke liye.
    very boring tedious Kaam aur process ke liye. Film ke liye dull.
    very important crucial Office aur likhai dono me strong aur saaf.
    very interested keen Interview me sona: "I'm keen to learn."
    very expensive exorbitant Shikayat ya report me. Casual me pricey.
    very poor (quality) shoddy Kaam aur saamaan ke liye. Insaan ke liye nahi.
    very busy (jagah) bustling Bazaar, station, shehar. Insaan ke liye swamped.

    45.4 Chaudah aur ek-shabd swaps¶

    Ye list do hisson me hai. Pehle wale shabd aap aaj se bol sakte ho. Doosre wale kitaabi ya rare hain — padhte waqt pehchaano, likhai me kabhi-kabhi lagao, par baat-cheet me sambhal kar. Har line par 🔊 dabao.

    Roz kaam aane wale¶

    very + … → ek shabd Example Matlab Kab use karein
    very wet → drenched We got drenched walking back from the station. Poori tarah bheeg jaana, nichodne laayak. Har jagah, bahut natural. Soaked bhi isi jaisa.
    very angry → incensed Residents were incensed by the sudden fare hike. Anyaay par bhadka hua gussa. News aur likhai me strong. Bolne me furious zyada aam.
    very happy → jubilant The jubilant crowd danced outside the stadium. Jeet ya safalta par jhoom uthne wali khushi. Bheed, team, jashn ke liye. Akele shaant khushi ke liye delighted.
    very scared → terror-stricken The terror-stricken passengers ran for the exits. Dehshat se sun, dar ke maare jaam. Bahut bhaari — haadsa, aag, hamla. Bolne me terrified kaafi hai.
    very friendly → affable Our new manager is affable and easy to talk to. Milansaar, jisse baat karna aasaan ho. Taareef me — reference letter, review. Thoda formal; dost ke liye really friendly.
    very empty → desolate After midnight the highway looked desolate. Sunsaan, veeran — khaali aur udaas. Jagah ke liye (sadak, gaon, maidan). Isme udaasi hai — khaali dabba desolate nahi hota.
    elegant → sophisticated She has a sophisticated taste in music. Nafees, duniya ki samajh rakhne wala; ya (machine) bahut advanced. Har jagah safe. Elegant = sundar saadgi; sophisticated = samajh aur parishkaar.
    talented → consummate He is a consummate professional — always prepared. Apne kaam me poora maahir, nipun. Noun se pehle hi: a consummate professional / musician. "He is consummate" akela nahi bolte.

    Kitaabi ya rare — pehchaano, sambhal kar bolo¶

    very + … → ek shabd Example Matlab Kab use karein
    very tired → enervated The long heatwave left everyone enervated. Andar se taaqat nichod li gayi ho, nidhaal. Literary aur rare. Dhoka mat khao — iska matlab energized nahi, ulta hai. Bolne me drained.
    very wise → sagacious Her sagacious advice saved the company. Doorandesh, gehri samajh wala. Bahut kitaabi — bolne me ajeeb lagega. Wise ya shrewd zyada natural.
    very rich → opulent They stayed in an opulent palace hotel in Udaipur. Shaahi, daulat aur shaan-o-shaukat se bhara. Cheez aur lifestyle ke liye (hotel, shaadi, interior). Insaan ke liye wealthy ya affluent.
    very funny → jocular He kept the meeting light with his jocular remarks. Mazaakiya, halke-phulke andaaz wala. Formal/literary — insaan ya lehja ke liye. Joke ya film bahut funny ho to hilarious.
    very confused → nonplussed I was nonplussed when he suddenly resigned. Itna hairaan-pareshaan ki samajh na aaye kya kahein. Asli matlab: confused, stumped. Par American bolchaal me log ise ulta "unbothered" bhi bolte hain — isliye ghalatfehmi ho sakti hai. Saaf kehna ho to baffled.
    very noisy → cacophonous The cacophonous traffic made it hard to think. Kaan phodne wala, besura shor. Likhai ka shabd, bolne me rare. Rozmarra me deafening ya really loud.

    Teen jaal is list me

    enervated ≠ energized — matlab thaka hua, josh se bhara nahi · opulent cheezon ke liye, insaan wealthy / affluent hota hai · jocular insaan ka andaaz hai — chutkula hilarious hota hai. Rare shabd galat matlab me bolna, simple shabd bolne se kahin zyada bura lagta hai.

    45.5 Teen rules jo bachate hain¶

    a) 'very' + sabse tez shabd kabhi nahi

    Exhausted, freezing, terrified, delicious — ye pehle se sabse upar hain. Inke aage "very" lagana jaise "bilkul poora pura" bolna.
    ✗ very exhausted · ✓ utterly exhausted ya sirf exhausted. Agar zor dena hi hai to 'absolutely', 'utterly', 'completely' lagao.

    b) Insaan aur cheez ke shabd alag hote hain

    Jagah bustling hoti hai, insaan swamped. Kaam shoddy hota hai, insaan careless. Shabd uthane se pehle dekho ki wo kis par lagta hai.

    c) Ek vaakya me ek hi tez shabd

    ✗ It was a sweltering, mammoth, tedious day. — teen tez shabd ek saath, aur asar zero.
    ✓ It was a sweltering day, and the work was tedious.

    45.6 Galtiyan jo asal me hoti hain¶

    Dhyan do

    • Galat: The tea was sweltering, I burnt my tongue.
    • Sahi: The tea was scalding, I burnt my tongue.

    • Kyun: Sweltering sirf mausam aur jagah ke liye. Jalaane wale garam liquid ke liye scalding.

    • Galat: I was distraught because the shop was closed.

    • Sahi: I was annoyed because the shop was closed.

    • Kyun: Distraught gehre sadme ke liye hai. Chhoti baat par lagane se aap over-dramatic lagte ho.

    • Galat: He is a very exhausted man today.

    • Sahi: He is exhausted today.

    • Kyun: Exhausted pehle se sabse upar hai — "very" bekaar hai. Zor chahiye to "utterly exhausted".

    • Galat: The market was very swamped on Sunday.

    • Sahi: The market was bustling on Sunday.

    • Kyun: Insaan swamped hota hai (kaam me dabaa hua), jagah bustling hoti hai (bheed se bhari).

    • Galat: My room is mammoth dirty.

    • Sahi: My room is filthy.

    • Kyun: Mammoth adverb nahi hai — wo "very" ki jagah nahi le sakta. Gandagi ka ek shabd: filthy.

    • Galat: She was very keen interested in the role.

    • Sahi: She was keen to take on the role.
    • Kyun: Keen ka matlab khud "bahut interested" hai — dono ek saath nahi aate.

    45.7 Ek paragraph, do roop¶

    Pehle — paanch 'very'

    It was a very hot day. The road was very dirty, the auto driver was very rude, and by the time I reached the office I was very tired. The meeting was very boring, but the news at the end made me very happy.

    Baad me — ek bhi 'very' nahi

    It was a sweltering day. The road was filthy, the auto driver was obnoxious, and by the time I reached the office I was exhausted. The meeting was tedious, but the news at the end left me thrilled.

    45.8 Practice — Precise adjectives¶

    Exercise 1

    Mumbai ka May — chipchipi, dum ghotne wali garmi. Kaunsa shabd?

    • sweltering
    • scalding
    • piping hot
    • warm

    Hint: Mausam ke liye khaas shabd.

    Jawaab

    sweltering

    Sweltering = chipchipi, bhaari garmi, sirf mausam aur jagah ke liye. Scalding garam liquid ke liye hai.

    Exercise 2

    Kaunsa vaakya galat hai?

    • I was distraught because my pen stopped working.
    • She was distraught after the accident.
    • He was distraught when his dog went missing.
    • They were distraught at the news.
    Jawaab

    I was distraught because my pen stopped working.

    Distraught gehre sadme ke liye hai. Pen band hone par bolna over-dramatic — wahan 'annoyed' sahi hai.

    Exercise 3

    Ek hi weekend me poora office shift karna — kaisa kaam tha?

    • a mammoth task
    • a colossal task
    • a hefty task
    • a vast task
    Jawaab

    a mammoth task

    Mammoth kaam aur paimane ke liye hai. Colossal dekhne me vishaal cheez, hefty bhaari bill ya fine, vast door tak faila maidan.

    Exercise 4

    'Very exhausted' galat kyun hai?

    • Exhausted pehle se sabse tez shabd hai
    • Exhausted adjective nahi hai
    • Exhausted sirf likhai me chalta hai
    • 'Very' ke baad -ed shabd nahi aate

    Hint: Shabd kitna tez pehle se hai?

    Jawaab

    Exhausted pehle se sabse tez shabd hai

    Jo shabd apne aap me 'sabse upar' hai, uske aage 'very' bekaar hai. Zor chahiye to 'utterly' ya 'absolutely' lagao.

    Exercise 5

    We skipped lunch, so by eight we were ___.

    Hint: very hungry ka ek shabd.

    Jawaab

    ravenous / starving

    Ravenous = bhookh se bechain. Ye bhaari shabd hone ke baawjood casual baat me bhi chalta hai.

    Exercise 6

    The Sunday market was ___ with shoppers.

    Hint: Jagah ke liye shabd, insaan ke liye nahi.

    Jawaab

    bustling

    Jagah bustling hoti hai (bheed se bhari), insaan swamped hota hai (kaam me dabaa hua).

    Exercise 7

    Ek shabd galat jagah par hai — use dhoondo.

    Careful, the soup is sweltering hot.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: sweltering.

    Sahi roop: Careful, the soup is scalding hot.

    Sweltering sirf mausam aur jagah ke liye. Jalaane wale garam khaane ya drink ke liye scalding.

    Exercise 8

    Ek shabd bekaar hai — us par tap karo.

    I am very freezing in this room.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: very.

    Sahi roop: I am freezing in this room.

    Freezing pehle se sabse tez hai, isliye 'very' ki zaroorat nahi.

    Exercise 9

    Gun ko uske sabse tez shabd se jodo.

    A B
    very hungry distraught
    very rude mammoth
    very upset ravenous
    very big a task boorish
    Jawaab

    very hungry — ravenous; very rude — boorish; very upset — distraught; very big a task — mammoth

    Yaad rakho: ye sab seedhi ke sabse upar wale shabd hain — chhoti baat par inhe mat lagao.

    Exercise 10

    Shabd ko uske sahi istemaal se jodo.

    A B
    bustling kaam — ghatiya quality
    swamped daam — behad mehnga
    shoddy jagah — bheed bhari
    exorbitant insaan — kaam me dabaa
    Jawaab

    bustling — jagah — bheed bhari; swamped — insaan — kaam me dabaa; shoddy — kaam — ghatiya quality; exorbitant — daam — behad mehnga

    Har tez adjective kisi ek kism ki cheez par lagta hai. Galat cheez par lagane se vaakya ajeeb ho jaata hai.

    Exercise 11

    Bina 'very' ke vaakya banao.

    Shabd: tedious / the / was / sweltering / and / meeting / day / a / was / It

    Jawaab

    It was a sweltering day and the meeting was tedious

    Ek vaakya me ek-ek tez shabd, apni jagah par. Teen tez shabd ek saath thoos dene se asar khatam ho jaata hai.

    Exercise 12

    Baarish me chhaata nahi tha — kapde nichodne laayak geele. Kaunsa shabd?

    • drenched
    • desolate
    • opulent
    • affable
    Jawaab

    drenched

    Drenched = poori tarah bheega hua. Ye roz ka, bilkul natural shabd hai — kisi bhi maahaul me chalega.

    Exercise 13

    'The heat left us enervated.' Iska matlab kya hai?

    • Garmi ne humari saari taaqat nichod li
    • Garmi ne humein josh se bhar diya
    • Garmi se hum gusse me the
    • Garmi se hum dar gaye

    Hint: Shabd 'energy' jaisa dikhta hai, par dhoka hai.

    Jawaab

    Garmi ne humari saari taaqat nichod li

    Enervated = nidhaal, taaqat khatam. Ye 'energized' ka ulta hai — aur kitaabi shabd hai, bolne me 'drained' behtar.

    Exercise 14

    Ye shabd insaan ke liye nahi, cheez ya lifestyle ke liye hai — use dhoondo.

    My uncle is an opulent businessman from Surat.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: opulent.

    Sahi roop: My uncle is a wealthy businessman from Surat.

    Opulent hotel, shaadi, mahal ke liye. Ameer insaan wealthy ya affluent hota hai.

    Exercise 15

    When India won the final, the crowd outside was ___.

    Hint: very happy — jashn wali khushi, ek shabd.

    Jawaab

    jubilant / ecstatic / elated / overjoyed

    Jubilant = jeet par jhoom uthne wali khushi, khaas kar bheed ya team ke liye.

    Exercise 16

    very + adjective ko uske ek shabd se jodo.

    A B
    very angry sagacious
    very friendly cacophonous
    very wise incensed
    very noisy affable
    Jawaab

    very angry — incensed; very friendly — affable; very wise — sagacious; very noisy — cacophonous

    Incensed aur affable likhai me aaram se chalte hain. Sagacious aur cacophonous rare hain — pehchaano, bolo kam.

    Exercise 17

    Ye line bolo. 'Ravenous' me zor pehle hisse par — RA-ve-nus.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: We skipped lunch so by eight we were ravenous

    Teen hisse hain par pehla hi zor leta hai. Bolne me ye shabd mushkil nahi, bas aadat chahiye.

    Aage kya karein

    Ek hafte ka challenge: jab bhi "very" bolne ja rahe ho, ek pal ruk kar socho — iski jagah ek shabd kya hoga? Jawaab na mile to "very" bol do, koi harj nahi. Par wo ek pal ka rukna hi aapki vocabulary badhata hai.
