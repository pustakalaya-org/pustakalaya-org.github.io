    Chapter 18: Sawaal poochhna¶

    Sawaal poochhna — kram badalta hai, shabd nahi

    Hindi me sawaal poochhna bahut aasaan hai: "Tum ja rahe ho." aur "Tum ja rahe ho?" — shabd wahi hain, bas sur (tone) badla. English me ye nahi chalta. English me sawaal banate waqt shabdon ka kram badalna padta hai — helping verb subject se aage aa jaata hai. Isiliye "Where you are going?" kaan ko kharaab lagta hai, aur "Where are you going?" bilkul theek.

    Ek line me

    In an English question, the helping verb comes before the subject. Helping verb (am/is/are/was/were/do/does/did/have/has/will/can…) subject se pehle chala jaata hai. Bas yahi poora rule hai.

    18.1 Sawaal ki machine — ek tasveer me¶

    Neeche dekho: ek seedha vaakya hai, aur usse do tarah ke sawaal banaye gaye hain. Dhyaan do ki helping verb kaise chhalaang lagaakar subject ke aage aa jaata hai.

    18.2 Yes/No sawaal — teen haalat¶

    (a) Jab vaakya me pehle se helping verb hai¶

    Bas usko aage le aao. Kuch jodna nahi padta.

    Seedha vaakya Sawaal
    She is ready. Is she ready?
    They were late. Were they late?
    He has finished. Has he finished?
    You can swim. Can you swim?
    It will rain. Will it rain?

    (b) Jab koi helping verb nahi hai — do / does / did bulao¶

    Present Simple aur Past Simple me helping verb chhipa hua hota hai. Sawaal banate waqt wo bahar aa jaata hai — aur tab main verb V1 me wapas chala jaata hai.

    Seedha vaakya Sawaal Dhyaan do
    You play cricket. Do you play cricket? I/you/we/they → do
    She plays cricket. Does she play cricket? -s "does" me chala gaya
    He went home. Did he go home? past "did" me chala gaya, verb V1

    Ek hi jagah tense dikhega

    Does she plays? ✗ · Does she play? ✓
    Did you went? ✗ · Did you go? ✓ Tense ka kaam do/does/did kar chuka — main verb ab saada rehta hai.

    (c) "am / is / are" khud hi helping verb hai¶

    • Are you free tomorrow? Kal khaali ho? — "Do you are free" aisa kabhi nahi hota.
    • Is this seat taken? Ye seat li hui hai?

    18.3 WH-words — jab "haan/naa" se kaam nahi chalta¶

    WH-word hamesha sabse aage aata hai, aur uske baad wahi kram chalta hai jo yes/no sawaal me tha: WH + helping + subject + verb.

    WH-word Kya poochhta hai Example
    What cheez / kaam What do you want?
    Who insaan (subject) Who called you?
    Whom insaan (object) Whom did you call?
    Whose kiska Whose bag is this?
    Which diye gaye options me se kaunsa Which shirt do you like?
    When samay When does the bus leave?
    Where jagah Where are you going?
    Why wajah Why did she leave early?
    How tareeka / haal How did you come?
    How much kitna (uncountable) How much sugar do we need?
    How many kitne (countable) How many brothers do you have?
    How long kitni der / kitna lamba How long have you worked here?
    How often kitni baar How often do you exercise?
    How far kitni doori How far is the station?

    Subject wala sawaal — yahan do/does/did nahi lagta

    Agar WH-word hi subject hai (kisne? kya hua?), to kram badalne ki zaroorat hi nahi — kyunki subject pehle se hi sabse aage hai.
    Who broke the window? "Who did break the window?" galat hai. Kaanch kisne toda — "Who broke".
    What happened yesterday?

    18.4 Question tags — "hai na?"¶

    Hindi me hum vaakya ke aakhir me "na?", "hai na?", "theek hai?" jod dete hain. English me bhi yahi hota hai, par tag vaakya ka ulta hota hai: positive vaakya ke baad negative tag, negative vaakya ke baad positive tag.

    Vaakya Tag Poora roop
    Positive negative You are tired, aren't you?
    Negative positive She doesn't smoke, does she?
    Positive, past negative He came late, didn't he?
    Positive, perfect negative They have left, haven't they?
    Positive, modal negative You can drive, can't you?

    Tag banane ke teen kadam

    1. Helping verb uthao (na ho to do/does/did lao).
    2. Ulta kar do — positive → negative, negative → positive.
    3. Subject ko pronoun bana do — Ravi → he, my sisters → they, this book → it.

    Chaar khaas tag jo yaad rakhne padte hain¶

    Vaakya Tag Kyun
    I am late, … aren't I? "amn't" angrezi me nahi hota
    Let's go, … shall we? Let's = suggestion
    Open the door, … will you? Order / request ke saath
    Nobody came, … did they? nobody / everybody ka pronoun "they" hota hai
    There is a problem, … isn't there? "there" hi subject ki jagah lega

    'isn't it?' sab jagah nahi chalta

    • Galat: You are coming tomorrow, isn't it?
    • Sahi: You are coming tomorrow, aren't you?

    • Kyun: Tag ka helping verb aur pronoun vaakya se uthana padta hai. "isn't it" sirf tab theek hai jab subject hi "it" ho — It is cold, isn't it?

    • Galat: He finished the work, no?

    • Sahi: He finished the work, didn't he?
    • Kyun: Hindi ka "na?" seedha "no?" nahi banta — poora tag banana padta hai.

    18.5 Shisht sawaal — kram wapas seedha ho jaata hai¶

    Jab sawaal ke andar sawaal aata hai (polite ya indirect question), to andar wala hissa seedhe vaakya ki tarah likha jaata hai. Ye bahut log galat karte hain.

    Seedha sawaal Shisht roop
    Where is the station? Could you tell me where the station is?
    What time does it start? Do you know what time it starts?
    Why did he leave? I wonder why he left.
    Can I sit here? Would you mind if I sat here?
    • Could you please tell me where the ticket counter is? Dhyaan do — "where is the ticket counter" nahi, "where the ticket counter is".

    18.6 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Sawaal ki top galtiyan

    • Galat: Where you are going?
    • Sahi: Where are you going?

    • Kyun: Helping verb "are" subject "you" ke pehle aayega. Hindi ka kram seedha utha lena yahan sabse aam galti hai.

    • Galat: Why you did not come yesterday?

    • Sahi: Why didn't you come yesterday?

    • Kyun: WH + helping + subject + verb. "didn't" pehle, "you" baad me.

    • Galat: What is your good name?

    • Sahi: May I know your name? / What's your name?

    • Kyun: "Good name" Hindi ke "shubh naam" ka seedha anuvaad hai — English me aisa nahi bolte. Shisht lagna ho to "May I know your name?" sabse achha hai.

    • Galat: Does she knows the answer?

    • Sahi: Does she know the answer?

    • Kyun: "does" ne -s le liya, isliye main verb bina -s rahega.

    • Galat: Tell me where is the office.

    • Sahi: Tell me where the office is.

    • Kyun: Indirect question ke andar kram seedha rehta hai — helping verb peeche jaata hai.

    • Galat: He is your brother, isn't it?

    • Sahi: He is your brother, isn't he?

    • Kyun: Pronoun vaakya se uthana hai. Subject "he" hai, isliye tag me bhi "he".

    • Galat: What you do?

    • Sahi: What do you do?
    • Kyun: Kaam-dhandha poochhne ka ye standard sawaal hai, aur isme "do" do baar aata hai — ek helping, ek main verb.

    18.7 Chhota naksha¶

    Sawaal ki kism Kram
    Yes / No HELPING + subject + verb + baaki?
    WH (object wala) WH + HELPING + subject + verb?
    WH (subject wala) WH + verb + baaki? (do/does/did nahi)
    Question tag Vaakya + ulta helping + pronoun?
    Indirect / polite Polite shuruaat + WH + subject + verb

    18.8 Practice — Sawaal aur question tags¶

    Exercise 1

    Kaunsa sahi hai?

    • Where you are going?
    • Where are you going?
    • Where you going are?
    • Where going you are?

    Hint: Helping verb kahan baithta hai?

    Jawaab

    Where are you going?

    Kram hai WH + helping + subject + verb. 'are' ko 'you' se pehle aana hoga.

    Exercise 2

    ___ she live in Delhi?

    • Do
    • Does
    • Did
    • Is

    Hint: Present Simple, subject 'she'.

    Jawaab

    Does

    He/she/it ke saath 'Does'. Aur dhyaan rakho — iske baad verb bina -s rahega: 'Does she live', not 'lives'.

    Exercise 3

    You have finished the project, ___ ?

    • isn't it
    • haven't you
    • have you
    • didn't you

    Hint: Vaakya positive hai, to tag kaisa hoga?

    Jawaab

    haven't you

    Positive vaakya → negative tag. Helping verb 'have' hai aur subject 'you' hai, isliye 'haven't you'.

    Exercise 4

    ___ broke this glass?

    • Who did
    • Whom did
    • Who
    • Who does

    Hint: Yahan WH-word khud subject hai.

    Jawaab

    Who

    Jab WH-word hi subject ho to do/does/did nahi lagta — 'Who broke this glass?' Seedha verb aata hai.

    Exercise 5

    ___ you like some tea?

    Hint: Shisht offer karne wala shabd.

    Jawaab

    Would / would

    'Would you like…?' kuch offer karne ka shisht tareeka hai. 'Do you want' se zyada polite lagta hai.

    Exercise 6

    How ___ brothers do you have?

    Hint: Bhai ginne layak hain ya nahi?

    Jawaab

    many

    Countable cheezon ke liye 'how many', uncountable ke liye 'how much'. Brothers gine jaa sakte hain.

    Exercise 7

    Let's start the meeting, ___ we?

    Hint: Ye khaas tag hai.

    Jawaab

    shall

    'Let's' ke saath hamesha 'shall we?' tag aata hai — ye yaad rakhne wali cheez hai.

    Exercise 8

    Is sawaal me ek galti hai — galat shabd par tap karo.

    Does your sister knows my address?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: knows.

    Sahi roop: Does your sister know my address?

    'Does' ne -s apne andar le liya hai, isliye main verb saada rehta hai — 'know', not 'knows'.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    Please tell me where is the railway station.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: is.

    Sahi roop: Please tell me where the railway station is.

    Indirect question ke andar kram seedha rehta hai. 'is' ko subject ke baad bhejna padega.

    Exercise 10

    Tag me ek galti hai — use pakdo.

    She writes poems, isn't she?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: isn't.

    Sahi roop: She writes poems, doesn't she?

    Vaakya Present Simple me hai, usme chhipa helping verb 'does' hai — isliye tag 'doesn't she' banega. 'isn't' tab aata jab vaakya me 'is' hota.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me sawaal banao.

    Shabd: been / city / in / have / you / this / long / How / living

    Jawaab

    How long have you been living in this city

    How long + helping verbs (have been) + subject (you) + main verb (living). 'How long you are living' galat kram hai.

    Exercise 12

    Sawaal ko sahi WH-word se jodo.

    A B
    Kitni doori hai? Whose
    Kitni baar? Which
    Kiska hai? How much
    Diye gaye options me se kaunsa? How far
    Kitna cheeni? How often
    Jawaab

    Kitni doori hai? — How far; Kitni baar? — How often; Kiska hai? — Whose; Diye gaye options me se kaunsa? — Which; Kitna cheeni? — How much

    How far = doori, how often = baar, whose = kiska, which = options me se chunna, how much = uncountable ki maatra.

    Exercise 13

    Ab ye sawaal bol ke dekho — aakhir me aawaz uthaani hai.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Could you please tell me where the bus stop is

    Ye station, dukaan, kahin bhi kaam aane wala sabse kaam ka vaakya hai. Yaad rakho — 'where the bus stop is', 'where is the bus stop' nahi, kyunki ye indirect sawaal hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj se jab bhi koi sawaal poochhna ho, bolne se pehle man me ek second ruk kar helping verb dhoondo aur use aage rakho. Shuru me ye dheema lagega — par yahi ek aadat aapki English ko turant saaf kar deti hai.
