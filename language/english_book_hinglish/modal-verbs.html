    Chapter 16: can, could, should, must¶

    Modals — kar sakna, karna chahiye, karna padega

    Hindi me hum verb ke saath "sakna", "chahiye", "padega", "shayad" jod kar apna mood batate hain. English me ye kaam chhote helping verbs karte hain jinhe modals kehte hain. Ye roz ki baatcheet ki jaan hain — vinamrata se kuch maangna ho, salaah deni ho, ya andaaza lagana ho, modal ke bina kaam nahi chalta.

    Ek line me

    A modal verb is a helping verb that adds ability, permission, advice, obligation or possibility to the main verb. Main verb kaam batata hai; modal batata hai ki kaam ke baare me hum kya soch rahe hain.

    • I can swim. Main tair sakta hoon. — kshamta
    • You should drink more water. Tumhe zyada paani peena chahiye. — salaah
    • It might rain in the evening. Shaam ko shayad baarish ho. — sambhavna

    16.1 Modal kaise kaam karta hai — teen pakke niyam¶

    Achhi baat ye hai ki saare modals ek hi tarah se chalte hain. Ye teen niyam ek baar pakke ho gaye, to aadhi galtiyan apne aap band ho jaayengi — chahe modal koi bhi ho.

    Modal ke teen niyam

    1. Modal ke baad verb ka pehla roop (V1) — bina -s, bina -ing, bina "to". 2. Modal khud nahi badalta — "cans" ya "musted" nahi hota. 3. Negative aur sawaal modal khud banata hai: can't go, Can you go?

    Teen modal 'to' ke saath aate hain

    ought to, have to, used to — inme "to" unka hissa hai: You ought to apologise. Baaki kisi modal ke baad "to" nahi: You must to go.

    16.2 Ability — can, could, be able to¶

    "Main ye kar sakta hoon" — ye roz ki baat hai: naukri ke interview me, form bharte waqt, dosto ke saath. Time badalne par sahi shabd chunna zaroori hai.

    Samay Shabd Example
    Abhi (present) can She can speak Tamil.
    Pehle, aam taur par could I could run fast when I was young.
    Pehle, ek khaas mauke par was / were able to We were able to catch the last bus.
    Aage (future) will be able to You will be able to drive in a month.
    Perfect tense me have been able to I haven't been able to sleep.

    Kshamta (ability)

    Abhi ke liye can, beete samay ke liye could. Jahan "can" fit nahi hota — future, perfect, ya doosre modal ke baad — wahan be able to lagao: will be able to.

    Do modal ek saath nahi

    You will can come. ✗ → You will be able to come. ✓
    Ek vaakya me ek hi modal. Doosri zaroorat ho to "be able to" ya "have to" jaisa roop lo.

    16.3 Permission aur Request — kitni vinamrata chahiye?¶

    Kuch maangte waqt shabd ka chunaav batata hai ki aap saamne wale ki kitni izzat kar rahe ho. Dost se "Can I…?" theek hai, par boss ya anjaan se "Could you…?" zyada achha lagta hai.

    Anumati aur request

    Permission: Can I (casual) → Could I → May I (formal). Request: Can you → Could you → Would you mind + -ing. "May" sirf apne liye permission maangne me — "May you…?" nahi hota.

    • Could you send me the file by evening? Kya aap shaam tak file bhej sakte hain? — office me perfect.
    • Would you mind closing the window? Agar bura na lage to khidki band kar denge? — jawaab "No, not at all" matlab "haan, kar deta hoon".

    16.4 Advice aur Obligation — should, must, have to¶

    "Karna chahiye" aur "karna padega" me bada farq hai — ek salaah hai, doosra majboori. English me bhi ye farq saaf hai, aur galat shabd chunne se aapki baat ya to bahut kadak lagti hai ya bahut halki.

    Salaah aur majboori

    should / ought to = salaah. have to = niyam ya halaat ki majboori. must = pakki zaroorat. Dhyan: mustn't = mana hai, par don't have to = zaroori nahi.

    • You should see a doctor. Doctor ko dikhana chahiye — salaah hai, faisla aapka.
    • I have to wear a uniform at work. Company ka niyam hai — majboori bahar se aayi.
    • Past me: I had to wait for two hours. "must" ka past nahi hota — "had to" lagao.

    16.5 Possibility — might se must tak, kitna yakeen?¶

    Andaaza lagate waqt modal batata hai ki aapko kitna yakeen hai. "Wo shayad ghar par ho" aur "wo pakka ghar par hoga" — dono ke liye alag modal hai. Isse aapki English sochi-samjhi lagti hai.

    Yakeen ka meter

    might / could (shayad) → may (ho sakta hai) → should (hona chahiye) → must (pakka hoga). Ulta pakka andaaza: can't — He can't be at home; I just saw him at the station.

    Andaaze wale 'must' ka ulta 'mustn't' nahi

    She must be at work. (pakka hai ki office me hai) → ulta: She can't be at work.
    "She mustn't be at work" ka matlab ho jaayega — use office me hona mana hai!

    16.6 will, would, shall, need, used to¶

    Ye paanch modal (ya modal jaise shabd) roz aate hain, par har ek ka apna khaas kaam hai. Ek jagah dekh lo, to alag-alag yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi.

    Shabd Kaam Example
    will future, vaada, turant faisla I will call you tonight.
    would vinamra iccha, kalpana I would like a cup of tea.
    would purani aadat (kaam) Dadi would tell us stories every night.
    shall I / we ke saath offer, sujhaav Shall I open the window?
    need zaroorat You need to sign here. / You needn't worry.
    used to pehle hota tha, ab nahi I used to live in Patna.

    used to vs be used to — do alag cheezein

    used to + V1 = pehle karta tha: I used to wake up at 9. (ab nahi)
    be used to + -ing = aadat pad gayi hai: I am used to waking up early. Doosre me "to" preposition hai, isliye -ing. Sawaal me: Did you use to…? (bina -d)

    16.7 Past modals — should have, could have, must have¶

    Beeti baat par afsos, chhoota hua mauka, ya pichli ghatna ka andaaza — ye sab modal + have + V3 se bolte hain. Ye pattern sunne me mushkil lagta hai, par roz ki baatcheet me bahut aata hai.

    Past modals

    modal + have + V3: should have done = karna chahiye tha (afsos), could have done = kar sakte the (mauka chhoota), must have done = kiya hoga (pakka andaaza). "have" kabhi "has" nahi banta.

    • I should have studied harder. Mujhe aur mehnat se padhna chahiye tha. (nahi padha — afsos)
    • The roads are wet. It must have rained last night. Raat ko baarish hui hogi — sadak geeli dekh kar pakka andaaza.

    Bolne me 'should've'

    Baatcheet me should have "should've" (shud-uv) sunai deta hai. Isliye kai log "should of" likh dete hain — wo galat hai. Likhte waqt hamesha have.

    16.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Modal ki sabse aam Indian-English galtiyan

    • Galat: Can you able to come tomorrow?
    • Sahi: Can you come tomorrow? / Will you be able to come?

    • Kyun: "can" aur "able to" ka matlab ek hi hai — dono saath lagaana dohraav hai. Ek chuno.

    • Galat: You must to submit the form today.

    • Sahi: You must submit the form today.

    • Kyun: Modal ke baad seedha V1, beech me "to" nahi. Sirf ought to, have to, used to me "to" hota hai.

    • Galat: May you help me with this?

    • Sahi: Could you help me with this?

    • Kyun: "May" se sirf apne liye permission maangte hain (May I…?). Kisi se kaam karwana ho to Could you / Would you.

    • Galat: I could not able to attend the meeting.

    • Sahi: I couldn't attend / I wasn't able to attend the meeting.

    • Kyun: Wahi dohraav — "could" aur "able" saath nahi. Ek khaas mauke ki baat ho to "wasn't able to" sabse natural hai.

    • Galat: You should have went to the doctor.

    • Sahi: You should have gone to the doctor.
    • Kyun: "have" ke baad hamesha V3 — gone, seen, done. "went" V2 hai, wo yahan nahi aata.

    16.9 Practice — Modals¶

    Exercise 1

    She ___ speak four languages.

    • can
    • cans
    • can to
    • is can

    Hint: Modal ke baad verb ka kaunsa roop?

    Jawaab

    can

    Modal khud kabhi nahi badalta — na -s, na 'to', na 'is' ke saath. Bas 'She can speak'.

    Exercise 2

    Boss se vinamrata se poochhna hai: '___ you check my report, please?'

    • Could
    • May
    • Must
    • Shall
    Jawaab

    Could

    Kisi se kaam karwane ki vinamra request = Could you…? 'May you' galat hai — may sirf apne liye permission ke liye (May I…?).

    Exercise 3

    Tomorrow is a holiday, so you ___ come to the office.

    • don't have to
    • mustn't
    • can't
    • shouldn't have

    Hint: Mana nahi hai — bas zaroorat nahi.

    Jawaab

    don't have to

    Chhutti hai to aana zaroori nahi — 'don't have to'. 'mustn't' ka matlab hota ki aana mana hai, jo yahan matlab nahi.

    Exercise 4

    He has been working for 14 hours. He ___ be very tired.

    • must
    • might
    • can't
    • should have

    Hint: Pakka andaaza.

    Jawaab

    must

    14 ghante kaam — iske baad thakna lagbhag pakka hai. Pakke andaaze ke liye 'must'.

    Exercise 5

    When I was a child, I ___ climb trees easily.

    • could
    • can
    • am able to
    • will be able to
    Jawaab

    could

    Bachpan ki aam kshamta — beeta samay — isliye 'can' ka past 'could'.

    Exercise 6

    I missed the bus. I should have ___ earlier. (leave)

    Hint: have + V3

    Jawaab

    left

    Past modal ka formula: should + have + V3. 'leave' ka V3 'left' hai. Matlab: jaldi nikalna chahiye tha (par nahi nikla).

    Exercise 7

    Next year I will ___ able to vote.

    Hint: Do modal ek saath nahi aate.

    Jawaab

    be

    'will can' galat hai, kyunki do modal saath nahi lagte. Future ki kshamta = will be able to.

    Exercise 8

    Galat shabd par tap karo.

    Can you able to send the document today?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: able.

    Sahi roop: Can you send the document today?

    'Can' aur 'able to' dono ka matlab 'sakna' hai — saath me dohraav ho jaata hai. 'able to' hata do, ya bolo 'Will you be able to…?'

    Exercise 9

    Ek extra shabd hai — dhoondo.

    You must to wear a helmet on the bike.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: to.

    Sahi roop: You must wear a helmet on the bike.

    'must' ke baad seedha V1 aata hai, 'to' nahi. 'to' sirf ought to, have to, used to me hota hai.

    Exercise 10

    Modal ko uske kaam se jodo.

    A B
    should shayad, kam yakeen
    mustn't pehle hota tha, ab nahi
    might salaah
    used to mana hai
    Jawaab

    should — salaah; mustn't — mana hai; might — shayad, kam yakeen; used to — pehle hota tha, ab nahi

    should = chahiye, mustn't = mat karo, might = shayad, used to = purani aadat jo ab chhoot gayi.

    Exercise 11

    Vinamra request banao.

    Shabd: mind / door / Would / the / opening / you

    Jawaab

    Would you mind opening the door

    'Would you mind' ke baad verb -ing me aata hai — opening. Ye sabse vinamra tareeka hai kisi se kaam karwane ka.

    Exercise 12

    Ye line bolo — 'should have' ko 'should've' jaisa jod kar.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I should have called you yesterday

    Native speakers 'should have' ko 'shud-uv' bolte hain. Ise jod kar bolne ki aadat se sunna bhi aasaan ho jaata hai, aur formula (modal + have + V3) bhi pakka hota hai.

    Aage kya karein

    Kal office ya college me jab bhi kuch maango, "Can you" ki jagah "Could you" bolo. Aur din ke aakhir me ek "should have" wala vaakya apne baare me likho — kya karna chahiye tha jo nahi kiya.
