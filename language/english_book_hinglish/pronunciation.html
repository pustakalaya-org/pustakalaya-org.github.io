    Chapter 26: Uchchaaran theek karo¶

    Accent badalne ki zaroorat nahi hai — Indian accent me koi kharaabi nahi. Jo badalna hai wo hai wo chhah-saat aawazein jo galat jagah se nikal rahi hain, kyunki unki wajah se log kuch shabd samajh nahi paate. Is chapter me har aawaz ke liye saaf nirdesh hai: hoth kahan, zubaan kahan, aawaz hai ya sirf hawa. Padho aur usi waqt karo. Padh kar chhod dena bekaar hai.

    Teen kadam ka tareeka — har aawaz ke liye wahi

    1. 🔊 dabao aur suno. 2. Nirdesh padho aur muh ko wahi position do — bina aawaz, sirf position. 3. Ab aawaz nikalo, dheere, paanch baar. Tez bolna lakshya nahi hai. Saaf bolna lakshya hai. Tezi apne aap aa jaati hai.

    26.1 V aur W — sabse badi ladaai¶

    Hindi me ek hi "व" hai. English me do bilkul alag aawazein hain, aur dono ka muh alag hota hai. Yahi ek cheez theek kar lo to aapki English turant saaf lagne lagegi — kyunki ye aawazein roz sau baar aati hain.

    V — kaise banao

    Apne upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par rakho — halka sa dabao, jaise hoth ko kaat rahe ho par bilkul halke se. Ab gale se aawaz nikalo: vvvvv. Hawa daant aur hoth ke beech se ragad kar nikalti hai. Hoth gol nahi hote. Gale par ungli rakho — kampan hona chahiye.

    W — kaise banao

    Daant bilkul nahi lagte. Dono hoth ko gol karo — jaise "oo" bolne ja rahe ho, ya jaise mombatti bujhani ho. Phir usi gol position se aawaz shuru karo aur hoth kholte jao: oo-wet. W ek phisalti hui aawaz hai, ragad wali nahi.

    Minimal pairs — V banaam W¶

    Dono bajao, farq suno, phir khud bolo. Ye hi asli test hai.

    V Suno W Suno Matlab
    vet wet jaanwaron ka doctor / geela
    vine wine bel / sharaab
    vest west baniyan / pashchim
    veil whale ghoonghat / whale machhli
    verse worse kavita ki pankti / aur bura
    vary wary badalna / satark
    • We were very worried about the weather. Teen W aur ek V ek hi line me. "very" par daant lagao, baaki teen par gol hoth.
    • The vet gave the dog water. vet aur gave me daant; water me gol hoth. Ek vaakye me dono.

    Dhyan — ulti galti bhi hoti hai

    Bahut log V theek karne ke chakkar me har jagah daant lagane lag jaate hain — "wery" ki jagah "vater", "vindow", "ve are". Ye pehli galti se zyada ajeeb lagta hai. Niyam seedha hai: jo W likha hai, wo W hi hai. Jo V likha hai, wo V.

    26.2 TH — do aawazein, ek position¶

    TH ke liye zubaan ko bahar nikalna padta hai — ye kisi ko ajeeb lagta hai, par yahi sahi hai. Hindi ka "थ" aur "ध" dono daanton ke peeche se nikalte hain; English ka TH daanton ke beech se.

    TH — kadam-ba-kadam

    1. Zubaan ki patli nok upar ke do saamne ke daanton ke neeche se zara bahar nikalo — bas 2-3 millimetre, zubaan baahar latkani nahi hai.
    2. Daant halke se zubaan ko chhuein.
    3. Ab hawa bahar phenko: thhh — ye think wali TH hai (sirf hawa).
    4. Wahi position rakho, par ab gale se aawaz do: dhhh — ye this wali TH hai (kampan).

    Pakka test

    Haath apne muh ke saamne rakho. "think" bolo — hawa ka jhonka mehsoos hona chahiye. Agar nahi hua, to aap "tink" ya "sink" bol rahe hain, "think" nahi.

    TH (sirf hawa) Suno Jo galti se nikalta hai Suno
    think sink (basin)
    three tree (ped)
    thin tin (dabba)
    thank tank (tanki)
    mouth mouse (chooha)
    path pass
    TH (kampan wali) Suno Note
    this, that, these, those Roz sabse zyada aane wale chaar shabd — inhi par sabse pehle kaam karo.
    the "da" nahi — zubaan daanton ke beech, phir halki "uh".
    they, them, their "de, dem, der" sabse aam Indian pattern hai. Zubaan bahar nikalo.
    mother, father, brother, other Beech me aane wali TH sabse zyada chhoot jaati hai.
    • I think this is the third thing. Chaar TH ek line me — do hawa wali (think, third, thing) aur do kampan wali (this, the).
    • My brother and my mother both thanked them. Beech wali TH ki drill. Dheere bolo — har TH par zubaan bahar.

    26.3 P aur F — Hindi ka "फ" beech me aata hai¶

    Hindi ka "फ" dono hoth se banta hai. English ka F daant aur hoth se. Isi liye "coffee" kabhi "copy" jaisa, aur "phone" kabhi "pone" jaisa sunai deta hai.

    F vs P — position ka farq

    P: dono hoth bilkul band, hawa ko rok kar ek dum chhodo — phat se. Hawa ka dhakka hota hai.
    F: upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par (bilkul V ki tarah), par gale se aawaz nahi — sirf lambi, patli hawa: ffff. Hoth band nahi hote, hawa rukti hi nahi.

    P Suno F Suno Matlab
    pan fan kadhai / pankha
    pin fin pin / machhli ka par
    copy coffee nakal / coffee
    pool fool talaab / bewkoof
    supper suffer raat ka khaana / takleef sehna
    • Please put the fan near the phone. put me dono hoth, fan aur phone me daant-hoth. Teeno ek line me.

    26.4 S aur SH — zubaan kitni peeche¶

    S vs SH

    S: zubaan ki nok upar ke daanton ke bilkul peeche, hoth chaudaa (jaise halki muskaan). Patli, teekhi hawa: ssss — jaise saanp.
    SH: zubaan ko thoda peeche sarkao (talu ki taraf) aur hoth gol karo, aage dhakelo. Mote, bhaari hawa: shhh — jaise chup karaana.

    S Suno SH Suno Matlab
    sea she samudra / wo (ladki)
    sip ship ghoont / jahaaz
    save shave bachaana / shave karna
    sort short chhaantna / chhota
    seat sheet seat / chaadar

    26.5 Z aur J — bhanbhanaahat vs jhatka¶

    /z/ Hindi me nahi hai, isliye hum ya "s" bol dete hain ya "j". Dono galat hain.

    Z vs J — ek lagataar, doosra ek dum

    Z: /s/ ki bilkul wahi position — zubaan daanton ke peeche — par gale ka kampan jodo. Aawaz lagataar chalti rahegi: zzzzzz, jitni der saans chale.
    J: zubaan talu se chipka kar hawa roko, phir ek dum chhodo — jhatka hota hai. Aawaz kheench nahi sakte. Test: "zzzz" 5 second tak kheench sakte hain. "j" nahi kheench sakte.

    Z Suno J / S Suno Note
    zoo Jew Z kheenchti hai, J jhatka hai
    buzz bus bhanbhanaana / bus
    prize price inaam / keemat — roz kaam aata hai
    eyes ice aankhein / baraf
    rise rice uthna / chawal

    Chhota par bahut kaam ka niyam

    Plural aur third-person ka "s" bahut baar /z/ bolta hai, "s" nahi: dogs = dogz, jobs = jobz, plays = playz, comes = comz, is = iz, was = woz. Agar pehle wali aawaz me gale ka kampan hai, to "s" bhi /z/ ban jaata hai.

    26.6 "school" — "iskool" nahi¶

    Hindi me shabd do consonant se shuru nahi hote, isliye hamari zubaan aaraam ke liye aage ek chhoti "i" jod deti hai. Ilaaj seedha hai, aur 5 minute me kaam karta hai:

    Teen-kadam ka ilaaj

    Kadam 1: Sirf ssssssss bolo, 3 second — koi vowel nahi.
    Kadam 2: Usi saans ko tode bina aage jodo: sssss-kool.
    Kadam 3: Dheere-dheere "sss" chhota karte jao: sss-kool → ss-kool → school. Asli trick ye hai ki beech me saans na toote. Saans toota = "i" aa gaya.

    Galat Sahi Suno
    iskool school
    istation station
    istudy study
    ispeak / isport speak / sport
    istreet street
    iskin skin
    filim film
    pleeze / pilees please
    • I study at a school near the station. Teen "s" jode — study, school, station. Ek bhi "i" nahi.
    • The street is small but the sky is clear. street, small, sky — teeno jude consonant. Saans todna nahi.

    26.10 Practice — Uchchaaran¶

    Exercise 1

    W bolte waqt daant kahan hote hain?

    • Kahin nahi lagte — hoth gol hote hain
    • Upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par
    • Zubaan daanton ke beech
    • Dono hoth band

    Hint: Aaine me kya dikhega?

    Jawaab

    Kahin nahi lagte — hoth gol hote hain

    W me daant kaam me nahi aate. Daant lagte hi wo V ban jaata hai.

    Exercise 2

    'think' bolte waqt haath muh ke saamne rakhein to kya mehsoos hona chahiye?

    • Hawa ka jhonka
    • Kuch nahi
    • Gale ka kampan sirf
    • Hoth ka dhakka
    Jawaab

    Hawa ka jhonka

    /θ/ me hawa daanton aur zubaan ke beech se bahar nikalti hai. Hawa nahi aayi = 'tink' ya 'sink' bola gaya.

    Exercise 3

    English ka F aur Hindi ka 'फ' me kya farq hai?

    • F me daant hoth par lagte hain; 'फ' me dono hoth lagte hain
    • Koi farq nahi
    • F me zubaan daanton ke beech aati hai
    • F me gale ka kampan hota hai
    Jawaab

    F me daant hoth par lagte hain; 'फ' me dono hoth lagte hain

    Isi liye 'coffee' kabhi 'copy' jaisa sunai deta hai. F ki position V jaisi hai, bas aawaz nahi — sirf hawa.

    Exercise 4

    Z aur J me pehchaan ka sabse aasaan test kya hai?

    • Z ko 5 second kheench sakte hain, J ko nahi
    • J me gale ka kampan hota hai, Z me nahi
    • Z me hoth gol hote hain
    • J me zubaan daanton ke beech aati hai

    Hint: Ek aawaz kheench sakte hain, doosri nahi.

    Jawaab

    Z ko 5 second kheench sakte hain, J ko nahi

    Z lagataar bhanbhanaahat hai; J ek jhatka hai jisme hawa pehle rukti hai.

    Exercise 5

    'dogs' ka aakhri 's' asal me kaunsi aawaz hai?

    • /z/ — dogz
    • /s/ — dogs
    • Chup rehta hai
    • /sh/
    Jawaab

    /z/ — dogz

    Pehle wali aawaz (g) me gale ka kampan hai, isliye 's' bhi /z/ ban jaata hai: jobz, playz, comz.

    Exercise 6

    'iskool' ki aadat chhodne ka sahi tareeka?

    • 'sss' kheencho, phir bina saans tode 'kool' jodo
    • 'school' ko tez bolo
    • Pehle 'i' bolo phir chhod do
    • 'k' ko chup rakho
    Jawaab

    'sss' kheencho, phir bina saans tode 'kool' jodo

    Saans toote hi vowel ghus jaati hai. Ek hi saans me jod kar bolo: sss-kool, phir chhota karte jao.

    Exercise 7

    Is vaakya me ek shabd galat uchchaaran se likha gaya hai — us par tap karo.

    My brother studies in a iskool near my house.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: iskool.

    Sahi roop: My brother studies in a school near my house.

    school — seedha 's' se. Aage jodi gayi 'i' Hindi ki aadat hai.

    Exercise 8

    Galat likha hua shabd dhoondo.

    We are going to see a filim tonight.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: filim.

    Sahi roop: We are going to see a film tonight.

    film — 'l' aur 'm' ek saath, beech me koi 'i' nahi. Dheere bolo: fi-lm.

    Exercise 9

    Minimal pair ke matlab jodo.

    A B
    vet teen
    wet ped
    three jaanwaron ka doctor
    tree geela
    Jawaab

    vet — jaanwaron ka doctor; wet — geela; three — teen; tree — ped

    Ek aawaz ka farq, poora matlab badal gaya. Isi liye in jodiyon par kaam karna zaroori hai.

    Exercise 10

    Shabd ko uski chup akshar wali sahi aawaz se jodo.

    A B
    know wok
    thumb ri-seet
    walk no
    receipt tham
    Jawaab

    know — no; thumb — tham; walk — wok; receipt — ri-seet

    K, B, L aur P — chaaron chup. Jo likha hai wo bolna zaroori nahi.

    Exercise 11

    V aur W ek saath. Aaine me dekho — kahan daant dikhne chahiye?

    Jawaab

    Bolo: We were very worried about the weather

    'very' par upar ke daant neeche ke hoth par. 'we, were, worried, weather' me hoth gol, daant nahi.

    Exercise 12

    TH ki drill. Zubaan daanton ke beech se bahar.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I think this is the third thing

    think, third, thing = sirf hawa. this, the = gale ka kampan. Position dono me ek hi.

    Exercise 13

    V aur F — dono me daant hoth par, par ek me aawaz hai.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Very few of my friends have a van

    very, have, van me kampan (V). few, friends me sirf hawa (F).

    Exercise 14

    S aur SH ka farq. Zubaan ko peeche sarkao aur hoth gol karo SH ke liye.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: She sells sheets by the sea

    She, sheets me SH (gol hoth). sells, sea me S (chaudaa hoth, patli hawa).

    Exercise 15

    /z/ ki drill. Gale par ungli rakh kar bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: The prize is zero rupees

    prize, is, zero, rupees — chaaron me /z/. 'price' bol diya to matlab badal gaya.

    Exercise 16

    Jude consonant — 'i' bilkul nahi lagana.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: I study at a school near the station

    study, school, station — teeno seedha 's' se. Ek hi saans me jodo.

    Exercise 17

    Aakhri imtihaan — saari aawazein ek saath.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Three of those women work in a very small school

    Three (hawa TH), those (kampan TH), women (W), very (V), small aur school (s- jod). Dheere bolo, poora bolo.

    Aage kya karein

    Ek baat yaad rakho: aawazein theek ho jaayein, phir bhi agar aap har shabd barabar zor se bolenge to English robotic lagegi. Agla chapter — Rhythm aur stress — wahi hissa hai jo sach me "fluent" wali feeling deta hai.
