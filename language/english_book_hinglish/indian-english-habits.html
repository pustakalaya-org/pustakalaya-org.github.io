    Chapter 57: Indian English ki aadatein¶

    Pehle ek baat saaf kar dein: is chapter ki ek bhi cheez "ganwaarpan" nahi hai. Indian English ek asli, poori tarah vikasit English hai — iski apni lay hai, apne shabd hain, aur ise bolne wale karoron log hain. Prepone ek hoshiyaar shabd hai: postpone ka ulta, bilkul taarkik. Out of station angrezi raaj ke zamaane se seedha chala aa raha hai. Ye aadatein hain — kami nahi, aur bewakoofi to bilkul nahi.

    To phir ye chapter kyun? Kyunki ek khaas kamre me — international call, videsh ka interview, bahar ke client ko email — saamne wale ko ye phrases samajh nahi aate. Wahan "galat-sahi" ki baat nahi hoti; sirf samajh na aane ki baat hoti hai. Aur jab aapki baat samajh nahi aati, to aapki kaabiliyat bhi kam dikhne lagti hai — jo bilkul na-insaafi hai. Isliye maqsad apni English badalna nahi hai. Maqsad hai ek doosra gear seekhna, jo zaroorat padne par daal sakein.

    Is chapter ka asli sawaal

    "Ye galat hai ya sahi?" nahi — balki "ye kamra kaunsa hai?" Dilli ke office me "out of station" bilkul theek hai. London ke client ke saath "out of town" bolna behtar hai. Dono sahi — jagah alag.

    57.1 Asli dikkat kahan hoti hai — ek naksha¶

    Neeche beech ka column sabse zaroori hai. Wo batata hai ki jab aap ye phrase bolte ho, to saamne wale ke dimaag me kya chalta hai. Wo naraz nahi hota — wo confuse hota hai, aur ruk kar sochne lagta hai. Bas us ek pal ka rukna aapki baat ka asar kam kar deta hai.

    57.2 Apna parichay — "Myself Ravi"¶

    Ye Hindi ki seedhi chhaap hai: "Main Ravi" — bilkul poora vaakya, bina verb ke. English me myself reflexive pronoun hai (I hurt myself), subject nahi ban sakta. Isliye "Myself Ravi" sunkar saamne wala ruk jaata hai.

    Parichay ke teen roop

    • Galat: Myself Ravi, I am from Pune.
    • Sahi: I'm Ravi, and I'm from Pune.

    • Kyun: Casual aur normal — phone par, meeting me, naye log se milte waqt. Sabse aam roop yahi hai.

    • Galat: I am Ravi this side.

    • Sahi: Hi, this is Ravi.

    • Kyun: Phone aur video call par "this is" hi natural hai. "This side" sirf India me chalta hai.

    • Galat: What is your good name?

    • Sahi: May I have your name?
    • Kyun: "Good name" Hindi ke "shubh naam" ka seedha tarjuma hai — pyaara hai, par bahar samajh nahi aata.
    • Hi, I'm Ravi — I handle the vendor side of things. Namaste, main Ravi — vendor ka kaam main dekhta hoon.

    57.3 Email ke do bade phrases¶

    "Please revert back"¶

    Do cheezein hain yahan. Pehli: revert ka asli matlab hai purani haalat me laut jaana — "The file reverted to its old version." Jawaab dene ke matlab me ye sirf Indian English me chalta hai. Doosri: revert me "waapas" pehle se shaamil hai, isliye back dobara bekaar hai. Yahi return back aur reply back me bhi hota hai.

    Jawaab maangne ke saaf tareeke

    • Galat: Kindly revert back at the earliest.
    • Sahi: Please get back to me by Thursday.

    • Kyun: Do sudhaar: "revert back" → "get back to me", aur "at the earliest" → asli taarikh. Taarikh dene se kaam asal me hota hai.

    • Galat: Please reply back soon.

    • Sahi: Please reply when you can.
    • Kyun: Reply, return, revert — teeno me "waapas" shaamil hai. Inke saath "back" kabhi nahi.

    "Kindly do the needful"¶

    Ye phrase purani sarkari English se aaya hai, aur India me iska matlab sab samajhte hain: "jo karna chahiye wo kar dijiye". Par bahar ke padhne wale ke liye ye sabse bada sawaal chhod deta hai — needful kya hai? Email ka maqsad hi ye hai ki saamne wale ko pata chale ki use kya karna hai. "Do the needful" wo jaankari chhupa deta hai.

    Saaf maang likhna seekho

    • Galat: Kindly do the needful and revert back.
    • Sahi: Could you approve the invoice and forward it to accounts? Let me know once it's done.

    • Kyun: Saamne wale ko do cheezein chahiye: kya karna hai, aur kab tak. Dono likho — email aadhi lambi aur dus guna zyada kaam ki ho jaati hai.

    • Galat: Kindly share the file.

    • Sahi: Could you share the file?
    • Kyun: "Kindly" galat nahi hai, par bahar ye bahut purane zamaane ka ya thoda akda hua lagta hai. "Could you" sabse safe hai.

    Email ka ek-line formula

    Kaam + kaun + kab tak.
    "Could you approve the PO by Friday so we can release payment?" Teen cheezein ek line me. Isme "kindly", "needful" aur "at the earliest" ki zaroorat hi nahi padti.

    57.4 "Prepone" — ek achha shabd, jo bahar nahi pahuncha¶

    Imaandaari se: prepone bahut samajhdaar shabd hai. Post ka ulta pre hai, to postpone ka ulta prepone kyun nahi? Logic bilkul theek hai, aur kuch dictionaries ab ise "Indian English" ke tag ke saath rakhti bhi hain. Par American ya British colleague ne ye shabd zindagi me suna hi nahi hoga.

    Jo kehna hai India me Bahar ke liye
    Pehle kar dena prepone the meeting move the meeting up / bring it forward
    Baad me kar dena postpone the meeting push the meeting back / postpone it
    Bilkul radd karna cancel call it off / cancel
    • Could we move Thursday's call up to Tuesday? Thursday ki call Tuesday par le aa sakte hain?

    Dhyan do — 'postpone' bilkul sahi hai

    Sirf "prepone" ka jodi-shabd bahar nahi hai. Postpone poori duniya me chalta hai, isliye use chhodne ki zaroorat nahi.

    57.5 Padhai, parivaar aur safar ke shabd¶

    'Passed out from college'

    • Galat: I passed out from college in 2019.
    • Sahi: I graduated in 2019.

    • Kyun: Pass out ka matlab bahar hai "behosh ho jaana"! Ye line sunkar log hairan ho jaate hain. Sahi roop: graduated from Pune University in 2019, ya finished college in 2019.

    • Galat: I am doing my graduation.

    • Sahi: I'm doing my bachelor's degree. / I'm an undergraduate.

    • Kyun: Bahar "graduation" ka matlab hai degree milne ka samaroh (convocation), padhai nahi.

    • Galat: I gave my twelfth standard exam.

    • Sahi: I took my final school exams. / I finished high school.
    • Kyun: Do cheezein: exam "give" nahi karte, take karte hain (teacher give karta hai). Aur "standard" ki jagah "grade" ya "class" zyada samajh aata hai.

    'Cousin brother' aur rishton ke naam

    • Galat: He is my cousin brother.
    • Sahi: He's my cousin.

    • Kyun: English me "cousin" me ladka-ladki ka farq nahi hota, isliye brother/sister jodna ajeeb lagta hai. Agar farq batana zaroori hai: "my cousin — my uncle's son".

    • Galat: My real brother lives in Indore.

    • Sahi: My brother lives in Indore.

    • Kyun: "Real brother" se lagta hai ki baaki bhai naqli hain. English me "brother" ka matlab seedha sagaa bhai hota hai.

    • Galat: She is my co-brother's wife.

    • Sahi: She's my wife's sister. / My sister-in-law.
    • Kyun: Hindi me rishton ke naam bahut zyada aur bahut saaf hain; English me nahi. Isliye rishta seedha samjha do — "my wife's brother", "my mother's cousin".

    'Out of station'

    • Galat: I will be out of station next week.
    • Sahi: I'll be out of town next week. / I'm travelling next week.
    • Kyun: Ye phrase angrezi raaj ke "posting station" se aaya hai — India me saaf hai, bahar nahi. Email ke liye sabse saaf: "I'm away from the office until the 14th."

    57.6 "I have a doubt" — chhota shabd, bada farq¶

    Class me "Sir, I have a doubt" bolna India me bilkul normal hai. Par English me doubt = shaq, bharosa na hona. "I doubt it" ka matlab hai "mujhe nahi lagta". Isliye international class ya meeting me ye line sunkar teacher ko lagta hai ki aap unki baat par shaq kar rahe ho.

    Jo kehna hai Sahi roop
    Sawaal poochhna hai I have a question.
    Ek hissa samajh nahi aaya I'm not clear on the second step.
    Thoda confusion hai Could you clarify that bit?
    Sach me shaq hai I doubt that will work. (yahan doubt sahi hai)
    • I have a question about the second step — should it run before or after the backup? Doosre step ke baare me ek sawaal hai — wo backup se pehle chalega ya baad me?

    57.7 Chaar chhoti aadatein¶

    'Same to same'

    • Galat: Both reports are same to same.
    • Sahi: Both reports are exactly the same. / They're identical.
    • Kyun: "Same to same" bahut hi expressive hai, par ye sirf India ka roop hai. Ek shabd me kaam ho jaata hai: identical.

    'Discuss about'

    • Galat: Let's discuss about the budget.
    • Sahi: Let's discuss the budget. / Let's talk about the budget.
    • Kyun: Discuss ke andar hi "about" ka matlab hai. Isi family ke aur shabd: enter the room (not "enter into"), reach Delhi (not "reach at"), marry her (not "marry with"), consider it (not "consider about").

    'Order for'

    • Galat: I ordered for two coffees.
    • Sahi: I ordered two coffees.

    • Kyun: Order ke baad seedha cheez aati hai. "For" tab aata hai jab insaan ke liye ho: "I ordered a coffee for you."

    • Galat: Let me request for a copy.

    • Sahi: Let me request a copy. / Let me ask for a copy.
    • Kyun: Request ke baad bhi "for" nahi. Par "ask" ke saath "for" zaroori hai. Yahi jodiyan yaad rakhni padti hain.

    Teen aur jo roz dikhti hain

    • Galat: I will do it now itself.
    • Sahi: I'll do it right now.

    • Kyun: "Itself" ko zor dene ke liye lagana Hindi ke "hi" se aaya hai (abhi hi). English me "right" lagao: right now, right there, right away.

    • Galat: What all did he say?

    • Sahi: What exactly did he say? / What did he say?

    • Kyun: "What all", "where all", "who all" — Hindi ke "kya kya" se aaye hain. Pyaare hain, par bahar nahi chalte.

    • Galat: He is my batchmate only.

    • Sahi: We were in the same class. / He's a classmate of mine.
    • Kyun: "Batchmate" aur vaakya ke aakhir me "only" dono Indian English ke hain. "Only" ka ye istemaal bahar bilkul samajh nahi aata.

    57.9 Aakhir me — do gear wali English¶

    Duniya ke har bhasha-bolne wale ke paas do gear hote hain: ghar ka roop aur bahar ka roop. Ek Scotsman apne gaon me jo bolta hai, wo New York ki meeting me nahi bolta. Ek Texan London me apna lehja dheema kar leta hai. Isme koi sharm nahi — ye hunar hai.

    Ye yaad rakho

    Apni Indian English chhodne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Wo aapki hai, aur wo poori tarah theek hai. Bas doosra gear paas rakho — international call, bahar ka interview, videsh ka client. Gear badalna seekh gaye, to aap kahin bhi baat kar sakte ho. Do gear = do taakat. Ek gear chhod kar doosra lena nuksaan hai.

    57.10 Practice — Indian English ki aadatein¶

    Exercise 1

    Videsh ke interview me apna parichay kaise dein?

    • Hi, I'm Ravi.
    • Myself Ravi.
    • I am Ravi this side.
    • Myself is Ravi.

    Hint: Myself subject nahi ban sakta.

    Jawaab

    Hi, I'm Ravi.

    'Myself' reflexive pronoun hai, subject nahi. 'This side' sirf India me chalta hai — phone par bahar 'this is Ravi' bolte hain.

    Exercise 2

    'I passed out from college in 2019' sunkar bahar ka insaan kya samajhta hai?

    • Aap college me behosh ho gaye the
    • Aapne degree li
    • Aap college chhod aaye
    • Aap exam me fail ho gaye
    Jawaab

    Aap college me behosh ho gaye the

    'Pass out' ka matlab hai behosh ho jaana. Degree lene ke liye 'graduated' bolo.

    Exercise 3

    In sab me se kaunsi baat sach hai?

    • Indian English ek asli roop hai; dikkat sirf tab hoti hai jab saamne wala use na samjhe
    • Indian English galat English hai
    • Indian English sirf gaon me boli jaati hai
    • Indian English ko poori tarah chhod dena chahiye
    Jawaab

    Indian English ek asli roop hai; dikkat sirf tab hoti hai jab saamne wala use na samjhe

    Sawaal 'galat ya sahi' ka nahi, 'kaunsa kamra' ka hai. Do gear rakho — ek chhod kar doosra lena nuksaan hai.

    Exercise 4

    'Kindly do the needful and revert back' me sabse badi dikkat kya hai?

    • Saamne wale ko pata hi nahi chalta ki karna kya hai
    • Vaakya bahut chhota hai
    • 'Kindly' bahut rude hai
    • Isme koi verb nahi hai

    Hint: Email ka maqsad kya hota hai?

    Jawaab

    Saamne wale ko pata hi nahi chalta ki karna kya hai

    Email ka kaam hai kaam saaf batana. 'Do the needful' wahi jaankari chhupa deta hai. Formula: kaam + kaun + kab tak.

    Exercise 5

    I'll be out of ___ next week, so let's meet after the 14th.

    Hint: Station nahi.

    Jawaab

    town

    'Out of station' India me saaf hai par bahar confusion banata hai. 'Out of town' ya 'away from the office' bolo.

    Exercise 6

    I have a ___ about the second step — does it run before the backup?

    Hint: Doubt matlab shaq hota hai.

    Jawaab

    question / query

    Doubt = shaq. Sawaal poochhne ke liye 'question'. Agar samajh nahi aa raha to 'I'm not clear on…' bolo.

    Exercise 7

    Could we move the Thursday call ___ to Tuesday?

    Hint: Prepone ki jagah kaunsa particle?

    Jawaab

    up / forward

    Pehle laana = move it up / bring it forward. 'Prepone' logical shabd hai par bahar maujood nahi.

    Exercise 8

    Ek shabd bekaar hai — us par tap karo.

    Please revert back to me by Thursday.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: back.

    Sahi roop: Please get back to me by Thursday.

    Revert, reply aur return — teeno me 'waapas' pehle se hai, isliye 'back' bekaar hai. Aur 'revert' ka matlab bahar purani haalat me jaana hota hai.

    Exercise 9

    Ek shabd hata dena chahiye — use dhoondo.

    Let us discuss about the budget tomorrow.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: about.

    Sahi roop: Let's discuss the budget tomorrow.

    Discuss ke andar hi 'about' shaamil hai. Isi family me: enter the room, reach Delhi, order two teas, consider my request.

    Exercise 10

    Ek shabd ajeeb lagta hai — us par tap karo.

    He is my cousin brother from Indore.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: brother.

    Sahi roop: He's my cousin from Indore.

    English me 'cousin' me ladka-ladki ka farq nahi hota, isliye brother jodna ajeeb lagta hai. Zaroori ho to: 'my cousin — my uncle's son'.

    Exercise 11

    Aadat ko uske international roop se jodo.

    A B
    Myself Ravi exactly the same
    I have a doubt right now
    same to same I'm Ravi
    now itself I have a question
    Jawaab

    Myself Ravi — I'm Ravi; I have a doubt — I have a question; same to same — exactly the same; now itself — right now

    Chaaron India me poori tarah saaf hain — bas bahar ke kaan ke liye doosra roop chahiye.

    Exercise 12

    Phrase ko uski dikkat se jodo.

    A B
    prepone kaunsa station, saaf nahi hota
    pass out from college 'back' bekaar hai
    out of station ye shabd bahar maujood nahi hai
    revert back matlab behosh hona ban jaata hai
    Jawaab

    prepone — ye shabd bahar maujood nahi hai; pass out from college — matlab behosh hona ban jaata hai; out of station — kaunsa station, saaf nahi hota; revert back — 'back' bekaar hai

    Har aadat ki dikkat alag hai — kahin shabd hi nahi hai, kahin matlab badal jaata hai, kahin ek shabd bekaar hai.

    Exercise 13

    Email ki ek saaf line banao — kaam, kaun aur kab tak.

    Shabd: approve / by / the / release / payment / you / so / we / can / invoice / Could / Friday

    Jawaab

    Could you approve the invoice by Friday so we can release payment

    Yahi formula hai: kya karna hai, kisko, kab tak. Isme 'kindly', 'needful' aur 'at the earliest' ki zaroorat hi nahi padti.

    Exercise 14

    Apna parichay bol kar dekho. Shuru me 'I'm' ek hi aawaz me nikalna chahiye.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Hi I am Ravi and I graduated in 2019

    Interview ki pehli do line hi sabse zyada yaad rehti hain. 'I'm' aur 'graduated' — dono yahan hain, aur dono safe hain.

    Aage kya karein

    Apni pichhli paanch bheji hui emails kholo aur sirf teen shabd dhoondo: kindly, revert, needful. Jahan milein, wahan seedha likho ki kya karna hai aur kab tak. Ek baar me aapki likhit English do kadam aage chali jaayegi — aur wo bhi bina kuch "seekhe".
