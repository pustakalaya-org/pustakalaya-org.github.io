    Chapter 33: Phone aur video call¶

    Phone aur video call — bina chehra dekhe baat karna

    Aamne-saamne baat me haath, aankhein aur muskaan madad karti hain. Phone par sirf aawaaz hai — isi liye wahan English sabse mushkil lagti hai. Achhi khabar ye hai ki phone ki English bahut fixed hoti hai: gine-chune vaakya baar-baar aate hain. Wo vaakya rat lo, aur aadhi ghabraahat khatam. Is chapter me call uthane se lekar "You're on mute" tak sab hai — har line par 🔊 ke saath.

    33.1 Call uthana aur call karna¶

    Call ke pehle das second tay karte hain ki baaki call kaisi jaayegi. Saamne wale ko teen cheezein turant chahiye: aap kaun ho, kahan se ho, aur kyon call kiya.

    • Hello, Priya speaking. Call uthate waqt apna naam — office me sabse saaf tareeka.
    • Good morning, Sharma Traders. How can I help you? Company ka phone uthaate waqt.
    • Hi, this is Arjun from BlueLeaf Design. Phone par apna naam = "This is…", "I am…" nahi.
    • Could I speak to Ms Rao, please? Kisi khaas insaan se baat karni ho.
    • I'm calling about the invoice you sent last week. "I'm calling about…" = main is baare me call kar raha hoon.
    • Is this a good time to talk? Abhi baat kar sakte ho? — izzat wala sawaal, khaas kar mobile par.

    Pehle das second

    Greeting + naam + kaam: "Hi, this is Arjun from BlueLeaf. I'm calling about the invoice." Phone par apna naam hamesha "This is…" se — "I am Arjun this side" nahi.

    33.2 Kaun bol raha hai — poochhna, rukwaana, message lena¶

    Office ya ghar ka phone aap uthao aur call kisi aur ke liye ho, to teen kaam aate hain: caller ka naam poochhna, unhe rukwaana, ya message lena. Teeno ke liye seedhe "Who are you?" ki jagah naram lines hain.

    Kehna hai English line Suno
    Aap kaun? May I ask who's calling?
    Ek minute ruko Could you hold on a moment, please?
    Main jod deta hoon I'll put you through.
    Wo abhi nahi hain I'm afraid she's in a meeting right now.
    Kuch kehna hai? Can I take a message?
    Unse callback karwa dena Could you ask her to call me back?
    Galat number Sorry, I think you have the wrong number.

    Naram sawaal, saaf kaam

    "Who are you?" ki jagah "May I ask who's calling?" Aur bura khabar dene se pehle "I'm afraid…" lagao — "I'm afraid he's not in today." "I'm afraid" yahan darr nahi, "maaf kijiye, par…" ka matlab hai.

    Mini-dialogue — office reception

    Haalat: aap reception par ho. Kisi ka call aata hai jo aapki manager Ms Rao se baat karna chahta hai, par wo meeting me hain.

    • Aap: Good afternoon, Zenith Travels. How can I help you? Greeting + company + madad ki peshkash.
    • Caller: Hi, could I speak to Ms Rao, please? Caller ne naam nahi bataya — ab aapko poochhna hai.
    • Aap: Of course. May I ask who's calling? "Who are you?" nahi — ye naram tareeka hai.
    • Caller: It's Karan Mehta from Skyline Hotels. Phone par "It's…" ya "This is…" — dono sahi.
    • Aap: Thank you, Mr Mehta. I'm afraid she's in a meeting right now. Can I take a message? Bura khabar "I'm afraid" ke saath, turant hal ke saath.
    • Caller: Yes, could you ask her to call me back before five? Message: paanch baje se pehle callback.
    • Aap: Sure. I'll make sure she gets the message. Bharosa dilaane wali line.

    33.3 Sunai nahi diya, samajh nahi aaya¶

    Phone par aadhi galtiyan English ki nahi, network ki hoti hain. Darr ye hota hai ki dobara poochha to bura lagega — par andaaze se "yes, yes" bolna usse kahin zyada mehnga padta hai. Problem pehchaano, aur uski line bolo.

    • Sorry, could you repeat that? Sabse kaam ki line. "Sorry?" akela bhi chalta hai — aawaaz upar le jaao.
    • Sorry, I didn't catch your name. "catch" = pakadna — naam sunai nahi diya.
    • Sorry, you're breaking up. Can I call you back? Aawaaz toot rahi hai — main dobara call karta hoon.
    • Could you speak a little more slowly, please? Thoda dheere — koi bura nahi maanta.
    • Just to confirm, you said Thursday at three? Pakka kar loon — gurubaar, teen baje? Galatfahmi yahin rukti hai.

    Andaaza nahi, sawaal

    Samajh na aaye to "What?" ya "Haan?" nahi — "Sorry, could you repeat that?" Aur har zaroori date, time, number ko "Just to confirm…" ke saath dohraao. Andaaze se bola gaya "yes, yes" sabse mehngi galti hai.

    33.4 Naam aur number spell karna¶

    Phone par B, D, P, V aur T ek jaise sunai dete hain; M aur N bhi; F aur S bhi. Isliye har letter ke saath ek pehchaan wala shabd jodo. Duniya bhar me ye list chalti hai — rat lo, ya kam se kam apne naam ke letters.

    • My surname is Rawat. That's R for Romeo, A for Alpha, W for Whiskey, A for Alpha, T for Tango. "That's…" se spelling shuru karo, har letter ke baad zara ruko.
    • My number is nine eight two zero one, four five six seven eight. Number ko 5 + 5 ke tukdon me bolo, beech me ruk kar. Ek saans me das ank koi nahi likh paata.
    • That's double seven, not one seven. "77" = double seven. India aur UK me aam hai; American log "seven seven" bolte hain.
    • My email is priya dot sharma at gmail dot com. . = dot, @ = at, _ = underscore, - = hyphen ya dash, / = slash.

    Spell karne ka tareeka

    Letter + for + shabd: "S for Sierra, H for Hotel." Number tukdon me bolo (98201 · 45678), aur email me dot, at, underscore. Apne naam, surname aur email ki spelling aaj hi ek baar zor se bol kar taiyaar kar lo.

    33.5 Voicemail chhodna¶

    Beep ke baad kai log ghabra ke call kaat dete hain, ya bina ruke kuch bhi bol dete hain. Voicemail ek chhota sa script hai — tees second, paanch hisse. Pehle se socha ho to aasaan hai.

    • Hi, this is Neha Kapoor from Zenith Travels. Naam + company.
    • I'm calling about your booking for Goa next month. Call ki wajah — ek line me.
    • Could you call me back to confirm the dates? Unhe kya karna hai — saaf shabdon me.
    • My number is nine eight two zero one, four five six seven eight. Again, nine eight two zero one, four five six seven eight. Number do baar, aur jaan-boojh kar dheere.
    • Before six today would be great. Thanks, bye! Kab tak + shukriya. Bas — tees second poore.

    Voicemail ka saancha

    Naam → wajah → kya chahiye → number (do baar, dheere) → kab tak + thanks. Tees second se zyada nahi. Beep se pehle hi pehli line dimaag me taiyaar rakho — "Hi, this is…"

    33.6 Video call — "You're on mute"¶

    Video call me teen nayi museebatein hain — mute, camera aur screen share. Aur inke liye English me bilkul taiyaar lines hain jo har meeting me baar-baar aati hain. Inhe sunte hi pehchaan lo.

    Haalat English line Suno
    Shuruaat me Can everyone hear me OK?
    Kisi ka mic band Sorry, I think you're on mute.
    Aapka mic band tha Sorry, I was on mute.
    Screen dikhaani hai Let me share my screen.
    Dikh raha hai? Can you see my screen?
    Video atak gayi You froze for a second there.
    Dono ek saath bole Sorry, go ahead.
    Net kamzor I'll turn my camera off. My connection is a bit weak.

    Video call ke chaar niyam

    Na bolo to mute. Camera aankh ke level par. Do minute pehle join karo. Badi meeting me bolne se pehle naam: "This is Ravi — quick question." Aur dono ek saath bol pade to: "Sorry, go ahead." — pehle doosre ko mauka.

    33.7 Call khatam karna¶

    Phone par "bye" achaanak aata hai to lagta hai call kaat di. Ant me teen cheezein: kya tay hua, agla kadam, aur shukriya. Phir bye — aur bye ke baad thoda ruk kar phone rakho.

    • So, just to sum up, you'll send the report by Friday. Kya tay hua — ek line me dohraao.
    • I'll send you an email with the details. Agla kadam — aapki taraf se.
    • Is there anything else I can help you with? Kuch aur? — jab aap madad wali taraf ho.
    • Thanks for your time. Talk to you soon. Bye! Shukriya + jald baat karenge + bye.

    Mini-dialogue — vendor se call ka ant

    Haalat: aapne printer company se order ke baare me baat ki. Ab call samet rahe ho.

    • Aap: Great. So, just to confirm, the delivery is on Tuesday morning? Kya tay hua — pakka karo.
    • Vendor: That's right, between nine and eleven. "That's right" = haan, sahi.
    • Aap: Perfect. Could you send me the invoice by email? Agla kadam unki taraf se.
    • Vendor: Sure, I'll send it this afternoon. Anything else? "Anything else?" — ab ant ka signal.
    • Aap: No, that's everything. Thanks for your help. "Bas itna hi" — saaf aur polite.
    • Vendor: You're welcome. Have a good day! Garam ant.
    • Aap: You too. Bye! Bye ke baad ek second ruko, phir phone rakho.

    33.8 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Ye lines India me roz sunai deti hain aur desh ke andar sab samajh bhi lete hain. Par client, interview ya videsh ki call par yahi lines turant "translated" lagti hain.

    Phone ki aam galtiyan

    • Galat: Hello, I am Rahul this side.
    • Sahi: Hi, this is Rahul from Infotech.

    • Kyun: "Is taraf se Rahul" ka anuvaad. Phone par apna naam = "This is…" ya "It's…"

    • Galat: Who is speaking? / Who are you?

    • Sahi: May I ask who's calling?

    • Kyun: Seedha "Who are you?" bahut rookha lagta hai. Office me hamesha naram wala sawaal.

    • Galat: Am I audible?

    • Sahi: Can you hear me OK?

    • Kyun: "Audible" sahi shabd hai, par is sawaal me Indian English hi bolta hai. Duniya bhar me "Can you hear me?"

    • Galat: Your voice is cutting.

    • Sahi: You're breaking up. / The line is bad.

    • Kyun: "Aawaaz kat rahi hai" ka anuvaad. English me aawaaz "break up" hoti hai.

    • Galat: (Phone uthaate hi) Tell me.

    • Sahi: Hi Rahul! How can I help? / Hi, what's up?
    • Kyun: "Haan, bolo" ka anuvaad — English me hukum jaisa lagta hai. Pehle greeting, phir sawaal.

    33.9 Practice — Phone aur video call¶

    Exercise 1

    Aap reception par ho. Caller ka naam jaanna hai. Sabse polite line kaunsi hai?

    • May I ask who's calling?
    • Who are you?
    • Who is speaking there?
    • Tell your name first.
    Jawaab

    May I ask who's calling?

    'May I ask…' naram sawaal hai. 'Who are you?' phone par rookha aur shak bhara lagta hai.

    Exercise 2

    Network kharab hai aur saamne wale ki aawaaz toot rahi hai. Kya bologe?

    • Sorry, you're breaking up.
    • Your voice is cutting.
    • Am I audible?
    • What? What?
    Jawaab

    Sorry, you're breaking up.

    English me phone ki aawaaz 'break up' hoti hai. 'Your voice is cutting' Hindi ka seedha anuvaad hai.

    Exercise 3

    Email address me '_' ko phone par kaise bolte hain?

    • underscore
    • dash
    • slash
    • dot
    Jawaab

    underscore

    _ = underscore, - = hyphen ya dash, / = slash, . = dot. Email galat gaya to poora kaam atak jaata hai.

    Exercise 4

    Hi, ___ is Arjun from BlueLeaf Design.

    Hint: Phone par apna naam batane ka shabd — 'I am' nahi.

    Jawaab

    this

    Phone par 'This is Arjun' ya 'It's Arjun'. Saamne wala aapko dekh nahi raha, isliye 'this'.

    Exercise 5

    Sorry, could you ___ that, please? The line is bad.

    Hint: Dobara bolna.

    Jawaab

    repeat / say

    'Could you repeat that?' — sunai na de to sabse kaam ki line. Andaaza lagane se behtar.

    Exercise 6

    Video call ki is line me ek shabd Indian English wala hai — us par tap karo.

    Hi everyone, am I audible?

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: audible.

    Sahi roop: Hi everyone, can you hear me?

    'Am I audible?' sirf India me bola jaata hai. Duniya bhar me 'Can you hear me?'

    Exercise 7

    Sahi kram me lagao — kisi khaas insaan se baat karni hai.

    Shabd: to / Could / speak / Ms / I / Rao / please

    Jawaab

    Could I speak to Ms Rao please

    Could I + verb… — request ka sabse polite saancha.

    Exercise 8

    Line ko uske matlab se jodo.

    A B
    You're on mute. wo nahi hain, kuch kehna hai?
    I'll put you through. aapki video atak gayi
    Can I take a message? aapka mic band hai
    You froze. call aage jod raha hoon
    Jawaab

    You're on mute. — aapka mic band hai; I'll put you through. — call aage jod raha hoon; Can I take a message? — wo nahi hain, kuch kehna hai?; You froze. — aapki video atak gayi

    Ye chaaron lines itni aam hain ki inhe sunte hi matlab samajh aana chahiye — bina anuvaad ke.

    Exercise 9

    Saamne wale ki baat sunai nahi di. Ye line bolo.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Sorry, could you repeat that

    'could you' ko jodkar 'kud-ju' bolo. 'Sorry' se shuru karne se line naram ho jaati hai.

    Exercise 10

    Meeting me ek colleague bol raha hai par aawaaz nahi aa rahi. Use batao.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: You're on mute

    'You're' = 'yor'. Teen shabd, ek saans — aur saath me muskura do.

    Exercise 11

    Screen share kiya hai. Ab poochho.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: Can you see my screen

    Haan/na wala sawaal — 'screen' par aawaaz upar.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj apne naam, surname aur email ki spelling "for" wale shabdon ke saath zor se teen baar bolo. Agli baar customer care ko call karo to kam se kam ek line yahan se use karo.
