    Chapter 22: Kisi ki baat aage batana¶

    Kisi ki baat aage batana — reported speech

    Din bhar hum yahi karte hain: "Mummy ne kaha ki khaana khaa lo", "Boss bola ki kal aa jana". English me ise reported speech kehte hain, aur isme ek hi bada rule hai — tense ek kadam peeche chala jaata hai. Jaise kisi ne jo kaha, wo ab purani baat ban gayi, to uski tense bhi ek seedhi peeche ki seedhi utar jaati hai. Is chapter me wo seedhi (ladder) aapko tasveer me dikhegi.

    Ek line me

    If the reporting verb is in the past (said, told, asked), move the tense inside one step back. Bahar wala verb past me hai, to andar wala tense ek kadam peeche — present → past, past → past perfect.

    22.1 Paanch cheezein badalti hain¶

    # Kya badalta hai Direct Reported
    1 Quotes aur comma hat jaate hain, "that" aa jaata hai He said, "I am tired." He said that he was tired.
    2 Pronoun badalte hain "I am tired." … he was tired.
    3 Tense ek kadam peeche "I am tired." … he was tired.
    4 Samay ke shabd badalte hain "I will come tomorrow." … he would come the next day.
    5 Jagah ke shabd badalte hain "Sit here." … to sit there.

    22.2 Tense ki seedhi — ek kadam peeche¶

    Yahi is poore chapter ka dil hai. Bayein taraf wo tense hai jo bolne wale ne use kiya, aur dayein taraf wo tense jo report karte waqt aata hai. Dhyaan do — sab kuch ek kadam neeche utar raha hai.

    22.3 Pronoun kaise badalte hain¶

    Rule aasaan hai: pronoun report karne wale ke nazariye se badalta hai. "Main" bolne wale ka tha — ab wo "wo" ban jaata hai.

    Direct Reported Example
    I, me, my he/she, him/her, his/her He said, "I lost my pen." → He said he had lost his pen.
    we, us, our they, them, their They said, "We are busy." → They said they were busy.
    you, your me/him/her (kisse kaha, uske hisaab se) She told me, "You are late." → She told me I was late.
    he, she, it, they wahi rehte hain She said, "He is away." → She said he was away.

    22.4 Samay aur jagah ke shabd¶

    Direct Reported
    now then
    today that day
    tonight that night
    tomorrow the next day / the following day
    yesterday the previous day / the day before
    last night the previous night
    next week the following week
    two days ago two days before
    here there
    this / these that / those
    come go
    • Direct: She said, "I am going to Delhi tomorrow."
    • Reported: She said that she was going to Delhi the next day. Teen badlaav: I → she, am → was, tomorrow → the next day.

    22.5 Jab tense nahi badalti¶

    Har baar tense peeche nahi jaati. Chaar mauke aise hain jahan tense waisi hi rehti hai — aur ye jaanna bahut zaroori hai, warna English banaawati lagti hai.

    Mauka Example
    Reporting verb present me hai He says that he is busy.
    Hamesha sach rehne wali baat The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
    Baat abhi bhi sach hai She said she works in a bank. (aaj bhi karti hai)
    Pehle se Past Perfect hai He said, "I had finished." → He said he had finished.

    22.6 Sawaal ko report karna¶

    Yahan do cheezein hoti hain: "that" nahi aata, aur kram seedha ho jaata hai — helping verb wapas subject ke peeche chala jaata hai. Question mark bhi hat jaata hai.

    WH wale sawaal — WH-word hi joint banta hai¶

    Direct Reported
    He asked, "Where do you live?" He asked where I lived.
    She asked, "What is your name?" She asked what my name was.
    They asked, "Why did he leave?" They asked why he had left.

    Yes/No sawaal — "if" ya "whether" lagao¶

    Direct Reported
    He asked, "Are you ready?" He asked if I was ready.
    She asked, "Did you call him?" She asked whether I had called him.
    Mother asked, "Have you eaten?" Mother asked if I had eaten.

    Sawaal reported hone ke baad sawaal nahi rehta

    • Galat: He asked me where was I going?
    • Sahi: He asked me where I was going.
    • Kyun: Reported sawaal ab ek bayaan (statement) hai. Isliye kram seedha, aur aakhir me full stop — question mark nahi.

    22.7 Hukm, request aur salaah¶

    In sab me ek hi trick hai: "to" + V1. Negative ho to "not to" + V1. Aur reporting verb baat ke suroor ke hisaab se badalta hai.

    Kism Reporting verb Direct Reported
    Hukm ordered / told "Stand up!" He ordered me to stand up.
    Request requested / asked "Please help me." She requested me to help her.
    Salaah advised "You should rest." He advised me to rest.
    Mana karna forbade / told… not to "Don't touch it." She told me not to touch it.
    Sujhaav suggested "Let's go out." He suggested going out.
    Prarthna / dua prayed / wished "May you succeed!" He wished that I might succeed.

    22.8 say, tell, ask — teen shabd, teen aadat¶

    Ye teen shabd Indian English ki sabse badi reported-speech galti banate hain. Farq sirf itna hai ki kaun kisko leta hai.

    Verb Kaise aata hai Sahi
    say insaan seedha nahi aata — "to" lagta hai He said to me… / He said that…
    tell insaan seedha aata hai — "to" nahi He told me that…
    ask insaan seedha aata hai He asked me if…

    'She said me' — sabse aam galti

    • Galat: She said me that the shop was closed.
    • Sahi: She told me that the shop was closed.

    • Kyun: "Say" ke baad insaan seedha nahi aata. Do raaste hain: She told me… ya She said to me…. Aasaan tareeka — insaan ka naam lena ho to hamesha tell use karo.

    • Galat: He told that he would come.

    • Sahi: He said that he would come. / He told me that he would come.
    • Kyun: "Tell" ke baad kisko bataya — ye batana padta hai. Akela "told that" adhoora hai.

    22.9 Wo galtiyan jo hum sach me karte hain¶

    Reported speech ki top galtiyan

    • Galat: She said that she is coming tomorrow.
    • Sahi: She said that she was coming the next day.

    • Kyun: "said" past me hai, to andar ka tense ek kadam peeche — is → was, aur tomorrow → the next day.

    • Galat: He asked me that where I live.

    • Sahi: He asked me where I lived.

    • Kyun: Sawaal me "that" nahi aata. WH-word khud jodne ka kaam karta hai.

    • Galat: My father told me that study hard.

    • Sahi: My father told me to study hard.

    • Kyun: Hukm aur salaah ke liye "to + V1" — "that" nahi.

    • Galat: She asked me do I know the way.

    • Sahi: She asked me if I knew the way.

    • Kyun: Yes/no sawaal ke liye "if" ya "whether", aur kram seedha. "do/does/did" gayab ho jaate hain.

    • Galat: He said that he will help me.

    • Sahi: He said that he would help me.

    • Kyun: will → would. Isi tarah can → could, may → might, must → had to.

    • Galat: The teacher said that water boiled at 100 degrees.

    • Sahi: The teacher said that water boils at 100 degrees.

    • Kyun: Hamesha sach rehne wali baat me tense peeche nahi jaati.

    • Galat: He told me that don't go there.

    • Sahi: He told me not to go there.
    • Kyun: Mana karne ke liye "not to + V1" — chhota aur seedha roop.

    Chaar kadam, har baar

    1. Kism pehchaano — bayaan, sawaal, ya hukm?
    2. Sahi joint lagao — that / if-whether / WH-word / to.
    3. Pronoun badlo.
    4. Tense ek kadam peeche, aur samay-jagah ke shabd badlo.

    22.10 Practice — Reported speech¶

    Exercise 1

    Report karo: She said, "I am very tired."

    • She said that she is very tired.
    • She said that she was very tired.
    • She said that I was very tired.
    • She told that she was very tired.

    Hint: said past me hai, to andar kya hoga?

    Jawaab

    She said that she was very tired.

    Do badlaav hue: pronoun I → she, aur tense am → was. 'said' past me hai isliye tense ek kadam peeche jaati hai.

    Exercise 2

    Report karo: He asked, "Where is the bank?"

    • He asked where is the bank.
    • He asked that where was the bank.
    • He asked where the bank was.
    • He asked where was the bank?

    Hint: Sawaal reported hone par kram seedha ho jaata hai.

    Jawaab

    He asked where the bank was.

    Reported sawaal ab bayaan hai — isliye 'the bank was', aur aakhir me full stop. 'That' bhi nahi aata.

    Exercise 3

    Report karo: Mother said, "Don't play in the sun."

    • Mother said that don't play in the sun.
    • Mother told not to play in the sun.
    • Mother told me not to play in the sun.
    • Mother said me not to play in the sun.

    Hint: Mana karna hai.

    Jawaab

    Mother told me not to play in the sun.

    Mana karne ke liye 'not to + V1'. Aur 'tell' ke baad kisko kaha — ye batana zaroori hai, isliye 'told me'.

    Exercise 4

    Kis vaakya me tense peeche NAHI jaayegi?

    • He said, "I am ill."
    • She said, "The earth moves round the sun."
    • They said, "We are leaving."
    • He said, "I will call you."

    Hint: Kaunsi baat hamesha sach hai?

    Jawaab

    She said, "The earth moves round the sun."

    Hamesha sach rehne wali baat (universal truth) me tense waisi hi rehti hai — 'She said that the earth moves round the sun.'

    Exercise 5

    He said, "I will come tomorrow." → He said that he ___ come the next day.

    Hint: will ka reported roop?

    Jawaab

    would

    will → would. Isi tarah can → could, may → might, shall → should.

    Exercise 6

    She asked, "Are you coming?" → She asked ___ I was coming.

    Hint: Yes/no sawaal ka joint.

    Jawaab

    if / whether

    Yes/no sawaal report karte waqt 'if' ya 'whether' lagta hai. Dono sahi hain — 'whether' thoda zyada formal hai.

    Exercise 7

    Ravi ___ me that the train was late.

    Hint: Insaan ('me') seedha aa raha hai — to kaunsa verb?

    Jawaab

    told

    'Tell' ke baad insaan seedha aata hai — told me. 'Said me' galat hai; 'said to me' chalta hai.

    Exercise 8

    Is vaakya me ek galti hai — galat shabd par tap karo.

    My sister said me that she would help.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: said.

    Sahi roop: My sister told me that she would help.

    'Say' ke baad insaan seedha nahi aata. 'Told me' bolna hai, ya 'said to me'.

    Exercise 9

    Galat shabd dhoondo.

    He asked me that where I was going.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: that.

    Sahi roop: He asked me where I was going.

    Sawaal report karte waqt 'that' nahi lagta. WH-word khud dono hisson ko jod deta hai.

    Exercise 10

    Ek shabd galat hai — pakdo.

    The doctor advised him that rest for a week.

    Jawaab

    Galat shabd: that.

    Sahi roop: The doctor advised him to rest for a week.

    Salaah aur hukm ke liye 'to + V1' aata hai — 'to rest'. 'That' yahan nahi chalta.

    Exercise 11

    Sahi kram me reported vaakya banao.

    Shabd: she / work / finished / me / that / her / told / She / had

    Jawaab

    She told me that she had finished her work

    'Tell' ke baad insaan (me), phir 'that', phir tense ek kadam peeche — 'has finished' ban gaya 'had finished'.

    Exercise 12

    Direct roop ko uske reported roop se jodo.

    A B
    I write he would write
    I wrote he could write
    I will write he had to write
    I can write he wrote
    I must write he had written
    Jawaab

    I write — he wrote; I wrote — he had written; I will write — he would write; I can write — he could write; I must write — he had to write

    Present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could, must → had to. Seedhi ka har kadam ek hi disha me utarta hai.

    Exercise 13

    Ab ye line bol ke dekho — roz kaam aane wali reported speech.

    Jawaab

    Bolo: He told me that he would call me the next day

    Teen cheezein ek saath sahi karni hain: 'told me' (said me nahi), 'would' (will nahi), aur 'the next day' (tomorrow nahi). Bolkar practice karne se ye teeno saath aane lagte hain.

    Aage kya karein

    Aaj raat sone se pehle, din bhar ki teen baatein kisi ko report karke bolo — "My boss told me that…", "My friend asked me if…", "Mummy told me to…". Teen vaakya, roz. Ek hafte me ye seedhi apne-aap dimaag me baith jaayegi.
