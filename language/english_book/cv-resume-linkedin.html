    Chapter 52: CV, resume & LinkedIn¶

    CV and LinkedIn — action verbs, bullet points, profile summary

    A recruiter often spends only ten to fifteen seconds on a CV. In that time they are not looking for your "hard-working and sincere" personality — they are looking for what you did and what result it had. This chapter shows you how to structure a CV, how to write every bullet with one formula, what freshers should do, which old Indian CV habits to drop, and how to write your headline and About section on LinkedIn.

    52.1 CV or resume — what is the difference?¶

    In India the two words mean almost the same thing, and nobody will reject you for using the "wrong" word. Still, it is good to know the difference, especially if you are applying abroad.

    Point Resume CV
    Length 1–2 pages UK/India: 2 pages · Academic: long
    Used mostly in US, Canada, private jobs in India UK, Europe, academic jobs
    What it contains Work chosen for this job Like a resume in the UK; the full record in academia
    Change it for each job? Yes Yes (less for an academic CV)

    In one line

    Fresher or under 10 years of experience: 1 page. More than that: 2 pages, no more. For every job, adjust your CV a little to use that job's words. Sending one CV everywhere = fitting nowhere.

    • Please find my CV attached. The simplest line for sending a CV by email. The full email is in Writing emails and the cover note is in Applications and messages.
    • I've tailored my resume to the job description. tailor = to sew to someone's measurements — here: to adjust it to fit the job.

    52.2 Structure — from top to bottom¶

    A recruiter's eye starts at the top left and moves down. So the most important things go at the top. People with experience put experience first; freshers put education and projects first.

    Section What to write What not to write
    Header Name, city, phone, email, LinkedIn Full address, a "Curriculum Vitae" heading
    Profile A 3–4 line introduction "Objective: To work in a reputed organisation…"
    Experience Role, company, years, 3–5 bullets A long list of every duty
    Skills Tools, languages, certifications "Hard-working, team player"
    Education Degree, college, year, CGPA The whole story from Class 5

    52.3 Profile summary — a three-line introduction¶

    The profile summary is the "trailer" of your CV. You write it without "I", in short sentences. The formula: who you are + how much experience + what you are especially good at + one big result + what you want next.

    Summary formula

    Role + years + specialty + one result + goal. "Data analyst with 3 years' experience in fintech. Built dashboards that cut reporting time by 50%. Looking to move into product analytics." Drop "I am" and "my" — on a CV they are understood without being written.

    ✗

    Objective: To work in a challenging environment where I can utilise my skills for the growth of the organisation.

    ✓

    Profile: Mechanical engineer with 2 years' experience in quality control at an auto-parts plant. Reduced defect rates by 18% through new inspection checklists. Seeking a process-improvement role.

    who you are

    what you have already done

    • Customer support lead with five years' experience in e-commerce. five years' experience — the apostrophe comes after the s (the experience of five years).
    • Final-year B.Com student with strong Excel and Tally skills. A fresher's profile — degree + skills.
    • Seeking an entry-level role in digital marketing. "Seeking" / "Looking to move into" — the closing goal line.

    52.4 Action verbs — words that bring your work to life¶

    "Responsible for" and "worked on" do not tell the recruiter what you yourself did. Start every bullet with a strong past-tense verb. For your current job the present tense is also fine — but keep to one tense within a single job.

    Type of work Verbs Example
    Running a team led, managed, coordinated, mentored Led a team of six.
    Making something built, designed, developed, launched Launched a mobile app.
    Making things better improved, increased, streamlined Increased sales by 20%.
    Reducing reduced, cut, lowered, saved Cut costs by ₹4 lakh.
    Understanding analysed, identified, researched Identified a billing error.
    Communicating presented, negotiated, trained Trained 40 new staff.
    Solving problems resolved, fixed, solved Resolved 50+ tickets a day.
    • Led a team of six to deliver the project two weeks early. Delivered the project two weeks early with a team of six people.
    • Built an inventory tracker in Excel used by four branches. Made a stock tracker in Excel — four branches use it.
    • Reduced customer complaints by 30% in six months. Complaints went down by 30% in six months.
    • Negotiated a new contract with the courier partner, saving ₹2 lakh a year. A new contract with the courier company — the company saves ₹2 lakh a year.
    • Trained 25 interns on the new billing software. Taught 25 interns the new software.

    No 'I' before the verb

    Start the bullet straight with the verb: Led…, Built…, Reduced… — not "I led…", and definitely not "I was responsible for…". For stronger verbs, see the Verb upgrades chapter too.

    ✗

    Responsible for handling social media.

    ✓

    Grew Instagram followers from 2,000 to 15,000 in a year.

    changed

    52.5 The bullet formula — verb + task + result + number¶

    Every good bullet is built from four pieces. Even if you have no number, always write the result — how many people, how much time, how much money, how many percent.

    • Automated monthly GST reports using Python scripts, cutting preparation time from three days to four hours. "cutting / saving / increasing…" — add the result with an -ing word.
    • Redesigned the onboarding emails, which increased trial sign-ups by 22%. You can also add the result with "which…".
    • Streamlined the loan approval process from ten steps to six, halving waiting time. streamline = to remove unnecessary steps and make something simpler; halve = to cut in half.
    • Resolved an average of 45 customer queries a day with a 95% satisfaction score. Even if nothing changed, say "how much" — how many per day, how satisfied.
    • Identified a pricing error that was costing the company ₹50,000 a month. Even a small discovery can be a big result — say it in money.

    Where do you get the numbers?

    Ask yourself: how many people? how much time? how much money? how many percent? If you have no exact number, give an honest estimate: about 30%, 50+ clients. An estimate is fine, a lie is not — you will be asked about it in the interview.

    ✗

    Worked on sales.

    ✓

    Closed 40 new B2B accounts in Gujarat, exceeding the annual target by 15%.

    ✗

    Did coding of website and handled the testing part.

    ✓

    Developed a college events website in React and tested it with 300 students before launch.

    Developed

    tested

    ✗

    Increased sales. Am managing a team of 4. Prepared reports.

    ✓

    Increased sales… · Managed a team of four… · Prepared reports…

    52.6 Skills, education and a fresher's CV¶

    Skills — show them, don't just say them¶

    In the skills section, write things that can be tested: software, tools, programming languages, spoken languages, certificates. Show "hard-working, honest, team player" with proof in your bullets, not in the skills list.

    • Skills: Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP), SQL, Power BI, Tally ERP Use brackets to show what you can do inside a tool.
    • Languages: English (fluent), Hindi (native), Marathi (conversational) Give the level too — fluent / native / conversational / basic.

    Education — degree, college, year¶

    • B.Tech in Computer Science, Shivaji Institute of Technology, Pune, 2025 · CGPA 8.4/10 Degree in a subject, college, year, score. Write the score "out of 10" — CGPA scales are different abroad.
    • Class XII (CBSE), 2021 · 91% Once you have some experience, you can drop Class X/XII.

    If you're a fresher — what goes in place of experience?¶

    A fresher has no job, but does have work: internships, college projects, hackathons, freelance work, organising a college fest, NSS/NCC, giving tuitions. Write these with the same formula — verb + task + result + number. The order: Profile → Education → Projects / Internships → Skills → Achievements.

    • Organised a three-day tech fest with 1,200 participants and a ₹3 lakh budget. A college fest is real work too — budget, people, deadline.
    • Completed a two-month internship at a CA firm; reconciled accounts for 15 clients. reconcile = to check that two sets of accounts match.
    • Taught maths to 20 Class 8 students as a volunteer; average scores rose by 12 marks. Volunteer work + a result.

    Before → after: a fresher's CV

    Before

    CURRICULUM VITAE · [photo] · Name: Rohit Kumar · Father's Name: Shri R. P. Kumar · DOB: 12/03/2003 · Marital Status: Unmarried · Objective: To work in a reputed company and grow with it. · Strengths: Hard-working, sincere, punctual. · Hobbies: Listening music, surfing net. · Declaration: I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge.

    After

    • ROHIT KUMAR · Lucknow · 98xxx xxxxx · rohit.k@mail.com Only the name in the heading — not "Curriculum Vitae". The photo, father's name, DOB and marital status are gone.
    • Profile: BBA graduate with a summer internship in retail sales. Comfortable with Excel and customer calls. Seeking a sales-trainee role. A three-line profile instead of an objective.
    • Sales Intern, a fashion retail store, Lucknow (May–July 2024): Helped the store hit its monthly target by upselling accessories; added ₹60,000 in sales. Proof with a number, instead of writing "hard-working".

    The declaration and hobbies were removed. The space that was freed went to projects and skills.

    52.7 Common mistakes in Indian CVs¶

    Many habits come from old government forms. In private companies and for jobs abroad they make a CV look dated — and some even raise the risk of discrimination. However, if the job ad itself asks for a photo or date of birth (some government, Gulf or airline jobs), then give them.

    Remove all of these

    • Wrong: Photo, Father's Name, Date of Birth, Marital Status, Religion
    • Right: Name · City · Phone · Email · LinkedIn

    • Why: They have nothing to do with the work, and they increase unconscious bias. Save the space, show your work. (Give them only if the job ad asks.)

    • Wrong: I hereby declare that the above information is true to the best of my knowledge.

    • Right: (Nothing — delete the line.)

    • Why: This is the language of an affidavit. A private-sector CV does not need it; a government form that asks for it is a different matter.

    • Wrong: Heading: CURRICULUM VITAE / RESUME

    • Right: Heading: ANANYA IYER

    • Why: Everyone knows it is a CV. Make your name the biggest line.

    • Wrong: Hobbies: Listening music, surfing net, reading books.

    • Right: Interests: Long-distance running (2 half-marathons), Carnatic music.

    • Why: Grammar: listening to music. And "surfing net" says nothing. Write a hobby only when it says something about you — otherwise drop the section.

    • Wrong: I have done my B.Com from Delhi University.

    • Right: B.Com, University of Delhi, 2024
    • Why: A CV uses fragments, not full sentences. When speaking: I did / completed my B.Com at Delhi University.

    Never lie, never copy-paste

    Do not inflate your numbers. In an interview, any bullet can be questioned: "Walk me through how you cut the time by 50%." If you cannot explain a bullet for 2 minutes, do not keep it on your CV. To prepare, see Interview and office.

    • Walk me through your CV. The interviewer: explain your CV to me from the beginning.
    • Sure. I'll start with my current role and then go back. Your answer follows the CV's order too — newest first.

    52.8 LinkedIn — headline and About¶

    Your LinkedIn headline appears right under your name — it also shows up in search. Instead of the default "Student at XYZ College", say what you do and what you are good at.

    Headline formula

    Role | Specialty | Proof or value — "Data Analyst | SQL & Power BI | Turning sales data into weekly decisions". You get about 220 characters — keep it short and clear.

    Weak headline Strong headline
    Looking for opportunities Final-year B.Tech (CSE) | Java & Spring Boot | 2 internships
    Working at ABC Pvt Ltd HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025
    Hard-working professional Chartered Accountant | GST & audit | Helping SMEs stay compliant

    The About section — first person, like a conversation¶

    Unlike a CV, the LinkedIn About section is written with "I" — as if you were introducing yourself to someone you have just met. Three short paragraphs: what you do, one or two results, and what kind of work or people you want to connect with.

    • I'm a data analyst who helps sales teams make faster decisions. The first line — what you do and who for.
    • At my current company, I built a weekly dashboard that replaced six manual reports. One result — the same bullet formula, but with "I".
    • I'm currently exploring roles in product analytics. Feel free to reach out. The last line — what you want. reach out = to contact someone.

    ✗

    Myself Priya, a passionate and dynamic professional.

    ✓

    I'm Priya, a UX designer who makes banking apps easier for first-time users.

    Indian English habits

    what you actually do

    52.9 Practice — CV and LinkedIn¶

    Exercise 1

    How many pages should a fresher's CV be?

    • 1 page
    • 2 pages
    • 3 pages
    • The longer the better
    Answer

    1 page

    Fresher or little experience: 1 page. 10+ years: up to 2 pages.

    Exercise 2

    Which bullet is the strongest?

    • Reduced invoice errors by 40% by adding Excel checks.
    • Responsible for invoices.
    • Worked on invoice-related tasks.
    • I was handling the invoice part.
    Answer

    Reduced invoice errors by 40% by adding Excel checks.

    Verb + task + result + number — the others only state a duty.

    Exercise 3

    ___ a team of six to deliver the project two weeks early. (past of lead)

    Hint: Running a team — past tense.

    Answer

    Led / led

    lead → led. CV bullets start straight with a past-tense verb.

    Exercise 4

    Find the mistake in the hobbies line.

    Hobbies: listening music and travelling.

    Answer

    Wrong word: listening.

    Corrected: Hobbies: listening to music and travelling.

    listen needs 'to' after it: listening to music.

    Exercise 5

    Which of these should you keep in a private company's CV?

    • LinkedIn profile link
    • Father's name
    • Marital status
    • I hereby declare…
    Answer

    LinkedIn profile link

    In the header, only name, city, phone, email, LinkedIn. The rest only if the job ad asks.

    Exercise 6

    Match the work to the right verb.

    A B
    Ran a team Launched
    Reduced expenses Trained
    Built an app and released it Led
    Taught people Cut
    Answer

    Ran a team — Led; Reduced expenses — Cut; Built an app and released it — Launched; Taught people — Trained

    Every kind of work has a strong action verb.

    Exercise 7

    Find the weak part of this bullet.

    Responsible for managing the company Instagram page.

    Answer

    Wrong word: Responsible.

    Corrected: Grew the company Instagram page from 2,000 to 15,000 followers.

    'Responsible for' is only a duty — you need an action verb and a result.

    Exercise 8

    What should NOT be in a profile summary?

    • To work in a challenging environment for the growth of the organisation
    • Years of experience
    • One big result
    • What you want next
    Answer

    To work in a challenging environment for the growth of the organisation

    This is the old 'objective' — the same on every CV, it tells you nothing.

    Exercise 9

    Customer support lead with five ___ experience in e-commerce.

    Hint: The experience of five years — where does the apostrophe go?

    Answer

    years' / years’

    five years' experience — the apostrophe comes after the plural 'years'.

    Exercise 10

    Which is the best LinkedIn headline?

    • HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025
    • Looking for opportunities
    • Hard-working and sincere professional
    • Working at ABC Pvt Ltd
    Answer

    HR Executive | Campus hiring | Hired 120+ freshers in 2025

    Role | Specialty | Proof — the headline formula.

    Exercise 11

    How do you write the LinkedIn About section?

    • First person — 'I'm…, I built…'
    • In fragments without verbs
    • Third person — 'He is…'
    • Just a list of skills
    Answer

    First person — 'I'm…, I built…'

    A CV has no 'I'; the LinkedIn About uses 'I', like a conversation.

    Exercise 12

    From what you hear, which CV bullet is the best?

    Read this aloud or have someone read it to you:

    In my last job I handled customer complaints. I set up a new ticket system, and our average response time came down from two days to six hours.

    • Set up a ticket system that cut response time from two days to six hours.
    • Responsible for customer complaints.
    • Handled complaints and tickets.
    • Worked in customer support for two days.
    Answer

    Set up a ticket system that cut response time from two days to six hours.

    Verb (Set up) + task (ticket system) + result + number (2 days down to 6 hours).

    Exercise 13

    What did the response time become?

    Read this aloud or have someone read it to you:

    In my last job I handled customer complaints. I set up a new ticket system, and our average response time came down from two days to six hours.

    • Six hours
    • Two days
    • Two hours
    • Six days
    Answer

    Six hours

    'came down from two days to six hours' — it fell from 2 days to 6 hours.

    Exercise 14

    Listen to this bullet and write it down.

    Dictation. Have someone read the sentence aloud, then write it down.

    Answer

    Led a team of five to launch the app in three months.

    Led (past of lead) + team + launch + number.

    Exercise 15

    Put the bullet in the right order.

    Words: complaints / percent / in / 30 / customer / months / by / Reduced / six

    Answer

    Reduced customer complaints by 30 percent in six months

    Verb + task + result (by 30 percent) + time.

    Exercise 16

    Find the tense mistake in one job's bullets.

    Increased sales by 20%. Am managing a team of four.

    Answer

    Wrong word: Am.

    Corrected: Increased sales by 20%. Managed a team of four.

    A CV bullet has no helping verb like 'am', and one job uses one tense.

    Exercise 17

    Say one of your own bullets out loud, as in an interview.

    Answer

    Say: I reduced customer complaints by thirty per cent

    When speaking, add 'I' — you don't write it on a CV, but it is needed in conversation.

    What to do next

    Today, rewrite three bullets from your CV with the formula. Then see Applications and messages for the cover note you send with your CV, and Interview and office for interview preparation.
