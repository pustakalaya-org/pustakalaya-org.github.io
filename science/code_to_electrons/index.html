    Preface¶

    Computing is one of humanity’s greatest achievements, yet for most people, it remains mysterious. We type code on keyboards, click buttons on screens, and somehow our intentions become reality through an invisible process involving billions of microscopic switches.

    This book demystifies that process. We follow a simple addition operation through twelve distinct stages of transformation, from the high-level code you write to the electrons flowing through silicon transistors. Each stage represents decades of engineering innovation and human ingenuity.

    Our journey spans multiple disciplines: software engineering, computer architecture, digital logic design, semiconductor physics, and quantum mechanics. Yet we present it all in an accessible way, with plenty of examples, analogies, and visual aids.

    Whether you’re a student learning computer science, a software developer curious about what happens "under the hood," or simply someone fascinated by how our digital world works, this book will give you a deep appreciation for the engineering marvel that is modern computing.
