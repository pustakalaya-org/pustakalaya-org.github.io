    Editions and Translations of the Principal Texts¶

    References to primary texts in this volume give chapter, verse or section numbers rather than pages, so that they can be found in any good edition. The list below names, for the texts most often cited, the edition or translation whose numbering is followed and which readers may consult for the full text. Where no edition is named, the numbering is the one in general scholarly use. Translations quoted in the displayed passages are made for this volume unless otherwise stated.

    Text Edition or translation
    Vedic and Upaniṣadic
    Ṛgveda Śākala recension; English translation Jamison and Brereton (2014)
    Early Upaniṣads (Bṛhadāraṇyaka, Chāndogya, Taittirīya, Aitareya, Kaṭha, Muṇḍaka, Praśna, Māṇḍūkya, Īśā) Text and translation Olivelle (1998); Radhakrishnan (1953) for other Upaniṣads
    Philosophy and psychology
    Sāṃkhyakārikā Verse numbers as in Larson (1979)
    Yogasūtra with the bhāṣya Sūtra numbers in standard use; translation with digest of commentaries Bryant (2009)
    Nyāyasūtra, Brahmasūtra Sūtra numbers in standard use; summaries in Potter (1981)
    Bhagavad Gītā Chapter and verse of the vulgate, as in the critical edition of the Mahābhārata (book 6)
    Vākyapadīya Verse numbers of both Rau’s and Iyer’s editions are given where they differ; study Iyer (1969)
    Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya Translation Singh (1963)
    Yogavāsiṣṭha Abridged translation Venkatesananda (1984)
    Buddhist and Jain
    Pali discourses (Dīgha, Majjhima, Saṃyutta, Aṅguttara Nikāya) Cited by collection and discourse number; Majjhima Nikāya in Ñāṇamoli and Bodhi (1995)
    Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha Text and translation Bodhi (1993)
    Visuddhimagga Translation Buddhaghosa (1956), cited by chapter
    Abhidharmakośabhāṣya English translation Vasubandhu (1988–1990), cited by chapter
    Mūlamadhyamakakārikā Translation and commentary Garfield (1995)
    Tattvārthasūtra Sūtra numbers in standard use (they differ slightly between recensions); doctrine in Jaini (1979)
    Medicine, law, polity and the arts
    Carakasaṃhitā Text and translation Sharma (1981–1994), cited by section, chapter and verse
    Manusmṛti Critical edition and translation Olivelle (2005)
    Arthaśāstra Translation Olivelle (2013)
    Nāṭyaśāstra Translation Ghosh (1950), cited by chapter and verse
    Rasa texts (Abhinavagupta, Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka as reported, Viśvanātha and others) Translations Pollock (2016); Gnoli (1956)
    Dhvanyāloka with the Locana Translation Ingalls et al. (1990)
    Mahābhārata, Rāmāyaṇa Critical editions (Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune; Oriental Institute, Baroda)

    Fawāʾid al-Fuʾād Translation Sijzī (1992)

    Ḥujjat Allāh al-Bāligha Translation Walī Allāh (1996)
    Guru Granth Sāhib Cited by page of the standard 1,430-page printed edition; selections in Shackle and Mandair (2005)
