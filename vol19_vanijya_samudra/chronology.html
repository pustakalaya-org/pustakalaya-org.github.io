    Chronology¶

    The dates below are approximate and many are disputed; where scholarly opinion varies, a range is given. Traditional dates are not used. The chronology covers the period treated in detail in this volume, to the introduction of metric weights in 1958, and adds a few later dates for events discussed in Chapter 21.

    Date Event or development
    c. 2600–1900 BCE Mature Harappan cities; standard weights and seals; trade with the Gulf and Mesopotamia (Meluhha in Akkadian texts)
    c. 2400–1900 BCE Mature phase at Lothal; function of its brick basin (dock or reservoir) debated
    c. 1500–1200 BCE Core hymns of the Ṛgveda
    c. 1200–500 BCE Later Vedic texts
    c. 6th–5th c. BCE Second urbanisation in the Ganga valley; spread of Northern Black Polished Ware
    c. 5th–4th c. BCE Earliest punch-marked silver coins (date debated)
    c. 480–400 BCE The Buddha (short chronology)
    c. 321–185 BCE Maurya empire; Megasthenes at Pāṭaliputra c. 300 BCE
    c. 268–232 BCE Reign of Aśoka; edicts mention wells, rest-houses and trees along roads
    2nd c. BCE–1st c. CE Indo-Greek bilingual coinage; donative inscriptions at Bharhut and Sāñcī
    c. 50 BCE–300 CE Composition and redaction of the Arthaśāstra in its present form (Olivelle)
    1st c. CE Periplus Maris Erythraei (mid-century); Pliny’s Natural History (c. 77 CE); peak of the Indo-Roman trade
    1st–3rd c. CE Kuṣāṇa empire and gold coinage; Sātavāhanas and Western Kṣatrapas; Uṣavadāta’s endowments with weavers’ guilds at Nasik
    2nd c. CE Muziris papyrus; Ptolemy’s Geography
    c. 320–550 CE Gupta empire and gold coinage; guild sealings at Vaiśālī (Basarh)
    436–437 and 472–473 CE Dates in the Mandasor inscription of the silk-weavers’ guild
    6th c. CE Varāhamihira’s Bṛhatsaṃhitā; Cosmas Indicopleustes on Sri Lanka
    671–695 Yijing travels by sea to India via Śrīvijaya
    682 Kedukan Bukit inscription of Śrīvijaya (Old Malay)
    711–712 Umayyad conquest of Sindh; Daybul taken
    c. 800 Borobudur built under the Śailendras
    c. 849 Kollam (Quilon) copper plates; Maṇigrāmam and Añjuvaṇṇam
    9th–10th c. Arab and Persian trade from Sīrāf and Baṣra to India and China
    c. 9th c. Beginning of zinc distillation at Zawar, Rajasthan
    985–1014 Rājarāja I Chola; conquests in Sri Lanka and the Maldives
    c. 1010 Rājarājeśvara temple at Thanjavur completed; its inscriptions record the temple’s lands, staff and endowments
    c. 1010–1055 Bhoja of Dhārā, to whom the Yuktikalpataru is attributed
    1025 Rājendra I’s naval raid on Śrīvijaya and its ports
    1088 Tamil merchant inscription at Barus (Sumatra)
    12th c. Abraham Ben Yijū at Mangalore (Cairo Geniza letters); Angkor Wat built
    1206–1526 Delhi Sultanate
    1281 Tamil inscription at Quanzhou, China
    c. 1303–1316 ʿAlāʾ al-Dīn Khaljī’s market regulations at Delhi
    c. 1318 Ṭhakkura Pheru’s Dravyaparīkṣā on the coins of the Delhi mint
    1329–1332 Muḥammad bin Tughluq’s token currency
    1333–1347 Ibn Baṭṭūṭa in India, the Maldives and Bengal
    1336–1565 Vijayanagara
    1347 Bahmanī sultanate founded in the Deccan
    1350s Ilyās Shāh unites Bengal; independent sultanate to the sixteenth century
    1405–1433 Voyages of Zheng He; Ma Huan accompanies three of them and describes Calicut and Bengal
    1414 A Bengal embassy brings a giraffe to the Ming court
    1487–1493 African (Habshī) commanders rule Bengal as sultans
    c. 1490 Ibn Mājid’s navigational treatise Kitāb al-Fawāʾid
    1498 Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut
    1509 Battle of Diu
    1540–1545 Sher Shāh Sūr; silver rupiya; great road from Bengal to the Indus
    1556–1605 Akbar; zabṭ and the dahsāla revenue system; Āʾīn-i Akbarī (c. 1595)
    1563 Akbar abolishes the tax on Hindu pilgrims
    1568 Synagogue of the Paradesi Jews built at Cochin
    1600 and 1602 English and Dutch East India Companies founded
    c. 1600–1626 Malik ʿAmbar, regent of Ahmadnagar
    1612–1613 English factory at Surat
    17th c. Indian merchant communities in Iran, Central Asia and Astrakhan; peak of Gujarati and Coromandel textile exports
    1681–1707 Aurangzeb campaigns in the Deccan; debate on the “jāgīrdārī crisis”
    1684 Treaty of Tingmosgang reserves the pashm trade to Ladakh and Kashmir
    1686–1687 Mughal conquest of Bijapur and Golconda
    1736 Lowjee Nusserwanjee Wadia brought to Bombay as master builder
    early 18th c. Kānhojī Āṅgre’s Maratha navy on the Konkan coast
    1757 Battle of Plassey
    1765 East India Company receives the dīwānī of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa
    1770 Great Bengal famine
    1774 George Bogle’s mission to Tashilhunpo, seeking trade with Tibet
    1788–1792 War between Nepal and Tibet, ended by Qing intervention
    1793 Permanent Settlement in Bengal
    1799 Fall of Srirangapatna; death of Tīpū Sulṭān
    1817 HMS Trincomalee launched at Bombay
    1833 Bengal regulation fixes the tolā at 180 grains, the standard sīr at 80 tolās and the man at 40 sīrs
    1834 Beginning of indentured emigration to Mauritius
    1840 Pilgrim taxes at Purī, Gayā and Prayāga abolished
    1843 Indian Slavery Act withdraws legal recognition from slavery
    1850s First cotton mills in Bombay and jute mills near Calcutta; first railways
    1860 Indian Penal Code makes the buying and selling of slaves an offence
    1863 Religious Endowments Act withdraws the government from managing temples and mosques
    1876–1878 Great Famine in south and west India
    1892 M. G. Ranade’s lecture “Indian Political Economy” at Poona
    1893 Indian mints closed to the free coinage of silver
    1898–1899 Rupee fixed at 1s. 4d.
    1903–1904 Younghusband’s armed mission to Tibet; trade marts opened at Gyantse, Yatung and Gartok
    1901 Naoroji, Poverty and Un-British Rule in India
    1905 Swadeshi movement in Bengal; Arthaśāstra manuscript reaches Mysore
    1906 V. O. Chidambaram Pillai’s Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company
    1913 Keynes, Indian Currency and Finance; Mussalman Wakf Validating Act
    1923 B. R. Ambedkar, The Problem of the Rupee
    1925 Sikh Gurdwaras Act
    1926–1927 Hilton Young Commission; rupee fixed at 1s. 6d.; Madras Hindu Religious Endowments Act
    1930 Gandhi’s Salt March to Dandi
    1931 Indian Statistical Institute founded at Calcutta
    1934 M. Visvesvaraya, Planned Economy for India
    1935 Reserve Bank of India established
    1938 National Planning Committee of the Congress
    1939 Standards of Weights Act
    1943 Bengal famine
    1944 The Bombay Plan, the Gandhian Plan and the People’s Plan
    1950 Planning Commission set up; National Sample Survey begun
    1956 Second Five-Year Plan (Mahalanobis strategy); Standards of Weights and Measures Act
    1958 Metric weights introduced (from 1 October)
    Some later dates discussed in Chapter 21
    1961 Shipping Corporation of India founded
    1999 Geographical Indications of Goods Act (in force 2003)
    2006 Border trade through the Nathu La reopened
    2017–2018 Navika Sagar Parikrama: INSV Tarini sailed round the world by women officers
    2025 INSV Kaundinya, a sewn-plank sailing ship, inducted into the Navy
