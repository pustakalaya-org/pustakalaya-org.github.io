    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to hardware roots of trust, TPMs, secure and measured boot, trusted execution, attestation, and the root CAs and certificates that secure the internet. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, and a table of the chips, standards, protocols, products and terms you need to know — from the silicon that boots first to the root certificate in your browser.
