    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to quantum machine learning — encoding data in qubits, variational circuits, quantum kernels, fault-tolerant algorithms, tools, honest benchmarks and careers. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, a diagram, and a table of the algorithms, circuits, techniques, tools & papers you need to know.
