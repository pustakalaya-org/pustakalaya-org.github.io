    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to quantum computing — qubits, gates, circuits, algorithms, hardware, error correction, programming, applications, cryptography and careers. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, a diagram, and a table of the concepts, algorithms, hardware, tools & milestones you need to know.
