    Glossary¶

    Terms are Sanskrit unless marked (Pa. = Pali, Pk. = Prakrit, Ta. = Tamil, Mar. = Marathi, Gu. = Gujarati, Ar. = Arabic, Pe. = Persian, Hi. = Hindi).

    Term Meaning
    aḍhāī dvīpa (Pk./Hi.) “Two and a half continents”: in Jain cosmography, the region of the universe where human beings live; also a painted diagram of it
    akam, puṟam (Ta.) “Interior” and “exterior”: the two genres of classical Tamil poetry, on love and on public life
    akṣa, akṣāṃśa Terrestrial latitude
    aṅgula Finger-breadth; the smallest common unit of length
    ānūpa Wet, marshy land
    Āryāvarta “Abode of the Āryas”: in normative texts, the land between the Himālaya and the Vindhya
    aṭavī, āṭavika Forest country and its peoples, distinguished in Aśokan and Gupta inscriptions from the settled subjects of a kingdom
    bhogabhūmi “Land of enjoyment”: a region where beings enjoy the fruits of past merit without acting (contrast karmabhūmi)
    bhūgola The earth-sphere of the astronomers
    bhukti A territorial division in inscriptions: a province in Gupta and Pāla Bengal, a small unit in the Rāṣṭrakūṭa Deccan
    bhuvanakośa “Treasury of the world”: the cosmographical section of a Purāṇa; also a section of Bhāskara II’s Golādhyāya
    cara Ascensional difference: the correction for the length of day at a given latitude
    catuḥsīmā The four boundaries by which a land grant describes the land it conveys
    cāturmāsya The four months of the rainy season, during which Jain (and other) ascetics stay in one place
    chauth, sardeśmukhī (Mar.) The quarter and the additional tenth of the revenue claimed by the Marathas from territories outside their home lands
    dakṣiṇāpatha “Southern road”; the route to the south and the Deccan itself
    dām (Pe.) Mughal copper coin, forty to the rupee; the unit in which revenue was assessed
    dargāh (Pe.) Tomb-shrine of a Sufi saint
    deśa Region, country
    deśāntara “Difference of place”: longitude correction relative to the reference meridian
    dhām (Hi.) “Abode”; especially one of the four great pilgrimage centres
    dhanus “Bow”: a unit of length of four cubits
    dharmaśālā Rest-house for pilgrims and travellers
    digvijaya “Conquest of the quarters”: a king’s ideal campaign in all directions, a literary form that lists peoples and places
    dikpāla Guardian deity of a direction
    dṛggaṇita “Computation in accordance with sight”: astronomical parameters revised to agree with observation
    dvīpa Island; in cosmography, a ring-shaped continent
    ghāṭ (Hi.) Steps leading to water; also a mountain pass, especially in the Western Ghats
    gola Sphere; golādhyāya, the chapter on the sphere in an astronomical treatise
    goruta “Cow’s call”: a unit of distance, a quarter of a yojana in the Arthaśāstra
    grāmadevatā Village deity, often a goddess guarding the settlement
    huṇḍī Bill of exchange used by Indian merchants
    iqlīm (Ar.) One of the seven east–west “climes” into which Islamic geographers divided the inhabited world
    iqṭāʿ (Ar.) Assignment of the revenue of a territory to a military commander under the Delhi Sultanate
    jamʿ (Pe.) The assessed revenue of a territorial unit in the Mughal records
    Jambūdvīpa “Rose-apple continent”: the central continent of Purāṇic, Jain and Buddhist cosmography, containing Bhāratavarṣa
    janapada Territory of a people; an early state
    jāṅgala Dry, open country (the origin of English “jungle”)
    jarīb (Pe.) Measuring chain used in the Mughal land survey
    jyotirliṅga One of twelve shrines where Śiva is worshipped as a column of light
    kamāl (Ar.) Simple navigational instrument for measuring the altitude of stars
    karmabhūmi “Land of action”: a region where deeds bear fruit and liberation is possible
    khagola The celestial sphere
    kos mīnār (Hi./Pe.) Masonry pillar marking distances along Mughal roads
    kos, krośa A unit of road distance; values varied widely
    kṣetra Sacred field or territory; in Jain cosmography, a region of a continent
    kulaparvata One of the seven principal mountain ranges of Bhāratavarṣa
    Kūrmavibhāga “Division of the tortoise”: Varāhamihira’s arrangement of the regions of India in nine groups
    lamba Co-latitude
    Laṅkā (astronomy) The point on the equator under the reference meridian of Ujjain
    lokapuruṣa “Cosmic person”: the Jain universe imagined in human shape
    madhyadeśa The “Middle Country” of the Gaṅgā–Yamunā region
    mahājanapada “Great janapada”: one of the sixteen states listed in early Buddhist texts
    māhātmya Text praising a sacred place
    Majjhimadesa (Pa.) The “Middle Country” of the Buddhist monastic code
    maṇḍala (politics) The “circle” of states in the Arthaśāstra
    maṇḍala (territory) A territorial division in inscriptions; Tamil maṇḍalam, a major region such as the Cōḻamaṇḍalam
    manzil (Pe.) A day’s stage on a journey
    mauẓaʿ, mauzā (Ar./Pe.) A village as a unit of revenue administration
    Meru The golden mountain at the centre of the cosmos
    mleccha “Barbarian”: a person outside the Brahmanical social order
    mukti-kṣetra A sacred place where death brings liberation, such as Kāśī
    nadī River
    nāḍu (Ta.) A cluster of villages forming an agrarian locality; the basic territorial unit of medieval Tamil inscriptions
    palabhā The noon shadow of a twelve-unit gnomon at the equinox, used to find latitude
    pāḷaiyam (Ta.) A fortified camp; the territory of a pāḷaiyakkārar (“poligar”) chief
    pālkhī (Mar.) Palanquin; the procession carrying the sandals of a saint to Paṇḍharpur
    pañcāṅga Traditional almanac, computed for a particular place
    pargana (Pe.) A sub-district made up of a cluster of villages; the basic local unit of Sultanate and Mughal administration
    parikramā, pradakṣiṇā Circumambulation, keeping the sacred object on one’s right
    pātāla The netherworlds of Purāṇic cosmography
    patwārī, qānūngo (Pe.) Village accountant and sub-district record-keeper in the Mughal revenue system
    pīṭha “Seat”; especially a seat of the Goddess (śākta pīṭha)
    pratyanta Frontier; the frontier kingdoms of Samudragupta’s Prayāga inscription
    qibla (Ar.) The direction of Mecca, toward which Muslims pray
    rahmānī (Gu.) Book of sailing directions used by pilots of western India
    sandeśakāvya “Messenger poem”, modelled on the Meghadūta, describing a route
    saṅgama Confluence of rivers
    saṅkalpa Declaration of intent at the start of a ritual, naming time and place
    śaṅku Gnomon: a vertical rod whose shadow is used to find directions, time and latitude
    sarāy (Pe.) Roadside inn for travellers and caravans
    sarkār (Pe.) A district of a Mughal province
    sārtha, sārthavāha Caravan; caravan leader
    setu Embankment, dam or bridge; also Rāma’s bridge to Laṅkā
    sīmā Boundary, especially of a village
    śreṇī Guild of merchants or artisans
    ṣūba (Pe.) Province of the Mughal empire
    Śulbasūtra Vedic manual of altar construction using cords
    Suvarṇabhūmi, Suvarṇadvīpa “Land of gold”, “Island of gold”: names for parts of Southeast Asia
    svarājya (Mar.) “Own rule”: the home territory of the Maratha state
    tahsīl, tāluk (Ar./Pe.) A revenue sub-division of a district in British and modern India
    tiṇai (Ta.) Landscape or situation in classical Tamil poetics
    tīrtha Ford; sacred place, especially one associated with water
    tīrthayātrā Pilgrimage to sacred places
    uttarāpatha “Northern road”; the north-west and the route to it
    vaḷanāḍu (Ta.) A group of nāḍus; a division of the Cōḻa kingdom
    vārī (Mar.) The annual walking pilgrimage of the Vārkarīs to Paṇḍharpur
    varṣa One of the regions of Jambūdvīpa
    vāstupuruṣamaṇḍala Square grid of deities used in planning buildings
    viṣaya District; a territorial unit of the inscriptions
    vyāhṛti The sacred utterances bhūḥ, bhuvaḥ, svaḥ, naming the three worlds
    wāqiʿa-nawīs (Pe.) News-writer posted by the Mughal government in a province or district
    yātrā Journey; pilgrimage
    yojana The largest common unit of distance; its length varied, most estimates falling between about 7 and 15 km
    zabṭ (Pe.) The Mughal system of revenue assessment based on the measurement of fields
    zīj (Ar./Pe.) Astronomical handbook with tables
    zilā (Ar./Pe.) District; from Arabic ḍilaʿ, through Persian
    ziyārat (Ar.) Pilgrimage to a saint’s shrine
