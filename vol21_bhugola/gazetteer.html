    Gazetteer of Places¶

    The gazetteer gives the modern location of the places most often named in this volume, with coordinates (to the nearest tenth of a degree) where the site is known, and the chapters in which each is discussed. Ancient names are given first, with the modern name after them. Where an identification is uncertain or a name covers a region, no coordinates are given and the entry says so. Coordinates of regions and of uncertain sites should not be used to locate them on the ground.
