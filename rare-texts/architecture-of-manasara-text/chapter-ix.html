    1. I shall now briefly describe in order the planning of villages in this science (of architecture).

    2-4. Daṇdaka, Sarvatobhadra, Nandyâvarta, Padmaka, Svastika, Prastara, Kârmuka, and Chaturmukha : these are the eight kinds of villages defined according to their shapes.
