The question admitting of an absolute reply is such as ' Will all that is born, die ?' (The reply is): “Oh ! Yes.” On the other hand, there is another question (not admitting of an absolute reply) such as “ will every object that dies be born ? The answer to this question must be a qualified one viz., the wise one whose desires have been destroyed und who has attained discrimination, will not be born ; others besides such a one, will be surely born.
