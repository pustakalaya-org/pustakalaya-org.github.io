    About this book¶

    The first volume of the first English translation of Suśruta’s treatise on surgery and medicine, with the translator’s notes (Calcutta, 1907). Text read by machine (OCR) from the scanned pages (hosted by the Internet Archive, item in.ernet.dli.2015.43171) and set as a book; it has not been proofread and contains recognition errors. Passages printed in Devanagari are not read: a note sends the reader to the scan.
