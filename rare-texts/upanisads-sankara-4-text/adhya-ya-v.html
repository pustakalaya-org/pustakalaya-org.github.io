    Com.—The question that is taken in hand first is about the water in the fifth libation ; because, an explanation of this would make easier the explanations of the other questions. The beginning of the two libations of the Agnihotra have been described in the Vâjasanâya;. and the questions refer to that. The starting up of the libation is the Way, the satisfaction is Firmness, and the rise is the Return into the world. The explanations of these have also been given in the same book : " These two libations, on being poured, start up; and they enter; the sky ; they make the sky the A'havaniya Fire ; they i make the Air fuel, the rays the white libation ; then they satisfy the Sky ; and then rise up &c., &c. Whereas, what is meant to be laid down here is the means of attaining to the northern path, in the shape of worshipping, as Fire, the aforesaid commencement, in the shape of the Ap(rva of the Agnihotra sacrifice, after having divided this latter into its five component parts.
