    About this book¶

    Griffith’s English verse translation of the first books of the Ṛgveda with his notes, in the 1920 printing. Text read by machine (OCR) from the scanned pages (hosted by the Internet Archive, item in.ernet.dli.2015.237767) and set as a book; it has not been proofread and contains recognition errors. Passages printed in Devanagari are not read: a note sends the reader to the scan.
