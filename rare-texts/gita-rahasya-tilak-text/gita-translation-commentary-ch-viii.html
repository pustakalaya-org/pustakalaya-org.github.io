But, in considering the respective superiority of the organs of speech, or of seeing, or of hearing. etc., and of Vitality ( prâṇa), the matter is considered once from the Metaphysical (adhyâtma) point of (view by taking the subtle form of the organs of the voice, the eyes, and the ears, and again from the Intuitionist (adhidaivata) point of view, taking the Agni, the Sun, and Ether, as the deities of those organs (Br. 1. 5. 21-23 ; Chan. 1. 2, 3; Kaugl. 4. 12, 13).
