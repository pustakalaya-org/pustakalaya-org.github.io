The measure of a thing Sects. becomes known by its being compared with another thing which belongs to the same species and is assumed as a unit by general consent. Thereby the difference between the object and this standard becomes known. By weighing, people determine the amount of gravity of heavy bodies, when the tongue of the scales stands at right angles on the horizontal plane. Hindus want the scales very little, because their dirhams are determined by number, not by weight, and their fractions, too, are simply counted as so-and-so many fulâs.
