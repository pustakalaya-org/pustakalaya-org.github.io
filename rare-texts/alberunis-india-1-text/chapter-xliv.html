They plucked off the stalks of the lotus from the ponds to eat of them. Meanwhile came The Law (Dharma) and concealed her from them. Every one of them felt ashamed of the other, and they swore oaths which were approved of by Dharma. In order to honour them, Dharma raised them to that place where they are now seen (sic).
