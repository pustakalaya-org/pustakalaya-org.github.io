    About this book¶

    The eleventh-century scholar al-Bīrūnī’s study of Indian science and religion, from Sanskrit sources, in Sachau’s English translation (1888): the Vedas, Purāṇas, Sāṅkhya, Yoga, astronomy, calendars and customs. Text read by machine (OCR) from the scanned pages (hosted by the Internet Archive, item alberunisindiaac01brnm) and set as a book; it has not been proofread and contains recognition errors. Passages printed in Devanagari are not read: a note sends the reader to the scan.
