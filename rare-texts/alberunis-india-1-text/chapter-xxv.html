Between the mouths of the rivers Sarsati and Ganges is the mouth of the river Narmadâ, which descends from the eastern mountains, takes its course in a southwestern direction, and falls into the sea near the town Bahroj, nearly sixty yojana east of Somanâth.     The river Ganges, which is the middle and main stream, flows through the Gandharva, the musicians, Kirmnara, Yakshas, Râkshasa, Vidyâdhara, Uraga, i.e. those who creep on their breasts, the serpents, Kalâpagrama, i.e. the city of the most virtuous, Kimpurusha, Khasa (7), the mountaineers, Kirâta, Pulinda, the hunters in the plains, robbers, Kuru, Bharata, Paficâla, Kaushaka (7), Mâtsya, Magadha, Drahmottara, and Tâmalipta. These are the good and bad beings through whose territories the Ganges flows.
