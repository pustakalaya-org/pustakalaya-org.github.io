Four points must be noticed : (1) The catholicity of the Jaina attitude. The worship and reverence are given to all human souls worthy of it, in whatever country or clime they may be. (2) The worship is impersonal. It is the aggregate of the qualities that is worshipped rather than any particular individual. (3) The arhat, the living embodiment of the highest goal of Jainism, is named before the free soul who has left the world and cannot be approached by humanity, ¹ which requires to see truth before it can seek it.
