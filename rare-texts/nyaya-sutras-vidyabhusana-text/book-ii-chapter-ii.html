    Presumption is explained as the knowledge of a thing derived through the consideration of it from the opposite standpoint.     169. That the substratum of sound is intangible is no counter-argument.—39.

    Sound has not for its substratum any of the tangible substances, — viz., earth, water, fire and air, for it is found to be produced even where ¹ these do no exist.
