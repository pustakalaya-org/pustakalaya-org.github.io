    About this book¶

    Forty folk tales of the Panjab collected by Flora Annie Steel, with notes by R. C. Temple and drawings by Lockwood Kipling: Rāja Rasālu, the clever jackal, fairy and ghost stories. Text read by machine (OCR) from the scanned pages (hosted by the Internet Archive, item cu31924021524677) and set as a book; it has not been proofread and contains recognition errors. Passages printed in Devanagari are not read: a note sends the reader to the scan.
