    Chronology¶

    The dates below are approximate, and many are disputed; where scholarly opinion varies widely, a broad range is given. Traditional dates are not used.

    Date Authors, works and events
    c. 2600–1900 BCE Mature Indus civilisation; Indus script (undeciphered)
    c. 1500–1200 BCE Hymns of the Ṛgveda (core)
    c. 1200–500 BCE Later Vedic texts: Brāhmaṇas, Āraṇyakas, early Upaniṣads; Śākalya’s padapāṭha
    before Pāṇini (perhaps 5th c. BCE) Yāska’s Nirukta; the Nighaṇṭu
    c. 4th c. BCE (some scholars: 5th) Pāṇini’s Aṣṭādhyāyī
    c. 3rd c. BCE Kātyāyana’s vārttikas
    r. c. 268–232 BCE Aśoka; earliest dated inscriptions in Brāhmī and Kharoṣṭhī
    mid-2nd c. BCE Patañjali’s Mahābhāṣya
    last centuries BCE (uncertain) Piṅgala’s Chandaḥsūtra; Prātiśākhyas in their present form (dates uncertain)
    1st c. BCE Pali canon written down in Sri Lanka (by tradition)
    last c. BCE – 5th c. CE Tolkāppiyam (layered; Poruḷatikāram probably the latest part); Tamil-Brāhmī inscriptions
    early centuries CE Hāla’s Gāhāsattasaī; Kātantra grammar
    150 CE Rudradāman’s Sanskrit inscription at Junāgaḍh
    5th c. Bhartṛhari’s Vākyapadīya; Candragomin’s grammar; Buddhaghosa
    c. 480–540 Dignāga; the theory of apoha
    c. 5th–7th c. Amarasiṃha’s Amarakośa
    6th–7th c. Bhaṭṭi’s Bhaṭṭikāvya, a poem illustrating Pāṇini’s rules
    7th c. Kāśikā of Jayāditya and Vāmana; Kumārila Bhaṭṭa; Prabhākara; Dharmakīrti; Brahmagupta (628)
    779 Uddyotanasūri’s Kuvalayamālā
    early 9th c. The Sanskrit–Tibetan Mahāvyutpatti
    9th c. Svayambhū’s Apabhraṃśa Paümacariu; Pālyakīrti Śākaṭāyana; Kavirājamārga (c. 850)
    c. 950–1016 Abhinavagupta
    10th c. Halāyudha’s commentary on Piṅgala; Helārāja on the Vākyapadīya
    972–73 Dhanapāla’s Pāiyalacchīnāmamālā, the earliest surviving Prakrit lexicon
    11th c. Kaiyaṭa’s Pradīpa; Vīracōḻiyam; Bhoja’s grammar, the Sarasvatīkaṇṭhābharaṇa
    first half of 12th c. Dāmodara’s Uktivyaktiprakaraṇa, teaching Sanskrit through Old Kosali
    1140s Nāgavarma II’s grammars of Kannada
    c. 1150 Hemacandra’s grammar, lexicons and Chandonuśāsana
    1154 Aggavaṃsa’s Saddanīti (Pagan)
    1159 Sarvānanda’s Ṭīkāsarvasva on the Amarakośa
    early 13th c. Pavaṇanti’s Naṉṉūl
    c. 1260 Keśirāja’s Śabdamaṇidarpaṇa
    13th c. Ketana’s Āndhrabhāṣābhūṣaṇamu; Vopadeva’s Mugdhabodha; the Sinhala Sidat Saṅgarāva
    c. 1300 Farhang-i Qawwās, among the earliest Persian dictionaries compiled in India
    1356 Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita’s Gaṇitakaumudī
    14th c. Sāyaṇa’s Vedic commentaries; Līlātilakam
    16th c. Jīva Gosvāmin’s Harināmāmṛta
    1578 First book printed in an Indian script (a Tamil catechism)
    early 17th c. Bhaṭṭoji Dīkṣita’s Siddhāntakaumudī
    1604 Bhaṭṭākalaṅka’s Karṇāṭakaśabdānuśāsana
    1608 Farhang-i Jahāngīrī
    1640 Thomas Stephens’s Konkani grammar (Arte da lingoa Canarim) printed
    1651–54 Burhān-i Qāṭiʿ (Hyderabad) and Farhang-i Rashīdī
    1656 Appakavi’s Appakavīyamu
    late 17th c. Mīrzā Khān’s Tuḥfat al-Hind
    c. 1670–1750 Nāgeśa Bhaṭṭa
    c. 1698 J. J. Ketelaar’s grammar of Hindustani
    1739–40 Tek Chand Bahār’s Bahār-i ʿAjam
    1743 Manoel da Assumpção’s Bengali–Portuguese Vocabulario
    d. 1756 Sirāj al-Dīn ʿAlī Khān Ārzū, author of the Muthmir
    d. 1785 Civañāṉa Muṉivar, defender of the independence of Tamil grammar
    1786 William Jones’s address to the Asiatic Society
    1796–1800 Gilchrist’s Grammar of the Hindoostanee Language; the College of Fort William founded
    1805 Colebrooke’s Grammar of the Sanskrit Language
    1807–08 Inshā and Qatīl’s Daryā-yi Laṭāfat
    1816 F. W. Ellis on the southern languages (the “Dravidian proof”)
    1837 Prinsep’s decipherment of Brāhmī; Persian replaced in the courts of British India
    1839–40 Böhtlingk’s edition of Pāṇini
    1856 Caldwell’s Comparative Grammar of the Dravidian Languages
    1858 Paravastu Cinnaya Sūri’s Bālavyākaraṇamu
    1872; 1894 Gundert’s Malayalam and English Dictionary; Kittel’s Kannada–English Dictionary
    1896 A. R. Rājarāja Varma’s Kēraḷapāṇinīyam
    1903–1928 Grierson’s Linguistic Survey of India
    1920s Ol Chiki script for Santali
    1933 Bloomfield’s Language
    1956 Reorganisation of Indian states on linguistic lines
    1961 Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology set up
    1985 Briggs’s article on Sanskrit and artificial intelligence
    2007 onwards International symposia on Sanskrit computational linguistics
