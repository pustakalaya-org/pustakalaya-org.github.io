    Chronology of Principal Thinkers and Texts¶

    Dates are approximate and follow the ranges used in the chapters; where a traditional date differs from the academic one, it is given and labelled. Many dates, especially of the sūtra texts and of early Jain and Buddhist authors, are disputed.

    Date Thinker or text Tradition
    c. 1500–1200 BCE Ṛgveda, including the Nāsadīya Sūkta (10.129) Vedic
    c. 800–300 BCE Early Upaniṣads Vedic
    c. 480–400 BCE The Buddha (short chronology; traditional Theravāda dates 624–544 BCE) Buddhist
    5th c. BCE Mahāvīra, an older contemporary of the Buddha (traditional dates 599–527 BCE); Makkhali Gosāla; Ajita Kesakambalī Jain; Ājīvika; materialist
    c. 4th c. BCE Pāṇini Grammarians
    mid-2nd c. BCE Patañjali the grammarian, Mahābhāṣya Grammarians
    c. 2nd c. BCE Jaimini, Mīmāṃsā Sūtra (estimates 3rd–1st c. BCE) Mīmāṃsā
    c. 1st c. BCE – 1st c. CE Vaiśeṣika Sūtra Vaiśeṣika
    c. 1st–5th c. CE Brahma Sūtra, final form Vedānta
    c. 2nd c. CE Nāgārjuna; the Mahāvibhāṣā Buddhist
    c. 2nd–3rd c. CE Nyāya Sūtra, final redaction Nyāya
    c. 2nd–6th c. CE (disputed) Umāsvāti, Tattvārthasūtra; Kundakunda Jain
    c. 200–400 CE Śabara, commentary on the Mīmāṃsā Sūtra Mīmāṃsā
    c. 325–425 CE Pātañjala Yogaśāstra (Yoga Sūtra with Bhāṣya) Yoga
    c. 350–450 CE Īśvarakṛṣṇa, Sāṃkhyakārikā Sāṃkhya
    c. 4th–5th c. CE Asaṅga and Vasubandhu Buddhist (Yogācāra)
    5th c. CE Vātsyāyana; Bhartṛhari; Siddhasena Divākara Nyāya; Grammarians; Jain
    c. 480–540 Dignāga Buddhist
    c. 6th c. Praśastapāda; Gauḍapāda; Bhāviveka; Uddyotakara (late 6th c.); the Tamil Maṇimēkalai (date disputed) Vaiśeṣika; Advaita; Buddhist; Nyāya
    6th–7th c. Dharmakīrti (often c. 600–660) Buddhist
    7th c. Kumārila (c. 600–650); Prabhākara; Candrakīrti; Purandara Mīmāṃsā; Buddhist; Cārvāka
    7th–9th c. The Nāyaṉmār poets (Appar, Campantar, Cuntarar) Tamil Śaiva
    late 7th – 8th c. Maṇḍana Miśra; Sadyojyotis (c. 675–725) Mīmāṃsā and Advaita; Śaiva Siddhānta
    c. 700–750 Śaṅkara (older conventional dating 788–820) Advaita
    8th c. Haribhadra; Akalaṅka; Śāntarakṣita (c. 725–788) and Kamalaśīla; Jayarāśi; Bhāskara (8th–9th c.) Jain; Buddhist; sceptic; Bhedābheda
    late 9th c. Jayanta Bhaṭṭa Nyāya
    9th or 10th c. Vācaspati Miśra Nyāya, Sāṃkhya, Advaita
    c. 900–1016 Somānanda (c. 900–950); Utpaladeva (c. 925–975); Abhinavagupta (c. 950–1016) Pratyabhijñā
    10th c. Bhaṭṭa Rāmakaṇṭha; Śrīdhara (991) Śaiva Siddhānta; Vaiśeṣika
    late 10th – 11th c. Udayana Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika
    11th c. Ratnakīrti; Bhoja; Yādava Prakāśa Buddhist; Śaiva Siddhānta; Bhedābheda
    traditionally 1017–1137 Rāmānuja (some scholars c. 1077–1157) Viśiṣṭādvaita
    12th c. Śrīharṣa; Hemacandra (1088/89–1172); Aghoraśiva Advaita; Jain; Śaiva Siddhānta
    c. 1200 Sack of Nālandā and other monasteries of Bihar and Bengal Buddhist
    c. 1238–1317 Madhva (traditionally 1199–1278) Dvaita
    13th c. Meykaṇṭār, Civañāṉapōtam; Citsukha (probably 13th c.); Nimbārka (disputed, c. 12th–13th c.) Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta; Advaita; Dvaitādvaita
    14th c. Gaṅgeśa, Tattvacintāmaṇi; Mādhava, Sarvadarśanasaṅgraha; Jayatīrtha; Umāpati Civācāriyar Navya-Nyāya; doxography; Dvaita; Tamil Śaiva Siddhānta
    c. 1460–1540 Raghunātha Śiromaṇi; Vyāsatīrtha (c. 1460–1539) Navya-Nyāya; Dvaita
    1479–1531; 1486–1534 Vallabha; Caitanya Śuddhādvaita; Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava
    16th c. Appayya Dīkṣita; Vijñānabhikṣu; Jīva Gosvāmī; Madhusūdana Sarasvatī (16th or 17th c.) Advaita; Sāṃkhya-Yoga; Gauḍīya
    1655 Dārā Shikōh, Majma‘ al-Baḥrayn Indo-Persian
    17th c. Jagadīśa; Gadādhara; Dharmarāja Adhvarin; Yaśovijaya (1624–1688); Khaṇḍadeva; Āpadeva; Kauṇḍa Bhaṭṭa Navya-Nyāya; Advaita; Jain; Mīmāṃsā; Grammarians
    c. 1700 Nāgeśa Grammarians
    18th c. Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava
    1863–1902 Svāmī Vivekānanda Modern Vedānta
    1872–1950 Śrī Aurobindo Modern Vedānta
    1875–1949 K.C. Bhattacharyya Modern philosophy
    1888–1975 Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Modern philosophy
    1891–1956 B.R. Ambedkar Modern Buddhist thought
    1924–2007 Daya Krishna Modern philosophy
    1935–1991 B.K. Matilal Modern philosophy
