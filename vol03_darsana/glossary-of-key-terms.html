    Glossary of Key Terms¶

    Sanskrit Term Meaning
    Ābhāsa Manifestation; in the Pratyabhijñā, each aspect under which consciousness manifests a thing
    Abhāva Absence; a category in later Vaiśeṣika, known through non-apprehension in Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā
    Adhyāsa Superimposition; in Advaita, the mutual false attribution of self and non-self
    Adṛṣṭa “The unseen”; merit and demerit inhering in the self (Vaiśeṣika, Nyāya)
    Ahaṃkāra “I-maker”; the principle of individuation (Sāṃkhya)
    Ājīvika Śramaṇa order associated with Makkhali Gosāla, known for its doctrine of destiny (niyati)
    Ālayavijñāna Store-consciousness (Yogācāra)
    Anātman Non-self; the Buddhist denial of a permanent self
    Āṇava mala The innate impurity that limits the soul (Śaiva); a substance for the Siddhānta, a contraction of awareness for the Pratyabhijñā
    Anekāntavāda Doctrine of the many-sidedness of reality (Jain)
    Anirvacanīya Indefinable as either real or unreal; the Advaita status of the world-appearance
    Anumāna Inference
    Anupalabdhi Non-apprehension, as a means of knowing absence
    Apoha “Exclusion”; the Buddhist theory of word meaning
    Apauruṣeya “Not of human origin”; authorless (said of the Veda in Mīmāṃsā)
    Apūrva The unseen potency produced by a ritual act (Mīmāṃsā)
    Ārambhavāda Theory that the effect is a new beginning (Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika)
    Arthāpatti Postulation; presumption to explain an otherwise inexplicable fact
    Arthakriyā Causal efficacy; for the Buddhist epistemologists, the mark of what is real
    Āstika / Nāstika One who affirms / denies another world; later, Brahmanical labels for schools that accept / reject Vedic authority
    Avacchedaka Limitor; technical term of Navya-Nyāya
    Avidyā Ignorance
    Bhāṣya Commentary
    Bodhisattva One who seeks Buddhahood for the sake of all beings (Mahāyāna)
    Buddhi Intellect; in Sāṃkhya the first evolute of prakṛti
    Catuṣkoṭi The four alternatives (is, is not, both, neither), all rejected by Nāgārjuna
    Citta Mind (Yoga)
    Darśana “View”; a system or school of philosophy
    Dharma Duty, law, right; Vedic injunction (Mīmāṃsā); medium of motion (Jain); element of existence (Buddhist)
    Dīkṣā Initiation; in the Śaiva Siddhānta, the rite through which Śiva’s power removes the soul’s bonds
    Dravya Substance
    Duḥkha Suffering, unsatisfactoriness
    Guṇa Quality (Vaiśeṣika); constituent strand of prakṛti — sattva, rajas, tamas (Sāṃkhya)
    Hetu Reason, the logical mark in an inference
    Hetvābhāsa Pseudo-reason; fallacy
    Īśvara The Lord; God
    Jalpa Disputation aimed at victory, permitting quibbles and false rejoinders (Nyāya)
    Jīva Living being; individual soul
    Kaivalya “Isolation”; liberation in Sāṃkhya and Yoga
    Kalpanāpoḍha “Free from conceptual construction”; Dignāga’s definition of perception
    Kevalānvayin “Purely positive” inference, whose property to be proved is present everywhere (Nyāya)
    Khyātivāda Theory of error
    Kleśa Affliction (Yoga, Buddhism)
    Kṣaṇikavāda Doctrine of momentariness (Buddhist)
    Lokāyata “Worldly”; materialism (Cārvāka)
    Mala Impurity; the root bond of the soul in Śaiva theology
    Manas Mind; the internal organ
    Māyā Illusion; in Advaita, the indescribable power that projects the world-appearance
    Mithyā False; in Advaita, neither real nor unreal
    Mokṣa Liberation
    Naya Standpoint; partial perspective (Jain)
    Nirvikalpaka /
    Savikalpaka    		 Indeterminate (pre-conceptual) and determinate perception (Nyāya)
    Nirvāṇa “Blowing out”; liberation in Buddhism
    Padārtha Category; “meaning of a word”
    Pakṣa The subject of an inference (the hill in “the hill has fire”)
    Paramāṇu Atom
    Pariṇāma Real transformation
    Pati, paśu, pāśa Lord, bound soul and bond: the three categories of the Śaiva Siddhānta
    Prakāśa Light; the manifesting aspect of consciousness, inseparable from vimarśa (Pratyabhijñā)
    Prakṛti Primordial matter (Sāṃkhya); archetypal rite (Mīmāṃsā)
    Pramā Valid cognition
    Pramāṇa Means of valid knowledge
    Prasaṅga Reduction to absurdity; drawing out the unwelcome consequences of an opponent’s thesis
    Pratītyasamutpāda Dependent origination (Buddhist)
    Pratiyogin Counterpositive; the thing whose absence is in question (Navya-Nyāya)
    Pratyabhijñā Recognition; the non-dual Śaiva school of Somānanda, Utpaladeva and Abhinavagupta
    Pratyakṣa Perception; direct knowledge
    Pudgala Matter (Jain); “person” (Buddhist Personalists)
    Puruṣa Pure consciousness, the self (Sāṃkhya)
    Sādhya The property to be proved in an inference (fire)
    Samavāya Inherence (Vaiśeṣika)
    Sāmānya Universal
    Saṃsāra The cycle of rebirth
    Saptabhaṅgī Sevenfold predication (Jain)
    Satkāryavāda Doctrine that the effect pre-exists in its cause (Sāṃkhya)
    Śabda Word; verbal testimony
    Sphoṭa The unitary meaning-bearing word or sentence (Grammarians)
    Śūnyatā Emptiness of intrinsic nature (Madhyamaka)
    Svabhāva Intrinsic nature, own-being
    Svalakṣaṇa Unique particular (Buddhist epistemology)
    Svasaṃvedana Reflexive self-awareness of cognition (Buddhist epistemology; adapted by the Śaivas)
    Svataḥ-prāmāṇya Intrinsic validity of cognition
    Svātantrya Freedom; the sovereignty of consciousness (Pratyabhijñā)
    Syādvāda Doctrine of qualified predication (Jain)
    Tanmātra Subtle element (Sāṃkhya)
    Tarka Hypothetical or suppositional reasoning
    Tattva Principle; reality
    Trairūpya The three marks of a valid reason (Dignāga)
    Upamāna Comparison, as a means of knowledge
    Upādhi Limiting adjunct
    Vāda Discussion aimed at establishing the truth (Nyāya)
    Vaiśeṣika “Of particularity”; the school of Kaṇāda
    Vimarśa Reflective awareness; the self-grasping of consciousness as “I” (Pratyabhijñā)
    Viśeṣa Particularity; ultimate differentiator
    Vitaṇḍā Refutation without establishing a counter-thesis (Nyāya)
    Vivarta Apparent transformation (Advaita; Bhartṛhari)
    Vṛtti Fluctuation or modification of the mind
    Vyāpti Pervasion; invariable concomitance between reason and conclusion
