    Glossary of Sanskrit Terms¶

    Term Meaning
    Adhvaryu The priest who performs the ritual actions, muttering Yajurvedic formulas
    Agnicayana “Piling of Agni”: the building of the great brick fire altar
    Agnihotra The daily milk offering into the fire, morning and evening
    Agniṣṭoma The Soma sacrifice with a single pressing day, the model of all Soma rites
    Agnyādheya The rite of establishing the three śrauta fires
    Āhavanīya The square eastern fire, into which most śrauta oblations are poured
    Āhitāgni A householder who has established the three śrauta fires
    Anudātta “Not raised”: the low pitch of the Vedic accent
    Anukramaṇī An index listing the seer, deity and metre of each hymn
    Apauruṣeya “Not of human origin”: the doctrine that the Veda has no author
    Āprī A hymn of the animal sacrifice inviting a fixed series of ritual entities, one to a verse
    Āraṇyaka “Forest book”: text on secret or dangerous rites and their meaning
    Arthavāda Explanatory or laudatory passage in a Brāhmaṇa
    Ārya Self-designation of the Vedic people; later “noble”
    Aṣṭakā Domestic offering to the ancestors on the eighth day of a dark fortnight in winter
    Aśvamedha The royal horse sacrifice
    Ātman Breath; self; in the Upaniṣads the innermost self
    Bandhu “Connection”: equivalence between ritual, cosmic and bodily elements
    Brahman (neuter) sacred formulation; ultimate reality. (masc.) the overseeing priest
    Brāhmaṇa (1) a member of the priestly class; (2) a prose text explaining the ritual
    Brahmavādinī “One who discourses on brahman”: a learned woman such as Maitreyī (BU 4.5.1)
    Brahmodya A ritual exchange of riddles about the nature of the world
    Caraṇa A Vedic school as a community of teachers and pupils
    Cāturmāsya The four-monthly seasonal sacrifices
    Chandas Metre; the Vedāṅga of prosody
    Dakṣiṇā The fee given to the priests
    Dakṣiṇāgni The semicircular southern fire of the śrauta ritual
    Dānastuti “Praise of the gift”: verses praising a patron’s generosity, closing a hymn
    Darśapūrṇamāsa The new- and full-moon offerings, model of all iṣṭis
    Dāsa, Dasyu In the Ṛgveda, enemies of the Ārya; later “servant, slave”
    Deva “Heavenly one”: a god
    Dharma Ordinance, law, duty, righteousness
    Dhī Insight, visionary thought; the poet’s inspired conception
    Dikpāla One of the eight guardians of the directions, such as Indra (east) and Varuṇa (west)
    Dīkṣā Consecration of the sacrificer before a Soma rite
    Gārhapatya The round western “householder’s fire” of the śrauta ritual
    Ghana The most complex of the standard recitation patterns
    Gṛhya Sūtra Manual of domestic rites
    Hotṛ The priest who recites Ṛgvedic verses
    Iṣṭi A śrauta offering of cakes, milk or ghee, without Soma or an animal victim
    Jaiminīya A school of the Sāmaveda, surviving in Kerala and Tamil Nadu
    Jaṭā “Braid”: a recitation pattern (1–2, 2–1, 1–2, …)
    Jāti “Birth”: an endogamous caste group
    Jyotiṣa Astronomy and calendar; one of the Vedāṅgas
    Kalpa Ritual procedure; the Vedāṅga comprising the Śrauta, Gṛhya, Dharma and Śulba Sūtras
    Kāṇḍa A book or section of a text
    Kauthuma The most widespread school of the Sāmaveda
    Kavi A poet or sage, a person of insight; also said of gods
    Khila A supplementary hymn transmitted with the Ṛgveda
    Krama Recitation in overlapping pairs of words
    Mahāvedi The great trapezoidal altar ground of the Soma sacrifice
    Maṇḍala One of the ten books of the Ṛgveda
    Mantra A sacred verse or formula
    Muhūrta A thirtieth part of a day (48 minutes)
    Nakṣatra Star; one of the 27 or 28 lunar asterisms
    Nirukta Etymology; Yāska’s work explaining Vedic words
    Niyoga The appointment of a man, usually the husband’s brother, to beget a son on a widow
    Pada pāṭha The word-by-word recitation of a text
    Paippalāda A recension of the Atharvaveda, surviving in Odisha
    Pākayajña “Cooked offering”: the basic domestic rite
    Paṇi In the Ṛgveda, stingy non-sacrificers who hid the cows in the Vala cave
    Paśubandha The śrauta animal sacrifice
    Prajāpati “Lord of offspring”: the creator god of the Brāhmaṇas
    Prakṛti A model rite, whose procedure is transferred to its variants (vikṛti)
    Prāṇa Breath; vital force
    Prātiśākhya A phonetic manual of a particular Vedic school
    Pravargya Śrauta rite of the heated clay pot filled with milk
    Puruṣa Man, person; the cosmic Man of RV 10.90
    Rājasūya The royal consecration
    Ṛc A verse, especially of the Ṛgveda
    Ṛṣi A seer or inspired poet
    Ṛta Cosmic and moral order; truth
    Śākhā “Branch”: a Vedic school and its recension
    Sāman A chant; a melody of the Sāmaveda
    Saṃhitā A collection of verses or formulas; the continuous recited text
    Saṃskāra A life-cycle rite
    Saṃsthā One of the seven forms of the Soma sacrifice
    Śastra A recitation of verses by the hotṛ priests, answering a stotra
    Sattra A Soma “session” of twelve or more days in which all officiants are sacrificers
    Satya Truth
    Śikṣā Phonetics; one of the Vedāṅgas
    Smṛti “What is remembered”: tradition; texts of human authorship
    Snātaka A young man who has completed Vedic study and taken the ritual bath
    Soma A sacred plant, the drink pressed from it, and its god
    Śrauta Relating to the solemn rites with three fires
    Śruti “What is heard”: revealed scripture, the Veda
    Sthālīpāka A pot of rice or barley cooked and offered in the domestic fire
    Stobha A chant syllable inserted in a sāman
    Stotra A chant of sāmans sung by the udgātṛ priests at the Soma sacrifice
    Sūkta A hymn
    Śulba Sūtra Manual of altar geometry
    Svara Accent; tone; musical note
    Svarita The falling tone that follows an udātta
    Tapas Heat; ascetic effort
    Udātta “Raised”: the high pitch that marks the accented syllable
    Udgātṛ The chief chanter of sāmans
    Upākaraṇa Annual rite opening the term of Vedic study (today the Upākarma)
    Upanayana Initiation into Vedic study
    Upaniṣad A text of esoteric teaching, especially on ātman and brahman
    Upaveda A “subsidiary Veda”: a practical science attached to a Veda
    Vājapeya “Drink of strength”: a Soma sacrifice with a chariot race
    Vala The rock cave holding the cows and the dawns, broken open by Indra or Bṛhaspati
    Varṇa “Colour, kind”: one of the four classes of Brahmanical society
    Vedāṅga “Limb of the Veda”: one of six auxiliary sciences
    Vedi The shallow, strewn altar-ground between the fires
    Vidhi An injunction
    Vikṛtipāṭha One of the “modified” recitation patterns
    Vipra An inspired poet; later, a Brahmin
    Vyākaraṇa Grammar; one of the Vedāṅgas
    Yajamāna The sponsor of a sacrifice
    Yajña Sacrifice, worship with offerings
    Yajus A sacrificial formula
    Yūpa The wooden post to which a sacrificial animal is bound
