    Appendix B: Principal Texts of Indian Astronomy¶

    The table lists the main texts discussed in this volume, with their authors, dates, genres and, where one exists, an edition or translation cited in the bibliography. A dash means that no edition is cited here; most of these texts have been printed in Sanskrit editions, some with translations, and Pingree’s Census lists them (Pingree 1970–1994). Dates are those of the text’s own statements or epochs where available; otherwise they are approximate. Genres: S = siddhānta or tantra; K = handbook (karaṇa) or tables; C = commentary; A = astrology or omens; O = other.
