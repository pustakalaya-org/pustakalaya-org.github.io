    About this book¶

    An English translation of the Tirukkuṟaḷ, chapter by chapter, made by V. V. S. Aiyar (1881–1925), Tamil scholar and freedom fighter, while in exile in Pondicherry, and published at Madras in 1916 with his preface and notes. The Tamil text is on the shelf Classic Texts.
