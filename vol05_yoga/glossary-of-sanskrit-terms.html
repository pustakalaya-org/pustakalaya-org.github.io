    Glossary of Sanskrit Terms¶

    Term Meaning
    abhyāsa Repeated practice; with vairāgya, the means to stilling the mind (YS 1.12)
    ahiṃsā Non-injury; the first yama
    ājñā cakra The two-petalled centre between the eyebrows
    akhāṛā (Hindi) A regiment or lodge of an ascetic order; the nāgā akhāṛās of the Daśanāmīs and Rāmānandīs
    amanaska “No-mind”; the effortless absorption taught as Rājayoga in the Amanaska; a synonym of samādhi in the HYP (4.4)
    amṛta “Nectar of immortality”; in Haṭha texts, the vital fluid dripping from the head
    aṣṭāṅga “Eight-limbed”; Patañjali’s eightfold yoga
    āsana Seat, posture; the third limb of aṣṭāṅga yoga
    avidyā Ignorance; the root affliction (YS 2.4)
    bandha “Lock”; muscular contraction used to control the breath (mūla, uḍḍīyāna, jālandhara)
    bhakti Devotion to a personal deity
    bhāṣya Commentary; in Yoga, the prose explanation transmitted with the sūtras and ascribed to Vyāsa
    bīja “Seed”; a monosyllabic mantra such as hrīṃ
    bindu “Drop”; semen or vital essence; in Tantra, the point of concentrated power
    cakra “Wheel”; a centre of the subtle body
    citta Mind, mind-field; in Yoga the whole mental apparatus
    Cittar (Tamil) “Accomplished one” (Sanskrit siddha); one of the Tamil poet-yogins, traditionally eighteen
    darśana “View”; a school of philosophy
    Daśanāmī “Ten-named”; the order of Śaiva renouncers traditionally traced to Śaṅkara
    dhāraṇā Concentration; fixing the mind on one place (YS 3.1)
    dharmamegha “Cloud of dharma”; the final samādhi before liberation (YS 4.29)
    dhyāna Meditation; an unbroken flow of awareness (YS 3.2)
    dīkṣā Initiation, especially in Tantric traditions
    granthi “Knot”; one of three obstructions in the central channel
    guru Teacher, spiritual preceptor
    haṭha “Force”; Haṭha Yoga is the yoga of physical technique
    iḍā The left (lunar) channel of the subtle body
    Īśvara The Lord; in Patañjali a special, never-bound self
    jālandhara bandha The chin lock
    japa Repetition of a mantra
    jīvanmukti Liberation while still embodied
    jñāna Knowledge; liberating insight
    kaivalya “Isolation”; liberation in Sāṃkhya-Yoga
    Kānphaṭa (Hindi) “Split-eared”; a Nāth yogi, from the large earrings worn after initiation
    karma Action and its consequences
    khecarī mudrā Turning the tongue back into the nasal cavity
    kleśa Affliction; the five causes of suffering (YS 2.3)
    kumbhaka Breath retention
    kuṇḍalinī “The coiled one”; the dormant goddess-power at the base of the body
    laya Dissolution, absorption of the mind
    mahāmudrā “Great seal”; a seated posture combining breath-retention and locks, among the earliest Haṭha techniques (Amṛtasiddhi)
    mahāvākya “Great saying”; the four Upaniṣadic statements of identity with Brahman
    mantra Sacred formula or syllable
    māyā In Advaita, the power by which the One appears as many
    mokṣa Liberation from the cycle of rebirth
    mudrā “Seal”; a Haṭha technique, or a ritual hand gesture
    mūlādhāra The root centre at the base of the body
    nāda Sound, especially the inner sound heard in meditation
    nādānusandhāna “Attending to the inner sound”; the foremost method of laya in the HYP (4.66)
    nāḍī Channel of the subtle body through which prāṇa flows
    Nātha An order of ascetics associated with Haṭha Yoga
    nirbīja “Seedless”; the highest samādhi (YS 1.51)
    nirodha Cessation, restraint (YS 1.2)
    niyama Observance; the second limb of aṣṭāṅga yoga
    nyāsa Ritual placing of mantras on the body
    pañca-kośa The five sheaths of the self (Taittirīya Upaniṣad)
    pañca-makāra The “five Ms” of left-hand Śākta ritual
    Pāśupata The earliest Śaiva ascetic order, whose yoga is taught in several Purāṇas
    piṅgalā The right (solar) channel of the subtle body
    prāṇa Breath, vital force; also the first of the five vital breaths
    prāṇāyāma Breath control; the fourth limb
    prakṛti Primal matter, nature, in Sāṃkhya
    pratyāhāra Withdrawal of the senses; the fifth limb
    puruṣa Pure consciousness, the self, in Sāṃkhya-Yoga
    rāja yoga In Haṭha texts, the goal-state of samādhi; since Vivekānanda, Patañjali’s yoga
    Rāmānandī The Vaiṣṇava order tracing itself to Rāmānanda; its renouncers (vairāgīs) form North India’s largest ascetic order
    sahasrāra The thousand-petalled lotus at the crown of the head
    śakti Power; the divine feminine
    samādhi Absorption; the eighth limb
    saṃskāra Latent impression left by experience
    saṃyama Dhāraṇā, dhyāna and samādhi applied together (YS 3.4)
    ṣaḍaṅgayoga “Six-limbed yoga”; the yoga of the Śaiva scriptures and of early Haṭha texts such as the Vivekamārtaṇḍa
    ṣaṭkarma The six purifications of Haṭha Yoga
    siddhi Accomplishment; supernormal power
    suṣumnā The central channel of the subtle body
    svādhyāya Study of scripture or recitation; a niyama
    tantra “Loom”; a class of scriptures and the traditions based on them
    tapas Ascetic heat, austerity
    tarka Reasoning; in the six-limbed yoga of the Śaiva scriptures, discerning judgement of what to reject and what to seek
    tattva Principle, category of reality
    turīya “The fourth”; the state beyond waking, dream and sleep
    uḍḍīyāna bandha The abdominal lock
    vairāgya Dispassion, non-attachment
    vāsanā Latent tendency formed by accumulated impressions (saṃskāra)
    vikṣepa Distraction; obstacle to yoga (YS 1.30)
    viveka-khyāti Discriminative discernment between puruṣa and prakṛti
    vṛtti Fluctuation or modification of the mind
    yama Restraint; the first limb of aṣṭāṅga yoga
    yoga “Yoking”; discipline, method; in Patañjali, concentration
