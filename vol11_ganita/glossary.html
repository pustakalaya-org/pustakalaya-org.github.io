    Glossary¶

    Terms are Sanskrit unless marked (Ar. = Arabic, Mal. = Malayalam, Pers. = Persian, Pk. = Prakrit).

    Term Meaning
    adhvan “Extent”: in prosody, the space needed to write out all the patterns of a metre
    ahargaṇa The count of days elapsed since an astronomical epoch
    ananta Infinite; in Jain texts, the highest class of quantity
    anekavarṇasamīkaraṇa Equation in several unknowns
    aṅkapāśa “Net of numbers”: permutations and combinations (Bhāskara II, Nārāyaṇa)
    āryā A moraic metre, used in the Āryabhaṭīya
    asaṃkhyāta Innumerable; the Jain class between the numerable and the infinite
    āsanna “Near”: approximate (of Āryabhaṭa’s value of \(\pi\))
    avyakta “Unmanifest”: an unknown quantity
    avyaktagaṇita Calculation with unknowns; algebra
    āyatavṛtta “Elongated circle”: an oval figure (Mahāvīra)
    bhāgānubandha A fraction “in association”: a quantity increased by a fraction of itself
    bhāgāpavāha A fraction “in dissociation”: a quantity diminished by a fraction of itself
    bhāṇḍapratibhāṇḍa “Goods for goods”: barter, treated by compound proportion
    bhāvanā “Production”: Brahmagupta’s rule for composing two solutions of \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    bhāvita Product of unknowns; an equation containing such products
    bhūtasaṃkhyā Word-numerals: digits expressed by names of objects, read from the units upward
    bīja “Seed”: the underlying principle of a rule; hence bījagaṇita, algebra
    cakravāla “Cyclic” method for solving \(Nx^2 + 1 = y^2\) (Jayadeva, Bhāskara II)
    chāyā Shadow; the mathematics of the gnomon
    citi Pile (of bricks or balls); a “determination” of arithmetic
    dhana “Wealth”: a positive quantity
    dhūlīkarman “Dust-work”: calculation on the dust board; arithmetic
    ekavarṇasamīkaraṇa Equation in one unknown
    gaṇaka Calculator; an astronomer-mathematician
    gaṇita Calculation; mathematics; mathematical astronomy
    gola Sphere; spherical astronomy
    guru A heavy (long) syllable in prosody
    icchā “Requisition”: the third term of the rule of three, whose fruit is sought
    iṣṭakarman “Operation with an assumed number”: the method of false position
    jyā, jīvā “Bowstring”: the Indian sine, \(R\sin\theta\)
    jyeṣṭha “Greater root”: \(y\) in \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    jyotiṣa The science of the heavenly bodies: astronomy, divination and astrology
    jyotpatti “Production of sines”: the construction of a sine table; the title of a section of Bhāskara II’s Golādhyāya
    kalāntara, vṛddhi Interest on a loan
    kaniṣṭha “Lesser root”: \(x\) in \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    karaṇa A practical astronomical handbook with a recent epoch
    karaṇī A surd; a quantity of which the square root is to be taken
    karṇa Hypotenuse; in the Yuktibhāṣā, a line from the centre of the circle to a point on the tangent
    kaṭapayādi Notation in which consonants stand for digits (ka, ṭa, pa, ya \(= 1\))
    kha, śūnya “Sky”, “empty”: zero
    khaccheda, khahara A quantity with zero as divisor
    khāta Excavation; the mensuration of pits
    koṭijyā The Indian cosine, \(R\cos\theta\)
    krayavikraya Purchase and sale; problems of profit and loss
    kṣepa The additive or subtractive quantity \(k\) in \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    kṣetra Field; a plane figure; hence kṣetragaṇita, geometry
    kuṭṭaka “Pulveriser”: the method for solving linear indeterminate equations
    laghu A light (short) syllable in prosody
    madhyamāharaṇa “Removal of the middle term”: solving a quadratic equation by completing the square
    mātrā A unit of syllabic duration (mora)
    mātrāvṛtta A moraic metre, defined by total duration
    meruprastāra The triangle of binomial coefficients (Halāyudha)
    miśraka “Mixture”: problems of interest, partnership, alloys and the like
    mūla “Root”: a square root; in problems of interest, the principal
    naṣṭa “Lost”: finding the pattern at a given place in an enumeration
    pañcarāśika The “rule of five”: compound proportion with five terms
    parikarman An arithmetical operation (Brahmagupta lists twenty)
    pāṭī The board on which calculations were made
    pāṭīgaṇita “Board calculation”: arithmetic and mensuration
    phala “Fruit”: the term corresponding to the measure in the rule of three
    prakīrṇaka “Miscellaneous”: problems in which an unknown whole is found from its parts
    prakṛti “Nature”: the coefficient \(N\) in \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    prakṣepaka “Contribution”: division in proportion to contributions; partnership
    pramāṇa “Measure”: the first term of the rule of three
    prastāra “Spreading out”: the systematic listing of all patterns
    rajju Cord; the measuring cord of the Śulbasūtras
    ṛṇa “Debt”: a negative quantity
    rūpa “Form”: unity; the absolute term of an equation
    saṃkhyāta Numerable; the lowest Jain class of quantity
    saṅkalita Summation; the sum of a series
    saṅkramaṇa “Concurrence”: finding two numbers from their sum and difference
    śaṅku Peg; also the gnomon
    saviśeṣa “With an excess”: the Śulba approximation to the diagonal of a square
    siddhānta A comprehensive treatise of mathematical astronomy
    siyāq (Pers.) Special numerals used by revenue accountants, derived from Arabic number words
    śreḍhī Series, progression
    sthūla, sūkṣma “Gross” and “accurate”: approximate and exact (or more exact) rules
    śulba Cord; hence Śulbasūtra, “rules of the cord”
    sūtra A concise rule; a text composed of such rules
    suvarṇagaṇita Calculation with gold: alloys and their fineness
    tantra An astronomical treatise computing from the current age
    tātkālikī gati Instantaneous velocity of a planet (Bhāskara II)
    trairāśika The rule of three
    uddiṣṭa “Indicated”: finding the place of a given pattern in an enumeration
    upapatti Demonstration, justification of a rule
    utkramajyā The versed sine, \(R - R\cos\theta\)
    vargaprakṛti “Square-nature”: the equation \(Nx^2 + k = y^2\)
    varṇa “Colour” or touch: the fineness of gold, pure gold having sixteen
    vāsanā Explanation, justification (Bhāskara II’s term)
    vedi A Vedic altar or sacrificial ground
    viloma “Reversal”: solving a problem by inverting its operations
    viṣamakarman “Dissimilar operation”: finding two numbers from the difference of their squares and their difference
    vyakta Known, manifest (quantity); hence vyaktagaṇita, arithmetic
    vyastatrairāśika The inverse rule of three
    vyavahāra A “determination”: a class of applied problems (mixtures, series, figures, etc.)
    vyutkalita The sum of a run of terms cut from a progression (Mahāvīra)
    yāvattāvat “As much as so much”: the first unknown in algebra
    yuga An age; a period of time used as a cycle in astronomy
    yukti Reasoning, rationale; hence Yuktibhāṣā (Mal.), “discourse on rationales”
    ẓabṭ (Pers.) Mughal assessment of revenue from the measured area of each crop
    zīj (Ar.) An astronomical handbook with tables
