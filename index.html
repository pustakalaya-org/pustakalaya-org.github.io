    Bhāratīya Jñāna Paramparā¶

    Indian Knowledge Systems: a series in twenty-four volumes

    Bhāratīya Jñāna Paramparā is a series of twenty-four volumes on the knowledge traditions of the Indian subcontinent, by Nikhil Kumar Rajput of Ramanujan College, University of Delhi. The volumes run from the Vedic corpus, the Upaniṣads and the schools of philosophy, through Buddhist, Jain, yogic and tantric traditions, the epics, Purāṇas, poetics, music and theatre, to grammar, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, chemistry, agriculture, architecture, crafts, polity, trade, warfare, geography, psychology and education, and the knowledge of tribal and folk communities. The last volume sets the traditions side by side and asks what they share and where they really differ.

    The books follow common conventions. Sanskrit words are given in the International Alphabet of Sanskrit Transliteration (IAST). Dates are given in the form accepted in current scholarship, and traditional dates are labelled as such. Where scholars or traditions differ, the volumes state both views. Quotations from primary texts are kept only where their wording and source could be confirmed; they have not yet been collated against critical editions. Material on medicine, astrology and similar practices is presented as the history of ideas and not as advice. Each volume ends with a select bibliography, a glossary and, in most cases, a chronology.

    Language¶

    A book for learning English, in three editions

    A book for learners of English, with grammar, speaking, vocabulary and writing, and a short set of exercises, with answers, at the end of every chapter. It is published in three editions with the same chapters: the English edition, the Hinglish edition (Hindi in the Roman script, as the book was first written) and the Hindi edition (in Devanagari).

    Pustakālaya is a reading room. Tap a cover to open the book in reading mode, in the manner of an e-book reader: page by page, at the place where you stopped, with your choice of type size, spacing and a white, sepia or dark page. The small i on a cover opens the book's own page, with its full contents. Its first collection is the series Bhāratīya Jñāna Paramparā, on the knowledge traditions of the Indian subcontinent; the others hold books on science and technology, quantum computing, computing and networks, cyber security, university courses and the English language. Chapter, section, figure and table numbers, and the cross-references, are those of the books' own editions. Use the search box in the header to search the text of all books. The site can be installed as a reading app on an iPad, a phone or a laptop, and any book can be kept on the device for reading without a connection (see About this edition).

    More about how the books were made, and about their images, is on the About this edition page.
