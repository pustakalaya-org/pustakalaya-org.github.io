    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to how data centres are designed, built, powered, cooled, run and certified — including the AI data-centre boom. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, and a table of the equipment, standards, metrics, vendors, tools and careers you need to know.
