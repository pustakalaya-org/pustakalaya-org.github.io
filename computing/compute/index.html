    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to how computers compute — from logic gates to CPUs, GPUs, AI accelerators, memory, operating systems, HPC and cloud. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, and a table of the chips, architectures, standards, tools and vendors you need to know.
