    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to machine learning — data, classic algorithms, deep learning, transformers and LLMs, MLOps, responsible AI and careers. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, a diagram, and a table of the algorithms, models, techniques, tools & concepts you need to know.
