    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to databases — SQL, data modelling, internals, PostgreSQL to MongoDB, distributed and vector databases, analytics, operations and careers. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, a diagram, and a table of the databases, concepts, languages, tools & standards you need to know.
