    About this book¶

    A student's plain-English guide to mobile networks — from 1G to 5G-Advanced and 6G: radio, spectrum, RAN, Open RAN, the 5G core, slicing, security, use cases and careers. Every topic is explained with an everyday analogy, a worked example, step-by-step mechanics, a diagram, and a table of the standards, network functions, technologies & tools you need to know.
