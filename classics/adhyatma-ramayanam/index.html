    About this book¶

    The Rāmāyaṇa of Tuñcattu Rāmānujan Eḻuttacchan (sixteenth century), called the father of modern Malayalam: a devotional retelling of the Sanskrit Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa in the kiḷippāṭṭu (parrot-song) form, in which a parrot is asked to sing the story. Its six kāṇḍas are read through in Kerala households in the month of Karkkaṭakam (July–August). The text as transcribed on Malayalam Wikisource, kāṇḍa by kāṇḍa and episode by episode.
