    Master Lesson Plan¶

    How to Use This Plan¶

    The syllabus allocates 45 lecture hours across four units (10 + 10 + 15 + 10). This master plan converts those hours into 45 self-contained one-hour lessons. Each lesson has its own detailed note PDF in the corresponding Unit-n folder, containing:

    • Learning outcomes for that hour
    • Formal definitions
    • Full mathematical development with derivations
    • 4–5 fully solved problems
    • Practice problems with answers
    • A one-page summary and a link to the interactive web platform for that unit

    Pacing advice. Optimization Methods is a procedural subject: students learn by executing algorithms by hand. Reserve the last 15 minutes of every lecture for one solved problem worked live on the board, and let students verify it afterwards on the unit's interactive platform.

    Unit I — Linear Programming, Transportation and Assignment (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    1 L01 Operations Research and the Modelling Approach History, phases of an OR study, model taxonomy, decision variables, objective, constraints
    2 L02 Introduction to Linear Programming and Formulation Standard/canonical forms, assumptions of LP, formulating product-mix, diet and blending problems
    3 L03 Graphical Method Feasible region, corner-point theorem, iso-profit lines, unbounded / infeasible / multiple optima
    4 L04 Simplex Method I — Theory Slack variables, basic solutions, BFS ↔ corner points, optimality and entering/leaving rules
    5 L05 Simplex Method II — The Tableau Algorithm Full tableau iterations, pivoting, minimum-ratio test, reading the optimal solution
    6 L06 Big-M and Two-Phase Methods; Special Cases Artificial variables, degeneracy, unboundedness, alternate optima, infeasibility
    7 L07 Duality and Sensitivity (support topic) Primal–dual pairs, weak/strong duality, shadow prices, complementary slackness
    8 L08 Transportation Problem — Formulation and Initial BFS Balanced/unbalanced models, NWCM, LCM, VAM
    9 L09 Transportation Problem — Optimality by MODI / Stepping Stone \(u_i+v_j\) multipliers, opportunity costs, looping, degeneracy resolution
    10 L10 Assignment Problem and the Travelling Salesman Problem Hungarian method, unbalanced and maximisation cases, TSP via assignment with sub-tour elimination

    Unit I checkpoint: 20-mark class test after Hour 10.

    Unit II — Game Theory, Sequencing, Integer Programming and Networks (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    11 L11 Game Theory — Two-Person Zero-Sum Games, Pure Strategies Payoff matrix, maximin/minimax, saddle point, value of the game
    12 L12 Mixed Strategies and Dominance Odds method for \(2\times2\), dominance rules, expected payoff
    13 L13 Graphical and LP Solution of Games \(2\times n\) and \(m\times 2\) graphical method, LP formulation of an arbitrary game
    14 L14 Sequencing — n Jobs through 2 Machines Johnson's rule, total elapsed time, idle time computation
    15 L15 Sequencing — n Jobs through 3 Machines and 2 Jobs through m Machines Reduction to two machines, graphical method with 45° movement
    16 L16 Integer Programming — Formulation and Gomory's Cutting Plane Why rounding fails, fractional cut derivation, pure IP algorithm
    17 L17 Gomory's Method for Mixed Integer Programs Mixed cut construction, worked MILP
    18 L18 Branch and Bound Method Branching, bounding, fathoming, 0–1 knapsack and general IP
    19 L19 Network Scheduling — Basic Terms and CPM AON/AOA, forward/backward pass, floats, critical path, crashing
    20 L20 PERT Three time estimates, Beta distribution, project variance, probability of completion

    Unit II checkpoint: 20-mark class test after Hour 20.

    Unit III — Queuing Theory and Inventory Control (15 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    21 L21 Characteristics of Queuing Systems Input process, service mechanism, queue discipline, Kendall–Lee notation, terminology
    22 L22 The Poisson Process and the Exponential Distribution Axioms, derivation of the Poisson pmf, memorylessness, inter-arrival times
    23 L23 Birth–Death Processes and Steady-State Balance Equations Rate diagrams, general birth–death solution, ergodicity
    24 L24 Model I — (M/M/1):(∞/FCFS) Derivation \(P_n=(1-\rho)\rho^n\), \(L_s, L_q, W_s, W_q\), Little's law
    25 L25 Model I — Applications and Problems Costing, design decisions, extensive numerical practice
    26 L26 Model II — (M/M/1):(N/FCFS) Finite Capacity Truncated queue, effective arrival rate, blocking probability
    27 L27 Model III/IV — (M/M/C):(∞/FCFS) Multi-Server Queue Erlang-C, derivation of \(P_0\), \(L_q\), server utilisation
    28 L28 Model IV/V — (M/M/C):(N/FCFS) and Finite-Source Queues Finite waiting room with \(c\) servers, machine-repair model
    29 L29 Applications and Economic Analysis of Queues Cost of waiting vs cost of service, optimal number of servers, simulation cross-check
    30 L30 Inventory Control — Concepts and Cost Structure Functions of inventory, holding/ordering/shortage costs, deterministic vs probabilistic, ABC
    31 L31 EOQ Model with No Shortage Derivation of \(Q^*=\sqrt{2C_oD/C_h}\), total cost, cycle time, sensitivity
    32 L32 EOQ Model with Shortages Permitted Backorder model, optimal \(Q^*\) and \(S^*\), cost split
    33 L33 Production (Finite Replenishment) Model without Shortage EPQ derivation, \(Q^*=\sqrt{2C_oD/(C_h(1-D/P))}\)
    34 L34 Production Model with Shortage Four-parameter model, maximum inventory and shortage levels
    35 L35 Quantity Discounts and Consolidation All-units discount algorithm, multi-item constraints, unified revision

    Unit III checkpoint: 30-mark test after Hour 35.

    Unit IV — Search Methods and Non-Linear Programming (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    36 L36 Introduction to Non-Linear Programming Classification of optimisation problems, convex sets and functions, unimodality
    37 L37 Conditions for Unconstrained Optimality Stationary points, Hessian test, single- and multi-variable optimality conditions
    38 L38 One-Dimensional Minimisation — Bracketing and Elimination Unrestricted search, exhaustive search, dichotomous and interval-halving methods
    39 L39 Fibonacci Search Method Fibonacci sequence, reduction ratio, algorithm and full numerical trace
    40 L40 Golden Section Search Golden ratio derivation, algorithm, comparison with Fibonacci
    41 L41 Interpolation and Gradient-Based 1-D Methods Quadratic interpolation, Newton–Raphson, secant, quasi-Newton
    42 L42 Unconstrained Multivariable Optimisation Steepest descent, Newton's method, conjugate gradient (Fletcher–Reeves)
    43 L43 Constrained Optimisation — Graphical Solution and Lagrange Multipliers Equality constraints, bordered Hessian, sensitivity meaning of \(\lambda\)
    44 L44 Kuhn–Tucker (KKT) Conditions Inequality constraints, complementary slackness, constraint qualification, sufficiency under convexity
    45 L45 Quadratic Programming QP standard form, KKT system, Wolfe's method, Beale's method

    Interactive Support¶

    Each unit has a browser-based interactive platform in the folder Optimization Methods/Interactive-Web-Platform/. Open index.html in any modern browser — no installation, no internet connection required.

    Unit Platform What Students Practise
    I unit1-lp-lab.html Graphical LP solver, simplex tableau stepper, transportation and Hungarian solvers
    II unit2-games-networks-lab.html Game solver, Johnson's rule Gantt chart, branch-and-bound tree, CPM/PERT network
    III unit3-queue-inventory-lab.html Live M/M/1 and M/M/c calculators, discrete-event queue simulator, EOQ sawtooth explorer
    IV unit4-nlp-search-lab.html Function plotter with Fibonacci/golden-section traces, gradient descent on contours, KKT checker
