    Master Lesson Plan¶

    How to Use This Plan¶

    The syllabus allocates 45 lecture hours across four units (10 + 15 + 10 + 10). This master plan converts those hours into 45 self-contained one-hour lessons. Each lesson has its own detailed note PDF in the corresponding Unit-n folder, containing:

    • Learning outcomes for that hour
    • Formal definitions
    • Full mathematical development with derivations
    • 4–5 fully solved problems
    • Practice problems with answers
    • A one-page summary and a link to the interactive web platform for that unit

    Pacing advice. A one-hour lesson note is intentionally denser than one hour of chalk-talk. Use the Explanation and Mathematics sections in class and set the Solved Problems section as guided self-study. The Unit interactive platform is designed to be opened live in class.

    Unit I — Fundamentals of Quantum Computing (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    1 L01 Introduction and Motivation Moore's law limits, reversible computation, classical vs quantum models, BQP, DiVincenzo criteria
    2 L02 Linear Algebra I — Complex Vector Spaces and Dirac Notation \(\mathbb{C}^n\), kets, bras, linear independence, basis, spanning sets
    3 L03 Linear Algebra II — Inner Products and Hilbert Spaces Inner product axioms, norm, orthonormality, Gram–Schmidt, Cauchy–Schwarz
    4 L04 Linear Algebra III — Operators, Eigenvalues, Tensor Products Hermitian and unitary operators, spectral theorem, outer products, \(\otimes\)
    5 L05 Qubits, Superposition and the Bloch Sphere Qubit postulate, global vs relative phase, Bloch coordinates
    6 L06 Quantum Measurement and the Postulates Born rule, projective measurement, observables, expectation, collapse
    7 L07 Single-Qubit Gates and the Circuit Model \(X,Y,Z,H,S,T\), rotation gates, circuit diagrams, unitarity, reversibility
    8 L08 Multi-Qubit Gates and Entanglement CNOT, CZ, SWAP, Toffoli, Bell states, product vs entangled states, universality
    9 L09 Quantum Parallelism, Interference and No-Cloning \(U_f\) oracle, evaluating \(f\) on all inputs, constructive/destructive interference, no-cloning proof
    10 L10 Teleportation, Superdense Coding and Error Correction Teleportation protocol, superdense coding, bit-flip/phase-flip codes, Shor 9-qubit code

    Unit I assessment checkpoint: a 20-mark class test after Hour 10 covering L01–L10.

    Unit II — Quantum Algorithms and Post-Quantum Security (15 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    11 L11 Quantum Oracles and Phase Kick-Back Query model, \(U_f\) construction, the \(\lvert-\rangle\) trick, phase kick-back
    12 L12 The Deutsch Algorithm Constant vs balanced for \(n=1\), one query vs two
    13 L13 The Deutsch–Jozsa Algorithm \(n\)-bit generalisation, Hadamard transform identity, exponential separation
    14 L14 The Bernstein–Vazirani Algorithm Hidden string recovery, one query vs \(n\) queries
    15 L15 Simon's Algorithm Hidden subgroup, linear system over \(\mathbb{F}_2\), exponential speed-up
    16 L16 Number Theory Recap and the RSA Cryptosystem Euler totient, modular exponentiation, key generation, correctness proof
    17 L17 Factorization Attack on RSA and Order Finding Reduction from factoring to period finding, classical difficulty
    18 L18 The Quantum Fourier Transform DFT vs QFT, product-form derivation, \(O(n^2)\) circuit
    19 L19 Quantum Phase Estimation Eigenvalue extraction, precision analysis, success probability
    20 L20 Shor's Algorithm for Integer Factorization Full algorithm, continued fractions, worked factorisation of 15 and 21
    21 L21 Grover's Algorithm — Oracle and Diffusion Amplitude amplification circuit, inversion about the mean
    22 L22 Grover's Algorithm — Analysis and Optimality Rotation-in-2D picture, optimal \(\lfloor \pi\sqrt{N}/4 \rfloor\) iterations, lower bound
    23 L23 Grover Applied to Cryptography — Hash Preimage Attacks Key search, preimage search, the "double your key length" rule
    24 L24 The HHL Algorithm for Linear Systems Solving \(A\vec{x}=\vec{b}\), condition number, caveats
    25 L25 Post-Quantum Cryptography — Kyber and PQ Signatures Lattices, LWE/MLWE, ML-KEM (Kyber), ML-DSA (Dilithium), SPHINCS+

    Unit II assessment checkpoint: 30-mark test after Hour 25.

    Unit III — Quantum Machine Learning and Optimization (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    26 L26 Introduction to QML and Data Encoding Basis/amplitude/angle encoding, feature maps, PQCs, the four QML quadrants
    27 L27 Quantum Kernel Methods and QSVM Kernel trick recap, quantum kernel estimation, SVM dual, QSVM pipeline
    28 L28 Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC) Ansatz design, cost functions, hybrid optimisation loop
    29 L29 Quantum Neural Networks and the Parameter-Shift Rule QNN architecture, analytic gradients, barren plateaus
    30 L30 Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks (QCNN) Convolution and pooling layers on qubits, MERA connection
    31 L31 Quantum Linear Regression Least squares via HHL, quantum-enhanced regression
    32 L32 Quantum k-Means Clustering Swap test, distance estimation, the quantum Lloyd algorithm
    33 L33 Quantum Boltzmann Machines Classical RBM recap, transverse-field Hamiltonian, training QBMs
    34 L34 Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) QUBO/Ising mapping, cost and mixer Hamiltonians, Max-Cut worked end-to-end
    35 L35 Quantum Annealing and the Adiabatic Model Adiabatic theorem, spectral gap, D-Wave, annealing vs QAOA

    Unit IV — Quantum Simulation Tools and Platforms (10 hours)¶

    Hr Lesson Topic Key Ideas Delivered
    36 L36 The Quantum Software Landscape NISQ hardware, stack layers, OpenQASM, transpilation, simulator types
    37 L37 IBM Qiskit I — Circuits and Simulation QuantumCircuit, Aer, Sampler/Estimator primitives, statevector
    38 L38 IBM Qiskit II — Transpilation, Noise and Hardware Coupling maps, optimisation levels, noise models, error mitigation
    39 L39 Google Cirq Devices, moments, cirq.Simulator, gate sets, qubit topologies
    40 L40 PennyLane QNodes, devices, autodiff, interfaces with PyTorch/JAX

    Interactive Support¶

    Each unit has a browser-based interactive platform in the folder Quantum Computing and its Applications/Interactive-Web-Platform/. Open index.html in any modern browser — no installation, no internet connection required.

    Unit Platform What Students Practise
    I unit1-qubit-lab.html Bloch sphere, gate action, state vectors, entanglement, measurement statistics
    II unit2-algorithms-lab.html Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Grover, QFT, RSA/Shor factoring
    III unit3-qml-lab.html Feature maps, kernels, VQC training, QAOA on Max-Cut, annealing
    IV unit4-platforms-lab.html Circuit builder with code emission for Qiskit / Cirq / PennyLane / Q#
