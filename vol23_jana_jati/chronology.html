    Chronology¶

    Dates before the colonial period are approximate. Traditional dates are not used.

    Date Event
    Mesolithic onwards Rock paintings at Bhimbetka and other shelters of central India
    mid-3rd c. BCE Aśoka’s Thirteenth Rock Edict addresses the forest peoples (aṭavi)
    c. 50 BCE–300 CE Composition of the Arthaśāstra, with its policy towards forest chiefs
    1228–1826 Ahom kingdom in the Brahmaputra valley
    1564 Rani Durgavati of Garha-Mandla dies fighting a Mughal army
    1730 Khejarli: according to Bishnoi tradition, Amrita Devi and others killed defending khejṛī trees
    1855–56 Santal hul led by Sidhu and Kanhu Murmu
    1865, 1878 Indian Forest Acts assert state control of forests
    1866 Hislop’s papers, with the first record of the Gond Lingo narrative, published
    1871 Criminal Tribes Act
    1873 Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation creates the Inner Line
    1887 Skrefsrud publishes the Santal traditions narrated by Kolean guru
    1894 National Forest Policy; Ramdas Tudu “Reska” publishes his book on Santal religion and custom
    1897 Dietrich Brandis, Indian Forestry, notes the sacred groves
    1899 Seng Khasi movement founded
    1899–1900 Birsa Munda’s ulgulān; Birsa dies in Ranchi jail (1900)
    1906 W. H. R. Rivers, The Todas
    1908 Chotanagpur Tenancy Act
    1910 Bhumkal rebellion in Bastar
    1911, 1924 Criminal Tribes Act extended and consolidated; settlements for notified communities, several run by missions
    1912 S. C. Roy, The Mundas and Their Country; his The Oraons of Chota Nagpur follows in 1915
    1914 Tana Bhagat movement begins among the Oraon
    1916 Henry Whitehead, The Village Gods of South India
    1922–23 Devi movement among the Bhils of south Gujarat
    1922–24 Rampa rebellion led by Alluri Sitarama Raju
    1925 Raghunath Murmu’s Ol Chiki script for Santali (usual date)
    1927 Indian Forest Act
    1934 Bihar earthquake; W. G. Archer sees Mithila wall paintings
    1935 Government of India Act creates Excluded and Partially Excluded Areas; Young Lushai Association (renamed Young Mizo Association in 1947) founded at Aizawl
    1938 Adivasi Mahasabha founded in Chotanagpur (Jaipal Singh president from 1939)
    1939 Verrier Elwin, The Baiga
    1940 Death of Komaram Bheem
    1943 G. S. Ghurye, The Aborigines—“So-called”—and Their Future
    1946 Jaipal Singh’s speech in the Constituent Assembly (19 December)
    1949–50 Committee of inquiry recommends repeal of the Criminal Tribes Act; Adivasi Mahasabha becomes the Jharkhand Party
    1950 Constitution in force, with the Fifth and Sixth Schedules
    1952 Criminal Tribes Act repealed and the communities denotified; Jharkhand Party becomes the main opposition in the Bihar Assembly; National Forest Policy; Vanvasi Kalyan Ashram founded
    1955 States Reorganisation Commission rejects the demand for a Jharkhand state
    1958 India ratifies ILO Convention 107
    1959 Nehru’s “Tribal Panchsheel” in Elwin’s A Philosophy for NEFA; mautām famine in the Mizo hills
    1963 Nagaland becomes a state; Jharkhand Party merges with the Congress
    1965 Lokur Committee on the scheduling of tribes
    1966 Mizo uprising
    1972 Wild Life (Protection) Act; Meghalaya becomes a state
    1972–73 Jharkhand Mukti Morcha founded
    1973 Project Tiger; Chipko movement begins in Garhwal
    1975 Gadgil and Vartak’s paper on sacred groves
    1978 Nagaland Village and Area Councils Act gives village councils statutory form
    1978 Irula Snake Catchers’ Industrial Co-operative Society founded
    1980 Forest (Conservation) Act; Laxman Mane’s Upara
    1981–82 Jangarh Singh Shyam joins Bharat Bhavan, Bhopal (opened 1982)
    1984–87 Bhagwandas Patel records the Bhārath of the Dungri Bhils
    1985 Tarun Bharat Sangh begins johaḍ work in Alwar
    1986–87 Mizo Accord (1986); Mizoram and Arunachal Pradesh become states (1987)
    1987 Laxman Gaikwad’s Uchalya; Jharkhand Coordination Committee formed
    1988 National Forest Policy puts forest dwellers’ needs first
    1990 National guidelines on Joint Forest Management
    1991 Gondwana Gantantra Party founded
    1992 73rd Amendment (panchayats); Convention on Biological Diversity
    1993 World Bank withdraws from the Sardar Sarovar project
    1995 Jharkhand Area Autonomous Council
    1996 PESA; Madhu Kishwar v. State of Bihar; Bhasha Research and Publication Centre founded at Vadodara
    mid-1990s Benefit-sharing arrangement between TBGRI and the Kani of Kerala
    1997 Samatha judgment; Patel’s Bhīlonuṃ Bhārath published in Gujarati
    1998 Death of Budhan Sabar in police custody; Budhan Theatre founded in Chharanagar, Ahmedabad; Narayan’s Kocharethi; Khonoma bans hunting
    2000 Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh become states; neem patent revoked by the European Patent Office; first Hornbill Festival in Nagaland
    2001 Traditional Knowledge Digital Library established; Jharkhand anti-witch-hunting law
    2002 Biological Diversity Act; G. N. Devy’s anthology Painted Words
    2003 Santali and Bodo added to the Eighth Schedule; Bodoland Territorial Council; Muthanga firing
    2004 Indian Ocean tsunami strikes the Andaman and Nicobar Islands
    2005 Salwa Judum launched in Bastar; Janani Suraksha Yojana
    2006 Forest Rights Act
    2007 UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples; Indira Gandhi National Tribal University
    2008 Report of the National Commission for Denotified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes
    2009 Mendha-Lekha receives community forest rights
    2010 Sowa-Rigpa recognised as a system of medicine
    2011 Nandini Sundar v. State of Chhattisgarh; Mendha-Lekha sells its bamboo; Vahia and Halkare survey Gond sky knowledge in fifteen villages
    2012 India ratifies the Nagoya Protocol; the Adivasi-run press adivaani founded in Kolkata
    2013 Niyamgiri gram sabhas reject bauxite mining; Land Acquisition Act requires gram sabha consent in Scheduled Areas
    2014 Report of the High Level Committee chaired by Virginius Xaxa; Apatani cultural landscape placed on India’s World Heritage Tentative List
    2017–18 Pathalgaḍī movement in Khunti, Jharkhand; Jharkhand bans Hansda Sowvendra Shekhar’s The Adivasi Will Not Dance (2017, later withdrawn); second national commission on the Denotified Tribes reports
    2019 Supreme Court eviction order under the FRA stayed
    2020 Jharkhand Assembly resolution for a Sarna religion code
    2021 15 November declared Janjātīya Gaurav Divas
    2022 Living root bridge landscapes of Meghalaya (jingkieng jri) placed on India’s World Heritage Tentative List
    2023 Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act; International Year of Millets
