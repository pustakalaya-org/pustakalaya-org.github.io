    Chronology¶

    The dates below are approximate; many early dates are disputed, and the ranges given are those generally accepted in current scholarship. Traditional dates are not used.

    Date Event or text
    c. 2600–1900 BCE Mature Harappan cities with walls and citadels
    c. 1500–1200 BCE Core hymns of the Ṛgveda; chariot warfare; battle of the ten kings
    c. 1200–500 BCE Later Vedic period; spread of iron
    6th–5th c. BCE The sixteen mahājanapadas; rise of Magadha
    c. 400 BCE–400 CE Composition of the Mahābhārata and Rāmāyaṇa
    326 BCE Alexander defeats Porus at the Hydaspes (Jhelum)
    c. 321 BCE Candragupta Maurya founds the Mauryan empire
    c. 303 BCE Treaty with Seleucus; 500 elephants sent west
    c. 261 BCE Aśoka’s conquest of Kaliṅga (Rock Edict XIII)
    c. 50 BCE–300 CE Arthaśāstra (Olivelle’s dating)
    c. 1st–3rd c. CE Tamil Caṅkam war poetry (core)
    c. 2nd c. CE Mānava Dharmaśāstra
    4th–5th c.  Gupta empire; Samudragupta’s Allahabad inscription; Delhi iron pillar
    late 5th–6th c.  Hūṇa invasions
    634 Aihoḷe inscription of Pulakeśin II records the defeat of Harṣa
    711–712 Arab conquest of Sindh
    c. 8th–11th c.  Agni Purāṇa compiled, with dhanurveda chapters
    1000–1027 Raids of Maḥmūd of Ghazni
    c. 1025 Cōḻa raid on Śrīvijaya
    c. 1129–1131 Mānasollāsa of Someśvara III
    1191, 1192 First and second battles of Tarain
    1206 Delhi Sultanate established
    1296–1316 Reign of ʿAlāʾ al-Dīn Khaljī; Chittor taken 1303; southern campaigns 1307–1311
    1336, 1347 Foundation of Vijayanagara and of the Bahmanī sultanate
    1398 Tīmūr sacks Delhi
    15th c.  Firearms appear in Indian warfare
    1498 Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut
    1518 Maḥmūd II of Malwa restored with Gujarati help; the Purbiya soldiers driven from Mandu
    1526 First battle of Pānīpat; Bābur founds the Mughal empire
    1528 Bābur storms Chanderi, held by the Purbiya chief Medinī Rāy
    1536 The Ahoms sack the Kachari capital, Dimapur
    c. 1562–1563 Koch invasion of the Ahom kingdom under Cilārāy
    1565 Battle of Talikota (Rakshasa–Tangadi)
    1567–1568 Akbar’s siege of Chittor
    1576 Battle of Haldighati
    1595–1596 Chand Bībī’s defence of Ahmadnagar
    1600 Execution of the fourth Kuñjāli Marakkār
    1659 Śivājī kills Afzal Khān at Pratapgad
    1671 Ahom victory over the Mughals at Saraighat
    1674 Coronation of Śivājī
    1681–1707 Aurangzeb’s wars in the Deccan
    c. 1698–1729 Kānhojī Āṅgre, Maratha admiral
    1699 Guru Gobind Singh institutes the Khalsa
    1734 Hastividyārṇava completed in Assam
    1742–1751 Maratha (bargī) raids into Bengal
    1748 Sikh fighting bands act together as the Dal Khālsā
    1757 Battle of Plassey
    1761 Third battle of Pānīpat
    1763 Mīr Qāsim of Bengal, with his new regular battalions, defeated by the Company
    1764 Battle of Buxar
    1767–1799 Anglo-Mysore wars; Mysore rockets; Srirangapatna falls 1799
    1768–1769 Gorkha conquest of the Kathmandu valley
    1784 Benoît de Boigne enters the service of Mahādjī Shinde
    1788–1792 Wars between Nepal and Tibet; a Qing army enters Nepal (1792)
    1798 The Nizam’s French-officered corps disbanded under a subsidiary alliance
    1799–1839 Ranjit Singh’s kingdom of Punjab
    1801 Awadh cedes about half its territory to the Company
    1803 Battle of Assaye
    c. 1804 Congreve’s rocket experiments in Britain, influenced by Mysore rockets
    1808–1809 Rising of Vēlu Tampi against the Company in Travancore
    1814–1816 Anglo-Nepal war; Treaty of Sugauli
    1815 The Company begins to recruit Gurkhas after the surrender at Malaun; the British annex Kandy in Sri Lanka
    1817 Paika rising in Odisha
    1817–1818 Pindārī and Third Anglo-Maratha wars; Khadki, Sitabuldi, Mahidpur, Koregaon
    1822 Allard and Ventura enter Ranjit Singh’s service
    1824 Rising of Cennammā of Kittūr
    1826 Treaty of Yandabo: Burma renounces Assam, Manipur, Cachar and Jaintia
    1831–1832 Kol rising in Chota Nagpur
    1834–1841 Zorāwar Siṅgh’s campaigns in Ladakh, Baltistan and western Tibet
    1843 Miani and Dubba (Sindh); Maharajpur and Panniar (Gwalior)
    1845–1849 Anglo-Sikh wars
    1855–1856 Santal rising (hul)
    1857–1858 The great rising; death of the Rāṇī of Jhānsī
    1878 Indian Arms Act
    1888–1889 Imperial Service Troops of the princely states instituted
    1899–1900 Birsa Munda’s rising (ulgulān) in Chota Nagpur
    1911 Indian soldiers become eligible for the Victoria Cross
    1914–1918 About 1.3 million Indians serve in the First World War
    1932 Indian Military Academy opens at Dehradun
    1939–1945 The Indian Army grows to about 2.5 million
    1946 Mutiny of the Royal Indian Navy at Bombay and other ports
    1947 Independence and Partition; the armed forces divided between India and Pakistan
    1959 Arms Act replaces the Act of 1878
