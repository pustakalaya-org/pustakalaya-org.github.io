    About this book¶

    The first three books of the Mahābhārata (Ādi, Sabhā and Vana Parva) in Ganguli's prose translation of 1883–96. Translated into English prose from the Sanskrit text by Kisari Mohan Ganguli (died 1908), published in parts at Calcutta between 1883 and 1896. This book holds the first three books (Ādi, Sabhā and Vana Parva), about 640,000 words; the other fifteen books are in the same translation. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
