    About this book¶

    The introduction to the Jātakas and the first of the Buddha's birth stories, in the translation of T. W. Rhys Davids (1880). The Jātaka commentary, text edited by V. Fausbøll (1821–1908) and translated by T. W. Rhys Davids (1843–1922); the translation of the first volume was begun by R. C. Childers (died 1876). London, 1880. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
