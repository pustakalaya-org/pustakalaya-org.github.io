    About this book¶

    The Hitopadeśa, the tale of Nala and Damayantī, selections from the Rāmāyaṇa and Śakuntalā, with poems by Toru Dutt. An anthology introduced by Epiphanius Wilson (died 1916), 1900, with translations by Sir Edwin Arnold (died 1904: the Hitopadeśa), Sir Monier Monier-Williams (died 1899: Śakuntalā), Ralph T. H. Griffith (died 1906: the Rāmāyaṇa) and poems by Toru Dutt (died 1877). This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
