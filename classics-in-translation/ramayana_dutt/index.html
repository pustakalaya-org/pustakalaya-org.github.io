    About this book¶

    All seven kāṇḍas of the Rāmāyaṇa in Manmatha Nath Dutt's prose translation (1891–94). Translated into English prose from the Sanskrit of Vālmīki by Manmatha Nath Dutt (1855–1912), Calcutta, 1891–1894, in four volumes. This edition is in the public domain: its author and its translator or editor died more than sixty years ago (India), and it was published before 1930 (United States).
